Before every flight, a pilot walks around the aircraft and checks it with their own eyes. Look at the control surfaces, the tyres, the fuel level and the fuel quality. Confirm that the weather and the route suit the flight, and that the paperwork is on board.

A checklist keeps this routine reliable. Work through it in the same order every time, so a missed step stands out.
