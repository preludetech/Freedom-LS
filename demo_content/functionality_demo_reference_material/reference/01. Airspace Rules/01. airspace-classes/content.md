Airspace is divided into classes, from A to G. The class says who may fly there and what contact they must keep with air traffic control.

In the most controlled classes, every aircraft needs a clearance and must follow instructions. In the least controlled class, pilots may fly without contact, and they must watch for other traffic themselves.
