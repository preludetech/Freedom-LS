title: No-fly Zones
uuid: af9a04e1-8e51-456c-8173-15f1edba2746
---

Some areas are closed to most or all flights. Examples are airports without permission to enter, military sites, prisons and areas around major events.

Check a current airspace chart or notice before each flight. Restrictions change, and a zone that was open last week may be closed today.
