# Research: content structure and completion, for test-out and revision questions

Scope: what FLS already has that a "test-out" (pre-assessment that skips sections, including a section's blocking quiz) and section-tied revision questions would build on. All paths are under the repo root.

## 1. Course structure

- `Course` (`freedom_ls/content_engine/models/courses.py`) holds an ordered list of children through `ContentCollectionItem` (a generic-FK through model: `collection` is a Course or CoursePart, `child` is any content type, plus `order` and the per-placement due-date columns). Course and CoursePart each get `collection_items()` and `children()`, memoised per instance.
- `CoursePart` ("a chapter or section") has `title`, `slug`, `description`, `category` (free text) and its own items. It is the only grouping concept. The Part-level status (`derive_part_status`, `get_content_status` in `freedom_ls/learner_interface/utils.py`) already summarises a part from its children, and the UI already shows "part-level progress" and a "needs retry" part (docs/product/learner-experience.md, Course Parts).
- Parts do not nest. `Course.collection_items_flat()` flattens exactly one level (`isinstance(item.child, CoursePart)` then `item.child.collection_items()`); the content schema's `CoursePart.children` is a list of `Child`, whose own `children` field only carries due-date overrides (`PartChildDueDate`), not further parts.
- A CoursePart is a shared content row (slug unique per site), so one part can be placed in several courses; `CoursePartChildDueDate` exists because per-course values for a shared part's children must hang off the course's placement of the part.
- Placement, not content, is the unit of progress. The same topic or form placed twice is two items (`TopicProgress.collection_item`, `CourseFormAttempt.collection_item`). Any "skipped" record has to key on the placement (`ContentCollectionItem.id`), and a part placement is a `ContentCollectionItem` whose child is a CoursePart.
- Demo/real use of parts is not consistent. Under `demo_content/`, only `functionality_demo_course_parts` (and `functionality_demo_course_parts_placement`) use parts (3 parts: Getting Started, Core Concepts with a knowledge-check form, Wrapping Up with a feedback form). The other demo courses (`functionality_demo_end_with_quiz`, `_end_with_topic`, `_standard_markdown`, `_content_widgets`, price courses) are flat lists of topics and forms. Parts are optional; a course can be a flat list, with a final quiz placed at the end or a quiz in the middle. A test-out design cannot assume every course has parts.
- The "final blocking quiz" in the idea is therefore not a structural thing: it is just a `Form` with `strategy == QUIZ` and a `quiz_pass_percentage`, placed as the last child of a part (or at any point in a flat course). Nothing marks a form as "the section's final quiz".

## 2. Completion and gating

- Topic: learner POSTs "mark done" in `freedom_ls/learner_interface/views.py` (around line 1668); it `get_or_create`s `TopicProgress` (`freedom_ls/learner_progress/models.py`) for (course_progress, collection_item) and stamps `complete_time` on first completion. Opening creates the row (`start_time`); `complete_time` null means opened, not done.
- Form: the attempt is a `form_engine.FormProgress` (`completed_time`, `scores`, answers) linked to a course by `CourseFormAttempt` (course_progress, collection_item, one-to-one form_progress). Many attempts per placement are kept; retakes are unlimited.
- Pass mark: `Form.quiz_pass_percentage` (required when strategy is QUIZ, forbidden otherwise; `freedom_ls/form_engine/schema.py`). `FormProgress.passed()` compares `quiz_percentage()` to it. `quiz_verdict(form, form_progress)` and `attempt_completes_form(attempt)` in `freedom_ls/form_engine/queries.py` give the verdict: a failed scored quiz does NOT complete the item; surveys, unscored forms and quizzes without a pass mark complete on submission.
- Which sitting decides a placement: `completed_form_item_ids(attempts)` in `freedom_ls/learner_progress/queries.py` (latest completed attempt by (completed_time, start_time), placement-keyed). `completed_collection_item_ids(record)` is the single definition of "done" for a placement (topic rows with `complete_time` plus the form set). Percentage, outline and finish page all read it.
- Percentage: `calculate_course_progress_percentage(course, completed_ids)` in `freedom_ls/learner_progress/utils.py` = completed placements / TOPIC-or-FORM placements (Activities and part sentinels excluded), rounded. Recomputed by `recalculate_progress_percentage(record)` in `freedom_ls/learner_progress/signals.py`, triggered by `post_save` on `TopicProgress` (only on the None-to-set transition, `CourseItemProgress.newly_completed_item`) and by the `form_attempt_completed` signal. Bulk writes bypass it; `recalculate_progress_percentages` management command is the repair tool.
- Course complete: `CourseProgress.completed_time` is set in exactly one place, the `course_finish` view (`freedom_ls/learner_interface/views.py` ~line 2169): only when `outstanding_items(course_progress, course)` (`learner_interface/utils.py`) is empty, i.e. every TOPIC/FORM placement is in `completed_collection_item_ids`. That same branch fires the `course.completed` webhook and `record_course_completed` (GA4 `course_completed`). So course completion is lazy (needs a visit to the finish page), not derived by the signal. `progress_percentage == 100` does not by itself set `completed_time`.
- Gating (sequential unlock) is computed, not stored: `get_content_status(collection_item, next_status, topic_progress_map, form_placement_map)` in `learner_interface/utils.py` walks placements in order; an item is READY only if the previous returned COMPLETE (`COMPLETE, READY` / otherwise `BLOCKED`). FAILED, IN_PROGRESS and BLOCKED all pass `BLOCKED` to the next item, so a failed or unfinished quiz blocks everything after it, including later parts. For a CoursePart it recurses over children and uses `derive_part_status`; an empty part is COMPLETE. `get_course_index(...)` builds the rows; `_blocked_item_redirect(...)` in `views.py` turns away a BLOCKED item server-side (redirect to `course_detail`); `current_entry_status` reads the row. `_fetch_player_progress_maps` feeds the maps (two queries). Deadlines can add CLOSED status on top (closed items do not unlock the next).
- So yes, a "blocking quiz" already exists in practice: a scored quiz with a pass mark blocks later content until passed; a quiz without a pass mark or a survey does not block once submitted.

## 3. Other ways to be "done"; what reads completion

- Nothing today marks a child done other than studying it. No skip, exempt, waived, "complete by", or admin "mark complete" status exists on `TopicProgress`, `CourseFormAttempt`, `FormProgress` or `CourseProgress` (grep of freedom_ls and spec_dd for exempt, skip, mastery, test-out found nothing relevant). docs/product/roadmap.md lists "recertification" under "Not planned"; spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md line 154 lists "recertification, retakes" as out of scope for the deadlines work.
- `TopicProgress.complete_time` is nullable and the placement-keyed completion set is the single read path for percentage, outline, finish page and `outstanding_items`. The natural extension point is `completed_collection_item_ids(record)` in `learner_progress/queries.py`: add a third source (an exemption row keyed by (course_progress, collection_item)) and every one of those readers follows. Items that exist only as an exemption row would count as complete with no TopicProgress/attempt, so the readers below that bypass this function need checking.
- Readers that use the shared definition: `calculate_course_progress_percentage`, `outstanding_items`/`course_finish`, `get_content_status` (via `_fetch_player_progress_maps`, which calls `completed_form_item_ids` and reads `TopicProgress.complete_time` directly, so a new source would need adding there too), dashboard/listing status (`CourseListingStatus`, `derive_listing_status`: COMPLETE needs `completed_time`).
- Readers that bypass it and read raw rows (an exemption would be invisible to them unless they are changed): `freedom_ls/reports/indexes.py` and `reports/gather.py` (cohort PDF report; per-learner topic `complete_time` and latest form attempt `completed_time`, keyed by topic/form id, not placement), educator learner page (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/views.py`, `quick_views.py`; shows items completed, quiz attempts, scores and pass mark; at-risk flags in `reports/at_risk.py`), Django admin (`learner_progress/admin.py`: `TopicProgress.is_complete`, `CourseFormAttempt.is_complete`).
- Course-level consumers read `CourseProgress` columns only: `progress_percentage` and `completed_time` in `learner_progress/api.py` (the lead-monitoring course-progress API; `updated_at` is its changed-since cursor, so any new write must move `updated_at`), `course.completed` webhook (fired only from `course_finish`; payload in `base/webhook_event_types.py`), `course_access/backends.py` (a finished course never has access ended; deadline policy reads `completed_time is None`), dashboard "Learning history", deadline logic (an item completed shows no deadline; exempt items would need the same).
- A test-out status therefore would not change course-level readers if the exemption counts inside `completed_collection_item_ids`, but item-level reporting (educator page, reports, admin, API consumers that look at item rows) would show an exempted item as not done unless the exemption is surfaced there, and nothing distinguishes "passed the course" from "tested out of it" for the webhook/API consumers unless a field is added.

## 4. Content versioning

- No version, content hash or change marker on `Topic`, `Form`, `CoursePart`, `Course` or `FormQuestion`. `BaseContent` (`freedom_ls/content_base/models.py`) has `file_path`, `meta`, `tags`; `TimestampedModel` adds `created_at` and `updated_at`, but `content_save` uses `update_or_create` (`save_with_uuid`, `content_save.py` ~line 255), so `updated_at` moves whenever the row is saved and not only when the content changed. It is not a trustworthy "changed" signal. (The only hash in content_engine is `image_cache.py`, for images.)
- Reload is non-destructive to progress. Content rows keep their authored UUID (written back into the file by `update_file_with_uuid`), so `save_content_to_db` updates in place and ids are stable. `ContentCollectionItem` is `update_or_create` on (site, collection, child), so placement ids survive a reload and `order` is overwritten. Dropped children keep their placement row ("a sync keeps the row a dropped child left behind", comment ~line 1217); `TopicProgress.collection_item` and `CourseFormAttempt.collection_item` are `SET_NULL`, and `TopicProgress.topic` is `PROTECT`. Progress rows are never rewritten by a load.
- Consequence: a changed topic or quiz silently keeps old completions, and a new topic added to a part just appears as incomplete (the percentage drops on the next recalculation; recalculation is event-driven, not on content load). Scores are frozen at submission ("never recalculated", learner-experience.md Quiz Feedback), and the docs note a stored score can disagree with what the same answers would score now. There is no way today to say "this section changed since the learner completed it", which matters for the idea's "there might have been changes to the original course".
- A collection of rows can be compared only by structure (set of placement ids); question-level changes would need a new fingerprint.

## 5. Prior completions and re-doing a course

- Registrations: `LearnerCourseRegistration` has `UniqueConstraint(site, learner, course)` (`learner_management/models.py`), so a learner can hold only one individual registration per course ever (it can be deactivated/reactivated via `is_active`, which restores the same progress). `CohortCourseRegistration` is unique per (site, course, cohort), so a renewal could be granted through a different, new cohort.
- `CourseProgress` is one per registration per learner (`one_course_progress_per_learner_registration`, `one_course_progress_per_cohort_registration`; exactly one grant FK is set). `ensure_course_progress_record` (`learner_progress/utils.py`) is idempotent on the registration. A learner registered through two different registrations (two orgs, or cohort plus individual) gets two independent records; the old one is kept untouched (docs/product/learner-tracking.md). So a renewal two years later today is only possible by minting a new cohort registration (new cohort), which yields a fresh, separate record and keeps the old one; there is no "retake" or "renewal" object, no link between the two records, and the learner interface shows one registration's progress at a time with no switch (`course_progress_for(user, course)` picks the record).
- The old record stays complete (`completed_time` set), which is what a pre-test would want to read as prior evidence: its `TopicProgress`/`CourseFormAttempt` rows are per placement and survive. They are not linked to the new record.
- Related: `course_access/backends.py` treats a completed course as never ending access; deadlines and `time_limit_days` are per record.

## Implications for the cut

1. The section unit for test-out has to be the placement (`ContentCollectionItem`), with a CoursePart as the natural grouping when present. Parts are single-level, optional and used by only one demo course; a test-out feature either requires parts for the sections it covers, or covers arbitrary runs of placements, or the author names the group explicitly. Decide this first.
2. "A section's final blocking quiz" is just a scored quiz Form with a pass mark. Skipping it means making that placement count as complete without an attempt; the gating logic (`get_content_status`) and `completed_collection_item_ids` are the two places that must agree, and `_fetch_player_progress_maps` is a third that reads rows directly. A single exemption record keyed by (course_progress, collection_item) added to that shared set is the smallest change; the new kind of "done" is the only thing not yet in the model.
3. Whether test-out marks just the quiz, the whole part, or each child placement is a product decision: marking only the quiz would still leave the part's topics blocking the quiz's neighbours; marking every child as exempt makes the percentage jump and makes an exempted course finish on `course_finish` with no studying.
4. Course completion semantics need a decision: a learner who tests out of everything completes the course (webhook `course.completed`, GA4, API `completed_time`, Learning history) with nothing in those channels saying they tested out. Either add a distinguishing field to the exemption/record and surface it in the API and webhook payload, or accept equivalence. Reports, the educator learner page and admin read raw rows and would show exempt items as not done unless changed.
5. Course completion is only stamped when the learner visits `course_finish`; a fully tested-out course still needs that visit (or a change to stamp it when the last exemption is recorded).
6. The pre-test itself fits existing machinery: a `Form` with `strategy == QUIZ` sat via `CourseFormAttempt`; per-section scoring needs the questions to be tagged to a section/part. Form questions carry no section link today; that tag (or a pre-test form per part) is new. The pre-test needs to be reachable without being a sequential blocker (placements are ordered and unlock sequentially, so a "big up-front test" would block the rest unless it is exempted from sequence or is the first item).
7. Staleness: there is no change tracking, so "the course changed since you last did it" cannot be answered today. For the renewal scenario, a cheap fingerprint (content hash per part or per topic/form, written by `content_save`, compared at the time of the prior completion) would be a new field and a new loader step; without it, test-out must rely only on the pre-test result and treat every section as potentially changed, which matches the idea's primary design. Do not rely on `updated_at`.
8. Renewal (a second pass two years on) has no model today: one individual registration per learner per course; a second pass needs a new cohort registration or a new mechanism, and old and new records are unlinked. The cut should either state "renewal is a new registration, outside this work" or add a link from the new record to prior completed evidence. This also contradicts the roadmap statements that recertification is not planned, so the cut must reconcile with docs/product/roadmap.md.
9. Revision questions: nothing ties a question to a section (`FormQuestion` belongs to a `FormPage` of a `Form` only), and form attempts are per attempt and frozen at submission. A question bank tied to a CoursePart (or placement) and a per-learner response history are new models; they can reuse `FormQuestion`/`QuestionAnswer` types for authoring but need their own attempt records (not `CourseFormAttempt`, which is course-progress bound and drives completion). The trigger "when a section is finished, add its questions to the learner's bank" can hang off the same placement-completion signal path (`form_attempt_completed`, `post_save` on `TopicProgress`); exemptions would need to count as finished for this too.
10. Keep content-authoring in the content repo conventions: sections, pre-tests and bank membership must be expressible in course/part frontmatter and loaded by `content_save` with stable UUIDs, since progress, exemptions and revision history key on ids that must survive reload.
