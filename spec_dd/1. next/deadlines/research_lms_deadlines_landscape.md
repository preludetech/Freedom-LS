# Research: how comparable systems handle deadlines

Scope: input to cutting the FLS "deadlines" idea into specs. Sources are web search results (standard mode), mostly vendor docs and university help pages. Where a source is a forum post or third-party plugin, that is stated. Terms in quotes belong to the named system, not to FLS.

Coverage gaps (not verified): Thinkific has no confirmed access-expiry docs in the results (drip only). Cornerstone was not found. SuccessFactors recertification was not found. Docebo findings are from community forum threads, not official docs. No source found for the Teachable product. Open edX "shift dates" and Coursera "reset deadlines" details come partly from forum and community posts.

## (a) Where deadlines attach

- **Item-level absolute dates (Moodle).** Assignments have "Allow submissions from", "Due date" and "Cut-off date". Due date only marks work late; cut-off stops submission. https://docs.moodle.org/en/Assignment_overrides (summary also from https://www.monash.edu/learning-teaching/teachhq/moodle/assignment/how-to/extensions-and-resubmissions)
- **Item-level absolute dates (Canvas).** Three dates per assignment: Due, Available From, and "Until". Late status is by due date; submissions close at Until. If Until equals due, submissions close at due. https://intra.kth.se/en/utbildning/systemstod/canvas/guider/uppgifter/tillganglighetsperiod-1.1035771 and https://lasalle.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/2615/Portal/KB/PrintArticle?ID=172961
- **Item-level relative dates (Open edX).** In self-paced courses, "Personalized Learning Schedule" gives each learner suggested due dates for graded subsections, computed from the enrolment date. Default pacing spreads dates evenly over sections; custom pacing lets authors set "due in N weeks". Needs feature flags; works at graded-subsection level. The `edx-when` package stores start/end/due dates, absolute or relative. https://docs.openedx.org/en/ulmo/educators/how-tos/course_development/set_course_pacing.html , https://discuss.openedx.org/t/how-to-set-personalized-learner-schedules/5063 , https://pypi.python.org/pypi/edx-when/
- **Course-level relative window (TalentLMS).** A course can stay active a set number of days from the learner's enrolment, or between a fixed start and end date for everyone. With both a start date and a time limit, the countdown begins at the start date for people enrolled earlier, otherwise at enrolment. https://help.talentlms.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014572494-How-to-work-with-time-limits-and-expired-courses and https://help.talentlms.com/hc/en-us/articles/9651377966492-How-to-add-a-start-date-to-a-course-in-TalentLMS
- **Course-level relative window (LearnDash).** "Course Access Expiration": access ends N days after enrolment. Drip is separate: lessons release N days after enrolment or on a calendar date (the same date for all users). https://learndash.com/support/kb/add-ons/users/extend-access/ , https://www.learndash.com/?p=12763
- **Course-level validity (Docebo).** A validity period yields a due date that Docebo calls "expiration"; it can be a "soft deadline". Certifications have their own expiry event. Source is community threads. https://community.docebo.com/product-q-a-7/notifications-for-courses-coming-due-soon-or-past-due-soft-deadline-on-and-learning-plan-course-validity-periods-14525
- **Assignment-level required date (SAP SuccessFactors Learning).** Assigned learning has a "required date". Curriculum-assigned items inherit a date that can revert to the curriculum-calculated one after an admin edit. https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2840667 (via search result; wording summarised)
- **Drip (Thinkific).** Drip schedule based on course start date or specific calendar dates. https://bloggingwizard.com/thinkific-review/ (secondary source)
- **Observation.** Mature systems offer both absolute and relative-to-enrolment, and separate "when it opens" from "when it is due" from "when it closes".

## (b) Per-learner and per-group overrides and extensions

- **Moodle.** Assignment "user overrides" and "group overrides" can change submit-from, due and cut-off dates; only enabled fields are overridden. A user override beats group overrides; with several groups, the most lenient date wins. Moodle 5.2 docs mention a "Reason for override" note. Overrides are not updated when the base due date changes, and (per Royal Roads) do not survive backup/restore. https://docs.moodle.org/en/Assignment_overrides , https://www.monash.edu/learning-teaching/teachhq/moodle/assignment/how-to/extensions-and-resubmissions , https://royalroads.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ITKNOW/pages/5839211
- **Canvas.** "Assign To" gives specific students or sections their own due/available/until dates beneath "Everyone". Cleanest way to extend one student. Extending "Until" alone does not stop the late flag. https://confluence.esg.wsu.edu/spaces/KB/pages/362420692/The+Assign+Feature+in+Canvas+Availability+and+Exceptions , https://servicedesk.uwgb.edu/TDClient/33/Portal/KB/ArticleDet?ID=1016
- **TalentLMS.** Instructor can change the expiration date of a course for a specific user; expired users show an "Expired" label and the instructor picks a new date. https://help.talentlms.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014657413-How-to-change-the-expiration-date-of-a-course-for-specific-users
- **LearnDash.** Admin "extend access" for individuals or groups; the new end date replaces the existing one. A third-party plugin claims core mishandles stacked extensions (vendor claim). https://learndash.com/support/kb/add-ons/users/extend-access/
- **Moodle enrolment.** A suspended enrolment can be reactivated with a new "Enrolment ends" date. https://telsupport.brookes.ac.uk/articles/how-do-i-reactivate-suspended-enrolments-in-moodle/
- **Coursera: "Reset deadlines".** Learners behind their personalised deadlines see a "Reset" option that shifts assignment deadlines forward. Not available for degree, MasterTrack and some other courses (hard deadlines; may be able to switch sessions). Community posts report a reset can move a learner to a new course version and lose submissions. https://blog.coursera.org/improvements-to-sessions-experience-for-learners/ , https://ssw.umich.edu/academic-programs/msw/programs/online/essentials-certificate/faq?page=1 , https://community.deeplearning.ai/t/my-compleated-assignments-disappeared-with-no-warning-after-deadline-reset/5861
- **Observation.** Override precedence (user > group > default), an audit note or reason, and whether the system tracks the override relative to the base date are the recurring design points.

## (c) Pause / freeze semantics

No LMS result documented a first-class "pause enrolment" with clock-stop semantics. The LMS norm is to reactivate with a new end date (Moodle, TalentLMS, LearnDash above). Membership and billing products are the better model.

- **Stripe "pause payment collection".** Keeps the subscription status "active", pauses only collection; `resumes_at` sets the auto-resume date; without it the pause lasts until unset. A `behavior` setting decides what happens to invoices created during the pause (void, keep as draft, mark uncollectible). A newer separate full-pause feature requires flexible billing mode. https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/pause-payment , https://docs.stripe.com/changelog/dahlia/2026-05-27/pause-subscriptions
- **Gymbox (UK).** Max 3 months of freeze in any 12 months; freezes start on the 1st of the month; £25 fee per frozen month; a freeze extends the minimum term by the frozen months. https://www.support.gymbox.com/support/solutions/articles/103000386113-how-do-i-freeze-my-membership-
- **Nuffield Health (UK).** Freeze up to 12 months, only for listed reasons (serious illness, serious injury, pregnancy), sometimes with proof. https://www.nuffieldhealth.com/cancel-or-freeze-your-gym-membership
- **Dalhousie Sexton Gym.** Tiered admin fee by freeze length (1-4 months, 5-8 months). https://athletics.dal.ca/facilities/sexton_gym/Sexton_Memberships/handbook-and-policies.html
- **Pattern across these.** Limits on count and total length per period; reason-gated; optional proof (staff approval); fees; term extension equal to paused time (the "clock stops, end date shifts" model); auto-resume at a set date. Access during pause (Stripe keeps access running when only collection is paused; a gym freeze blocks use) is a deliberate choice.
- **Gap.** I found no source on how item-level due dates shift on resume. Coursera's reset and TalentLMS's "new expiration date" are the closest analogues (both shift forward by choice, not by computed paused duration).

## (d) Consequences of missing a deadline, and how they are configured

- **Flag late only.** Moodle due date; Canvas due date flags the submission late in the Gradebook, including resubmissions after the due date. https://docs.moodle.org/en/Assignment_overrides , https://resources.smu.edu/bin/OIT/software/Canvas%20LMS/Allow%20Late%20Assignment%20Submissions
- **Close submission.** Moodle cut-off date; Canvas "Until". Monash advises against cut-offs except where assessment security needs them. https://www.monash.edu/learning-teaching/teachhq/moodle/assignment/how-to/extensions-and-resubmissions
- **Grade penalty.** Canvas late policy lives in the gradebook, separate from the date settings; staff can clear it per submission by setting status to None. https://miamioh.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/1813/Portal/KB/Article/55555/Submissions-incorrectly-marked-late-Canvas (search summary)
- **Revoke access / expire.** TalentLMS: course expires and learner access is revoked; LearnDash: access ends and learner is notified. https://help.talentlms.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014572494-How-to-work-with-time-limits-and-expired-courses , https://www.learndash.com/?p=12763
- **No penalty / auto-reset.** Coursera: in some courses, missed personalised deadlines just auto-reset with no grade penalty (community report); strict programmes have hard deadlines. https://community.deeplearning.ai/t/how-can-i-reset-the-course-deadline-i-am-not-trying-to-pursue-a-grade/270822
- **Soft vs hard.** Docebo "soft deadline" on course validity. Behaviour of notifications with soft deadlines differs by version (forum). https://community.docebo.com/product-q-a-7/notifications-for-courses-coming-due-soon-or-past-due-soft-deadline-on-and-learning-plan-course-validity-periods-14525
- **Escalate.** SuccessFactors: overdue notices go to the learner; a community user reported no native way to notify managers only after the due date; escalation is a requested capability. https://groups.community.sap.com:443/t5/human-capital-management-q-a/escalate-notify-manager-when-learning-item-compliance-training-is-over-due/qaq-p/12480740
- **Configuration scope.** Mostly per item (Moodle, Canvas dates) or per course (TalentLMS, LearnDash), with notification defaults at process/org level. SuccessFactors lets item-level email settings override the automatic-process defaults, and settings changed at process level only apply to new items unless "Propagate settings to all active items" is used. https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2477571 (summary). Per-cohort or per-organisation consequence policy was not found as a first-class concept; Canvas/Moodle achieve it through course or gradebook policy.

## (e) Reminders, channels and at-risk views

- **SuccessFactors.** "Learning Expiration" automatic process: threshold window for upcoming/overdue items, plus a "Reminder Period" (days between repeats). Separate overdue notice to learner. Manager notification is a per-item checkbox. https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2460640 , https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2277719
- **Docebo.** Each reminder is a separate notification; "Course has expired" can be scheduled before or after expiration; "Learner has yet to complete course" keys off enrolment date; digest templates exist. Users report reminders failing silently and being unable to fire on the due date itself. Forum evidence. https://community.docebo.com/product-q-a-7/setting-up-course-completion-reminders-16422 , https://community.docebo.com/product-q-a-7/enrollment-deadline-8136
- **Moodle.** Core expiry warning not confirmed; a third-party Enrolment Timer block notifies a set period before enrolment ends and shows time left to the learner. https://github.com/learningworks/moodle-enrolmenttimer/
- **TalentLMS.** No confirmed automatic overdue alert in results; "Expired" label in instructor Users & Progress panel gives a basic at-risk view. https://help.talentlms.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014657413-How-to-change-the-expiration-date-of-a-course-for-specific-users
- **Coursera.** Learner-facing "behind schedule" state with a reset prompt (see b).
- **Observation.** Reminders are modelled as offsets (N days before or after a date), with a repeat interval, and per-recipient-role toggles (learner, manager). Digests are recommended for regular nudges.

## (f) Common pitfalls

- **End-of-day and date-only.** Date-only due dates treated as 00:00 flag same-day submissions late (Rock RMS community). Faculty Focus notes default midnight deadlines persist because they are the default. Sakai fixed a due date stored at 00:00 shifted by other systems to 23:59. https://community.rockrms.com/ideas/2220/add-time-feature-to-due-dates-in-lms-assignments , https://www.facultyfocus.com/?p=65587 , https://sakaiproject.atlassian.net/browse/SAK-48287
- **Timezone and DST.** UTC is fixed; local offset changes at DST switch, so an "11:59 PM" deadline shifts for local users. Say the timezone explicitly. One university reset its default timezone so assignments still show due on Sunday for all users. https://cse6040.gatech.edu/active/Logistics/utc_explained/ , https://learn.endeavour.edu.au/mod/forum/discuss.php?d=384522
- **Overrides not tracking base-date edits.** Moodle overrides do not follow a later change to the base due date; review them manually. https://www.monash.edu/learning-teaching/teachhq/moodle/assignment/how-to/extensions-and-resubmissions
- **Admin edits reverting.** SuccessFactors curriculum-assigned dates can revert to the computed date. https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2840667
- **Destructive resets.** Coursera reset reportedly lost submitted work when it moved learners to a new version. https://community.deeplearning.ai/t/my-compleated-assignments-disappeared-with-no-warning-after-deadline-reset/5861
- **Extension stacking.** LearnDash extension purchases reportedly reset or ignore existing access (vendor claim). https://learndash.com/support/kb/add-ons/users/extend-access/
- **Extending only the close date.** Canvas still flags late unless the due date or an assign-to override is also changed. https://lasalle.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/2615/Portal/KB/PrintArticle?ID=172961
- **Notification spam and silent failure.** One notification per reminder and per-item overrides in SuccessFactors/Docebo cause duplicates or surprise recipients; Docebo users report missed reminders with no log. Digests and a send log mitigate. https://community.docebo.com/product-q-a-7/notifications-for-courses-coming-due-soon-or-past-due-soft-deadline-on-and-learning-plan-course-validity-periods-14525
- **Reminder anchor mismatch.** Docebo ties some reminders to enrolment date rather than due date, so they do not move when deadlines are extended. Same source.

## Patterns most relevant to FLS

- Separate three dates per item: opens, due (flag late), closes (lock). A deadline is a policy decision, not just a timestamp (Moodle, Canvas).
- Support both absolute dates and relative-to-registration windows (Open edX relative dates, TalentLMS/LearnDash "N days from enrolment"). Compute the effective date from a base plus a per-registration offset, so pause and extension can adjust it.
- Course-level window (e.g. 3 months from registration) and item-level due dates are different concerns with different consequences; allow either or both.
- Override model: per-learner beats per-cohort beats default (Moodle precedence), with a reason and actor recorded. Decide up front whether overrides follow later base-date edits (Moodle's do not, which is a known pain).
- Pause as a first-class record, not an edit of the end date: start, optional planned resume date (Stripe `resumes_at`), reason, actor, status. On resume, shift the course window and unmet relative dates by the paused duration (membership "term extension" pattern, Gymbox).
- Pause policy knobs borrowed from gyms: max count and max total length per period, reason-gated, staff approval optional, auto-resume at the end date. Make them configurable per course/cohort/organisation.
- Consequences as a configurable policy per course, cohort or organisation, chosen from a small set (flag late, close item, lock, revoke access, escalate), resolved with most-specific-wins. Note that no surveyed system documents consequences as a first-class per-cohort policy, so this is design work for FLS.
- Reminders as offset rules (N days before/after, repeat interval) with per-recipient roles (learner, educator), de-duplicated and logged; anchor them on the effective deadline so extensions and pauses move them (avoid Docebo's enrolment-anchored mismatch). Provide a digest and an educator at-risk view.
- Store instants in UTC with an explicit timezone for display; make end-of-day semantics explicit (due "end of local day" rather than 00:00) and test DST boundaries.
- Make destructive actions (reset, reactivate, extend) non-destructive to learner work (Coursera's lost-submission reports).
