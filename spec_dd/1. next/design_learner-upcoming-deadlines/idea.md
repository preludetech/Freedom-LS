Give learners an upcoming deadlines page: every deadline they still have to meet, across all their
courses, in one place.

Use the "grouped list" design (`panel__panel-a`): deadlines grouped by when they fall, missed ones
first.

Build on the deadline tiers, short date format and shared deadline selection from
`design_learner-interface-deadlines`, so the page shows each deadline exactly as the dashboard band
and course player outline do.
