## Layer 5 — tie into `upgrade_notes.md`

`concrete-implementation-helpers` shipped structured per-spec `upgrade_notes.md`.
The authoritative schema is in
`claude_plugins/fls-dev/commands/update_upgrade_notes.md` (richer than that
spec's prose — it includes `changed_settings: []`, npm fields, etc.).

**Convention Part 2 establishes:** when a spec introduces a **hard config
requirement** (as `home_page` did with `COURSE_ACCESS_BACKEND`), its notes set
`requires_settings_change: true` with the specific keys in `changed_settings`, so
`update_fls` surfaces it — and ideally a Layer-4 check enforces the same
requirement at boot.

**Decision to record (D6):** `requires_settings_change: true` +
`changed_settings` is sufficient for the hard-requirement case (the field is
free-text and already carries the key list — Part 1's own notes prove it). No
new flag is required.
`claude_plugins/fls-dev/commands/update_upgrade_notes.md` gains guidance to
recognise a *hard/required* settings change (vs optional/informational) and,
when the spec also adds a Layer-4 check enforcing it, to say so in the notes.

This slice's **own** `upgrade_notes.md` (authored later in the SDD flow):
Layers 5/6 require no downstream settings change, but downstreams should be told
that the conformance suite is available (shipped by Layer 3) and that Layer 4's
checks exist — after that slice's trim, `freedom_ls_course_access.E003` and
`freedom_ls_learner_interface.W001`, plus its downstream-visible
`freedom_ls_course_access.E001` → `.E002` re-ID.

## Layer 6 — make the new conventions stick (Part-2 portion)

Part 1 covered the bulk of Layer 6 (testing/playwright skills, code-reviewer,
`update_fls.md` marker selection). Part 2 adds:

- **`claude_plugins/fls-dev/commands/concrete/update_fls.md`:** it currently runs
  neither `manage.py check` nor any conformance suite. Add both to the
  verification steps — invoke the Layer-3 conformance suite as the positive
  signal, and run `manage.py check` so Layer-4 failures surface during an
  upgrade. (Part 1 already switched the bare `uv run pytest` call sites to the
  documented `-m "not playwright and not fls_internal and not ci_only"`
  selection, at four call sites.)
- **`claude_plugins/fls-dev/commands/update_upgrade_notes.md`:** add the D6
  guidance above.
- **`claude_plugins/fls-dev/resources/template_repo_manifest.md`:** its `urls.py`
  checklist is out of sync with `config/urls.py` (omits `applications/`,
  `interest/`, sitemap/robots). The actual manifest edit is
  `/update_template_repo`'s job (SDD step 12), but this spec **requires** that
  step to (a) fix the checklist and (b) ship the reference sitemap/robots wiring
  + a `tests/` dir importing the conformance suite, so new projects get the
  forced sitemap/robots existence (D1) and the positive signal by default.

## Scope / non-goals

**In scope:** the upgrade-notes tie-in (Layer 5) and the Part-2 plugin-doc
touches (Layer 6) — the two `claude_plugins/fls-dev/commands/` edits above, and
nothing else. (The umbrella's scope line also listed Layers 3 and 4; those are
the sibling slices `test_portability_2_conformance_suite`, shipped, and
`test_portability_3_system_checks`.)

**Not in scope, though the original parent plan listed it:** a "Required &
app-specific settings" note in `docs/product/configuration-and-extension.md`
describing the per-app `config.py` convention. That is a Layer-0 deliverable, and
it already shipped as
`claude_plugins/fls-dev/skills/app-settings/SKILL.md` — the correct venue, since
`config.py` is an internal developer convention rather than user-facing product
documentation. `configuration-and-extension.md` already carries a current
`Conformance Suite` section and `Settings Reference` table.

**Depends on Part 1 (assumed present):** the `fls_internal` marker taxonomy,
`INSTALLED_APPS`-keyed collection-safety guards, and de-branded assertions. Note:
Part 1 shipped **no `e2e` marker** — `playwright` is the browser-test marker;
Part 2 must not assume `e2e`.

**Out of scope / deferred to `/update_template_repo` (SDD step 12):** the
concrete project's own pytest config (`testpaths`, `--ignore=submodules`, its
`tests/` dir) and the actual template-manifest edits. Called out in Layer 6 so
the two sides land together — Layer 3 is what makes a `tests/` dir *worth*
shipping downstream, and D1's "forced existence" depends on the template
shipping the wiring.
