Direct messaging — educator
Spec 4 of 8. One-to-one conversations between an educator and the learners they can see. Desktop at 1440 × 900, states as pane crops, mobile at 375 × 812.
Organisation context
Assumed: a conversation records the organisation it started in, so a pair holds one conversation per organisation. The thread header names it.
Message body
Assumed: plain text, line breaks kept, URLs linked. 2,000 character limit, counter shown from 1,800.
Recipients at scale
Search first, results grouped by cohort. Picking someone you already talk to opens that thread.
Policy stops a pair
History stays readable. The composer is replaced by a one-line reason. See states.
