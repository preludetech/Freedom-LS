# Answers: deadlines-3-extensions-and-pause

Asked by `/sdd:start` before the spec was written.

- Q: Deadlines resolve to a date (end of day, or a date plus a time). When a pause ends after, say, 3 days and 5 hours, how far do the unmet deadlines and the time limit move? → A: Whole days, rounded up. A 3d 5h pause shifts every date by 4 days; dates stay dates and the learner keeps the end-of-day they had. (asked up front)
- Q: A learner already has a 7-day extension on an item and staff grant another 5 days. Does the second extension add to the first or replace it? → A: Stack. Each extension says "N more days" (or "to this date") and is applied on top of whatever the deadline currently resolves to, pauses included. (asked up front)
- Q: In the admin, does one extension action act on a single deadline, or can staff also shift a learner's whole course in one go? → A: Both. Staff extend one item deadline or the time limit, or shift every unmet deadline and the time limit for this learner by N days; the whole-course case reuses the pause shift maths. Bulk across learners stays in spec 6. (asked up front)
- Q: When a course is paused while a learner has an open form attempt, what happens to that attempt during the pause? → A: Frozen. The player refuses to open the form while paused, the attempt and its answers are kept, and the learner continues it after resume. Learner work is never destroyed. (asked up front)
