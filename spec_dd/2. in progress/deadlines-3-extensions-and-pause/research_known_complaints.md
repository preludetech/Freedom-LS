## Cross-cutting acceptance checks suggested by the above

1. After extending: item not late, not locked, shows "extended to"; reminder, event and notification all use the same date.
2. Stacking two extensions, then a pause and resume: dates equal base + extensions + whole-day pause shift, in any order of operations.
3. Paused: no opening, no reminders, no overdue; open attempt frozen; learner still listed for educators.
4. Resume twice or race with automatic resume: one shift, one event.
5. Webhook payload has previous and new dates, actor, reason, event id; sent after commit; test sends flagged.
6. History rows exist for every action with who/why.
