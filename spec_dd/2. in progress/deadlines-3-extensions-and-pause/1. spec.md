# Spec: extensions and pause

Spec 3 of the Deadlines effort (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`, "Deadlines"). Built on
`deadlines-1-dates-and-overdue`, `deadlines-2-consequence-policy` and
`deadlines-8-per-placement-dates`, all merged. Source: `idea.md` in this directory; the up-front
answers are in `answers.md`.

## Purpose

Spec 1 resolves a learner's deadlines and spec 2 enforces their consequences, but once a date is
set the only remedy is a deadline override that replaces it. This spec gives staff two remedies in
the Django admin. An extension adds days to one deadline, to the course time limit, or to every
unmet deadline of a learner's course, and extensions stack. A pause stops the clock: nothing in the
course opens while it lasts, and when it ends every unmet deadline and the time limit move forward
by the days paused. Both are applied inside spec 1's resolver, so the overdue mark, the close
instant and the access end move together and no page can disagree. Each staff action is
audit-logged, fires a webhook and notifies the learner.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CoursePause` | coined | One pause of one `CourseProgress`: when it started, when it is planned to end, when it ended, who did each, and why. "Pause" is the learner-facing word; "suspend" is retired. |
| `DeadlineExtension` | coined | One grant of extra days to one `CourseProgress`: its scope, the days, who granted it and why. Not a `LearnerDeadlineOverride`, which replaces a value; an extension adds to whatever resolves. |
| shift | coined | The whole number of days a resolved deadline is moved forward by the pauses and extensions that apply to it. Computed when read, stored nowhere. |
| paused days | coined | The length of one pause in whole days, rounded up: `ceil((end - started_at) / 1 day)`. An open pause is measured to `now`. |
| planned resume | coined | `CoursePause.planned_resume_on`, a date. The pause ends at 00:00 of that day in the site time zone, with no sweep and no write. |
| paused | coined | The state of a `CourseProgress` with an open pause: one whose end instant is unset or after `now`. A read-time state like spec 2's "access ended"; the registration stays active. |
| scope | coined | `DeadlineExtension.scope`, what one extension moves: `item` (one collection item), `time_limit`, or `course` (every unmet deadline and the time limit). |

## Scope

In:

- `CoursePause` and `DeadlineExtension` models in `freedom_ls/deadlines`, their factories, and a
  migration that also adds three pause-limit settings to the six `*DeadlineConfig` layers.
- The shift, computed inside `course_deadlines_for` and applied in `resolve_deadline`.
- Service functions that pause, resume and extend: atomic, idempotent, audit-logged, firing a
  webhook and raising a learner notification after commit. Specs 4, 5 and 6 call them.
- The paused state in the access check, the player gates, the course index, course detail and the
  dashboard.
- Admin: three detail-page actions on the course progress record with a confirmation form each,
  read-only history inlines, read-only list admins for both models, and pause and extension days
  in the existing effective-deadlines panel.
- Three `noun.verb` codes shared by the audit log and the webhook registry, with sample payloads.
- Three notification categories.
- A hook for spec 5's sweep to announce a planned resume.

Out:

- Learner self-pause and enforcing the pause limits (spec 4). The limits are stored and resolved
  here, and bind nobody yet.
- Reminders, the housekeeping sweep itself, educator notifications, `deadline.missed` (spec 5).
- Educator-interface screens, bulk extension across learners, revoking an extension (spec 6).
- Email delivery of the notifications (`user-communication-2-notification-email`).
- Shortening a deadline, approval workflows, fees.

## Decisions

1. **Two records, one shift.** A pause and an extension are different facts with different
   lifecycles, so they are two models. The resolver turns both into one integer per deadline.
2. **The shift is computed when read, never stored.** A planned resume then takes effect with no
   sweep, a second resume cannot double-shift, and the audit entry and webhook record the number
   at the moment of the action. Spec 1's "nothing is written to enforce a deadline" rule stands.
3. **Whole days, rounded up, added to the local date.** `paused_days` is `ceil` of elapsed time
   over 86400 seconds; days are added to the deadline's local date before its time is attached, as
   `due_instant` already does for relative deadlines, so end of day and a `due_time` survive and a
   DST change moves nothing. A pause ended within the same instant is zero days; any longer one is
   at least one.
4. **A shift applies to a deadline only if its item was incomplete when the shift began.** That is
   what "met deadlines are untouched" means. The resolver reads each item's completion instant
   (`TopicProgress.complete_time`, the earliest completed `CourseFormAttempt`'s
   `form_progress.completed_time`) and skips a pause or course-scope extension that began after
   it. An `item` or `time_limit` extension always applies: staff chose the target.
5. **Extensions stack by addition.** Each grant is a row of `days`; the resolver sums them. "Move
   to this date" is converted to days against the deadline's effective instant at grant time and
   stored as days, so later pauses stack on top without re-deriving. A date on or before the
   effective instant is refused.
6. **An open pause counts up to `now`.** While paused, every unmet deadline is provisionally
   shifted by the days paused so far, so nothing becomes overdue, closes or ends during a pause,
   and an item that was already overdue or closed when the pause began keeps exactly that margin
   through and after it.
7. **A pause ends at the earlier of `ended_at` and the planned resume instant.** Read paths never
   write. A staff resume after the planned instant records `ended_at` as the planned instant, so
   the learner is not charged for the delay. Creating a new pause first closes a passed planned
   pause the same way, so the one-open-pause constraint holds.
8. **Paused beats access ended, and a finished course is never paused or extended.** The
   `DeadlineEnforcingBackend` reads the pause before the `end_course_access` gate, which today
   short-circuits. A record with `completed_time` set is refused by the services.
9. **Paused reads like `outline_and_results` without submit-and-exit.** The outline shows,
   completed items and the results of completed attempts open, and every other route redirects to
   course detail. `form_submit_and_exit` is refused, so an open attempt is frozen with its answers
   and continues after resume. Spec 2's `access_ended_at` is never set for a pause: that path
   finalises attempts.
10. **One code per change, shared by the audit log and the webhook registry**: `course.paused`,
    `course.resumed`, `deadline.extended`. `course.completed` is the precedent for `course` naming
    a learner's course record. The verbs `paused`, `resumed`, `extended` and the noun `deadline`
    are new glossary words.
11. **The reason is staff-only.** Learner copy says what changed and that staff did it. Payloads
    carry ids, dates and days, never the reason.
12. **Pause limits default to no limit.** `learners_may_pause` defaults off; `max_pauses` and
    `max_pause_days` are integers where null inherits, `0` means no limit and `N` is a cap.
    The settings layer ships `0` for both: a site that wants a cap configures one.
13. **Staff act on the course progress record.** A cohort move mints a new record, so pauses and
    extensions stay with the old one, as deadline overrides do.
14. **The services are the only writers.** The admin, spec 4's self-pause, spec 5's sweep and spec
    6's screens all call them. The model admins for both records are read-only.
15. **A planned resume is announced by spec 5's sweep** through a function this spec provides,
    with no actor. A staff resume done early sets the same marker, so the sweep never announces
    it twice.

### Shift and resolver (`freedom_ls/deadlines/shifts.py`, `resolver.py`)

`shifts.py`, pure functions:

- `paused_days(started_at, end_at) -> int`.
- `ShiftSources`: the record's pauses, course-scope extensions, item and time-limit extensions,

- `shift_for_item(sources, item_id, now, tz) -> int` and `shift_for_time_limit(sources, now, tz)
  -> int`, applying Decisions 3 to 6.

`resolver.py`:

- `due_instant(value, registration_date, tz, shift_days=0)` adds `shift_days` to the local date
  before attaching the time. `resolve_deadline(layers, registration_date, tz, shift_days=0)`
  passes it through. `ResolvedDeadline` gains `shift_days: int = 0`; `source` is unchanged.
- `CourseDeadlines` gains `pause: CoursePause | None`, the open pause when there is one, so the
  dashboard, course detail, spec 5's reminders and spec 7's at-risk views read the paused state
  from the one call they already make.
- `course_deadlines_for` reads, for all records at once, the pauses, the extensions, the topic
  completion instants and the completed form attempts: four queries more, so its ceiling in

  as an optional argument, defaulting to `timezone.now()`, so a request evaluates every record at
  one instant.
- `queries.py` `layers_beneath` and `canonical_beneath` ignore extensions: a stacked delta is not
  a value beneath, so the "Default has changed" note never fires because of one.

### Services (`freedom_ls/deadlines/actions.py`)

Shaped like `learner_management/learner_actions.py`: each runs in `transaction.atomic()`, locks
the `CourseProgress` with `select_for_update()`, writes the row, calls `record_entry`, and defers
the webhook and the notification to `transaction.on_commit`. Invalid states raise
`DeadlineActionError(ValueError)` with a message the admin shows as a form error.

- `pause_course(record, *, actor, reason, planned_resume_on=None, now=None) -> CoursePause`.
  Refused when the record is complete, already paused, or `planned_resume_on` is not after
  `now`'s date. Closes a passed planned pause first (Decision 7). Audit `course.paused`, details
  `{"course": title, "planned_resume_on": iso | None}`; webhook; notification `course.paused`.
- `resume_course(record, *, actor, reason="", now=None) -> CoursePause | None`. None when not
  paused. Sets `ended_at` (Decision 7), `ended_by`, `resume_announced_at`. Audit
  `course.resumed`, details `{"course": title, "shift_days": n}`; webhook; notification
  `course.resumed`.
- `announce_planned_resume(pause, *, now=None) -> bool`, for spec 5's sweep: for a pause whose
  planned instant has passed and whose `resume_announced_at` is null, sets `ended_at` to the
  planned instant when still null, claims the marker with a conditional update, and on success
  writes the audit entry with no actor, fires `course.resumed` with `actor_id: null` and
  `planned: true`, and raises `course.resumed`. Returns whether it announced. `CoursePause`
  gains a manager method `due_for_announcement(now, site_id)` for the sweep's query.
- `extend_deadline(record, *, actor, reason, scope, collection_item=None, days=None,
  requested_date=None, now=None) -> DeadlineExtension`. Exactly one of `days` and
  `requested_date`; `requested_date` is not allowed with `scope=course`. Refused when the record
  is complete; when the target resolves to no deadline ("This item has no deadline" / "This
  course has no time limit"); when `scope=course` and nothing resolves to a deadline; when
  `requested_date` is on or before the target's effective date. Audit `deadline.extended`,
  details `{"course", "scope", "item", "days", "due_at_before", "due_at_after"}` (the two
  instants null for `course` scope); webhook; notification `deadline.extended`. Extending a time
  limit whose access end has passed restores access, because the access end instant moves.
- `is_paused(record, now=None) -> bool`, the read used by the access backend and the services.

Every function takes `site_id` from the record, never from the request.

## Testing

Pure functions (`shifts.py`, `resolver.py`), explicit `now`:

- `paused_days`: same instant is 0; one second is 1; exactly 24 hours is 1; 24 hours and one
  second is 2; across a DST change the count follows elapsed time.
- A shift adds days to the local date: end of day stays end of day; a `due_time` stays; a date on
  the DST day lands on the local time.
- An open pause shifts to `now` and nothing becomes overdue, closed or ended during it; an item
  overdue at pause start is overdue by the same margin after resume.
- A pause that began after an item's completion does not move that item; a course-scope extension
  likewise; an `item` extension moves a completed item too.
- Two extensions on one item sum; a pause and an extension sum in either order; a `course`
  extension moves every unmet item and the time limit; a `time_limit` extension moves only the
  time limit; an item with no deadline resolves to none whatever the shift.
- `end_instant`: `ended_at` wins over a planned resume; a planned resume is 00:00 of its day in
  the site zone; neither gives None.

Resolver and policy:

- `course_deadlines_for` runs at most its new ceiling for 1 and for many records and items.
- Each new policy setting resolves through each layer; `0` at a lower layer beats `N` above it;
  null inherits; all unset gives the defaults; `sources` names the layer.
- E001 fails on a missing key, a negative integer, a non-integer, and passes on `0`.

Services (`actions.py`):

- Each writes its row, one audit entry with the stated details, one webhook after commit with the
  stated payload and one notification; a rolled-back action writes and fires nothing.
- `pause_course` refuses a complete record, a paused record and a planned date not after today;
  it closes a passed planned pause and starts a new one in one transaction.
- `resume_course` returns None and writes nothing when not paused; called twice, it shifts once
  and fires once; after a passed planned instant it records `ended_at` as that instant.
- `announce_planned_resume` announces once under two concurrent callers, writes `ended_at`, and
  skips a pause already announced or not yet due; `due_for_announcement` returns only another
  site's rows when given that site.
- `extend_deadline` converts `requested_date` to days against the effective instant; refuses a
  date on or before it, a missing item for `item`, a date for `course`, a target with no
  deadline, a course with nothing to move, and a complete record; a time-limit extension after
  access ended restores access.

Access and player (request level):

- A paused learner: `get_access` returns `can_access_content=False` with `paused_at` set and
  `access_ended_at` None, within the raised cap, for 1 and for many items; `get_access_many`
  agrees; a deactivated registration gets the inner decision; a complete course is never paused;
  a paused learner whose time limit has also passed reads paused, not ended.
- Every route: `view_course_item` on an incomplete item, `form_start`, `form_fill_page` GET and
  POST, `course_finish` and `form_submit_and_exit` redirect to `course_detail` and write nothing;
  an open attempt keeps its answers and `completed_time` null; `course_form_complete` for a
  completed attempt opens with no retry, next or previous; a completed item opens;
  `course_home` redirects.
- After resume, the same attempt continues on `form_fill_page` and the item's close instant has
  moved by the paused days.
- Course index: `PAUSED` leaves on an open row, `BLOCKED` unchanged under sequential unlock,
  `COMPLETE` rows linked, no due or close keys on paused rows; a part of paused children reads
  `PAUSED`.
- Dashboard card, row, `all_courses` and course detail show the paused copy with and without a
  planned resume, no deadline line, no Continue; when the planned instant passes, the next
  request shows the normal state with shifted dates and no write occurred.
- A planned pause, a staff resume and an extension leave the registration active, fire no
  `registration.deactivated`, and keep the course on the dashboard.
- Site isolation: another site's pauses and extensions never apply.

Admin:

- Each action: GET shows the form; POST with a blank reason re-renders with an error; a valid
  POST calls the service once, shows the success message and redirects; a repeated POST of the
  same resume is a no-op with a warning; Pause is hidden when paused and Resume when not;
  a user without change permission gets 403; another site's record is 404.
- The inlines list rows newest first and are not editable; the list admins offer no add.
- `effective_deadlines` shows the shift and the paused line.

Vocabulary and registry: every new code passes `test_vocabulary.py`, has a sample, and the
notification categories pass `comms/checks.py`.

## Open questions

- Q: Does a planned resume notify the learner, or only fire the webhook? → A: It notifies too,
  through `course.resumed` (assumed default: the learner was not there and their dates moved; the
  idea assigns only the webhook to spec 5's sweep. Overrule by editing `announce_planned_resume`
  under Services.)
- Q: Do the shift rules read completion instants, or snapshot the unmet items on each record? →
  A: Read instants, four bulk queries (assumed default: no per-item rows, and the resolver stays
  a function of records and progress. Overrule by editing Decision 4.)
- Q: `course.*` or a new noun for the pause events? → A: `course`, after `course.completed`
  (assumed default: an existing noun with the same meaning. Overrule by editing Decision 10.)

## Success criteria

- Staff pause a learner on 1 Dec, the learner opens nothing but their results, and on resume on
  4 Dec at 10:00 every unmet deadline and the time limit read four days later than before; a
  topic completed in November keeps its date.
- A pause with a planned resume of 12 Dec shows "Paused until 12 Dec" on the dashboard and, on
  12 Dec, the course opens with shifted dates and no sweep ran.
- Two extensions of 7 and 5 days on one item move its deadline and close instant by 12 days.
- Each action leaves one audit entry on the learner's History, one webhook event with the stated
  payload, and one notification linking to course detail.
- A project that overrides `DEADLINE_POLICY_DEFAULTS` without the three new keys fails system
  checks with E001.
