# Spec: extensions and pause

Spec 3 of the Deadlines effort (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`, "Deadlines"). Built on
`deadlines-1-dates-and-overdue`, `deadlines-2-consequence-policy` and
`deadlines-8-per-placement-dates`, all merged. Source: `idea.md` in this directory; the up-front
answers are in `answers.md`.

## Purpose

Spec 1 resolves a learner's deadlines and spec 2 enforces their consequences, but once a date is
set the only remedy is a deadline override that replaces it. This spec gives staff two remedies in
the Django admin: an extension, which adds days to a deadline and stacks, and a pause, which stops
the clock and moves every unmet deadline forward by the days paused.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CoursePause` | coined | One pause of one `CourseProgress`. "Suspend" is retired. |
| `DeadlineExtension` | coined | One addition of days to one `CourseProgress`'s deadlines. Not a `LearnerDeadlineOverride`, which replaces a value. |
| shift | coined | The whole days a resolved deadline is moved forward by the pauses and extensions that apply to it. Learner copy says "moved by N days". |
| paused days | coined | The length of one pause in whole days, rounded up (Decision 2). |
| planned resume | coined | `CoursePause.planned_resume_on`, a date; the pause ends at 00:00 of that day in the site time zone. |
| paused | coined | The state of a `CourseProgress` with an open pause. Not the sign-in lockout's "paused" in `accounts/lockout.html`. |
| unmet deadline | coined | The resolved deadline of a collection item that is incomplete when read, and the time limit while the course is incomplete. |
| target kind | coined | `DeadlineExtension.target_kind`: `collection_item`, `time_limit` or `course` (every unmet deadline and the time limit). |

## Scope

In: the two models and three pause-limit policy settings; the shift inside the resolver; service
functions that pause, resume and extend; the paused state in the access check, player, course
detail and dashboard; admin actions, history and read-only lists; audit codes, webhooks and
notifications; a hook for spec 5's sweep.

Out:

- Learner self-pause and enforcing the pause limits (spec 4). The limits are stored and resolved
  here and bind nobody yet; `pause_course` does not read them.
- Reminders, the housekeeping sweep itself, educator notifications, `deadline.missed` (spec 5).
  A paused record gets no reminder; spec 5 reads `CourseDeadlines.pause` to skip it.
- Educator-interface screens, bulk extension across learners, revoking an extension (spec 6).
- Email delivery of the notifications (`user-communication-2-notification-email`).
- Shortening a deadline, approval workflows, fees.

## Decisions

1. **The shift is computed when read, never stored.** Spec 1's rule that nothing is written to
   enforce a deadline stands.
2. **Whole days, rounded up, added to the local date.** `paused_days = ceil((min(end_instant,
   now) - started_at) / 86400 s)`, where an open pause's end is `now`. Days are added to the
   deadline's local date before its time is attached, as `due_instant` already does for relative
   deadlines, so end of day, a `due_time` and a DST change are untouched. A pause ended within
   the same instant is zero days; any longer one is at least one.
3. **A shift applies to a collection item only if the item was unmet when the shift began.** The
   resolver reads each item's completion instant: `TopicProgress.complete_time`, or the earliest
   `CourseFormAttempt` for which `attempt_completes_form` is true, so a failed sitting leaves the
   item unmet. A pause or `course` extension that began after that instant skips the item. Both
   always apply to the time limit. A `collection_item` or `time_limit` extension always applies.
4. **Extensions stack by addition.** Each row holds `days`; the resolver sums them. "Move to this
   date" is converted to days against the target's effective instant at grant time.
5. **An open pause counts up to `now`**, so nothing becomes overdue, closes or ends during a
   pause. An item already overdue or closed when the pause began stays so after resume to within
   the rounding: a margin of a day or more survives, a shorter one may be absorbed by the
   round-up.
6. **A pause ends at the earlier of `ended_at` and the planned resume instant.** Read paths never
   write; `pause_course` and `announce_planned_resume` are the two writers that close a passed
   planned pause, recording `ended_at` as the planned instant. Whichever closes it announces
   it, once, so `course.resumed` is never heard after the next `course.paused`.
7. **Paused beats access ended; a finished course is never paused or extended.**
8. **A paused record is not `access_ended_at`.** Spec 2's ended path finalises open attempts;
   a pause freezes them, so it has its own decision fields and route branch.
9. **One code per change, shared by the audit log and the webhook registry**: `course.paused`,
   `course.resumed`, `deadline.extended`, after `course.completed`, which already names a
   learner's course record.
10. **The reason is staff-only.** No payload, notification or learner copy carries it.
11. **Pause limits default to no limit.** Null inherits, `0` is no limit, `N` is a cap; the
    settings layer ships `0`. `learners_may_pause` defaults off.
12. **Staff act on the course progress record.** Pauses and extensions stay with it on a cohort
    move, as overrides do.
13. **The services are the only writers**; the admin, spec 4, spec 5's sweep and spec 6 call
    them.
14. **A planned resume is announced by spec 5's sweep** through `announce_planned_resume`, with
    no actor, and it notifies the learner too: they were not there and their dates moved.

- `CheckConstraint`s for the three field rules above. No uniqueness: rows stack. `clean()`

- `signals.py` carries `DeadlineExtension` by `course_progress` on a content move, moving every
  stale row without the uniqueness `exclude` the override carry uses, since stacked rows never
  clash; the `_CARRIED_PARENTS` type union gains the model.

Pause-limit settings on `DeadlineConfigFields`, one migration across the six layers:
`learners_may_pause` (`_switch_field`, joins `SWITCH_FIELDS`), `max_pauses` and `max_pause_days`
(`PositiveIntegerField(null=True, blank=True)`); all three join `CONFIG_FIELDS`, with help text
naming the `0` meaning. Factories: `CoursePauseFactory` (open, started an hour ago) and
`DeadlineExtensionFactory` (time limit, 7 days).

### Shift and resolver (`freedom_ls/deadlines/shifts.py`, `queries.py`, `resolver.py`)

- `shifts.py`, pure: `paused_days(started_at, end_at)`; `ShiftSources` (the record's pauses,
  extensions and item completion instants); `shift_for_collection_item(sources,
  collection_item_id, now, tz) -> int`; `shift_for_time_limit(sources, now, tz) -> int`.
- `queries.py`: `open_pause(record, now) -> CoursePause | None` and `open_pauses_for(records,
  now)`, the one read of the paused state, used by the access backend, the services and
  `course_deadlines_for`. Both are built on `pauses_for(records)`, the one query for the
  records' pause rows, open and ended; its result is what `pauses` below accepts. The admin reads `layers_beneath` and `canonical_beneath` ignore
  extensions, so the "Default has changed" note never fires because of one.
- `due_instant(value, registration_date, tz, shift_days=0)` adds the days to the local date
  before attaching the time; `resolve_deadline` passes it through. `ResolvedDeadline` gains
  `shift_days: int = 0`; `source` is unchanged.
- `CourseDeadlines` gains `pause: CoursePause | None`, the open pause when there is one.
- `course_deadlines_for(records, *, now=None, items=True, pauses=None)`: `now` defaults to
  `timezone.now()` and is the one instant every consequence check in a request uses, threaded
  through `_ended_or_none`, `_deadline_keys` and `_is_closed_for_learner`. Pauses and extensions
  are read in one query each for all records; `pauses` accepts rows a caller already loaded.
  The two completion-instant queries are
  `learner_progress.queries.completion_instants(course_progress_ids)`, so `deadlines` gains no
  edge to `form_engine`. They run only with `items=True` and only for records with a pause or
  a `course` extension; the time-limit path passes
  `items=False`. The ceiling in `tests/test_resolver.py` rises by the queries each path adds and
  stays constant in records and items.

### Services (`freedom_ls/deadlines/actions.py`)

Shaped like `learner_management/learner_actions.py`: each runs in `transaction.atomic()`, locks
the record through `CourseProgress._base_manager.select_for_update()` (the sweep has no request
site), writes the row, calls `record_entry`, and defers the webhook (`fire_webhook_event_on_commit`
with `record.site`) and the notification to `transaction.on_commit`. Invalid input raises
`DeadlineActionError(ValueError)`, which the admin shows as a form error; that covers a reason
longer than `REASON_LENGTH`, a complete record, and the cases below. The notification is skipped
when `actor` is the learner's own user.

- `pause_course(record, *, actor, reason, planned_resume_on=None, now=None) -> CoursePause`.
  Refused when already paused or `planned_resume_on` is not after `now`'s date in the site time
  zone. Audit details `{"course", "planned_resume_on"}`.
- `resume_course(record, *, actor, reason, now=None) -> CoursePause | None`. None when not
  paused. Sets `ended_at = now`, `ended_by`, `ended_reason`, `resume_announced_at`. Audit
  details `{"course", "shift_days"}`.
- `announce_planned_resume(pause, *, now=None) -> bool`, for spec 5's sweep: for a pause whose
  planned instant has passed and whose `resume_announced_at` is null, claims the marker with a
  conditional update, sets `ended_at` to the planned instant when null, and on success writes the
  audit entry with no actor, fires `course.resumed` with `planned: true`, and notifies. Returns
  whether it announced. `CoursePause.objects.due_for_announcement(now, site)` is the sweep's
  query; it reads the site's zone to compare `planned_resume_on`.
- `extend_deadline(record, *, actor, reason, target_kind, collection_item=None, days=None,
  requested_date=None, now=None) -> DeadlineExtension`. Exactly one of `days` and
  `requested_date`; a date is refused for `course`. Refused when the target resolves to no
  deadline ("This item has no deadline", "This course has no time limit"), when `course` has
  nothing to move, or when `requested_date` is on or before the target's effective instant.
  Audit details `{"course", "target_kind", "collection_item", "days", "due_at_before",
  "due_at_after"}`, the two instants null for `course`. A time-limit extension after access
  ended restores access, because the access end instant moves.

## Testing

- `paused_days`: same instant is 0; one second is 1; exactly 24 hours is 1; one second more is
  2; across a DST change the count follows elapsed time. A shift keeps end of day, a `due_time`
  and a DST-day local time.
- An open pause shifts to `now` and nothing becomes overdue, closed or ended during it; an item
  overdue by a day or more at pause start is still overdue after resume, and one overdue by two
  hours is not after a one-hour pause.
- A pause that began after a topic's completion, or after a passing form attempt, does not move
  that item; one that began after a failed attempt does; the time limit always moves.
- Two extensions on one item sum; a pause and an extension sum in either order; after any
  extension an item that was overdue or closed is neither, and the overdue flag and close instant
  never disagree.
- `end_instant`: `ended_at` wins; a planned resume is 00:00 of its day in the site zone.
- `course_deadlines_for` and both backend entry points stay within their caps for 1 and for many
  records and items; `items=False` skips the completion reads.
- Policy: each new setting resolves through each layer; `0` below beats `N` above; null inherits;
  E001 fails on a missing key, a negative, a non-integer and a bool, and passes `0`.
- Services: a rolled-back action writes and fires nothing; each action leaves one audit entry,
  one webhook after commit and one notification, none when the actor is the learner;
  `resume_course` called twice shifts and fires once; `announce_planned_resume` announces once
  under two concurrent callers and skips an announced or not-yet-due pause; `pause_course`
  closes and announces a passed planned pause and starts a new one in one transaction; `extend_deadline`
  converts a date to days and refuses each invalid input; a time-limit extension after access
  ended restores access.
- Access and player: a paused learner whose time limit has also passed reads paused with
  `access_ended_at` None; a deactivated registration gets the inner decision; every refused
  route redirects and writes nothing, an open attempt keeps its answers and `completed_time`
  null, and continues on `form_fill_page` after resume; a completed item and the results of a
  completed attempt open without writing the resume pointer or finalising a retake; a part of
  paused children reads `PAUSED` and `BLOCKED` stays under sequential unlock.
- When the planned instant passes, the next request shows the normal state with shifted dates
  and no write occurred; the registration stays active and no `registration.deactivated` fires.
- Content move: extension rows on both the stale and the new collection item are all carried.
- Site isolation: another site's pauses and extensions never apply; `due_for_announcement`
  returns only the given site's rows.
- Admin: a blank reason re-renders with an error; a repeated resume post is a no-op with a
  warning; a user without change permission gets 403; another site's record is 404.
