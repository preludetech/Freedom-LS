# Frontend QA: due dates, course time limit and overdue

Learners see a due date against each collection item that has one, an "Overdue" badge once it
passes, a "Finish by" or "Time limit ended" cell on the course detail page, and one deadline line on
the dashboard card. Admins set a per-site time zone, and override deadlines per cohort course
registration and per learner from the Django admin. This run walks the content defaults, the three
learner surfaces, both override layers, the "no deadline" override, the "default changed" note, the
site time zone and its label switch, the carry-over on re-sync, and the ways it could go wrong: a
visitor who is not registered, a completed item, an inconsistent override row, a second override for
the same item, a bad time zone name, and a removed content default.

Substitute the literal port number chosen by `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh` wherever
this document writes `$PORT`. Shell variables do not survive between tool calls.

Dates below are written relative to a learner's registration date, because the demo content uses
relative offsets. "Reg" is the date part of that learner's `CourseProgress.created_at` in the site
zone. The site zone for this run is `Africa/Johannesburg` (UTC+2, no DST, abbreviation `SAST`), so
an end-of-day instant is 21:59:59.999999 UTC and the browser, set to the same zone, prints `23:59`.

## §0 Setup

### 0.1 Server

1. `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh`: note the number and use it literally from here on.
2. Start `uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT` backgrounded, as its own call.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/` and confirm `#debug-branch-badge` names the
   `deadlines-1-dates-and-overdue` branch. If it names another branch, another server holds the
   port; go back to step 1.

### 0.2 Browser

Set the Playwright context's time zone to `Africa/Johannesburg` for every test unless a test says
otherwise. Where a test says "the New York browser", open a new context with `America/New_York`.

### 0.3 Seed data

Every item below must exist before §1. Missing data is created through the
`fls-dev:qa-data-helper` agent, never by hand. The names are fictional. Learners have the password
equal to their email address and a verified email address.

1. **Superuser:** `demodev@email.com` from `.claude/fls-dev/config.md`.
2. **Demo content loaded** from `demo_content/` with `content_save`, so `Functionality Demo -
   Course Parts` (slug `functionality-demo-course-parts`) carries `time_limit_days: 90` and the
   due fields on `Welcome` (fixed `2024-01-31`), `What to expect` (7 days), `Key ideas` (14 days,
   17:00), `Knowledge Check` (21 days) and `Feedback` (30 days). Call it *the parts course*.
   `Functionality Demo - End With Topic` carries no deadline; call it *the plain course*.
3. **Site time zone:** one `SiteTimeZoneConfig` row for the dev site, `Africa/Johannesburg`,
   `show_time_zone_to_learners` off.
4. **Organisation** `QA Deadlines Org` with a cohort `QA Deadlines Cohort` holding an active
   `CohortCourseRegistration` for the parts course.
5. **Learners:**
   - `qa_deadline_cohort@email.com`: member of `QA Deadlines Cohort`; its `CourseProgress` for
     the parts course has `created_at` set to 1 March 2026 10:00 UTC. The *cohort learner*,
     Reg = 1 Mar 2026.
   - `qa_deadline_solo@email.com`: an individual `LearnerCourseRegistration` for the parts course
     with `CourseProgress.created_at` 100 days before today, and an individual registration for
     the plain course. The *late learner*: its 90-day time limit has ended.
   - `qa_deadline_fresh@email.com`: an individual registration for the parts course made today.

   - `qa_deadline_none@email.com`: no registrations. The *visitor*.
6. **Nothing completed** by any of them yet.

### 0.4 Login

Log in through the site's login form at `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/accounts/login/` with the email

## §1 Content defaults on the table of contents

### 1.1 Due dates and the overdue mark on course detail

1. As the cohort learner open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/courses/functionality-demo-course-parts/`
   (the course detail page; follow the "Details" link from the dashboard if the URL differs).
2. Expand "Getting Started". Expect the `Welcome` row to show an **Overdue** badge (error tone)
   and "Due 31 Jan 2024, 23:59" in the right-hand column. Expect `What to expect` to show
   "Due 8 Mar 2026, 23:59" and no badge.
3. Expand "Core Concepts". Expect `Key ideas` "Due 15 Mar 2026, 17:00" and `Knowledge Check`
   "Due 22 Mar 2026, 23:59".
4. Expand "Wrapping Up". Expect `Feedback` "Due 31 Mar 2026, 23:59" and `Summary` with no date.
5. Expect no date and no badge on any part row ("Getting Started", "Core Concepts", "Wrapping Up").
6. Inspect one date element: it is a `<time>` whose `datetime` attribute ends in `+00:00` (UTC)
   and whose text shows no zone label.

### 1.2 The player sidebar agrees

1. Open `Welcome` from the outline. In the player's sidebar outline expect the same Overdue badge
   and "Due 31 Jan 2024, 23:59" against `Welcome`, and the same dates on the other rows.

### 1.3 Completing an item clears its date and badge

1. Still in the player, mark `Welcome` complete (the completion control at the foot of the topic).
2. Expect the sidebar row for `Welcome` to show neither the badge nor a date.
3. Return to the course detail page; expect the same on its outline. Other rows are unchanged.

### 1.4 A learner registered after a fixed due date is overdue at once

1. Log in as the fresh learner, open the parts course detail page, expand "Getting Started".
2. Expect `Welcome` Overdue with "Due 31 Jan 2024, 23:59", and `What to expect` due 7 days after
   today at 23:59.

### 1.5 A visitor sees no deadline

1. Log in as the visitor, open the parts course detail page.
2. Expect the outline rows to carry no dates and no badges, and the stats strip to carry no
   "Finish by" cell.
3. Log out and open the same page anonymously; expect the same.

## §4 Cohort deadline override

### 4.1 Add an override on the cohort course registration

1. As the superuser open Admin → Learner management → Cohort course registrations → the row for
   `QA Deadlines Cohort` / parts course.
2. In the **Deadline overrides** inline, open the collection item picker. Expect it to list only
   this course's topics and forms as "Title (path)", with the empty choice reading **Course time
   limit**.
3. Add a row: collection item `Welcome`, kind "Days after registration", days `3`, reason
   `QA cohort override`. Save.
4. Reopen the page. Expect the row to show, read-only: Content default "Fixed 31 Jan 2024,
   23:59 SAST" (or that wording's equivalent naming the zone), Effective "3 days after
   registration", set by `demodev@email.com`, and no "Default has changed" note.

### 4.2 The learner sees it

1. As the fresh learner open the parts course detail page and expand "Getting Started". Expect
   `Welcome` to show "Due <today + 3>, 23:59" with no Overdue badge.
2. Open the dashboard: the parts course card now shows "Next due <today + 3>, 23:59" (nothing is
   overdue any more).
3. The late learner (individual registration, not in the cohort) still sees `Welcome` Overdue
   with the 2024 date: the cohort override does not reach an individual registration.

### 4.3 Inconsistent rows are refused

1. Back on the cohort course registration page, add a row with kind "Fixed date" and no date.
   Save. Expect the inline row error "A fixed deadline needs a date." and nothing saved.
2. Add a row with collection item "Course time limit" and kind "Fixed date" with a date. Expect
   "The course time limit is counted in days after registration."
3. Add a second row for `Welcome` (any kind). Expect a uniqueness error on the inline.
4. Add a row with kind "No deadline" and days `5`. Expect "No deadline takes no date, time or days."

### 4.4 Override the course time limit for the cohort

1. Add a row: collection item "Course time limit", kind "Days after registration", days `200`.
   Save.
2. As the cohort learner, the course detail "Finish by" cell now reads "17 Sep 2026, 23:59"
   (Reg + 200). As the late learner (not in the cohort) nothing changes.

## §5 Learner deadline override

### 5.1 "No deadline" for one learner

1. As the superuser open Admin → Learner progress → Course progress → the fresh learner's record
   for the parts course.
2. Expect a read-only **Effective deadlines** field under a "Deadlines" heading listing each
   collection item with a deadline, its due instant in `SAST`, its source (`content` for most,
   `cohort_registration` for none, since this learner is not in the cohort), and the time limit
   end.
3. In the **Deadline overrides** inline add: collection item `Welcome`, kind "No deadline",
   reason `QA no deadline`. Save.
4. Reopen: the Effective deadlines field no longer lists `Welcome`; the inline row shows
   Content default "Fixed 31 Jan 2024 …", Effective "No deadline".
5. As the fresh learner, the parts course outline shows `Welcome` with no date and no badge; the
   dashboard card shows "Next due <today + 7>, 23:59" (`What to expect`).

### 5.2 A learner override beats the cohort override

1. On the cohort learner's Course progress record add: collection item `Welcome`, kind "Fixed
   date", date = today + 1, time `09:00`. Save.
2. Expect the row's Effective "<today + 1>, 09:00 SAST" and the Effective deadlines field naming
   source `learner` for `Welcome`.
3. The cohort learner completed `Welcome` in 1.3, so reset that completion through the data
   helper, then as the cohort learner expect "Due <today + 1>, 09:00" on `Welcome` on the
   outline, in the player sidebar, and "Next due <today + 1>, 09:00" on the dashboard card.

### 5.3 Deleting the override returns to the layers beneath

1. Delete the learner override row from 5.2 and save.
2. As the cohort learner, `Welcome` shows "Due 4 Mar 2026, 23:59" again (the cohort override's
   3 days after Reg): the cohort layer, not the content default, is what is beneath.

## §6 The content default changes under an override

1. Through the data helper, edit `demo_content/functionality_demo_course_parts/01. Getting
   Started/01. welcome/content.md` so `due_date: 2024-01-31` becomes
   `due_days_after_registration: 10`, re-run `content_save` for `demo_content/`, then restore the
   file afterwards (git checkout of that path) and re-run `content_save` once more at the end of
   §6.
2. As the superuser reopen the cohort course registration page. Expect the `Welcome` override
   row to show "Default has changed since this override was set (was fixed:2024-01-31, now
   relative:10)" and Content default "10 days after registration".
3. The learner-facing dates do not move: the cohort learner still sees the override's date.

## §7 Site time zone

### 7.1 Zone label switch

1. As the superuser open Admin → Site aware models → Site time zones → the dev site row. Tick
   `show_time_zone_to_learners`. Save.
2. As the cohort learner open the parts course detail page. Expect every date to carry a short
   zone label, e.g. "8 Mar 2026, 23:59 GMT+2" (the browser's wording for the zone).
3. In the New York browser, log in as the same learner and open the same page. Expect the same
   instant rendered in New York time, e.g. "8 Mar 2026, 16:59 EST" (16:59 because 23:59 SAST is
   21:59 UTC).
4. Untick the switch and save; the New York browser now shows "8 Mar 2026, 16:59" with no label.

### 7.2 A changed zone moves the instant

1. Change the row's `time_zone` to `Pacific/Auckland`. Save.
2. As the cohort learner, in the Johannesburg browser, `What to expect` now reads "8 Mar 2026,
   12:59" (end of day in Auckland is 10:59 UTC in March) and the `datetime` attribute ends in
   `T10:59:59.999999+00:00`.
3. Open the fresh learner's Course progress record in the admin: the Effective deadlines field
   names `NZDT` on its instants.
4. Set `time_zone` back to `Africa/Johannesburg`.

### 7.3 A bad name is refused

1. Set `time_zone` to `Mars/Olympus`. Save. Expect the field error "Not an IANA time zone name,
   e.g. Africa/Johannesburg." and the row unchanged.
2. Try to add a second row for the dev site. Expect a uniqueness error.

## §8 Carry-over on re-sync

1. Through the data helper: copy `demo_content/functionality_demo_course_parts` to a temporary
   directory as a new course with a fresh title `QA Carry-over Course` and fresh `uuid`s on every
   file (so it loads as a separate course), load it with `content_save`, register the fresh
   learner for it, and add a learner override (kind "Days after registration", `5`) on its
   `Key ideas` topic from the Course progress page.
2. Confirm as the fresh learner that `Key ideas` on the carry-over course shows "Due <today + 5>".
3. Through the data helper: move the `01. key-ideas` directory from `02. Core Concepts` to
   `03. Wrapping Up` in the temporary copy and run `content_save` again.
4. As the fresh learner open the carry-over course detail. `Key ideas` now appears under
   "Wrapping Up" **and still shows "Due <today + 5>"**. (The stale row under "Core Concepts" is
   still listed too; that is today's sync behaviour and not a defect of this change.)
5. In the admin, the override row on the fresh learner's Course progress for the carry-over course
   names `Key ideas` with its new path.
6. Through the data helper, delete the carry-over course and the temporary directory.
