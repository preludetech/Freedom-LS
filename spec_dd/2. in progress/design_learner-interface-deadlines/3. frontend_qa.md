# Frontend QA: learner interface deadlines

Every learner-facing deadline now carries a deadline tier (`UPCOMING`, `TODAY`, `MISSED`,
`CLOSED`) shown with colour, an icon and a word. The dashboard card's deadline line is a deadline
band along the bottom of the card's hero. The course player's outline panel opens with a
next-deadline card, outline rows carry their date on the row's own line at the right, and the
current item's deadline sits in a header card beside the item title. The "Closed" line on the
dashboard card now names the item.

This run covers:
- the deadline band for every tier, missed beating next, "Closes", "Access ends" and the no-band
  cases
- the next-deadline card for every tier, the time limit end as "Course", HTMX Previous/Next
- outline rows: inline dates, truncation at 375px, completed and part rows, the course detail
  outline's Closed chip, sequential lock
- the header card on topic pages, form pages and the closed-item page, stacked below `lg`
- the `mark_overdue` switch, the site-zone "Today" boundary, short and full dates in the
  browser's zone
- the dashboard's course row, the all-courses page, the course finish page, the results page under
  `nothing`, the exam runner, and the accessibility tree
- the ways it could go wrong: an organisation mark over the band, a long title squeezing a date,
  "Due" / "Overdue" copy, an icon announced by its semantic name, a year missing from a date in
  another year

**Wording.** Learner pages say "Deadline", "Deadline today", "Missed deadline", "Closed",
"Closes", "Next deadline", "Access ends" and "Access ended". If "Due", "Overdue", "was due",
"Next deadline:" (with a colon) or "Expired" shows on a learner page, that is a bug. Every chip
carries text or sr-only text, never colour alone.

Substitute the literal port number chosen by `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh` wherever
this document writes `$PORT`. Shell variables do not survive between tool calls.

The site zone for this run is `Africa/Johannesburg` (UTC+2, no DST). An end-of-day deadline is
21:59:59 UTC. The browser is set to the same zone. The browser prints full dates as
"Jan 31, 2024, 11:59 PM" and short dates as "Jan 31, 2024" (another year) or "Mar 2" (this year).
"Today" is the date of the run in that zone.

Design screenshots are under
`spec_dd/2. in progress/design_learner-interface-deadlines/design_screenshots/`. They are a
visual reference: compare layout and hierarchy, not colours, fonts, icons or copy.

## §0 Setup

### 0.1 Server

1. `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh`: note the number and use it literally from here on.
2. Start `uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT` backgrounded, as its own call.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/` and confirm `#debug-branch-badge` names the
   `design_learner-interface-deadlines` branch. If it names another branch, another server holds
   the port; go back to step 1.
4. If `#djDebugToolbar` is visible, click `#djHideToolBarButton` before any screenshot.

### 0.2 Browser

Set the Playwright context's time zone to `Africa/Johannesburg` for every test. Viewports:
1920x1080 ("desktop"), 880x1240 ("tablet"), 375x812 ("mobile"), unless a test says otherwise. The
dashboard shows cards at desktop and course rows at tablet and mobile. The outline panel is
docked at desktop and a bottom sheet (opened with "Open course outline") at tablet and mobile.

### 0.3 Seed data

Missing data is created through the `sdd:qa-data-helper` agent, never by hand. Learners have
the password equal to their email address and a verified email address.

1. **Demo content loaded** with `uv run python manage.py content_save demo_content DemoDev`, so
   `Functionality Demo - Course Parts` (slug `functionality-demo-course-parts`, *the parts

   `What to Expect` (7 days after registration), `Key Ideas` (14 days, 17:00), `Knowledge Check`
   (21 days, a form) and `Course Feedback` (30 days). Its parts are `Getting Started`, `Core
   Concepts` and `Wrapping Up`. `Functionality Demo - show end with Topic` (slug
   `functionality-demo-show-end-with-topic`) is *the plain course*.
2. **Seed command:** `uv run python manage.py qa_create_deadlines_seed`. On `DemoDev` it builds
   the superuser `demodev@email.com`, the site zone `Africa/Johannesburg` with the zone label
   hidden, and:
   - `qa_deadline_fresh@email.com`, the *fresh learner*: parts-course registration made now, no
     config rows. `Welcome` is missed (2024), `What to Expect` is due in 7 days, `Key Ideas` in
     14, `Knowledge Check` in 21, `Course Feedback` in 30, the time limit in 90.
   - `qa_deadline_solo@email.com`, the *late learner*: parts-course registration made 100 days ago
     with `close_items=on`, `grace_window=3 days`, `end_course_access=off`; `Welcome`, `What to
     Expect` and `Key Ideas` are closed, `Knowledge Check` is a closed form with an open attempt,
     `Course Feedback` was due yesterday and closes in two days. Also a plain-course registration
     with `end_course_access=on`, `after_access_ended=nothing`, ended 10 days ago.
   - `qa_deadline_restored@email.com`, the *restored learner*: parts-course registration made 100
     days ago, `end_course_access=on`, time limit overridden to 30 days from now.
   - `qa_deadline_cohort@email.com`, the *cohort learner*: cohort member, parts-course record
     created 1 March 2026, access ended 30 May 2026 under `outline_and_results`.
   - `qa_deadline_fresh@email.com`'s and the others' progress is reset, so nothing is completed.
3. **Today's deadline.** Through the data helper, give the fresh learner a
   `LearnerDeadlineOverride` on the parts course's `What to Expect` placement with a fixed
   `due_date` of today (site zone) and `due_time` `23:59`. Call this *the today item*. Remove it
   at the end of §5.
4. **A long title.** Through the data helper, create a plain course `QA Long Deadline Titles`
   (slug `qa-long-deadline-titles`) with two topics: `A deliberately long topic title that cannot
   fit a phone outline row beside its date` (fixed due date 2020-01-01) and `Second topic` (no
   due date); register the fresh learner on it. Give it an organisation that is not the site's
   default (`QA Deadlines Org` from the seed), so its dashboard card draws an organisation byline.
5. Nothing else: no `SiteDeadlineConfig`, `OrganisationDeadlineConfig` or `CourseDeadlineConfig`
   row exists before §1. A test that creates one deletes it at its end.

Design state: `cards__card-c.png` (1180 wide).

1. Log in as the fresh learner; open `/`. The parts-course card is in "In progress".
2. The card's hero (the coloured block with the course icon) carries a band along its bottom
   edge, inside the hero's rounded corners, red-tinted, reading left: warning icon, bold "Missed

Design checklist (from the plan's `cards/card-c` transcription):
- [ ] the band is inside the hero block, along its bottom edge, within the rounded corners
- [ ] band left: icon, bold tier word, then the title, in that order; the title truncates
- [ ] band right: date text whole, right-aligned
- [ ] `UPCOMING` neutral tint, `TODAY` amber tint, `MISSED` and `CLOSED` red tint
- [ ] a card with no deadline, a completed course or ended access shows no band
- [ ] the body shows no "Next deadline" line; "Access ends <date>." stays in the body
- [ ] the organisation mark does not cover the band's word or title
