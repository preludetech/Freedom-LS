# Spec: learner interface deadlines

## Purpose

Learners already see their deadlines as plain text: one muted line on each dashboard course card,
one line under each outline row, and one line above the course item's title in the course player.
Following `idea.md`, this work gives each deadline a deadline tier, shown with colour and an icon.
It moves the card's deadline to a card deadline line at the bottom of the card, adds an outline
deadline under the outline panel's progress bar, moves row dates onto the row's own line, puts the
current item's deadline on a header deadline line above its title, and redraws the closed-item
page. Every one of these is a tier-coloured icon and text: no chip, band, border or tint.

Resources:

- `spec_dd/2. in progress/design_learner-interface-deadlines/design.md`: the Claude Design design.
  Read it as that file says. The treatments this spec builds are `key__key`, `cards__card-b`, `hero__hero-c`
  (placement only, for the course row; not its pill), `p-outline__ol-a` (dates as text),
  `p-banner__bn-a` (its icon-and-bold-text treatment only, as the header deadline line; not the
  full-width bar), `tablet__tb-player`, `mobile__m-toc` (without its chip) and `mobile__m-player`
  (the closed state only). Take layout and hierarchy from them.
- `.claude/skills/brand-guidelines/visual.md`: the brand's colour roles and components, which
  every deadline surface uses. The deadline treatment itself is in "Deadline label component"
  below.
- `research_*.md` beside this file.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deadline tier | coined | How urgent an unfinished deadline looks: `UPCOMING`, `TODAY`, `MISSED` or `CLOSED`. Always written in full. It has nothing to do with the theming tiers in `docs/product/configuration-and-extension.md`. |
| card deadline line | coined | The last line of a dashboard course card or course row, below the progress bar, that names one deadline for the course, with Details at its right. |
| outline deadline | coined | The plain text block under the progress bar in the course player's outline header that names one deadline for the course. |
| header deadline line | coined | The line above the course item's title in the player's content header that shows that item's deadline. |

## Scope

In:

- A deadline tier for every dated, unfinished topic and form row, and for the time limit end.
- The card deadline line, on the dashboard course card and on the course row.
- The outline deadline.
- Outline row dates on the row's own line, at the right, in the player.
- The header deadline line.
- The closed-item page: a large centred icon with its text, in place of the callout.
- Fix the open deadlines-2 QA bug B1, where a "Closed" deadline on the card drops the item title.

Out:

- The upcoming deadlines page and any link to it. Its idea is
  `spec_dd/1. next/design_learner-upcoming-deadlines/idea.md`.
- Everything else in `design.md`: other card, panel, hero, alert, banner, drawer and popover
  options, the dashboard at tablet and mobile widths, durations, course part counts, tags and
  bookmarks.
- Deadlines on `CoursePart` (course part) rows, relative text ("3 days left"), countdowns,
  penalties, extensions, and any hard or soft deadline distinction. The system models none of these.
- Deadline information on the course detail page. Its outline shows no dates, no "Closed" text
  and no tier or lock icon; the page shows only the time limit's length.
- The exam runner (`_exam_runner_base.html`, `course_form_page.html`). It overrides `body` and
  draws no content header, and it shows no deadline today.
- Any change to deadline resolution, policy or consequences. `CourseDeadlines` only records the
  zone the resolver already uses.

## Decisions

1. **Copy stays "Deadline", "Missed deadline", "Closed", "Closes", "Access ends".** The domain
   glossary and `docs/product/learner-experience.md` forbid the design's "Due", "Overdue" and "was
   due".
2. **The `TODAY` tier uses the site zone; the word "Today" uses the viewer's.** The tier (colour,
   icon and word) is judged in the zone that already fixes an undated deadline's end of day. The
   short date text is rewritten by the browser, and it reads "Today" when the instant falls on the
   viewer's own date, so one row never mixes two zones. A learner far from the site zone can see a
   warning-coloured date that reads tomorrow; this is accepted.
3. **A past deadline with `mark_overdue` off is `UPCOMING`.** Deadlines-2 makes that switch
   suppress every late mark.
4. **Missed beats next.** The card deadline line and the outline deadline show the earliest past deadline
   when there is one, else the earliest upcoming one. The time limit end counts as one more
   deadline, titled "Course".
5. **Outline rows and the card deadline line use a short date, with the time when the deadline
   has one. The outline deadline and the header deadline line use the full date and time.** Rows stay short (`idea.md`);
   a deadline given as a date alone shows no time, and one given with a time shows it, because a
   learner planning around "Today" needs the cut-off. Rows also say when they close, so a row in
   its grace window says how long it has.
6. **Every short form has full screen-reader text.** A tier's icon and word carry its meaning
   without colour. On an outline row the tier word is sr-only beside the icon; every other
   surface shows the tier word.
7. **Project theme only.** The tiers use theme text colour roles and semantic icon names. No new
   tokens or icons. The one new shared component is `<c-deadline-label>`, which every deadline
   surface uses.
8. **Icon and text, never a chip.** A deadline is a tier-coloured `<c-icon>` and text, drawn by
   `<c-deadline-label>`, as "Deadline label component" says. No deadline surface uses
   `<c-chip>`, a badge, a border, a background tint or a `-light` tint colour.

## Requirements

### Deadline tier

In `freedom_ls/learner_interface/utils.py`:

- Add `DeadlineTier(StrEnum)` with `UPCOMING`, `TODAY`, `MISSED` and `CLOSED`.
- Add `deadline_tier(due_at: datetime, *, is_overdue: bool, is_closed: bool, now: datetime, zone: ZoneInfo) -> DeadlineTier`.
  It returns `CLOSED` if `is_closed`, else `MISSED` if `is_overdue`, else `TODAY` if
  `now < due_at` and `due_at.astimezone(zone).date() == now.astimezone(zone).date()`, else
  `UPCOMING`.
- `DeadlineKeys` gains `deadline_tier: DeadlineTier | None`. It is `None` when `due_at` is `None`
  or the row is complete, so completed rows show no deadline (user answer). Course part rows carry
  no deadline keys, as now.
- `CourseDeadlines` (`freedom_ls/deadlines/resolver.py`) gains `zone: ZoneInfo`: the site zone
  `course_deadlines_for` already resolves once per call and builds every instant in.
  `_deadline_keys` reads `course_deadlines.zone`, so the index never looks the zone up. A
  `site_time_zone(course.site)` call would cost one `Site` query per course on the dashboard and
  one more in the player, and both query budgets are at their ceilings.

Each tier on each surface. Every surface draws it with `<c-deadline-label>` (see Deadline label
component); the `tier` prop picks the icon, the colour and the main text.

| Tier | Icon | Colour of icon and main text | Main text (card line, outline deadline, header line) | Outline row (`minimal`) | Card deadline line date | Outline deadline and header line date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UPCOMING` | `deadline` | `text-primary` | "Deadline" | bold icon + short `due_at` in `text-primary`, sr-only "Deadline" | short `due_at` | full `due_at` |
| `TODAY` | `deadline` | `text-on-warning-light` | "Deadline" on the card line; "Deadline today" on the outline deadline and header line (`today_word`) | bold icon + "Today" (plus the time when set) in `text-on-warning-light`, sr-only "Deadline" | "Today" (plus the time when set) | full `due_at` |
| `MISSED` | `warning` | `text-error` | "Missed deadline" | bold icon + short `due_at` in `text-error`, sr-only "Missed deadline" | short `due_at` | full `due_at` |
| `CLOSED` | `locked` | `text-error` | "Closed" | bold icon + short `closes_at` in `text-error`, sr-only "Closed" | short `closes_at` | full `closes_at` |
| `ACCESS_ENDED` | `locked` | `text-error` | "Access ended" | not used | short `access_ended_at` | not used |

`ACCESS_ENDED` is a value of the component's `tier` prop only, for the card and row bottom line;
it is not a `DeadlineTier`.

`text-on-warning-light` is the theme's warning text token that passes contrast on the white page;
it is used as a text colour only, never with its `-light` background.

On every surface the icon and the bold main text share the tier colour. What was missed (the item
title, or "Course") is normal text in the standard text colour (`text-on-surface`). On the
`inline` and `stacked` layouts the date and time are subtle: `text-muted font-mono`. There are no
separator dots: the inline header line reads "Missed deadline Jan 31, 2024, 11:59 PM", and a
stacked label puts "Missed deadline", "Welcome" and "Jan 31, 2024" on three lines. A deadline
that is not closed and has a close instant ahead gets a second `<c-deadline-label>` directly under
it, in the same layout: the `locked` icon and bold "Closes" in the due tier's colour, then the close
date in the surface's date format. It is a separate element, never text inside the first label.

The tier icon is wrapped in `<span aria-hidden="true">`, as `cotton/button.html` does for its
icons, with the main text visible or sr-only beside it, because the icon backend falls back to the
semantic name as the `aria-label` and a screen reader would announce "warning".

### Deadline label component

Every learner deadline, "Access ended" included, renders through one cotton component,
`freedom_ls/learner_interface/templates/cotton/deadline-label.html` (`<c-deadline-label>`). No
surface hand-builds deadline markup.

Props:

- `tier`: `UPCOMING`, `TODAY`, `MISSED`, `CLOSED` or `ACCESS_ENDED`. Picks the icon, the colour and
  the main text, as the tier table says.
- `word`: main text that replaces the tier's own.
- `today_word`: when set, a `TODAY` deadline reads "Deadline today".
- `closes`: when set, the label is the close date: main text "Closes", the `locked` icon, and the
  colour of `tier` (the due tier above it).
- `detail`: what the deadline is for (the item title, or "Course"), in normal text.
- `layout`: `inline`, `stacked` or `minimal`.
- `class`: extra classes on the outer element.

The default slot is the date and time. Callers fill it with `deadline_labels.html#date-text`,
which keeps the date on one line so the label wraps between its parts, never inside a date.

Layouts:

- `inline`: one line that wraps between its parts: icon, bold main text, detail, date and time.
  The header deadline line uses it.
- `stacked`: the icon in its own column, aligned to the top beside the main text, beside three lines:
  bold main text, detail, date and time. The card deadline line and the course row use it with the
  short date; the outline deadline uses it with the full date and `today_word`.
- `minimal`: for outline rows. The icon and the short date, both bold in the tier colour; the main
  text and detail are sr-only. With no date it shows the main text instead.

### Deadline date partials

`partials/deadline_labels.html` writes the dates that fill the component's slot. It is a set of
`partialdef`s with an empty top level:

- `tier-date`: the date for the tier (`closes_at` for `CLOSED`, else `due_at`).
- `date-text`: `tier-date` in a `whitespace-nowrap` span.
- `closes-label`: while the item can still close, a `<c-deadline-label closes="true">` in the
  caller's `layout` and the due tier, with the close date in the caller's `date_format`. Every
  surface that draws a deadline includes it directly after the deadline's label.
- `access-ends-line`: a stacked `<c-deadline-label tier="UPCOMING" word="Access ends">` with the
  short `access_ends_at`, under the card and row deadline.

They read `child` and the optional `date_format` (`"short"` or unset). The component renders
`detail` for a closed deadline too, which fixes B1 on the card and on the course row
(`partials/course_row.html`: the all-courses page and the dashboard below `lg`).
`course_deadline_band.html` and `course_deadline_line.html` are removed, and so are the earlier
`tier-icon`, `tier-word`, `tier-text`, `deadline-text`, `row-date` and `header-line` partials.

### Short date format

`local_time` in `freedom_ls/base/templatetags/time_tags.py` (re-registered by `deadline_tags`) takes an optional `format`:

- `"short"`: written as `data-format="short"`. The server text is the day and abbreviated month in
  the site zone (`site_zone_text` gains a short form), plus the year when it differs from the
  current year in the site zone, or "Today" when the instant falls on the current date in the
  site zone. A deadline with a set time (`has_set_time`: its time is not `time.max`, judged in
  its own zone) also carries `data-timed="true"` and the server text adds ", HH:MM" and the zone
  name, as the long form does. The `localTime` component in
  `freedom_ls/base/static/base/js/alpine-components.js` formats it with
  `{day: "numeric", month: "short"}`, adds the year when it differs from the viewer's current
  year, writes "Today" instead when the instant falls on the viewer's own date, and adds the
  time (with the zone only when `data-show-zone` is true) for a timed deadline.
- Any other value raises `ValueError`.

The `datetime` attribute is unchanged in every format. Without `format`, the output is exactly
what it is today.

### Shared deadline selection

Move `CardDeadline`, `CardDeadlineLine` and `_card_deadline_line` from
`freedom_ls/learner_interface/views.py` to `freedom_ls/learner_interface/utils.py`. Rename
`_card_deadline_line` to the public `card_deadline_line`, and add `deadline_tier` and `is_past`
fields to `CardDeadline`. Row deadlines copy the row's `deadline_tier`; `is_past` is set on every
deadline in the missed list, so a template can tell a past deadline without comparing instants. `card_deadline_line` takes the nested course index and flattens it with
`_item_rows`, so the dashboard and the player flatten rows the same way. The time limit end's
tier is computed with `course_deadlines.zone`.

### Dashboard: card deadline line

Design: `cards__card-b` for the card, `hero__hero-c` placement for the course row.

- `freedom_ls/learner_interface/templates/cotton/course-card-shell.html` keeps the hero as on

  stacked `<c-deadline-label>` as the card; hero-c's pill is not copied. "Access ended" takes the
  same bottom-left slot on the row.

- The status eyebrow is unchanged and stays the card's only status chip. No deadline text goes in
  the hero or the eyebrow.

### Course player: outline deadline

Design: `mobile__m-toc` (without its chip) and `tablet__tb-player`.

- `_player_course_index` (`freedom_ls/learner_interface/views.py:1350`) returns a
  `PlayerCourseIndex` (`rows`, `course_deadlines`, `deadline_policy`, `now`): the index rows,
  the `CourseDeadlines`, policy and one `now` it resolves. When a caller
  passes `course_index` in (`view_course_item`), it also passes the values that came back with
  it. When the chrome builds the index itself (the form fill and results pages), it uses what
  `_player_course_index` returned. The gate-only callers take the index from the result.
  `_player_chrome_context` builds `next_deadline_card` from `card_deadline_line(...)`: the
  selected `CardDeadline`, or `None`, which is also what it gets when `course_index` is passed as
  `[]`. Nothing re-resolves the deadlines.
- It is rendered in `partials/course_toc_header.html` (`data-testid="outline-deadline"`)
  directly after the progress bar, inside `#course-toc-region`, so the HTMX Previous/Next
  out-of-band swap refreshes it.
- It is plain text: no border, no background, no padding box and no chip. It is a stacked
  `<c-deadline-label>` with `today_word` and the full date: the tier icon in its own column, then
  the bold main text in the tier colour ("Missed deadline"; `TODAY` reads "Deadline today"), the
  item title ("Welcome") in the standard text colour and the full `local_time` in
  `text-muted font-mono`. When `closes_at` is set and the deadline is not closed, a stacked
  "Closes" label with the full close date sits under it as its own element. For `CLOSED` the full `local_time` is of `closes_at`, and for every other tier it
  is of `due_at`.
- It is not rendered when there is no deadline, when the course is complete, when access has ended
  (`access_ended_outline`), or when `hide_outline_panel` is set. The player then never shows a
  deadline the dashboard card would not.

The outline panel is one `<dialog>`, docked from `lg` and a bottom sheet below it, so this one
change covers desktop, tablet and the mobile sheet. No new breakpoint or docked tablet layout is
added.

## Success criteria

- A dashboard card with a missed deadline shows, as its last line below the progress bar, a
  warning icon and bold "Missed deadline" in the error colour, with the item title and the short
  date beneath (the title in the standard text colour, the date muted mono), and Details at the right. Its hero holds only the course icon, and the
  organisation avatar overlaps the hero's bottom edge with the name beside it, as on main.
- A card whose only deadline is the time limit end, due later today in the site zone, shows a
  `TODAY` deadline line titled "Course".
- A card whose deadline is closed names the item in its deadline line.
- A course row shows the same deadline at its bottom, below the progress bar, after a divider,
