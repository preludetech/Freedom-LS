# Spec: learner interface deadlines

## Purpose

Learners already see their deadlines as plain text: one muted line on each dashboard course card,
one line under each outline row, and one line above the course item's title in the course player.
Following `idea.md`, this work gives each deadline a deadline tier, shown with colour and an icon.
It turns the card line into a deadline band, adds a next-deadline card to the outline panel, moves
row dates onto the row's own line, and puts the current item's deadline in a header card.

Resources:

- `spec_dd/2. in progress/design_learner-interface-deadlines/design.md`: the Claude Design design.
  Read it as that file says. The treatments this spec builds are `cards__card-c`, `p-sb__sb-b`,
  `p-outline__ol-a`, `p-head__hd-b`, `tablet__tb-player` and `mobile__m-toc`. Take layout and
  hierarchy from them.
- `research_*.md` beside this file.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deadline tier | coined | How urgent an unfinished deadline looks: `UPCOMING`, `TODAY`, `MISSED` or `CLOSED`. Always written in full. It has nothing to do with the theming tiers in `docs/product/configuration-and-extension.md`. |
| deadline band | coined | The strip along the bottom of a dashboard course card's accent hero that names one deadline. |
| next-deadline card | coined | The block at the top of the course player's outline panel that names one deadline for the course. |
| header card | coined | The block beside the course item's title in the player's content header that shows that item's deadline. |

## Scope

In:

- A deadline tier for every dated, unfinished topic and form row, and for the time limit end.
- The deadline band, replacing the dashboard card's deadline line.
- The next-deadline card.
- Outline row dates on the row's own line, at the right, in the player and in the course detail
  outline's Closed chip.
- The header card.
- Fix the open deadlines-2 QA bug B1, where a "Closed" deadline on the card drops the item title.

Out:

- The upcoming deadlines page and any link to it. Its idea is
  `spec_dd/1. next/design_learner-upcoming-deadlines/idea.md`.
- Everything else in `design.md`: other card, panel, hero, alert, banner, drawer and popover
  options, the dashboard at tablet and mobile widths, durations, course part counts, tags and
  bookmarks.
- Deadlines on `CoursePart` (course part) rows, relative text ("3 days left"), countdowns,
  penalties, extensions, and any hard or soft deadline distinction. The system models none of these.
- Deadline dates on the course detail page, which still shows none.
- The exam runner (`_exam_runner_base.html`, `course_form_page.html`). It overrides `body` and
  draws no content header, and it shows no deadline today.
- Any change to deadline resolution, policy or consequences. `CourseDeadlines` only records the
  zone the resolver already uses.

## Decisions

1. **Copy stays "Deadline", "Missed deadline", "Closed", "Closes", "Access ends".** The domain
   glossary and `docs/product/learner-experience.md` forbid the design's "Due", "Overdue" and "was
   due".
2. **The `TODAY` tier uses the site zone; the word "Today" uses the viewer's.** The tier (tint,
   icon and word) is judged in the zone that already fixes an undated deadline's end of day. The
   short date text is rewritten by the browser, and it reads "Today" when the instant falls on the
   viewer's own date, so one row never mixes two zones. A learner far from the site zone can see a
   warning chip whose date is tomorrow; this is accepted.
3. **A past deadline with `mark_overdue` off is `UPCOMING`.** Deadlines-2 makes that switch
   suppress every late mark.
4. **Missed beats next.** The band and the next-deadline card show the earliest past deadline
   when there is one, else the earliest upcoming one. The time limit end counts as one more
   deadline, titled "Course".
5. **Rows and the band use a short date, with the time when the deadline has one. The
   next-deadline card and header card use the full date and time.** Rows stay short (`idea.md`);
   a deadline given as a date alone shows no time, and one given with a time shows it, because a
   learner planning around "Today" needs the cut-off. Rows also say when they close, so a row in
   its grace window says how long it has.
6. **Every short form has full screen-reader text.** A tier's icon and word carry its meaning
   without colour: "Closed" is visible, and the other tier words are sr-only beside the icon,
   except where the surface's own eyebrow already names the tier.
7. **Project theme only.** The tiers use the existing `<c-chip>` variants, theme colour roles and
   semantic icon names. No new tokens, icons or shared components.

## Requirements

### Deadline tier

In `freedom_ls/learner_interface/utils.py`:

- Add `DeadlineTier(StrEnum)` with `UPCOMING`, `TODAY`, `MISSED` and `CLOSED`.
- Add `deadline_tier(due_at: datetime, *, is_overdue: bool, is_closed: bool, now: datetime, zone: ZoneInfo) -> DeadlineTier`.
  It returns `CLOSED` if `is_closed`, else `MISSED` if `is_overdue`, else `TODAY` if
  `now < due_at` and `due_at.astimezone(zone).date() == now.astimezone(zone).date()`, else
  `UPCOMING`.
- `DeadlineKeys` gains `deadline_tier: DeadlineTier | None`. It is `None` when `due_at` is `None`
  or the row is complete, so completed rows show no deadline (user answer). Course part rows carry
  no deadline keys, as now.
- `CourseDeadlines` (`freedom_ls/deadlines/resolver.py`) gains `zone: ZoneInfo`: the site zone
  `course_deadlines_for` already resolves once per call and builds every instant in.
  `_deadline_keys` reads `course_deadlines.zone`, so the index never looks the zone up. A
  `site_time_zone(course.site)` call would cost one `Site` query per course on the dashboard and
  one more in the player, and both query budgets are at their ceilings.

Each tier on each surface:

| Tier | Outline row | Deadline band (left word · right text) | Next-deadline card eyebrow | Header card word |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `UPCOMING` | muted `deadline` icon + short `due_at`, sr-only "Deadline", no chip | "Deadline" · short `due_at` | "Next deadline"; "Deadline" when `due_at` is past | "Deadline" |
| `TODAY` | `warning` chip: `deadline` icon + "Today" (plus the time when set), sr-only "Deadline" | "Deadline" · "Today" | "Next deadline" | "Deadline today" |
| `MISSED` | `error` chip: `warning` icon + short `due_at`, sr-only "Missed deadline" | "Missed deadline" · short `due_at` | "Missed deadline" | "Missed deadline" |
| `CLOSED` | `error` chip: `locked` icon + "Closed" + short `closes_at` | "Closed" · short `closes_at` | "Closed" | "Closed" |

An outline row that is not closed and has a close instant ahead adds "Closes <short `closes_at`>."
after its chip. The next-deadline card passes `tier_named` to the chip, so its eyebrow is the one
place the tier word is read.

Chips keep `class="forced-colors:border"`. The tier icon goes inside the chip slot wrapped in
`<span aria-hidden="true" class="inline-flex">`, as `cotton/button.html` does for its icons, with
sr-only text beside it. The chip's `icon` prop is not used for
it, because the icon backend falls back to the semantic name as the `aria-label`, and a screen
reader would announce "warning".

### Deadline copy partials

`partials/deadline_labels.html` stays the one file that writes deadline copy. It becomes a set of
`partialdef`s that the outline rows, band and both cards compose, each surface owning its own
layout: `tier-icon`, `tier-word`, `tier-date`, `tier-chip` (with `tier-chip-body`),
`closes-line`, `tier-tint`, `next-deadline-eyebrow`, `access-ends-line` and `line` (the line
form the course row keeps). They read `child` and the optional `date_format` (`"short"` or
unset), `title` and `today_word`. The closed branch of `line` renders `title` when it is given.
This fixes B1, and because `partials/course_row.html` (the course row: the all-courses page and
the dashboard below `lg`) reaches the partial through `course_deadline_line.html`, the course row
also picks up the B1 fix and the new chip icons.

### Short date format

`local_time` in `freedom_ls/deadlines/templatetags/deadline_tags.py` takes an optional `format`:

- `"short"`: written as `data-format="short"`. The server text is the day and abbreviated month in
  the site zone (`site_zone_text` gains a short form), plus the year when it differs from the
  current year in the site zone, or "Today" when the instant falls on the current date in the
  site zone. A deadline with a set time (`has_set_time`: its time is not `time.max`, judged in
  its own zone) also carries `data-timed="true"` and the server text adds ", HH:MM" and the zone
  name, as the long form does. The `localTime` component in
  `freedom_ls/base/static/base/js/alpine-components.js` formats it with
  `{day: "numeric", month: "short"}`, adds the year when it differs from the viewer's current
  year, writes "Today" instead when the instant falls on the viewer's own date, and adds the
  time (with the zone only when `data-show-zone` is true) for a timed deadline.
- Any other value raises `ValueError`.

The `datetime` attribute is unchanged in every format. Without `format`, the output is exactly
what it is today.

### Shared deadline selection

Move `CardDeadline`, `CardDeadlineLine` and `_card_deadline_line` from
`freedom_ls/learner_interface/views.py` to `freedom_ls/learner_interface/utils.py`. Rename
`_card_deadline_line` to the public `card_deadline_line`, and add `deadline_tier` and `is_past`
fields to `CardDeadline`. Row deadlines copy the row's `deadline_tier`; `is_past` is set on every
deadline in the missed list, so the next-deadline card can word its eyebrow without comparing
instants in a template. `card_deadline_line` takes the nested course index and flattens it with
`_item_rows`, so the dashboard and the player flatten rows the same way. The time limit end's
tier is computed with `course_deadlines.zone`.

### Dashboard: deadline band

- `freedom_ls/learner_interface/templates/cotton/course-card-shell.html` gains a named slot,
  `band`. It renders inside the accent hero block, full width along its bottom edge, within the
  hero's rounded corners. The organisation byline's raised mark overlaps the band's bottom line,
  never its top line. When the slot is empty, nothing renders. The `aria-hidden` moves from the hero
  block onto the icon, so the band stays in the accessibility tree. The hero block gains
  `relative overflow-hidden`, and the band sits at `absolute inset-x-0 bottom-0`, so the icon
  stays centred.
- A new partial, `learner_interface/partials/course_deadline_band.html`
  (`data-testid="course-deadline-band"`), fills the slot from `course.missed_deadline` or
  `course.next_deadline` (Decision 4). The hero block carries
  `data-testid="course-card-hero"`. The band has two lines. The top line holds the tier
  icon, the tier word and the deadline title across the hero's full width, with the title
  truncated. The bottom line is right-aligned, clear of the organisation mark, and never
  truncated. It holds the tier's right text from the table, then "Closes <short date>" when
  `closes_at` is set and the deadline is not closed.
- Band classes: `bg-surface-2 text-muted` for `UPCOMING`, `bg-warning-light text-on-warning-light`
  for `TODAY`, and `bg-error-light text-on-error-light` for `MISSED` and `CLOSED`.
- As today, a completed course, a course whose content access is withheld and a course whose access
  has ended carry no deadline, so they get no band. Neither does a course with no deadline.
- `partials/course_card.html` stops including `course_deadline_line.html`. The "Access ends
  <date>." line stays in the card body. The status eyebrow is unchanged and stays the body's only
  status chip.

### Course player: next-deadline card

- `_player_course_index` (`freedom_ls/learner_interface/views.py:1350`) returns a
  `PlayerCourseIndex` (`rows`, `course_deadlines`, `deadline_policy`, `now`): the index rows,
  the `CourseDeadlines`, policy and one `now` it resolves. When a caller
  passes `course_index` in (`view_course_item`), it also passes the values that came back with
  it. When the chrome builds the index itself (the form fill and results pages), it uses what
  `_player_course_index` returned. The gate-only callers take the index from the result.
  `_player_chrome_context` builds `next_deadline_card` from `card_deadline_line(...)`: the
  selected `CardDeadline`, or `None`, which is also what it gets when `course_index` is passed as
  `[]`. Nothing re-resolves the deadlines.
- It is rendered in `partials/course_toc_header.html` (`data-testid="next-deadline-card"`)
  directly after the progress bar, inside
  `#course-toc-region`, so the HTMX Previous/Next out-of-band swap refreshes it. It shows the
  eyebrow from the table, the deadline title, the tier's outline-row treatment (a muted `deadline`
  icon for `UPCOMING`, the tier chip otherwise) around the full `local_time`, and "Closes
  <date>." when `closes_at` is set and the deadline is not closed. For `CLOSED` the full
  `local_time` is of `closes_at`, and for every other tier it is of `due_at`.
- It is not rendered when there is no deadline, when the course is complete, when access has ended
  (`access_ended_outline`), or when `hide_outline_panel` is set. The player then never shows a
  deadline the dashboard band would not.

### Course player: outline rows

In `partials/course_minimal_toc.html`, the `due-date` partial moves from its own line under the
title onto the row line (`content-row-inner`), at the right, as the table's outline-row column
describes. The title keeps `truncate` and the date is `shrink-0`. `deadline_indent` and the second
line go away. Rows closed after a sequential lock (deadlines-2 rule 11) still show "Locked" with no
Closed chip. `hide_deadlines=True` (course detail) still shows only the Closed chip, now inline.

The outline panel is one `<dialog>`, docked from `lg` and a bottom sheet below it, so this one
change covers desktop, tablet and the mobile sheet. No new breakpoint or docked tablet layout is
added.

### Course player: header card

- `learner_interface/_course_base.html` overrides `page_title` with a wrapper that puts an inner
  block, `item_page_title`, beside the header card. They sit side by side from `lg`, with the
  card on the right, and stack below `lg`. `course_topic.html` and `course_item_closed.html` move
  their title include from `page_title` into `item_page_title`.
- `header_extra` no longer draws the deadline line.
- The card appears under today's condition (`current_entry.due_at` and status not `COMPLETE`). It
  shows the tier icon, the header word from the table, the full `local_time`, and "Closes
  <date>." when `closes_at` is set and the deadline is not closed. For `CLOSED` the full
  `local_time` is of `closes_at`, and for every other tier it is of `due_at`.
  `data-testid="player-item-deadline"` moves onto the card.

## Testing

- `deadline_tier`: every branch, plus these boundaries: due later today, due exactly `now`, due
  00:00 tomorrow in the site zone, a site zone far from UTC, and past with `mark_overdue` off.
  Completed rows get `None`.
- `course_deadlines_for` records the zone it resolved on every `CourseDeadlines`, and
  `get_course_index` adds no query for it.
- `local_time` with `format="short"` (server text including the year rule, "Today", the timed
  suffix, `data-format`, `data-timed`, `datetime`), and an unknown format refused. Default output
  unchanged.
- `card_deadline_line`: a tier on rows and on the time limit end.
- `deadline_labels.html`: a closed deadline with a title renders the title (B1), on the band and on
  the all-courses row.
- Dashboard: the band for each tier, with its word and icon; missed beating next; the Closes text;
  no band for a completed course, withheld access, ended access or no deadline; the "Access ends"
  line in the body; the band not covered by the byline.
- Player: the next-deadline card for each tier; the time limit end as "Course"; the "Deadline"
  eyebrow on a past deadline with `mark_overdue` off; absence with no deadline, a complete course
  or ended access; presence in the out-of-band `#course-toc-region` of an HTMX Previous/Next
  response.
- Outline rows: the inline date and chip for each tier; nothing on completed and course part
  rows; `hide_deadlines` keeping only the Closed chip; the sequential-lock rule.
- Header card: each tier, on a topic page and on the closed-item page; absence on a completed item;
  the moved `data-testid`.
- Screen readers: no tier icon is announced by its semantic name.
- Query budgets: `test_player_page_query_count_*` (54/51) and the dashboard ceilings
  (`IN_PROGRESS_MAX_QUERIES`, `BYLINE_DASHBOARD_MAX_QUERIES`,
  `test_in_progress_query_count_does_not_grow_with_cards_carrying_deadlines`) stay as they are.
- Playwright (`learner_interface/tests/playwright/test_course_browse_flow.py`): at 375px, a long
  row title truncates and its date stays whole. The band date stays whole on a narrow card, and at 1024px the band's tier word, title and date
  clear the organisation mark. The
  header card stacks under the title below `lg`.

## Success criteria

- A dashboard card with a missed deadline shows a band along the bottom of its hero reading
  "Missed deadline", the item title and the short date, with the error tint and the warning icon.
- A card whose only deadline is the time limit end, due later today in the site zone, shows a
  `TODAY` band titled "Course".
- A card whose deadline is closed names the item in its band.
- The player's next-deadline card names the same deadline as the dashboard band for the same
  learner and course, and still does after Previous/Next.
- Every dated, unfinished outline row shows its date on the row's own line, at the right. At
  375px, a long title truncates before the date does.
- Completed rows and course part rows show no deadline.
- The header card sits to the right of the title at `lg` and below it on mobile, on topic pages
  and the closed-item page.
- No learner-facing string reads "Due" or "Overdue".
- Each tier is identifiable with colour removed (forced colours or greyscale).
- All query-budget tests pass with unchanged ceilings.
