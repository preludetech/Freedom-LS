# Spec: Course restart

## Purpose

A learner who fails the in-person final exam must redo the course and pass every assessment again
(SACAA: "a learner is allowed a maximum of two re-writes thereafter, shall redo the course for
readmission to the exam"). FLS has no way to do this. The only resets are developer tools that
delete or zero progress in place, which destroys records that must be kept for 5 years (CAR
141.01.17), and deregistering then re-registering resumes the learner where they were.

This spec lets staff restart a learner's course from the educator learner page, with a required
reason. The learner starts again from the beginning on a new run. The earlier run is kept,
readable and unchangeable, and the restart is audited. It also lays down the data shape that
every later way of doing a course again (renewal, refresher: `learner-interface-features-2-test-out`
and later specs) builds on without rework.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **run** | coined | One learner's pass through one course: one registration plus that learner's `CourseProgress` under it. For an individual registration the registration is the run; for a cohort registration the run is the cohort registration as it applies to one learner. No existing term: `CourseProgress` alone is the record, not the grant, and "attempt" is taken by `CourseFormAttempt`. |
| **restart registration** | coined | A `LearnerCourseRegistration` whose `start_reason` is `restarted`: minted by a restart, linked to the registration of the run it follows. |
| **superseded** | coined | A `LearnerCourseRegistration` with `superseded_at` set: a later restart registration follows it. Distinct from `is_active`, never reactivated. |
| **closed for the learner** | coined | A cohort registration's run for one learner, after a restart registration of that learner names it in `previous_cohort_registration`. The cohort registration itself is unchanged for everyone else. |
| **earlier run** / **current run** | coined | A run that is superseded or closed for the learner, against the one run `learner_for_course` resolves for the learner and course. |

## Scope

In:

- The run chain on `LearnerCourseRegistration`: start reason, who started it, the link to the
  previous run's registration, and the superseded state.
- A `restart_course` service, its audit action, webhook event and learner notification.
- A Restart action on the educator learner page, and earlier runs shown there read-only.
- Every reader that resolves, counts or writes a learner's run made run-aware: the resolver,
  access, services that look a registration up by learner and course, registration rules, deadline
  config, the cohort move carry, reports, educator pages, the APIs and admin pickers.
- Updating `learner-interface-features-2-test-out/idea.md` open question 7 with the data shape.

Out:

- Automatic restarts, including from a quiz attempt cap (`sacaa-compliance-assessment-controls-2-attempt-limits`
  only locks the quiz).
- Booking, recording or gating the in-person final exam.
- Learners restarting their own course, and any learner-facing view of earlier runs.
- Any way of re-doing a course at a future date: renewals, refreshers, scheduled or recurring runs,
  run expiry, their triggers and notifications. The data shape takes them; later specs build them.
- Undoing a restart.
- A cap on restarts. The audit log counts them; a later cap counts `restarted` runs only.
- Deleting or purging earlier runs (`sacaa-complianc-deletion-and-declaration-1-force-delete` owns
  deletion), certificates, carrying any credit into the new run, and a bulk restart.
- A "your course was restarted" banner on the learner's course page. The notification and a 0%
  course carry the message.

## Decisions

The data shape is option C from `research_course_restart.md`: one registration is one run, and
runs are chained registration to registration. The owner chose it over the idea's recommended
option B (a superseded flag on `CourseProgress`); `answers.md` records each answer below.

1. **The new run is always a new `LearnerCourseRegistration`**, for individual and cohort learners
   alike. A cohort registration is shared by its cohort and can never be duplicated for one
   learner.
2. **The back-link is registration to registration.** A restart registration sets exactly one of
   `previous_learner_registration` or `previous_cohort_registration`. Each registration links only
   to its own `CourseProgress` (the existing grant columns). Nothing in `learner_management` points
   at `learner_progress`, so the app layering in `docs/app_structure.md` holds.
3. **Superseded is its own column, not `is_active`.** Flipping `is_active` would fire
   `registration.deactivated` and make the row reactivatable. A superseded row keeps its
   `is_active` value, stops granting access, and is refused by every reactivation path.
4. **A restart registration is the current run over any cohort registration** for that learner and
   course, including a cohort the learner joins later. Otherwise the cohort-beats-individual order
   in `learner_for_course` would send a restarted cohort learner back to the closed run.
5. **Restart only from an active, current run, in any progress state.** Untouched, in progress or
   complete: the in-person exam failure often follows a complete FLS run.
6. **No undo.** A mistaken restart stands; the earlier run stays readable and the audit log shows
   the mistake.
7. **One webhook, `registration.restarted`.** No `registration.added`, `registration.deactivated`
   or `course.registered` notification, so integrators do not read a restart as a deregistration
   plus a new enrolment.
8. **Deadline and messaging config carry to the new run; the clock does not.** The deadline start is
   the new `CourseProgress.created_at`, so the clock restarts by construction.
9. **Capability: `CHANGE_LEARNER_REGISTRATION`**, with the shared confirm dialog, as the idea
   recommends. No separate capability yet.

## Requirements

### Data model: `freedom_ls/learner_management/models.py`

New fields on `LearnerCourseRegistration`, in one new migration (never edit an existing one):

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `start_reason` | `CharField`, `TextChoices` `RunStartReason`: `registered` (default), `restarted` | Why this run started. Later specs add `renewal` and `refresher`; the field is sized for them. Existing rows get `registered`. |
| `started_by` | FK to `AUTH_USER_MODEL`, `SET_NULL`, null | Who started the run when someone other than the system did. Set by a restart to the actor. Null on every existing row. |
| `restart_reason` | `CharField(max_length=REASON_LENGTH)`, blank | The staff member's reason. Set only when `start_reason` is `restarted`. `REASON_LENGTH` is the audit log's (`freedom_ls/audit_log/models.py`). |
| `previous_learner_registration` | `OneToOneField("self")`, `PROTECT`, null, `related_name="superseded_by"` | The individual registration of the run this one follows. One-to-one: a run is superseded once. |
| `previous_cohort_registration` | FK to `CohortCourseRegistration`, `PROTECT`, null, `related_name="restart_registrations"` | The cohort registration of the run this one follows, closed for this learner. |
| `superseded_at` | `DateTimeField`, null | Set on this row when a restart registration follows it. |

Constraints:

- `unique_learner_course_registration` becomes conditional on `superseded_at IS NULL`, so a learner
  holds any number of superseded registrations for a course and at most one that is not.
- At most one of the two previous links is set; both are null exactly when `start_reason` is
  `registered`. A `restarted` row has a non-blank `restart_reason`.
- Unique `(learner, previous_cohort_registration)` where the link is set: a learner's cohort run is
  closed once.
- `PROTECT` on the links matches `CourseProgress`'s `PROTECT` on both grant columns: an earlier
  run's registration is never deleted while a later run follows it. Force delete of a learner
  removes the whole chain in one collector pass, so it is not blocked; its tests must prove this.

Query helpers in `freedom_ls/learner_management/queries.py`, used by every reader below so the
rule is written once:

- `LEARNER_REGISTRATION_GRANTS_ACCESS` adds `superseded_at__isnull=True`.
- A helper that excludes cohort registrations closed for the learner, for the single and the bulk
  resolver.
- `learner_for_course`: a non-superseded restart registration of the learner for the course wins.
  Otherwise today's order, skipping cohort registrations closed for the learner. The two-cohort
  tiebreak test from `better-course-progress-tracking` keeps passing.
- `latest_registration` ignores superseded rows instead of relying on `registered_at` order.

### Restart service: `freedom_ls/learner_management/learner_actions.py`

`restart_course(learner, course, *, actor, reason) -> LearnerCourseRegistration`, in the shape of
`deactivate_registration`: one `transaction.atomic()`, `select_for_update` on the learner, the state
change, then `record_learner_entry`.

- Resolves the current run with `learner_for_course`. Refuses with a typed error when there is no
  current run, its registration is inactive, the learner is inactive, the reason is blank, or
  the course is no longer open to the learner's organisation (`require_course_open_to`) or is
  retired.
- Individual current run: sets `superseded_at` on it with `update_fields`, so the registrations API
  syncs it on `updated_at`.
- Cohort current run: changes nothing on the cohort registration.
- Creates the restart registration: `start_reason=restarted`, `started_by=actor`,
  `restart_reason=reason`, the matching previous link, `self_registered=False`. The existing
  `post_save` receiver still mints its `CourseProgress` (so `created_at`, the deadline start, is the
  restart moment). It does not fire `registration.added` or raise `course.registered` for a row
  whose `start_reason` is `restarted`. `freedom_ls/learner_progress/signals.py`
  `ensure_course_progress_on_learner_registration` / `_ensure_and_announce` decide this from the
  row, not from a context flag.
- Copies registration-level config onto the restart registration
  (`freedom_ls/deadlines/models.py`, `freedom_ls/messaging_policy/models.py`):
  - From an individual run: its `LearnerCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig` and
    `LearnerCourseRegistrationMessagingConfig`, field by field.
  - From a cohort run: a `LearnerCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig` holding the effective values of
    the `CohortDeadlineConfig` and `CohortCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig` layers (registration
    wins). Each `CohortDeadlineOverride` of the cohort registration becomes a
    `LearnerDeadlineOverride` on the new `CourseProgress`, with `set_by=actor`. The cohort
    registration's messaging config becomes a `LearnerCourseRegistrationMessagingConfig`. The
    restarted learner then resolves the same deadline rule as before, against a new start.
  - `CoursePause`, `DeadlineExtension` and `LearnerDeadlineOverride` rows of the earlier run stay
    with it.
- Records `registration.restarted` through `record_learner_entry(..., reason=reason, details=...)`.
  `details` holds ids and labels only: course id and title, the earlier registration's id and
  kind (`learner` or `cohort`), the cohort id and name for a cohort run, the earlier
  `CourseProgress` id, and the new registration's id. The chain can be rebuilt from the log alone.
- Fires `registration.restarted` on commit through `fire_webhook_event_on_commit`.
- Raises the `course.restarted` notification to the learner, unless the actor is the learner's
  user.

Other services in the same file:

- `_ensure_registration` (`register_for_course`, `self_register`) looks up the learner's
  non-superseded row only, never a superseded one, and never reactivates a superseded row.
- `deactivate_registration` targets the non-superseded row. Deactivating a restart registration
  works as it does for any registration, and reactivating it works too.
- Reactivating a superseded row is refused in the service layer, not just hidden in the UI.
  The entry points are `LearnerRegistrationReactivation`, `register_for_course`, `self_register`,
  `RegisterForCourseBulkAction` and the registration rules engine.

### Audit log, webhook and notification registries

- `freedom_ls/audit_log/actions.py`: `AuditAction("registration.restarted", "Learner course restarted",
  <describer>, group="Course registrations")`. The describer names the course and, for a cohort
  run, the cohort. Add the row to `freedom_ls/audit_log/README.md`.
- `freedom_ls/base/webhook_event_types.py`: `registration.restarted` in `FLS_WEBHOOK_EVENT_TYPES`
  with the same label, and a sample in `WEBHOOK_EVENT_TYPE_SAMPLES`: `learner_webhook_payload`
  fields plus `course_id`, `course_title`, `previous_registration_id`, `previous_registration_kind`,
  `previous_course_progress_id`, `registration_id`, `course_progress_id`, `registered_at`, `reason`,
  `actor_id`. The registration id is a run id, not a stable learner-and-course key; `docs/product/webhooks.md`
  says so.
- `freedom_ls/base/notification_categories.py`: `course.restarted`, label "Course restarted",
  message "Your course %(course_title)s has been restarted. You start again from the beginning.",
  `url_builder` the course home (`course_home_url`), no rollup. The reason is not shown: staff wrote
  it for the audit trail. The checklist from the notifications skill:
  1. Actor: the staff member. A staff member restarting their own learner row is excluded by
     comparing the actor to the learner's user.
  2. Without it: nothing until the learner next opens the course, which then shows 0% with no
     explanation.
  3. They open the course and start again; the link goes there.
  4. At most a few times per course (the regulation allows two re-writes).
  5. In-app is enough until email delivery exists.
- `course.completed` and its GA4 event need no change: `course_finish` fires once per
  `CourseProgress`, so once per run.

### Earlier runs are read-only: `freedom_ls/learner_progress/`

- `course_progress_for` and `course_progress_by_course_for` (`queries.py`) resolve through the
  changed `learner_for_course` order and closure helper, and stay in step. The learner player,
  dashboard and progress bars then show the current run only.
- Every write path refuses a `CourseProgress` that is not the learner's current run: topic
  completion, `get_or_create_incomplete` and answer saves in `attempts.py`, `course_finish`, and the
  deadline actions (pause, resume, extend) in `freedom_ls/deadlines/actions.py`. One helper in
  `learner_progress` answers "is this record an earlier run"; it joins through the record's
  registration. A learner's HTMX post against an earlier run gets a 422 with the message "This
  course was restarted. Open it again to continue." It never writes to the earlier run.
- `recalculate_progress_percentage` and the content-change recalculation leave an earlier run's
  `progress_percentage`, `completed_time` and scores unchanged.
- `plan_progress_carry` (`utils.py`) and the move preview skip records of earlier runs. A cohort
  move of a restarted learner never carries the closed record or touches the restart
  registration's record. The destination fan-out may mint a record under the destination cohort
  registration, which never becomes current while the restart registration is active.

### Registration rules: `freedom_ls/registration_rules/`

- The engine's register, reactivate, retract and hand-over steps (`evaluation.py`) skip superseded
  rows. A `RuleRegistration` on a superseded row stays as history.
- A restart registration is not rule-owned: a staff restart is a staff decision, so a rule never
  retracts it. The receiver in `registration_rules/signals.py` that re-evaluates on registration
  save treats it as foreign.

### Educator learner page: `freedom_ls/educator_interface/`

Follow `claude_plugins/fls-dev/skills/educator-interface/` and `panel-framework/`.

- **Restart action.** `LearnerRestart` in `panels/common/registration_actions.py`: a `ConfirmAction`
  with `ReasonMixin`, `reason_required = True`, `capability = CHANGE_LEARNER_REGISTRATION`, calling
  `restart_course` with `actor=ctx.request.user, reason=ctx.reason`, then
  `registration_changed_response(learner)`. A learner-page subclass mixes in `EducatorReasonMixin`.
  It is a row action on each current run in `LearnerCoursesPanel` (`panels/learner/panels.py`),
  for individual and cohort runs alike: a `secondary`, `size="small"` trigger labelled "Restart
  course" with an `aria_label` naming the course.
- **Dialog.** Title "Restart <learner>'s <course>?". Body, one paragraph each: where the learner is
  now (percent through, completed, or not started); "They start again from the beginning and must
  complete every item and pass every assessment again. Nothing from this run counts."; "This run
  stays readable as an earlier run. Its progress, attempts and answers cannot be changed.";
  "Attempt counts, learning time and the deadline clock start again."; for a cohort run only,
  "Only <learner> restarts. <cohort> and its other learners are not changed."; "<learner> is told
  their course was restarted."; "This cannot be undone." The reason field is labelled "Reason", with
  help text saying it is kept in the audit log. Confirm `confirm_label = "Restart course"`,
  `loading_text = "Restarting…"`, `confirm_variant = "error"`. No typed confirmation. A stale
  dialog (the run was already restarted) answers with the refusal dialog, as
  `LearnerRegistrationReactivation` does.
- **Earlier runs.** Each course in `LearnerCoursesPanel` lists its earlier runs collapsed beneath the
  current run (row detail, hidden when there are none), newest first. Each earlier run shows the
  same fields in the same places: run number, how it started ("Registered", "Staff restart"),
  started and closed dates through `<c-date>`, completion state, and progress. Its quiz attempts
  stay reachable through the existing attempt quick view and review page. An earlier run has no
  actions. The restart reason shows under a "Reason" label in the earlier run's detail.
- **Registrations card** (`LearnerRegistrationsPanel`): superseded individual rows, and a cohort row
  closed for this learner, show an "Earlier run" badge and no actions. Restart registrations show
  "Staff restart" as how they started.
- **Learner list** (`panels/learner/data_tables.py` `learner_course_summaries`): progress per course
  is the current run's, not the maximum across runs.
- **Course page** (`panels/course/data_tables.py`): registration tables and counts exclude
  superseded rows.
- **Cohort pages.** `CohortCourseCompletionPanel` (`panels/cohort/panels.py`) counts each member's
  current run: a member restarted off the cohort registration counts through their restart
  registration's record, and the closed record does not count.
- The confirm dialogs that name learners keeping access (`members_keeping_access`, `other_grants_for`
  in `learner_management/queries.py`) count a restart registration as an individual grant. A
  restarted cohort learner keeps access when their cohort registration is deactivated or they leave
  the cohort, and the dialog names them.

### Reports: `freedom_ls/reports/`

- Cohort report (`gather.py`, `indexes.py`): for each roster learner, pick the one current-run
  record per course before any fold. For a learner restarted off the cohort registration, that is
  their restart registration's record; the closed cohort record is excluded. The learner's row is
  labelled "Staff restart". `load_first_attempt_ids` and the at-risk flags work within the current
  run.
- `gather_learner_course_details` (the educator learner-page Courses card) labels each detail with
  its run (current or earlier, run number, start reason). For a restart registration that follows a
  cohort run, it reads the cohort name through `previous_cohort_registration`.
- Exports label every earlier run's rows as an earlier run. Earlier and current runs are never
  mixed in one figure.

### APIs and admin

- Progress API (`freedom_ls/learner_progress/api.py`): each record adds `is_current_run`,
  `start_reason`, `previous_registration_id` and `previous_registration_kind`, declared in the
  OpenAPI schema.
- Registrations API (`freedom_ls/learner_management/api.py`): individual registrations add
  `start_reason`, `superseded_at`, `previous_learner_registration_id` and
  `previous_cohort_registration_id`. Superseded rows stay listed, for audit consumers.
- Admin: `LearnerRegistrationPickerForm` (`learner_management/admin_forms.py`) and the deadline and
  messaging config admins never offer a superseded registration. `CourseProgressAdmin` shows each
  record's run state and can filter on it.

## Testing

Follow `ds:testing` and `fls-dev:testing`. Prove:

- **Shape.** The conditional unique constraint allows superseded rows and blocks a second
  non-superseded row. The link and reason constraints reject a `restarted` row with no link, both
  links, or a blank reason. A chain of three runs (registered, restart, restart) reads back in
  order.
- **Individual restart.** `superseded_at` is set, `is_active` is untouched, a new row and a new
  `CourseProgress` exist, and the earlier run's `CourseProgress`, `TopicProgress`,
  `CourseFormAttempt`, `FormProgress` and `QuestionAnswer` rows are unchanged, including
  `completed_time`, `progress_percentage` and scores.
- **Cohort restart.** The cohort registration and every other member's record are unchanged, and
  the learner resolves to the restart registration's record. After the learner joins another
  cohort holding the course, the restart registration stays current. A cohort move carries nothing
  from the closed record.
- **Refusals.** Inactive registration, already superseded (stale), inactive learner, blank reason,
  course closed to the organisation or retired. Every reactivation path refuses a superseded row.
- **Resolution.** `learner_for_course`, `course_progress_for` and `course_progress_by_course_for`
  agree for a restarted individual learner, a restarted cohort learner, and the existing two-cohort
  tiebreak.
- **Read-only.** Topic completion, quiz attempts and answers, `course_finish`, and pause, resume and
  extend all refuse an earlier run. The learner's HTMX post gets the 422.
- **Side effects.** Exactly one audit entry with the details above. Exactly one
  `registration.restarted` webhook, and no `registration.added`, `registration.deactivated` or
  `course.registered`. One `course.restarted` notification, and none when the actor is the learner.
  `course.completed` fires again when the new run completes.
- **Config carry.** An individual run's deadline and messaging config are copied. A cohort run's
  effective deadline layers and overrides arrive on the new run, and the resolved deadlines equal
  the old ones shifted to the new start.
- **Registration rules.** The engine never retracts, reactivates or hands over a superseded row, and
  never retracts a restart registration.
- **Readers.** The cohort report, `CohortCourseCompletionPanel`, the learner list and the course
  page count the current run once and never mix runs. The APIs expose the new fields.
- **Force delete** of a learner with a restart chain succeeds.
- **Browser flow** (`fls-dev:playwright-tests`): an educator restarts a learner's course from the
  Courses card with a reason, the earlier run appears collapsed and read-only, and the learner
  opens the course at 0%.
- The existing invariants pass: `audit_log/tests/invariants/test_vocabulary.py` (same code and label
  for the audit action and the webhook, a sample for each event) and the panel-framework
  invariants.

## Open questions

- Q: How do the cohort-layer deadline config and cohort overrides carry when a cohort learner is
  restarted? → A: They are flattened onto the restart registration's config and the new record's
  `LearnerDeadlineOverride` rows. (assumed default: the owner's answer was "only the clock
  restarts", and without this a cohort learner would lose those deadlines silently. Overrule by
  editing "Restart service".)
- Q: Is messaging config copied as well as deadline config? → A: Yes, by the same reasoning.
  (assumed default: losing a peer-messaging setting on restart would be a silent change. Overrule
  by editing "Restart service".)
- Q: Is a restart registration rule-owned when the earlier run was? → A: No. (assumed default: a
  staff restart is a staff decision, and a rule retracting it would undo it. Overrule by editing
  "Registration rules".)
- Q: Can a course that has since closed to the learner's organisation, or been retired, be
  restarted? → A: No, it is refused. (assumed default: matches `register_for_course` and the rules
  engine. Overrule by editing "Restart service".)
- Q: Does a restarted cohort learner keep course access after leaving the cohort? → A: Yes,
  through the restart registration, and the confirm dialogs name them. (assumed default: it
  follows from an individual restart registration. Overrule by editing "Educator learner page".)

## Success criteria

- An educator holding `CHANGE_LEARNER_REGISTRATION` restarts an individual learner and a cohort
  learner from the Courses card with a reason. Each learner then opens the course at 0%.
- After a restart, the earlier run's progress, attempts, answers, completion date and scores are
  unchanged field for field, and every write path refuses them.
- The learner's History tab shows one "Learner course restarted" entry with actor and reason.
  The webhook log shows one `registration.restarted`, and the learner's bell shows one "Course
  restarted" notification.
- The cohort report, cohort completion panel, learner list, progress API and exports show the
  current run, label earlier runs, and never mix them.
- Reactivating a superseded registration fails through every path.
- `uv run pytest` passes, including the vocabulary and panel-framework invariants.
