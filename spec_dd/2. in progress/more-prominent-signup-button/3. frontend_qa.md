# Frontend QA: more prominent signup button

## Setup

```bash
uv run python manage.py qa_create_course_access_types   # free + application-gated course, plus a learner, on DemoDev
uv run python manage.py qa_create_course_visibility     # a coming-soon course for express interest
PORT=$(.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh)
uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT
```

- Base URL: `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT`
- Confirmation emails land in Mailpit at `http://localhost:8025`.
- Admin login for toggling the signup policy: `demodev@email.com` / `demodev@email.com`, at `/admin/accounts/sitesignuppolicy/`. Signups must be allowed on the DemoDev site unless a scenario says otherwise.
- Courses and their URLs:
  - Application-gated: `/courses/qa-application-gated-course-access-types/detail/`, whose "Apply now" goes to `/applications/apply/qa-application-gated-course-access-types/` (called `<apply-url>` below).
  - Free: `/courses/qa-free-course-access-types/detail/`, whose "Enrol for free" goes to `/courses/qa-free-course-access-types/access/` (called `<access-url>` below).
  - Coming soon: `/courses/qa-coming-soon-visibility/detail/`, with the "I'm interested" button.
- Existing learner: `demodev_access_learner@email.com` (password equals the email).
- Use a fresh private window for each scenario so no session carries over.
- Lockout warning: django-axes locks an email after 5 failed logins from one address for an hour. Keep failed attempts per email under 5. If you trip it, run `uv run python manage.py axes_reset`.

## Scenario 1: "Apply now" lands on signup and comes back to the apply page

1. Logged out, open the application-gated course and click **Apply now**.
2. **Expect:** the signup page, heading "Create an account", URL `/accounts/signup/?next=%2Fapplications%2Fapply%2F...`.
3. **Expect:** above the form, "Already have an account?" followed by a button-styled **Log in** link whose href carries the same `next`. The header still shows Login and Sign up.
4. Complete the form with a new email and submit. Expect the "verify your email" page.
5. Open the confirmation email in Mailpit and follow its link.
6. **Expect:** you are logged in and land on the apply page for that course.

## Scenario 2: "Enrol for free" lands on signup and enrols on return

1. Logged out, open the free course and click **Enrol for free**.
2. **Expect:** the signup page with `next` set to `<access-url>`.
3. Sign up with a new email, confirm via Mailpit.
4. **Expect:** you land in the course (the first item, or the course detail showing you as enrolled), not on the home page.

## Scenario 3: "I'm interested" (htmx) lands on signup and records the interest on return

1. Logged out, open the coming-soon course and click **I'm interested**.
2. **Expect:** a full-page navigation to the signup page (not a login form swapped into the button), with `next` pointing at `/courses/qa-coming-soon-visibility/deferred-express-interest/`.
3. Sign up with a new email, confirm via Mailpit.
4. **Expect:** you land on the coming-soon course detail page (`/courses/qa-coming-soon-visibility/detail/`) showing "Interested" with a "Remove interest" control.
5. Reload the page. **Expect** it still says Interested (no duplicate or lost row).

## Scenario 4: the login page switch

1. Logged out, visit `/accounts/login/?next=<apply-url>` directly.
2. **Expect:** heading "Log in"; the tab title says "Log in"; above the form "New here?" with a button-styled **Create an account** link. Its href is `/accounts/signup/?next=...` with the apply URL.
3. **Expect:** the form still has email, password, the "Remember me" checkbox and the "Forgot your password?" link under the password field, and a **Log in** submit button.
4. Click **Create an account**. **Expect** the signup page carrying the same `next`. Click its **Log in** switch. **Expect** you are back on the login page, still carrying `next`.
5. Log in as the existing learner. **Expect** you land on the apply page.

## Scenario 5: failed login shows the callout, and it does not reveal whether the account exists

1. Logged out, visit `/accounts/login/?next=<apply-url>`. Enter `nobody-here@example.com` and any password. Submit.
2. **Expect:** the page re-renders with the generic "The email address and/or password you specified are not correct." message, and directly beside it a callout titled "New here?" with a **Create an account** button. The "New here?" switch above is still there too.
3. Inspect the callout's button href. **Expect** `/accounts/signup/?next=...&email=nobody-here%40example.com` (order of the two parameters does not matter).
4. Now enter the existing learner's email `demodev_access_learner@email.com` with a wrong password (once only). **Expect** exactly the same error text and the same callout. Nothing on the page differs between the two attempts apart from the email in the href.
5. Click **Create an account** from the callout. **Expect** the signup page with the email field prefilled with what you typed and `next` still in the URL. The typed email must not need retyping.
6. Complete the signup from there with a fresh email (replace the prefilled one) and confirm via Mailpit. **Expect** you land on the apply page.

## Scenario 6: awkward input on the login form

1. Submit the login form with both fields empty. **Expect:** field errors, and the "New here?" callout still shows. Its button href has no `email` parameter (or an empty one is absent).
2. Submit with email `not-an-email` and any password. **Expect:** the callout shows; click its button. **Expect** the signup page renders normally with an empty email field (the invalid value is dropped, not rendered).
3. Submit with email `"><script>alert(1)</script>` and any password. **Expect:** no script runs, the page renders normally, the callout button's href is well formed with the value URL-encoded.

## Scenario 7: signups closed for the site

1. In the admin, open the DemoDev **Site signup policy** and untick `allow_signups`. Save.
2. Logged out, open the application-gated course and click **Apply now**. **Expect** the login page (not the signup page and not a "signup closed" page), with `next` set to the apply URL.
3. **Expect:** the login page shows no "New here?" switch and the header shows Login only.
4. Enter an unknown email and a wrong password. **Expect** the generic error with no "New here?" callout.
5. Repeat step 2 for **Enrol for free** on the free course and **I'm interested** on the coming-soon course. **Expect** the login page each time, with the same `next` values as scenarios 2 and 3.
6. Log in as the existing learner from that page. **Expect** you land on the apply page.
7. Tick `allow_signups` again and save.

## Scenario 8: other logged-out pages still go to login, not signup

1. Logged out, visit `/accounts/profile/`. **Expect** a redirect to `/accounts/login/?next=%2Faccounts%2Fprofile%2F`, not to signup.
2. Logged out, visit `/applications/status/<any-uuid>/`. **Expect** the login page, not signup.

## Scenario 9: an existing account signs up again through the callout

1. Logged out, fail a login with the existing learner's email (once), then click **Create an account** in the callout.
2. Submit the prefilled signup form with a password and names.
3. **Expect** allauth's normal enumeration-safe outcome: the "verify your email" page, an "account already exists" email in Mailpit, no error saying the account exists, and `next` still in the URL.

## Scenario 10: already logged in

1. Log in as the existing learner. Open the application-gated course and click **Apply now**. **Expect** the apply page directly, no auth page.
2. While logged in, visit `/accounts/signup/?next=<apply-url>`. **Expect** allauth redirects you away from the signup page (to the apply URL or the home page), no signup form.

## Scenario 11: accessibility and small screens

1. Logged out on `/accounts/login/?next=<apply-url>`, use only the keyboard: Tab from the top. **Expect** the **Create an account** switch receives focus before the form fields and Enter follows it.
2. Fail a login, then with a screen reader (or the browser's accessibility tree) check that the error region is exposed with role "alert" and contains both the error message and the "New here?" callout text.
3. At a phone-width viewport, repeat scenario 4 steps 2 to 4 and scenario 5 steps 1 to 3. **Expect** the switch and the callout button wrap onto their own line rather than overflowing, and remain tappable.
