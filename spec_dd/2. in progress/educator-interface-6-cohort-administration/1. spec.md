# Spec: cohort administration

Spec 6 of 12 in the educator interface rebuild. The source is `idea.md` in this directory. It
depends on specs 2, 3 and 5, which are done. Read the "Educator interface rebuild" section of
`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md` first.

Layout follows `spec_dd/1. next/educator-interface-full-polish/Educator LMS Interface Design/`:
screens 05 and 07 of `Educator Cohorts and Admin.dc.html`, and M08 and M10 of
`Educator Mobile Cohorts and Admin.dc.html`, cut to this scope and drawn with FLS components.
`spec_dd/1. next/educator-interface-full-polish/comparable-systems-learner-management.md` sections
4, 6 and 7 back the deactivate and unregister semantics.

## Purpose

An educator can create, rename and delete a cohort today, and nothing else. Course registration is
admin-only. The only way to retire a cohort is to delete it, and deletion fails as soon as any
member has a course progress record. This spec finishes the cohorts section: list, detail,
create, edit, deactivate, reactivate, delete when empty, and registering and unregistering a cohort
for courses. It also rebuilds the courses section as a read-only, organisation-scoped view. That
closes the last disclosure gap `CourseConfig.check_access_exempt_reason` declares.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inactive cohort | coined | A `Cohort` with the new `is_active=False`. The status word is "inactive", settled in `spec_dd/3. done/2026-09-29_14:19_educator-interface-4-panel-framework-components/research_status_vocabulary.md`. |
| access-granting cohort registration | coined | A `CohortCourseRegistration` with `is_active=True` whose `cohort.is_active` is also true. Only these give members access. |
| empty cohort | coined | A cohort with no `CohortMembership` rows and no `CohortCourseRegistration` rows, active or not, whatever the learners' `Learner.is_active`. |

## Scope

In:

- `Cohort.is_active` and its migration.
- Access, progress resolution and the learner quick view, all made to ignore inactive cohorts.
- The cohort list, with search, filters and sort.
- The create and edit modal.
- The cohort detail page: header, then overview, learners, courses and settings tabs.
- Deactivate, reactivate, and delete when empty.
- Registering a cohort for a course and unregistering it, from the courses tab.
- A read-only educators block on the overview.
- The courses section, rebuilt and organisation-scoped.
- A webhook seam for cohort registration changes.
- Docs and upgrade notes.

Out:

- Membership actions and the learners tab content (spec 7). The learners tab stays as it is.
- Row selection and bulk actions (spec 8). The selection column appears once spec 8 declares the
  cohort table's first `BulkAction`.
- Editing the educators block (spec 9). Needs attention data (spec 10). Audit log (spec 11).
- Deadlines, including how `deadline_utils.py` treats an inactive cohort's deadlines.
- Case-insensitive name uniqueness.

## Decisions

1. **An inactive cohort grants no course access.** The registration rows are not changed, so
   reactivating restores exactly what was there. Access goes through any other route the member
   has: an individual registration, or an access-granting registration in another cohort.
2. **Fan-out ignores `Cohort.is_active`.** Progress records are harmless while access is gated at
   read time. It also means reactivating a cohort needs no catch-up.
3. **An inactive cohort is read-only.** Its page stays reachable for anyone who could see it, because
   that is how it gets reactivated. The only actions it offers are Reactivate, and Delete when it is
   empty. Edit, Register and Unregister are hidden. A stale POST to any of those answers 422 with a
   fragment that says the cohort is inactive.
4. **Capabilities follow the spec 5 matrix, with no new permissions.** These are checked against the
   cohort:

   | Action | Capability | Who holds it |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Edit, Deactivate, Reactivate | `change_cohort` | Organisation admins and site admins. |
   | Delete | `delete_cohort` | Organisation admins and site admins. |
   | Register | `add_cohortcourseregistration` | Also cohort admins, on their own cohort. |
   | Unregister | `change_cohortcourseregistration` | Also cohort admins, on their own cohort. |

   Register covers re-registering, because that reactivates an existing row.
5. **Delete is offered only for an empty cohort, and enforced on the server.** `DeleteAction` alone
   would cascade memberships and registrations away. Only a progress record protects them.
6. **Unregister is reversible.** It sets `CohortCourseRegistration.is_active=False`. Registering again
   sets the same row back to true with `save()`, never `.update()`, so the `post_save` fan-out runs.
   The unique constraint `unique_cohort_course_registration` allows one row per cohort and course.
7. **The register picker offers published and hidden courses, never coming-soon ones.** It excludes
   courses the cohort already holds an active registration for. A course with an inactive
   registration is listed, and picking it reactivates that row.
8. **The courses section lists published courses, plus any course registered to a cohort or learner
   the educator can see in this organisation.** A hidden or coming-soon course appears only through
   such a registration. The registrations counted are the cohort's or learner's, active or not.
9. **"Save and add another" comes back on create.** Drop the `submit_buttons` override on
   `CreateCohortAction` so the framework default applies. "Save" still opens the new cohort.
10. **The educators block is shown only to holders of `view_organisationmember`.** This is the rule the
    denied experience already uses before it names staff.
11. **No cohort webhook events fire yet.** Spec 7 names them. Until then, every register and unregister
    calls one no-op seam.

## Requirements

### Model and migration (`freedom_ls/learner_management/`)

- `Cohort.is_active = models.BooleanField(_("active"), default=True)` in `models.py`. The migration is
  `0005_cohort_is_active.py`, and every existing cohort becomes active.
- `CohortAdmin` in `admin.py` adds `is_active` to `list_display`, `list_filter` and its form.
- `CohortFactory` keeps `is_active=True` as its default.

### Access and progress resolution

Add one helper and use it at every read site, so the rule cannot drift:

```python
# learner_management/queries.py
def access_granting_cohort_registrations() -> QuerySet[CohortCourseRegistration]:
    return CohortCourseRegistration.objects.filter(is_active=True, cohort__is_active=True)
```

Use a `Q` mirror where a site filters through a relation instead. Where a site already has a
single `filter()` over `cohort__cohortmembership__…`, the `cohort__is_active=True` condition goes in
that same call.

| Read site | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| `learner_management/utils.py` `is_registered_for_course` | Cohort branch. |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `is_registered_for_course_expression` | Cohort `Exists`. |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `learner_for_course` | Skips inactive cohorts, so it falls through to the individual registration. |
| `learner_progress/queries.py` `course_progress_by_course_for` | Changes in step with `learner_for_course`. |
| `learner_progress/queries.py` `registrations_with_progress_for_learner` | Same rule. |

Leave these alone: the fan-out in `learner_progress/signals.py` and `learner_progress/utils.py`,
`reports/indexes.py` `load_registrations` (a report can still be generated for an inactive cohort),
and `deadline_utils.py`.

### Registration functions (`freedom_ls/learner_management/utils.py`)

- `register_cohort_for_course(cohort: Cohort, course: Course) -> CohortCourseRegistration`: get the
  row or create it, set `is_active=True`, then `save()`. Re-registering keeps the row's pk.
- `unregister_cohort_from_course(registration: CohortCourseRegistration) -> None`: set
  `is_active=False`, then `save(update_fields=["is_active"])`.
- Both call `announce_cohort_registration_change(registration)`. It is a no-op for now. Its docstring
  says spec 7 decides which webhook events it fires.

### Query helpers (`freedom_ls/learner_management/queries.py`)

These live here because putting them in `educator_interface` would add a runtime edge to
`role_based_permissions`. Putting them in `learner_management` adds no edge to
`docs/app_structure.md`.

- `courses_visible_to(user, organisation) -> QuerySet[Course]`: Decision 8. It scopes through
  `cohorts_visible_to` and `learners_visible_to`, and follows `_resolved_or_none` for anonymous,
  inactive and superuser users.
- `registerable_courses_for(cohort) -> QuerySet[Course]`: Decision 7.
- `cohort_educators(cohort) -> list[tuple[User, str]]`: active users holding an active
  `cohort_admin` or `cohort_viewer` `ObjectRoleAssignment` on the cohort, gated on an active
  `OrganisationMember` of `cohort.organisation`. Copy the pattern from `active_organisation_admins`.
  The `str` is the role name. Display it through `BASE_ROLES[role].display_name`.
- `members_keeping_access(registration) -> QuerySet[Learner]`: active members of
  `registration.cohort` who keep access to `registration.course` after an unregister. A member
  qualifies through an active `LearnerCourseRegistration`, or through membership of another cohort
  that holds an access-granting registration for the course. It must be one query, not one per
  member.

### Cohort list (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/views.py`, `CohortDataTable`)

- Columns:
  - Name: a link with the quick view, sortable.
  - Status: a new cell template using `<c-panel-status-badge>`. It shows "Active" or "Inactive".
  - Learners: `Count("cohortmembership", filter=Q(cohortmembership__learner__is_active=True), distinct=True)`, sortable.
  - Courses: active registrations only, via a `Prefetch` of `course_registrations`.
  - Created: `created_at`, sortable.
- Default order is name, then pk as the tiebreaker.
- `search_fields = ["name"]`.
- Filters:
  - `BooleanFilter("show_inactive", "Show inactive")`. In the cohorts section the queryset excludes
    inactive cohorts unless the filter is set. `LearnerCohortsPanel` also uses `CohortDataTable`. It
    keeps listing every cohort the learner belongs to, with the status badge.
  - `RelatedChoiceFilter("course", "Course", ...)`, backed by `courses_visible_to`. It narrows to
    cohorts with an active registration for the chosen course. An out-of-scope value is dropped.
- A query-count test shows the list costs the same for 1 cohort as for 10.

### Create and edit

- Remove the `submit_buttons` override on `CreateCohortAction` (Decision 9). Also remove the comment
  in `views.py` about the cohort opening straight away, and the test
  `test_the_create_form_footer_offers_cancel_then_create_cohort_only`.
- `CohortForm` keeps name as its only field and keeps today's clash message. Add a test for renaming
  onto a sibling cohort's name through `EditAction`, and one for creating a name an inactive cohort
  already holds. Both answer 422 with the field error.
- `EditAction` is hidden on an inactive cohort (Decision 3).

### Cohort detail

- **Header** (`panel-page-header`): name, a status badge, an inline stat row (learners, courses) and
  actions. The actions are Edit when the cohort is active, plus the state-appropriate settings
  action.
- **`CohortTabSet`** becomes `{"overview", "learners", "courses", "settings"}`, with overview as the
  default. `details` and `CohortDetailsStack` go. `learners` keeps `CohortLearnersPanel`, its
  `table_key = "learners"` and its tab count, unchanged. Every new panel sets
  `refresh_events = (COHORT_CHANGED,)`. The courses tab panel also refreshes on
  `REGISTRATION_CHANGED`.
- **Overview tab.**
  - A details card: name, status, created, learner count, course count.
  - Course completion per access-granting registration: course title, then completed records over
    members with a record (`CourseProgress.cohort_registration`, `completed_time` not null), then a
    `panel-progress-bar`.
  - A needs-attention card holding a `panel-empty-state` placeholder, to be replaced by spec 10.
  - The educators block, from `cohort_educators` (Decision 10). It is read-only and lives in its own
    panel class so spec 9 can extend it.
- **Courses tab.**
  - A `DataTablePanel` over the cohort's registrations with columns course (link), status
    (registration `is_active` as a badge) and registered.
  - A unique `table_key`, `cohort_courses`, so check E006 passes.
  - The Register action. It is a `FormPanelAction` whose single course choice comes from
    `registerable_courses_for`. A course outside that set answers 422, and no row changes.
  - The Unregister action on each active row.
  - Register and Unregister are hidden when the cohort is inactive or the user lacks the capability.
- **Unregister confirmation.** It is a new `PanelAction` with a confirmation fragment modelled on
  `panel_framework/modal/delete_confirmation.html`. The registration is identified in the POST, and
  the action resolves it only within `cohort.course_registrations`. The fragment says:
  - how many members lose access;
  - who keeps access, from `members_keeping_access`, naming the first 10 and then "and N more";
  - that everyone keeps their progress;
  - that the cohort can be registered again.

  The confirm button is not styled as danger.
- **Settings tab.** Its capability is `change_cohort`, so the tab is absent for anyone without it.
  - Active cohort: Deactivate.
  - Inactive cohort: Reactivate.
  - Empty cohort: Delete.
  - Non-empty cohort: a sentence giving the member and registration counts and saying why delete is
    unavailable.
- **Deactivate confirmation.** It says:
  - members stay in the cohort and keep their progress;
  - while the cohort is inactive, its registrations stop giving access to its N courses;
  - members with another registration for a course keep access to it;
  - it can be reactivated;
  - why delete is unavailable, when the cohort is not empty.

  On success it sets `is_active=False`, sends `COHORT_CHANGED` and stays on the page.
- **Reactivate confirmation.** It says the registrations give access to N courses again. On success
  it sets `is_active=True` and sends `COHORT_CHANGED`.
- **Delete.** A `DeleteAction` subclass. `has_permission` is false unless the cohort is empty.
  `handle_submit` re-checks emptiness inside `transaction.atomic()`, with `select_for_update()` on
  the cohort. A cohort that is no longer empty answers 422 with the fragment, and nothing is deleted.
- Copy follows `.claude/skills/brand-guidelines/voice.md`. Titles are a verb plus the cohort name, and
  each button repeats its verb.
- **Quick view.** `quick_views/cohort.html` reads the status from `Cohort.is_active`. Remove the TODO
  there once that is done.

### Courses section (`CourseConfig`, `CourseDataTable`, `CourseInstanceView`)

- `CourseDataTable.get_queryset` starts from `courses_visible_to(request.user, request.organisation)`.
  Its counts and cohort cell stay scoped as they are today, and "Active Cohorts" also requires
  `cohort__is_active=True`.
- `CourseConfig.authorise_instance` raises `OrganisationScopeDenied` unless the course is in
  `courses_visible_to`.
- Delete `check_access_exempt_reason` and the `@claude` comment above it. The gap they declare is
  now closed.
- Course detail shows:
  - the course details;
  - its cohort registrations in this organisation, each linking to its cohort, with a status for
    both the cohort and the registration;
  - its individual registrations in this organisation.
- No actions anywhere in the section.
- `educator_interface/tests/interface_walk.py` `_seed_instance` seeds a course that is visible in the
  walked organisation. Its docstring stops citing the exemption.

### Webhooks

- `announce_cohort_registration_change` is the seam (see Registration functions). Nothing is added to
  `FLS_WEBHOOK_EVENT_TYPES`.

## Testing

What has to be proved:

- **Access.**
  - A member of an inactive cohort loses access through that cohort.
  - The member keeps access through an individual registration, and through an access-granting
    registration in another cohort.
  - Reactivating restores access.
  - `is_registered_for_course` and `is_registered_for_course_expression` agree on every case.
- **Resolution.** `learner_for_course` and `course_progress_by_course_for` skip an inactive cohort.
  Each of them then falls through to the individual registration.
- **Fan-out.**
  - Re-registering keeps one row with the same pk.
  - It mints records for members who joined while the registration was inactive.
  - It leaves existing records and their progress untouched. Use
    `django_capture_on_commit_callbacks(execute=True)`.
- **Delete.** These cohorts are refused: one with only a removed learner's membership, and one with
  only an inactive registration. A stale delete POST against a cohort that has since gained a member
  answers 422 and deletes nothing. An empty cohort deletes.
- **Inactive is read-only.** Edit, Register and Unregister are absent on an inactive cohort. A POST to
  each answers 422 and changes nothing.
- **Permissions.** These cover every new action and tab in `test_permission_matrix.py` and through
  `interface_walk`:
  - a cohort admin can register and unregister on their own cohort;
  - a cohort admin cannot see the settings tab;
  - a cohort viewer sees no write control;
  - a pasted action URL from a role without the capability answers 403.
- **Organisation isolation**, in `test_organisation_isolation.py`:
  - another organisation's cohort or registration pk answers 404 on every new tab and action;
  - a register POST naming a coming-soon course, or another site's course, answers 422 with no row
    created;
  - the course list and detail never show another organisation's cohort names, learner names or
    counts;
  - a course that is hidden, or registered only in another organisation, is absent from the list and
    its detail answers 404;
  - an out-of-scope `?cohorts-course=` value is ignored.
- **Config authorisation.** `CourseConfig` has no `check_access_exempt_reason`. A behavioural test
  shows its `authorise_instance` denies, because the existing introspection test cannot tell a stub
  from a real check.
- **Webhook absence.** Registering and unregistering a cohort create no `WebhookEvent`.
- **List.** Search, the show-inactive filter and its default, the course filter, sorting on the three
  columns, and the learner count excluding removed learners.
- **Unregister confirmation.** It names members kept by an individual registration and members kept
  by another cohort, and counts the rest.
- **Educators block.** It lists cohort admins and viewers with their roles. It excludes a user whose
  `OrganisationMember` is inactive. It is absent for a cohort admin.
- **Playwright.**
  - Create with "save and add another".
  - Deactivate then reactivate from the settings tab.
  - Register then unregister from the courses tab.
  - Delete an empty cohort.
  - The detail page at 375px.

## Open questions

These are defaults taken. Overrule any of them before planning.

- **The Interest column stays on the courses list.** It is a site-wide count of users who expressed
  interest, not organisation data. Dropping it would also close the last cross-organisation count in
  the section.
- **A cohort deadline in an inactive cohort still applies** to a member who has access another way,
  because deadlines are out of scope.
- **The learner detail page's Cohorts panel lists inactive cohorts with a badge**, rather than hiding
  them. The learner interface never lists cohorts, so "hide from learners" needs no change there.
- **The needs-attention card shows a placeholder**, not nothing, until spec 10 lands.

## Success criteria

- `uv run manage.py makemigrations --check` reports no changes, and `0005` adds `Cohort.is_active`
  with default true.
- `is_registered_for_course` returns false for a member of an inactive cohort with no other
  registration, and true for the same member once they hold an individual registration.
- `grep -n check_access_exempt_reason freedom_ls/educator_interface/views.py` finds nothing.
- The cohort list hides inactive cohorts until "Show inactive" is set.
- Delete is shown only for an empty cohort, and a forced POST on a non-empty one answers 422.
- Unregister then re-register leaves one `CohortCourseRegistration` row with the same pk.
- The register picker never lists a coming-soon course.
- A cohort admin can register and unregister on their own cohort, and sees no settings tab.
- The create modal offers "Save and add another".
- The full test suite and pre-commit pass.
