# Spec: deadline consequence policy

Spec 2 of the Deadlines effort (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`, "Deadlines"). Built on
`deadlines-1-dates-and-overdue`, which is merged. Source: `idea.md` in this directory.

## Purpose

Spec 1 gives content items due dates and courses a time limit, and shows a missed deadline, but
nothing happens when a date passes. This spec adds a deadline policy that decides what does happen:
whether the missed mark shows, whether an item closes, whether course access ends, and whether
educators are to be notified. The policy is set per site, organisation, course, cohort or
registration in the Django admin and is enforced when a page is read. Staff restore ended access
by giving the learner a new time limit end.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deadline policy | coined | The resolved set of consequence settings that applies to one `CourseProgress`. No existing term: "policy" alone is taken by `MessagingPolicy` and `SiteSignupPolicy`. |
| policy layer | coined | One of registration, cohort, organisation, course, site, settings. Each holds a sparse row of the settings; a missing row or an `inherit` value passes to the next layer. |
| grace window | coined | `DeadlinePolicyFields.grace_window`: the time after a due instant (or time limit end) before closing or ending takes effect. Never delays the missed mark. |
| close instant | coined | An item's due instant plus the grace window, when the policy closes items. |
| access end instant | coined | The time limit end plus the grace window, when the policy ends course access. |
| closed | coined | An incomplete collection item whose close instant has passed. |
| access ended | coined | A registered learner whose access end instant has passed. The registration stays active. |

## Scope

In:

- Six policy models in `freedom_ls/deadlines`, their admins, and a settings-layer default.
- A swappable `DeadlinePolicy` class behind the `DEADLINE_POLICY` setting, with a loader and
  system checks.
- Closing items in the course player and the course index; ending access through a wrapper around
  the course access backend.
- The `mark_overdue` switch over spec 1's missed mark.
- Learner-facing wording for closes, closed, access ends and access ended.
- A learner deadline override on the time limit may hold a fixed end date, so staff restore access
  by setting one.
- A read-only effective policy on `CourseProgressAdmin`.

Out:

- Due dates, the time limit, deadline overrides and the missed display beyond the switch (spec 1).
- Extensions, pause and pause limits (spec 3); learner self-pause (spec 4).
- Raising any notification, reminders and the `deadline.missed` webhook (spec 5). `notify_educators`
  is stored and resolved only.
- Educator-interface screens for policy or restore (spec 6); Needs attention and reports (spec 7).
- Locking a value from a higher layer so lower layers cannot change it.
- Score penalties, "no credit after the deadline", retakes, payments.

## Decisions

1. **Layer order, most specific first: registration, cohort, organisation, course, site,
   settings.** The organisation that uses a course has the final say for its own learners; a course
   row is that course's default across organisations.
2. **Every layer can hold every setting.** One abstract field set keeps resolution uniform, and
   specs 3 and 4 add their settings to the same set.
3. **Switches are three-state** (`inherit`, `on`, `off`), so a lower layer can turn off what a
   higher one turned on. `grace_window` and `after_access_ended` use null and `inherit` as their
   unset markers; a zero grace window is a legal set value.
4. **The registration layer keys on the registration `learner_for_course` resolves**, which is the
   one bound to the `CourseProgress`. A second registration's row is not read while the first
   governs.
5. **The policy resolves from a `CourseProgress`.** It names the course, the registration, the
   cohort (cohort path), the organisation (`cohort.organisation` or `learner.organisation`) and the
   site.
6. **The grace window is a free duration** (`DurationField`), added to the resolved due instant.
   Any deadline override that moves the date moves the close instant with it. One grace window
   serves both items and the time limit.
7. **Boundaries match spec 1's `is_overdue`**: a consequence applies when `now > instant`. With a
   zero grace window the missed mark and closing start at the same instant.
8. **The missed mark is shown at the due instant, not after the grace window.** `mark_overdue` off
   hides the mark and keeps the date.
9. **Closing applies to item due dates; ending access applies to the time limit.** A completed
   item never closes.
10. **A closed form with an open attempt can be submitted, not continued.** `form_start` and
    `form_fill_page` refuse it; `form_submit_and_exit` finalises the open attempt for every form,
    including save-on-exit forms, because a closed item has no later to resume in.
11. **Closing does not unlock later items.** Under sequential unlock a closed, incomplete item keeps
    the items after it blocked. A learner deadline override (spec 1) or a spec 3 extension is the
    remedy.
12. **Access ended is enforced by `DeadlineEnforcingBackend`, a wrapper the course access loader
    applies inside `VisibilityEnforcingBackend`.** Every caller of `get_access` sees it, any
    downstream backend is covered, and `is_registered_for_course` is unchanged, so hidden-course
    visibility, messaging peers and educator confirmations do not follow a deadline. This adds the
    dependency edge `course_access -> deadlines`.
13. **What an ended learner can open is the `after_access_ended` setting**: `outline_and_results`
    (default) lets them see the course outline, open completed items and view results;
    `nothing` refuses every player route and links no item. `form_submit_and_exit` stays open under
    both, so no attempt is stranded.
14. **Restore is a `LearnerDeadlineOverride` on the time limit.** Staff set either a fixed end date
    or a number of days after registration, exactly one, with a reason. Progress, completion times
    and the registration are untouched. Closed items stay closed unless staff also override their
    dates.
15. **Ending access never writes.** No `is_active=False`, no `course.unregistered`, no stored status.

## Requirements

### Policy models (`freedom_ls/deadlines/models.py`)

`PolicySwitch(models.TextChoices)`: `INHERIT = "inherit"`, `ON = "on"`, `OFF = "off"`.

`AfterAccessEnded(models.TextChoices)`: `INHERIT = "inherit"`,
`OUTLINE_AND_RESULTS = "outline_and_results"`, `NOTHING = "nothing"`.

Abstract `DeadlinePolicyFields(models.Model)`:

| Field | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mark_overdue` | `CharField(choices=PolicySwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `close_items` | `CharField(choices=PolicySwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `end_course_access` | `CharField(choices=PolicySwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `notify_educators` | `CharField(choices=PolicySwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `grace_window` | `DurationField(null=True, blank=True)` | null (inherit) |
| `after_access_ended` | `CharField(choices=AfterAccessEnded)` | `inherit` |

One `CheckConstraint` per choice field (value in its choices), as `MessagingFlags`, and one for
`grace_window >= 0`. Each field's `help_text` says what "inherit" passes to.

Concrete models, each `SiteAwareModel, TimestampedModel, DeadlinePolicyFields`:

| Model | Owner field | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SiteDeadlinePolicy` | none | `UniqueConstraint(fields=["site"])`, as `SiteTimeZoneConfig` |
| `OrganisationDeadlinePolicy` | `organisation` `OneToOneField(Organisation, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `CourseDeadlinePolicy` | `course` `OneToOneField(Course, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `CohortDeadlinePolicy` | `cohort` `OneToOneField(Cohort, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `CohortCourseRegistrationDeadlinePolicy` | `cohort_registration` `OneToOneField(CohortCourseRegistration, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `LearnerCourseRegistrationDeadlinePolicy` | `learner_registration` `OneToOneField(LearnerCourseRegistration, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |

Every owner field uses `related_name="deadline_policy"`. `freedom_ls/deadlines` gains the
dependency `deadlines -> organisations`.

### Time-limit override (`freedom_ls/deadlines/models.py`, `resolver.py`)

- `_validate_deadline_override` rejects `kind=fixed` on the time limit only for
  `CohortDeadlineOverride`. A `LearnerDeadlineOverride` with `collection_item=None` accepts `fixed`
  (a `due_date`, no `due_time`) or `relative` (days), never both; the existing kind rules already
  require exactly one. The end of that day in the site time zone is the time limit end.
- `resolve_deadline` (or the time-limit path of `course_deadlines` / `course_deadlines_for`)
  resolves a fixed learner time-limit override to that instant.
- `DeadlineOverrideFormSet` and the inline help text on `CourseProgressAdmin` say a fixed date or a
  day count restores ended access.

### Settings layer and loader (`freedom_ls/deadlines/config.py`, `loader.py`, `checks.py`)

New `config.py` (`DeadlinesConfig(AppSettings)`, sharing the `AppConfig`'s name as
`messaging_policy` does; skill `fls-dev:app-settings`):

- `DEADLINE_POLICY`: dotted path, default `"freedom_ls.deadlines.policy.LayeredDeadlinePolicy"`.
- `DEADLINE_POLICY_DEFAULTS`: the settings layer, a dict naming every field with a set value.
  Default:

  ```python
  {
      "mark_overdue": True,
      "close_items": False,
      "end_course_access": False,
      "notify_educators": False,
      "grace_window": timedelta(0),
      "after_access_ended": "outline_and_results",
  }
  ```

`loader.py::get_deadline_policy() -> DeadlinePolicy`, `@functools.cache`, `import_string` on
`DEADLINE_POLICY`. An autouse fixture in `freedom_ls/conftest.py` clears it, beside the existing
three.

`checks.py`, imported from `apps.py` `ready()` beside `signals`:

- `freedom_ls_deadlines.E001`: `DEADLINE_POLICY_DEFAULTS` names exactly the six fields, each with a
  set value of the right type (no `inherit`, no null).
- `freedom_ls_deadlines.E002`: `DEADLINE_POLICY` imports and subclasses `DeadlinePolicy`; a
  `freedom_ls.`-prefixed path must be in an installed app. Copies `comms/checks.py` E003/E004.

### Policy contract and resolver (`freedom_ls/deadlines/policy.py`)

```python
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class ResolvedDeadlinePolicy:
    mark_overdue: bool
    close_items: bool
    end_course_access: bool
    notify_educators: bool
    grace_window: timedelta
    after_access_ended: str          # AfterAccessEnded, never INHERIT
    sources: Mapping[str, str]       # field name -> layer that set it

class DeadlinePolicy:                # plain base, as CourseAccessBackend
    def resolve(self, course_progress: CourseProgress) -> ResolvedDeadlinePolicy: ...
    def resolve_for(self, records: Sequence[CourseProgress]) -> dict[int, ResolvedDeadlinePolicy]: ...
```

`LayeredDeadlinePolicy(DeadlinePolicy)`:

- `LAYER_ORDER = ("registration", "cohort", "organisation", "course", "site", "settings")`, the
  only place the order is written.
- Per field, the first layer whose value is set wins; `sources` records it.
- `resolve_for` reads each layer table once for all records (constant query count in the number
  of records), the way `course_deadlines_for` does. `resolve(x)` is `resolve_for([x])[x.id]`.
- Rows are read with the site of the records, not the thread-local site, so the resolver works
  outside a request (`_base_manager` with explicit `site_id`, as `deadlines/signals.py`).

### Consequences (`freedom_ls/deadlines/consequences.py`)

Pure functions over spec 1's `ResolvedDeadline`, a `ResolvedDeadlinePolicy` and an injected `now`;
no queries:

- `shows_missed(resolved, policy, completed, now) -> bool`: `policy.mark_overdue and
  is_overdue(resolved, completed, now)`. `is_overdue` itself stays policy-free.
- `close_instant(resolved, policy) -> datetime | None`: `due_at + grace_window` when `close_items`
  and the item has a due instant, else None.
- `is_closed(resolved, policy, completed, now) -> bool`: not completed and `now > close_instant`.
- `access_end_instant(time_limit, policy) -> datetime | None`: time limit end plus `grace_window`
  when `end_course_access`, else None.
- `has_access_ended(time_limit, policy, now) -> bool`.

A course already complete (`CourseProgress.completed_time` set) never has its access ended.

### Access wrapper (`freedom_ls/course_access`)

- `CourseAccessDecision` gains `access_ended_at: datetime | None = None` and
  `after_access_ended: str | None = None`. Existing callers are unaffected.
- `DeadlineEnforcingBackend(CourseAccessBackend)` in `course_access/backends.py`. For a user with a
  `CourseProgress` for the course (`course_progress_for`) whose access has ended, it returns
  `can_access_content=False`, `can_self_register=False`, no CTA, and the two new fields set.
  Otherwise it returns the inner decision. It runs a fixed number of queries per call, whatever the
  course's size.
- `course_access/loader.py::get_course_access_backend` returns
  `VisibilityEnforcingBackend(DeadlineEnforcingBackend(inner))`.

### Learner interface (`freedom_ls/learner_interface`)

Course index and player:

- `get_course_index` (`utils.py`) takes the resolved policy beside `course_deadlines` and adds to
  each child dict `closes_at` and `shows_missed`. A closed, incomplete child gets the new status
  `CLOSED`, distinct from `BLOCKED`, and keeps its URL. `_deadline_keys` sets `is_overdue` from
  `shows_missed`. `derive_part_status` treats `CLOSED` as incomplete.
- `_blocked_item_redirect` refuses `CLOSED` as well as `BLOCKED`. `form_start` and `form_fill_page`
  pass the course deadlines and policy so their index sees closing; today they build one without
  deadlines.
- `view_course_item` on a `CLOSED` item renders a closed notice in the player instead of the item:
  "Closed on <date>. Ask your educator if you need more time." When the item is a form with an open
  attempt (`get_latest_incomplete`), the notice adds a "Submit your answers" button posting to
  `form_submit_and_exit`.
- `form_submit_and_exit` finalises the open attempt of a closed item even when
  `form.submit_on_exit` is false.
- `course_form_complete` and `form_submit_and_exit` are not refused by `_course_access_redirect`
  for an ended learner. `course_form_complete` follows `after_access_ended`.
- `_course_access_redirect` lets an ended learner through to `view_course_item` for a completed
  item when `after_access_ended` is `outline_and_results`; everything else redirects to
  `course_detail`.
- `course_finish` refuses an ended learner.

Dates and labels (one shared partial for the TOC row, the item header and the card line, fed by the
index keys):

| State | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Due, closing on | "Deadline <date>. Closes <close date>." |
| Missed, still open, `mark_overdue` on | "Missed deadline" chip, then "Closes <close date>." when closing is on |
| Missed, `mark_overdue` off | "Deadline <date>." and, when closing is on, "Closes <close date>." |
| Closed | "Closed <close date>" chip with an icon, never colour alone |
| Time limit, ending on | "Access ends <date>." |
| Access ended | "Access ended <date>." |

Every date names the site time zone where spec 1's `shows_time_zone_to_learners` says so. "Locked"
is never used for a closed item.

Dashboard and course detail:

- `_annotate_registered_courses` reads the policy with `resolve_for` alongside
  `course_deadlines_for`; `_card_deadline_line` and the card's missed chip use `shows_missed`. The
  dashboard's query count stays independent of the number of courses' items.
- An ended course stays in the current courses list. `course_card.html` shows "Access ended
  <date>" in the `course-card-shell` eyebrow, with progress and no "Continue" action.
- `course_detail`, for an ended learner, shows "Your access to this course ended on <date>. Your
  progress is saved. Ask your educator to restore it." in place of the CTA, offers no
  start, continue or register action, and links only completed items in the outline under
  `outline_and_results`, none under `nothing`.

### Admin (`freedom_ls/deadlines/admin.py`)

- One `SiteAwareModelAdmin` per policy model, as `messaging_policy/admin.py`:
  `list_display = ["__str__", *policy fields]`, `autocomplete_fields` on the owner,
  `list_select_related`, `ConstraintValidationFormMixin` for the one-per-owner constraints.
- Switch selects read "Inherit / On / Off"; the grace window shows its help text with the
  `DD HH:MM:SS` format.
- `CourseProgressAdmin` gains a read-only "Effective deadline policy": each setting, its resolved
  value and the layer that set it, from `get_deadline_policy().resolve(...)`, plus the access end
  instant when there is one.

### QA data (`freedom_ls/qa_helpers`)

`qa_create_deadlines_seed` gains policy rows that produce: an item inside its grace window, a
closed item, a closed form with an open attempt, an ended course under each `after_access_ended`
value, and an ended course restored with a fixed-date learner override.

## Testing

Pure functions (`consequences.py`), with explicit `now`: each boundary at `instant - 1s`,
`instant`, `instant + 1s`; grace zero, positive and unset-to-default; no due instant; completed
items; `mark_overdue` off.

Resolver:

- Each layer alone sets each field; a more specific layer beats a less specific one in the order
  of decision 1; an organisation row beats a course row.
- A lower layer's `off` beats a higher layer's `on`; an explicit zero grace window beats a nonzero
  one above it; all unset gives `DEADLINE_POLICY_DEFAULTS`.
- `sources` names the right layer for every field.
- A learner with an individual and a cohort registration resolves through the cohort registration's
  row only.
- `resolve_for` query count is constant across 1 and many records; another site's rows never apply.

Checks: E001 fires on a missing, extra, null or `inherit` default; E002 on a bad path or a
non-subclass.

Override: a fixed learner time-limit override validates and resolves; the same on a cohort override
is rejected; a fixed time-limit override with a `due_time` is rejected.

Player and access (request level):

- An item inside its grace window opens; past the close instant `view_course_item` shows the closed
  notice, and `form_start` and `form_fill_page` redirect. The TOC and the redirect agree for the
  same item and `now`.
- A closed form with an open attempt: `form_fill_page` POST is refused and writes nothing;
  `form_submit_and_exit` finalises it, for a submit-on-exit and a save-on-exit form.
- A completed item past its close instant opens; its results page opens.
- A deadline override that moves the due date moves the close instant.
- Under sequential unlock, the item after a closed incomplete item stays blocked.
- Access ended: the registration stays active and no `course.unregistered` fires; dashboard card,
  `course_detail` and `course_home` show the ended state; the free backend offers no
  "Enrol for free"; `initiate_course_access` does not re-register.
- `outline_and_results`: completed items and results open, incomplete items redirect. `nothing`:
  every player route redirects except `form_submit_and_exit`.
- An ended learner on a hidden course still reaches its detail page.
- `course_finish` refuses an ended learner. A completed course never ends.
- Restore: a fixed-date learner override after the end restores access with progress, completion
  times and attempts unchanged.
- `close_items` and `end_course_access` off (the defaults): nothing closes or ends; `mark_overdue`
  off: no "Missed deadline" chip on the TOC, card or player, dates still shown.
- Dashboard query count does not grow with the number of registered courses beyond today's
  per-course cost.

Admin: each policy admin round-trips "Inherit" as `inherit`/null, not `off`/zero; the effective
policy panel shows values and sources.

## Success criteria

- With no policy rows, every page behaves as spec 1 left it.
- An organisation row with `close_items=on` and a one-day grace window closes that organisation's
  incomplete items one day after their due instant, and no other organisation's.
- A cohort row with `close_items=off` keeps items open for that cohort under the same organisation
  row.
- A closed form's open attempt can be submitted and cannot be edited.
- A learner past the access end instant sees "Access ended <date>" on the dashboard, keeps an active
  registration, and gets back in when staff add a fixed-date learner time-limit override.
- `CourseProgressAdmin` shows each effective setting with the layer that set it.
- `uv run manage.py check` passes with the defaults and fails with E001/E002 on a broken setting.
