# Spec: deadline consequence policy

Spec 2 of the Deadlines effort (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`, "Deadlines"). Built on
`deadlines-1-dates-and-overdue`, which is merged. Source: `idea.md` in this directory.

## Purpose

Spec 1 gives content items due dates and courses a time limit, and marks a missed deadline, but
nothing happens when a date passes. This spec adds a deadline policy that decides what does happen:
whether the overdue mark shows, whether an item closes, whether course access ends, and whether
educators are to be notified. Staff set the policy per site, organisation, course, cohort or
registration in the Django admin, and pages enforce it when they are read. Staff restore ended
access by giving the learner a new time limit end.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| deadline policy | coined | The resolved set of consequence settings that applies to one `CourseProgress` (`ResolvedDeadlinePolicy`). |
| policy layer | coined | One of registration, cohort, organisation, course, site, settings. Each holds a sparse `*DeadlineConfig` row; a missing row or an unset value passes to the next layer. Not `DeadlineLayer` in `deadlines/resolver.py`, which orders one deadline's value sources. |
| overdue mark | narrowed | The "Missed deadline" chip spec 1 shows when `is_overdue` is true (`docs/product/learner-experience.md`). |
| grace window | coined | `DeadlineConfigFields.grace_window`: the time after a due instant or time limit end before closing or ending takes effect. |
| close instant | coined | An item's due instant plus the grace window, when the policy closes items. Index key `closes_at`. |
| access end instant | coined | The time limit end plus the grace window, when the policy ends course access. Decision field `access_ended_at`. |
| closed | coined | An incomplete collection item whose close instant has passed. Status `CLOSED`. Not `MessagingFlag.CLOSED`. |
| access ended | coined | A registered learner whose access end instant has passed. |

## Scope

In:

- Six `*DeadlineConfig` models in `freedom_ls/deadlines`, their admins, and a settings-layer
  default.
- A swappable `DeadlinePolicy` class behind the `DEADLINE_POLICY` setting, with a loader and
  system checks.
- Closing items in the course player and the course index; ending access through a wrapper around
  the course access backend.
- The `mark_overdue` switch over spec 1's overdue mark.
- Learner-facing wording for closes, closed, access ends and access ended.
- Deadline overrides on the time limit may hold a fixed end date.
- A read-only effective policy on `CourseProgressAdmin`.

Out:

- Due dates, the time limit, deadline overrides and the overdue display beyond the switch (spec 1).
- Extensions, pause and pause limits (spec 3); learner self-pause (spec 4).
- Raising any notification, reminders and the `deadline.missed` webhook (spec 5). `notify_educators`
  is stored and resolved only.
- Educator-interface screens for policy or restore (spec 6); Needs attention and reports (spec 7),
  including whether `mark_overdue` off hides a learner from overdue lists. Here it is learner-facing
  only.
- Score penalties, "no credit after the deadline", retakes, payments.

## Decisions

1. **Layer order, most specific first: registration, cohort, organisation, course, site,
   settings.** The organisation that uses a course has the final say for its own learners; a course
   row is that course's default across organisations.
2. **Every layer can hold every setting.**
3. **Switches are three-state** (`inherit`, `on`, `off`), so a lower layer can turn off what a
   higher one turned on.
4. **The registration layer is the row of whichever registration minted the `CourseProgress`**
   (`CohortCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig` or `LearnerCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig`), which
   is the one `learner_for_course` resolves. A second registration's row is not read while the
   first governs.
5. **Layer owners come from the `CourseProgress`**: the course, the registration, the cohort
   (through `cohort_registration.cohort`, when a cohort registration minted the record), the
   organisation (`cohort.organisation` or `learner.organisation`) and the site. A missing owner
   skips its layer.
6. **The grace window is a free duration** (`DurationField`), added to the resolved due instant, so
   any deadline override that moves the date moves the close instant with it.
7. **Boundaries match spec 1's `is_overdue`**: a consequence applies when `now > instant`.
8. **The overdue mark shows at the due instant, not after the grace window.** `mark_overdue` off
   hides the mark and keeps the date.
9. **A completed item never closes.**
10. **A closed form with an open attempt can be submitted, not continued**, whatever its exit
    mode, because a closed item has no later to resume in.
11. **Closing does not unlock later items, and sequential unlock wins over closing.** An item
    sequential unlock withholds stays `BLOCKED` past its close instant; only an item it would offer
    becomes `CLOSED`.
12. **Access ended is enforced by `DeadlineEnforcingBackend`, a wrapper in the course access
    loader.** Any downstream backend is covered, and `is_registered_for_course` is unchanged, so
    hidden-course visibility, messaging peers and educator confirmations do not follow a deadline.
    `override_access_to_free()` bypasses it, as it bypasses every inner rule. This adds the
    dependency edges `course_access -> deadlines` and, for the record lookup through
    `course_progress_for`, `course_access -> learner_progress`.
13. **What an ended learner can open is the `after_access_ended` setting.** `outline_and_results`
    (default) shows the course outline and opens completed items. `nothing` shows no outline and
    opens no item. Under both, `form_submit_and_exit` and the results page of a completed attempt
    stay open, since a learner is always entitled to the score they earned. The results page
    offers an ended learner no retry, next or previous step, and under `nothing` carries no
    outline.
14. **Restore is a deadline override on the time limit.** Staff set a fixed end date or a number of
    days after registration on a `LearnerDeadlineOverride` (one learner) or a
    `CohortDeadlineOverride` (a cohort). Progress, completion times and the registration are
    untouched. Closed items stay closed unless staff also override their dates.
15. **Ending access never writes.** No `is_active=False`, no `course.unregistered`, no stored status.

## Requirements

### Config models (`freedom_ls/deadlines/models.py`)

`DeadlineSwitch(models.TextChoices)`: `INHERIT = "inherit"`, `ON = "on"`, `OFF = "off"`.

`AfterAccessEnded(models.TextChoices)`: `INHERIT = "inherit"`,
`OUTLINE_AND_RESULTS = "outline_and_results"`, `NOTHING = "nothing"`.

Abstract `DeadlineConfigFields(models.Model)`:

| Field | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mark_overdue` | `CharField(choices=DeadlineSwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `close_items` | `CharField(choices=DeadlineSwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `end_course_access` | `CharField(choices=DeadlineSwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `notify_educators` | `CharField(choices=DeadlineSwitch)` | `inherit` |
| `grace_window` | `DurationField(null=True, blank=True)` | null (inherit); zero is a set value |
| `after_access_ended` | `CharField(choices=AfterAccessEnded)` | `inherit` |

One `CheckConstraint` per choice field, named `%(class)s_<field>_is_a_deadline_switch` (or
`..._is_an_after_access_ended`), as `MessagingFlags` does, and one for `grace_window >= 0`. Each
field's `help_text` says what "inherit" passes to.

Concrete models, each `SiteAwareModel, TimestampedModel, DeadlineConfigFields`:

| Model | Owner field | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SiteDeadlineConfig` | none | `UniqueConstraint(fields=["site"])`, as `SiteTimeZoneConfig` |
| `OrganisationDeadlineConfig` | `organisation` `OneToOneField(Organisation, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `CourseDeadlineConfig` | `course` `OneToOneField(Course, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `CohortDeadlineConfig` | `cohort` `OneToOneField(Cohort, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `CohortCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig` | `cohort_registration` `OneToOneField(CohortCourseRegistration, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |
| `LearnerCourseRegistrationDeadlineConfig` | `learner_registration` `OneToOneField(LearnerCourseRegistration, CASCADE)` | one-to-one |

Every owner field uses `related_name="deadline_config"`. `freedom_ls/deadlines` gains the
dependency `deadlines -> organisations`.

### Time-limit override (`freedom_ls/deadlines/models.py`)

`_validate_deadline_override` drops its "The course time limit is counted in days after
registration" error, so both override models accept `kind=fixed` on the time limit
(`collection_item=None`) with a `due_date` and no `due_time`. The existing kind rules already make
a date and a day count exclusive. `due_instant` already turns a fixed date into the end of that day
in the site time zone, so the resolver needs no change. `DeadlineOverrideFormSet`'s help text says
a fixed date or a day count restores ended access.

`checks.py`, imported from `apps.py` `ready()` beside `signals`:

### Policy contract and resolver (`freedom_ls/deadlines/policy.py`)

```python
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class ResolvedDeadlinePolicy:
    mark_overdue: bool
    close_items: bool
    end_course_access: bool
    notify_educators: bool
    grace_window: timedelta
    after_access_ended: str          # AfterAccessEnded, never INHERIT
    sources: Mapping[str, str]       # field name -> layer that set it

class DeadlinePolicy:                # plain base, as CourseAccessBackend
    def resolve(self, course_progress: CourseProgress) -> ResolvedDeadlinePolicy: ...
    def resolve_for(self, records: Sequence[CourseProgress]) -> dict[UUID, ResolvedDeadlinePolicy]: ...
```

`LayeredDeadlinePolicy(DeadlinePolicy)`:

- `LAYER_ORDER = ("registration", "cohort", "organisation", "course", "site", "settings")`, the
  only place the order is written.
- Per field, the first layer whose value is set wins; `sources` records it.
- `resolve_for` reads each layer table once for all records, the way `course_deadlines_for` does.
- Rows are read with the site of the records, not the thread-local site, so the resolver works
  outside a request (`_base_manager` with explicit `site_id`, as `deadlines/signals.py`). Nothing
  reads the `deadline_config` reverse accessors.

### Consequences (`freedom_ls/deadlines/consequences.py`)

Pure functions over spec 1's `ResolvedDeadline`, a `ResolvedDeadlinePolicy` and an injected `now`;
no queries:

- `shows_overdue(resolved, policy, completed, now) -> bool`: `policy.mark_overdue and
  is_overdue(resolved, completed, now)`. `is_overdue` itself stays policy-free.
- `close_instant(resolved, policy) -> datetime | None`: `due_at + grace_window` when `close_items`
  and the item has a due instant, else None.
- `is_closed(resolved, policy, completed, now) -> bool`: not completed and `now > close_instant`.
- `access_end_instant(time_limit, policy) -> datetime | None`: time limit end plus `grace_window`
  when `end_course_access`, else None.
- `has_access_ended(time_limit, policy, now) -> bool`.

A course already complete (`CourseProgress.completed_time` set) never has its access ended.

### Access wrapper (`freedom_ls/course_access`)

- `CourseAccessDecision` gains `access_ended_at: datetime | None = None` and
  `after_access_ended: str | None = None`. Existing callers are unaffected.
- `DeadlineEnforcingBackend(CourseAccessBackend)` in `course_access/backends.py`, shaped like
  `VisibilityEnforcingBackend`: it overrides `get_access` and `get_access_for_request` (an
  anonymous user goes straight to the inner backend) and delegates every other method to the inner
  backend. Its `deadlines` imports are function-local.
- It checks deadlines only when the inner decision has `can_access_content=True`. Then, for a user
  whose `CourseProgress` for the course (`course_progress_for`) has passed its access end instant,
  it returns `can_access_content=False`, `can_self_register=False`, no CTA, and the two new fields
  set. Otherwise it returns the inner decision.
- Cost: no queries beyond the inner backend's when the inner decision refuses access; otherwise at
  most 18 (the record, the policy, and the time limit only when `end_course_access` is on),
  whatever the course's size.
- `course_access/loader.py::get_course_access_backend` returns
  `VisibilityEnforcingBackend(DeadlineEnforcingBackend(inner))`.

### Learner interface (`freedom_ls/learner_interface`)

Course index:

- `get_course_index` (`utils.py`) takes the resolved policy beside `course_deadlines` and adds to
  each child dict `closes_at` and `is_closed`; `_deadline_keys` sets `is_overdue` from
  `shows_overdue`, so the templates keep reading `is_overdue`, and `is_closed` from
  `is_closed`, only on a child sequential unlock would offer (`READY`, `IN_PROGRESS`,
  `FAILED`). Such a closed child gets the new status `CLOSED` and keeps its URL; a `BLOCKED`
  child is never `is_closed`.
- `derive_part_status` returns `CLOSED` for a part with a `CLOSED` child and no open child. The part
  row links its first `COMPLETE` child under `outline_and_results`, nothing otherwise.
- `get_course_index` gains `access_ended_outline: bool`. When true it loads progress although
  `can_access_content` is False, keeps URLs only on `COMPLETE` rows and marks the rest `BLOCKED`.
  `course_detail` and `_player_chrome_context` pass it for an ended learner under
  `outline_and_results`.

Player gates:

- `_blocked_item_redirect` gains `refuse_closed: bool`: true from `form_start` and
  `form_fill_page`, false from `view_course_item`. A `BLOCKED` item redirects to `course_detail`;
  a refused `CLOSED` item redirects to `view_course_item`, which renders the closed notice.
  `form_start` and `form_fill_page` pass the course deadlines and policy so their index sees
  closing.
- `view_course_item` on a `CLOSED` item renders a closed notice in the player instead of the item:
  "Closed on <date>. Ask your educator if you need more time." For a form with an open attempt
  (`get_latest_incomplete`) the notice adds a "Submit your answers" button posting to
  `form_submit_and_exit`. This branch returns before the view writes `last_accessed_item`,
  `last_accessed_time` or `started_at` and before `record_course_started`.
- `form_submit_and_exit` resolves whether its placement is closed (that item's deadline and the
  policy, a fixed few queries). When closed, or when the learner's access has ended, it skips the
  `submit_on_exit` guard, skips `submit_page` and the fill-page re-render, and only calls
  `complete()`.
- `_course_access_redirect` splits into `_course_access_decision` (the hidden-course 404 and the
  backend call, so a caller reads the ended state without a second backend call) and the redirect
  itself, which takes the decision plus keyword arguments `collection_item` and
  `route: PlayerRoute` (an enum of item, form results, submit-and-exit, finish). For an ended
  learner it lets through
  `submit-and-exit`, form results for a completed attempt, and, under `outline_and_results`, an
  item that is complete. One helper, `_is_complete_for_learner`, decides "this item is complete for
  this learner" for both `view_course_item` and `course_form_complete`. Everything else,
  `course_finish` included, redirects to `course_detail`.
- The views resolve the policy once per record through `_deadline_policy_or_none`, beside
  `_deadlines_or_none`, and `current_entry` (the row `current_entry_status` reads) feeds the item
  header.
- `course_home` redirects an ended learner to `course_detail`, as it does for any refused access.

Dashboard and course detail:

- `_annotate_registered_courses` reads the policy with `resolve_for` alongside

## Testing

Pure functions (`consequences.py`), with explicit `now`: each boundary at `instant - 1s`,
`instant`, `instant + 1s`; grace zero, positive and unset-to-default; no due instant; completed
items; `mark_overdue` off.

Resolver:

- Each layer alone sets each field; a more specific layer beats a less specific one; an
  organisation row beats a course row.
- A lower layer's `off` beats a higher layer's `on`; an explicit zero grace window beats a nonzero
  one above it; all unset gives `DEADLINE_POLICY_DEFAULTS`.
- `sources` names the right layer for every field. An individual registration's record skips the
  cohort layer.
- A learner with an individual and a cohort registration resolves through the cohort registration's
  row only.
- `resolve_for` runs at most eight queries (two owner lookups for the cohort and the learner's
  organisation, and six layer tables) for 1 and for many records; another site's rows never
  apply.

Checks: E001 fires on a missing, extra, null, `inherit`, wrong-type or out-of-choices default;
E002 on a bad path or a non-subclass.

Override: a fixed time-limit override validates on both models and resolves to the end of that day
in the site time zone; a fixed time-limit override with a `due_time` is rejected.

Player and access (request level):

- An item inside its grace window opens; past the close instant `view_course_item` shows the closed
  notice without writing resume progress, and `form_start` and `form_fill_page` redirect to the
  item's player page. The TOC and the redirect agree for the same item and `now`.
- A closed form with an open attempt: `form_fill_page` POST is refused and writes nothing;
  `form_submit_and_exit` finalises it without saving posted answers, for a submit-on-exit and a
  save-on-exit form, and does the same for an ended learner. An open save-on-exit form that is
  not closed is still not finalised.
- A completed item past its close instant opens; its results page opens.
- A deadline override that moves the due date moves the close instant.
- Under sequential unlock, the item after a closed incomplete item stays `BLOCKED`, and a part whose
  open children are all closed reads `CLOSED`.
- `DeadlineEnforcingBackend.get_access` runs the same number of queries for a course with 1 item and
  with many, at most 18, and none when the inner decision refuses access.
- `get_access_for_request` on `course_detail` sees the ended state.
- Access ended: the registration stays active and no `course.unregistered` fires; dashboard card,
  `course_detail` and `course_home` show the ended state; the free backend offers no
  "Enrol for free"; `initiate_course_access` does not re-register. A deactivated registration gets
  the inner decision, not "access ended".
- `outline_and_results`: completed items and results open, incomplete items redirect. `nothing`:
  no outline, every player route redirects except `form_submit_and_exit` and the results of a
  completed attempt (the results page then shows no outline). On the All courses page an ended
  course reads "Access ended" and links to its detail page.
- An ended learner on a hidden course still reaches its detail page.
- `course_finish` refuses an ended learner. A completed course never ends.
- Restore: a fixed-date learner override after the end restores access with progress, completion
  times and attempts unchanged; a fixed-date cohort override restores the cohort.
- `close_items` and `end_course_access` off (the defaults): nothing closes or ends; `mark_overdue`
  off: no "Missed deadline" chip on the TOC, card (item and time limit) or player, dates still
  shown.

Admin: each config admin round-trips "Inherit" as `inherit`/null, not `off`/zero; the effective
policy panel shows values and sources.

## Success criteria

- An organisation row with `close_items=on` and a one-day grace window closes that organisation's
  incomplete items one day after their due instant, and no other organisation's.
- A cohort row with `close_items=off` keeps items open for that cohort under the same organisation
  row.
- A learner past the access end instant sees "Access ended <date>" on the dashboard and gets back in
  when staff add a fixed-date learner time-limit override.
- `CourseProgressAdmin` shows each effective setting with the layer that set it.
