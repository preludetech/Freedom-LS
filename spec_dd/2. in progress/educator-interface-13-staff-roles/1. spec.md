# Spec: staff roles per cohort

Spec 13 of the Educator interface rebuild. Source: `idea.md`, with the user's answers in
`answers.md`. Research: `research_*.md` beside this file.

## Purpose

After this spec, an organisation admin finds people by name and gives each one different roles on
different cohorts. They add a staff member before choosing any roles, and they downgrade an
organisation admin, themselves included. A cohort admin manages their own cohort's staff from the
cohort's Settings tab. Two new roles let someone see the organisation's staff list without
managing it. Nobody can see or reach more accounts than they can today, except holders of the
new viewer roles.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `organisation_staff_viewer` | coined | Organisation-level role. Sees the names on the organisation's staff list. Changes nothing. |
| `organisation_staff_roles_viewer` | coined | Organisation-level role. Sees the organisation's staff list with each person's roles. Changes nothing. |
| `view_organisation_staff`, `view_organisation_staff_roles` | coined | The two permissions those roles carry, on `StaffMember`. |
| `ORGANISATION_ROLES` | coined | The staff roles assigned on the `Organisation` itself rather than on a cohort. |
| visible staff | coined | For one actor and one organisation, the staff members whose names the actor may see. A set of `User` rows by design, since staff are users. |
| pickable staff | coined | The users an actor may pick when adding staff. "Candidate" is taken by the messaging policy. |
| `target` (of a role) | coined | Where a staff role is held: `"organisation"` or `"cohort"`. Carried by `cohort_educators` rows (R24) and by `RoleEntry`, which keeps `scope` for its display text. |
| may edit own roles | coined | Decision 6 in code: whether an actor may add, change or deactivate their own role of a given type on a given target. It holds exactly when they may assign that role on that target. |
| Remove from cohort | narrowed | Button label for `deactivate_role` on one cohort. A copy exception to the glossary, where "removed" means a deleted row. `RemoveFromCohortBulkAction` keeps the label for its real deletion. The audit verb stays `role.deactivated`. |

## Scope

In: every requirement below. Out:

- Changing a role in place. A change is a deactivation plus an assignment, as spec 9 decided. No
  `role.changed` verb.
- Any change to `StaffMember` deactivation. It still suspends every grant and leaves the
  assignments untouched, and reactivation restores them.
- A guard against leaving a cohort with no cohort admin. Organisation admins can always manage it.
- Searching across every account on the site, and loosening `lookup_user`.
- Multi-person selection in the dialog.
- Cohort admins adding people who aren't on their visible staff. Without a viewer role, a cohort
  admin can add only staff already holding a role on another of their cohorts, which helps only
  someone who runs several cohorts.

## Decisions

1. **`staff_of` is the union of the organisation's `StaffMember` users and the holders of staff
   assignments.** It is not built from `StaffMember` alone after a backfill. `can()` ignores a
   grant without an active `StaffMember` row, so backfilling would hand access to people who are
   blocked today.
2. **Change role is `assign_role` then `deactivate_role`, inside one `transaction.atomic()`.**
   The new role goes first, so a person changing their own role still holds the old one while
   the new one is checked. Both functions already write their audit entries. History shows
   `role.assigned` then `role.deactivated`, with the same actor.
3. **Only an actor who holds `add_staffmember` on the organisation may create staff**: a new
   account, a no-role staff member, or staff status for a user who isn't staff yet. Today that
   means organisation admins and site admins. Everyone else picks from their pickable staff. This
   holds even for an organisation admin who also edits cohorts. The user's rule "only
   organisation admins add new staff" wins over "no one with cohort edit access turns a learner
   into staff".
4. **The viewer roles widen what their holder sees, never what they can grant.** They carry no
   `assign_*` permission.
5. **Search is a separate path from `lookup_user`.** It starts from the actor's pickable staff
   and narrows by the typed term. It never starts from `User.objects`, and it never takes the set
   from request input.
6. **Self-editing.** Anyone may add, change and deactivate their own role of a type they may
   assign on that target: a cohort admin may change their own cohort role on their cohort, and an
   organisation admin any of theirs. The last-organisation-admin lock still applies. Deactivating
   your own staff membership and changing your own site role stay refused with
   `RefusalReason.SELF`.

## Requirements

### Roles and permissions

- R1. `StaffMember.Meta.permissions` (`learner_management/models.py`) gains
  `view_organisation_staff` and `view_organisation_staff_roles`. This needs a migration, and
  `role_based_permissions/registry.py` gets their labels.
- R2. `BASE_ROLES` (`role_based_permissions/roles.py`) gains two roles, both `SCOPE_OBJECT` and
  assigned on the `Organisation`:
  - `organisation_staff_viewer`, "Organisation staff viewer": "Sees who is on the organisation's
    staff, and changes nothing." Its permission is `view_organisation_staff`.
  - `organisation_staff_roles_viewer`, "Organisation staff and roles viewer": "Sees who is on the
    organisation's staff and the roles they hold, and changes nothing." Its permissions are
    `view_organisation_staff` and `view_organisation_staff_roles`.
- R3. `ObjectRoleAssignment.Meta.permissions` gains `assign_organisation_staff_viewer` and
  `assign_organisation_staff_roles_viewer`, with a migration. `site_admin` and
  `organisation_admin` hold both, plus `view_organisation_staff` and
  `view_organisation_staff_roles`.
- R4. `cohort_admin` gains `assign_cohort_admin` and `assign_cohort_viewer`. `grantable_roles`
  already narrows each grant to the cohorts where the capability passes. It also gains
  `view_auditlogentry`, which opens the History tab of a cohort they administer, so they can read
  the role changes they make there. The organisation's Audit log stays with organisation and site
  admins, and so does a learner's History tab, because a learner's history covers every cohort
  they belong to.
- R5. `organisations_accessible_to` (`learner_management/queries.py`) lets a holder of either
  view permission at organisation level into the organisation. The Staff section is the only
  section they can open. Every other section keeps its own gate.
- R6. `STAFF_ROLES` lists the two viewer roles after `organisation_admin`. A new constant beside
  it, `ORGANISATION_ROLES = ("organisation_admin", "organisation_staff_viewer",
  "organisation_staff_roles_viewer")`, replaces every test of `role == "organisation_admin"` that
  means "a role on the organisation itself". Known sites:
  - `RoleEntry.scope`, `full_scope` and `staff_role_entries` (`educator_interface/staff.py`);
  - `grantable_roles` (`role_assignment.py`);
  - `AddStaffMemberForm.clean` (`educator_interface/forms.py`);
  - `AddStaffMemberAction._submit` and `_confirmation`;
  - `DeactivateRoleAction.get_context_data` and `handle_confirmed_submit`;
  - the Change cohorts exclusion in `staff_roles.html`.
  Every loop over `STAFF_ROLES` gets checked too: `StaffRoleFilter`,
  `StaffConfig.get_intro_context`, `offerable_roles` and `_role_note`.
  `is_last_organisation_admin` keeps meaning `organisation_admin` only.
- R7. `assign_role` refuses a role whose target type doesn't suit it: an `ORGANISATION_ROLES`
  role on a cohort, or a cohort role on an organisation.
- R8. Decision 6 in code: on the viewer's own row, `_refuse_unless` (`role_assignment.py`) lets
  an organisation or cohort role change through whenever the grantor may assign that role on that
  target; the ordinary `assign_<role>` check decides. Templates show own-row controls on the same
  per-role condition as anyone else's row, so no lock note is needed.

### Staff membership and visibility (`learner_management/`)

- R9. `staff_of(organisation)` follows Decision 1: the users with a `StaffMember` row in the
  organisation (active or not), together with the holders of staff assignments.
- R10. `add_staff_member(actor, user, organisation)` in `role_assignment.py` is the checked
  entry point for a no-role add. It refuses `NOT_PERMITTED` unless the actor holds
  `add_staffmember`. Inside `transaction.atomic()` it calls `get_or_create` on the
  `StaffMember`. It writes a `staff_member.added` entry only when it created the row: the target
  is the new `StaffMember`, with `organisation=` and `subjects=(user,)`, like the other
  `staff_member.*` entries. An existing active row is refused with a new
  `RefusalReason.ALREADY_STAFF`. An existing inactive row is refused with a new
  `RefusalReason.INACTIVE_STAFF_MEMBER`, and the dialog then points to Reactivate staff member.
- R11. `staff_member.added` is registered in `audit_log/actions.py`, group "Staff and roles".
- R12. `visible_staff(actor, organisation) -> QuerySet[User]`:
  - for a holder of `view_organisation_staff` on the organisation: the active `StaffMember` users
    of `staff_of(organisation)`;
  - for anyone else: the active staff members holding an active role on a cohort where the actor
    holds `assign_cohort_viewer`.
- R13. `pickable_staff(actor, organisation) -> QuerySet[User]`. For an actor holding
  `add_staffmember`, it is `visible_staff` plus the users with an active `Learner` row in the
  organisation. For anyone else, it is `visible_staff` alone. The idea's `members_of` does not
  exist and is not created. Both functions filter `site` explicitly, keep only `User.is_active`
  accounts, and leave out the actor.

### Who may add whom (`AddStaffMemberAction`, `AddStaffMemberToCohortAction`)

- R14. The email step resolves a typed email:
  - **Actor holding `add_staffmember`:** as today. `lookup_user` finds anyone on the site, and an
    unknown email creates a pending account through `match_or_create_user`. The other-site message
    never names the account.
  - **Anyone else:** the email is matched only against `pickable_staff`. With no match, the dialog
    answers 422 with "No staff member you can add has this email. Ask an organisation admin to
    add them." The text is the same whether the account exists, is a learner, or is staff
    elsewhere. No account is created.
- R15. The action enforces the same rule again on submit and on confirm. For an actor without
  `add_staffmember`, a POST naming a user outside `pickable_staff` is refused with a new
  `RefusalReason.NOT_VISIBLE` (422, same message). An actor holding `add_staffmember` keeps R14's
  full-email resolution.
- R16. For an actor holding `add_staffmember`, the role step offers **No roles yet** as its first
  choice, with the hint "They'll appear in Staff but can't see any cohort until you add a role."
  The role choices are a plain radio list separated by space: no box around each choice. The
  email step's button reads "Next"; the confirm step's button reads "Confirm". This needs:
  - an `AddStaffMemberForm` variant whose role is optional, with no cohorts required for "none";
  - a branch in `handle_submit`, `_submit` and `_confirmation` ahead of `_targets` that calls
    `add_staff_member`;
  - a confirmation template state for it. Every confirmation shows the role's description and
    its scope, and nothing more: no widening sentence and no paragraph about memberships.
  `AddStaffMemberToCohortAction` never offers the choice.
- R17. On the Settings tab, a cohort admin sees Add staff member only when their pickable staff
  is non-empty.

### Add role (Staff table)

- R18. A new row action **Add role** opens the Add-staff dialog at the role step with the person
  fixed. It is a new action class or a `user` parameter on `AddStaffMemberAction`, whichever the
  plan finds simpler.
  - The person shows as text (avatar chip, name, email), not an input.
  - The title is "Add a role for <name>", and the step lists their current roles.
  - A role already held on every grantable cohort is unavailable, with the reason beside it.
  - Duplicates keep today's 422.
- R19. Add role is offered when the viewer has any grantable role for that person. That covers
  inactive staff members, and the viewer's own row under Decision 6. It is the first button in
  the row's actions, above Deactivate staff member or Reactivate staff member.
  `can(cohort_admin, …, organisation)` is false, so Add role, Deactivate role and Change cohorts
  each override `has_permission` and `is_offered` to read `grantable_roles_for(request)`, and
  `assign_role` or `deactivate_role` re-checks each target on submit. A cohort admin who holds a
  viewer role then gets these actions for their own cohorts. Deactivate role and Change cohorts
  show only on a role item that includes a cohort the viewer can give; on any other item both
  are a 404. Giving that role on one of the viewer's own cohorts is Add role's job.
- R20. The Staff section opens for holders of `view_organisation_staff`, and that permission
  becomes `StaffConfig`'s `view_capability`. The Roles column and its per-role buttons render
  only for holders of `view_organisation_staff_roles`. A names-only viewer sees each person and
  nothing else: the toolbar offers no Role filter (no chip, nothing under Add filter, no Clear
  all). In the Add role and Add staff member dialogs, the "Now" and "After" lists show a
  names-only viewer the person's roles on the cohorts the viewer can give, which that cohort's
  Settings tab already shows them, and no other role.
- R21. A row with no role entries shows "No roles yet" in muted text in the Roles cell.
- R22. The Staff table has four columns: Staff member, Roles (each role's name and scope),
  Role actions (Deactivate role and Change cohorts on the same line as their role) and Staff
  member actions (Add role, Deactivate or Reactivate staff member). "Added <date> by <who>" is not
  in the table: the staff member's name opens a quick view listing each role with its scope and
  "Added <date> by <who>". A role entry with no `assigned_by` reads "Added <date>", with no "by"
  clause (spec 12 B6). The four columns need about 900 px, so the table shows as cards whenever
  its container is narrower than that: on a phone, at 768 px, and at 1280 px beside an open quick
  view. Every button stays in view and nothing scrolls sideways.

### Cohort Settings tab (a `PanelStack` of Name, Staff and Status panels)

The tab is three panels, each with one job: **Name** (the name field with Save), **Staff** (Add
staff member in the panel header; one row per person in three columns: person, role, buttons;
rows separated by space) and **Status** (the deactivate/reactivate explanation; Deactivate,
Reactivate and Delete in its footer). No panel holds a sub-heading for another job.

- R23. `CohortSettingsPanel` overrides `has_permission` to open for `change_cohort` or
  `assign_cohort_viewer` on the cohort. The tab strip uses the same check.
  - The name field, Deactivate, Reactivate and Delete keep `change_cohort`.
  - The deactivate/reactivate and "not empty" paragraphs render only when their actions do.
  - A cohort admin sees the Staff panel alone.
- R24. `cohort_educators(cohort)` returns rows carrying the user, the role key, the display name
  and `target` (`"cohort"` or `"organisation"`), in place of `(User, str)` pairs.
  - It adds the active `StaffMember` holders of an organisation role that grants `view_cohort`.
  - Site-level admins appear only through a role they hold in the organisation, as spec 9 decided.
  - Its callers and tests change with it.
- R25. Each cohort row has **Change role** and then **Remove from cohort**, small, in
  `<c-data-table-actions>`. Both use the cohort as `permission_object`. Each shows when the viewer
  may grant that row's role on this cohort, the viewer's own row included (Decision 6). The
  buttons sit in the row's third column.
- R26. **Change role** opens a dialog.
  - A radio group lists the cohort roles in `STAFF_ROLES` the viewer may grant on this cohort,
    with the current role selected.
  - The title is a question, and Cancel is focused.
  - The confirm text: "<name> will become <role> on <cohort> only."
  - The handler checks `assign_<old role>` and `assign_<new role>` before it opens the
    transaction, so a refusal is a clean 403 and never a rolled-back half.
  - Choosing a role the person already holds on this cohort answers 422.
- R27. **Remove from cohort** opens a confirm dialog: "<name> will lose <role> on <cohort>. Their
  other roles are unchanged." Confirming calls `deactivate_role(grantor, user, role, cohort,
  reason=...)` for this cohort only. It uses the standard confirm variant, not `error`.
- R28. An organisation-role row shows its role ("Organisation admin") and no buttons. Its buttons
  column is empty: no note and no link to Staff.
- R29. Both new actions re-read the assignment on every request. An inactive or missing
  assignment answers `action_unavailable`. Out-of-scope users and cohorts answer 404. Both refresh
  with `STAFF_CHANGED`.

### As-you-type search (Add-staff email step)

- R30. The search is a GET with `?q=` on the Add-staff action's own URL. That way it inherits
  `has_permission`, `permission_object` and `denied_response`, and a viewer without the
  capability gets 403, not an empty list.
- R31. Typing two or more characters searches `pickable_staff` by first name, last name and
  email. Each word of the term must match one of the three (`icontains`). Results are ordered by
  name and capped at eight, and expose name and email only.
  - Under two characters: "Type at least 2 characters to search, or enter a full email."
  - At the cap, a final non-selectable line: "Showing the first 8. Keep typing to narrow."
- R32. It uses the recipient picker's trigger attributes (`hx-trigger="input changed
  delay:300ms"`, `hx-sync="this:replace"`) and its `{% partialdef results %}` pattern. The markup
  is otherwise its own. `educator_interface` does not import `comms` internals. Any shared
  term-matching helper lives in a neutral lower app.
- R33. Each result is a person chip (`<c-panel-avatar-chip>`). Picking one fills the email and
  moves to the role step.
  - For an actor without `add_staffmember`, the field's help text directly under it reads
    "Searching staff you can add".
  - Results are separated by space, with no line between them; an empty result list renders
    nothing.
  - No match: "No one matches '<term>'." An actor holding `add_staffmember` also sees "Type their
    full email to add someone else."
- R34. Accessibility follows the WAI-ARIA list-autocomplete combobox. Alpine holds the keyboard
  state.
  - The input has `role="combobox"`, `aria-autocomplete="list"`, `aria-controls`,
    `aria-expanded` and `aria-activedescendant`.
  - Results are `role="option"` rows in a `role="listbox"` whose `id` stays stable across swaps.
    Options are never `<a>` elements.
  - Arrow keys move, Enter picks, Escape closes.
  - A polite `role="status"` region outside the swapped fragment announces the count, "No
    results" and loading.
  - Options are at least 44 px tall.

## Testing

- `staff_of` includes a no-role `StaffMember`. `can()` still ignores grants without an active
  `StaffMember`.
- `add_staff_member` covers four cases:
  - an organisation admin succeeds, with one `staff_member.added` entry targeting the row;
  - a cohort admin is refused `NOT_PERMITTED`;
  - an existing active member is refused `ALREADY_STAFF`;
  - an inactive one is refused `INACTIVE_STAFF_MEMBER`.
- `visible_staff` and `pickable_staff` for each actor kind:
  - a cohort admin without a viewer role sees only staff on their cohorts;
  - a learner who isn't staff is never in a cohort admin's set;
  - neither function ever includes an inactive account, the actor, or a user from another
    organisation or site.
- Forged POSTs to the add, Add role, Change role and Remove from cohort endpoints answer the
  right status:
  - for an actor without `add_staffmember`, a user outside `pickable_staff`: 422 `NOT_VISIBLE`;
  - a cohort outside scope: 404;
  - a cohort whose Settings tab the actor cannot open (a cohort viewer): 404, because the tab's
    URLs do not exist for them;
  - a role the actor may not grant: 403;
  - a cohort admin's own user: `SELF`.
- An organisation admin, a site admin and a superuser each add, change and deactivate their own
  roles. The last-organisation-admin lock still refuses the last one.
- `assign_role` refuses an organisation role on a cohort and a cohort role on an organisation.
- A cohort admin typing the full email of a learner, of an unknown address, and of staff they
  can't see gets the same 422 text, and no account is created.
- Change role writes `role.deactivated` then `role.assigned`. A refusal on either role leaves
  the original active.
- Remove from cohort deactivates only this cohort's assignment.
- On the Settings tab:
  - an organisation-role holder has no buttons;
  - a cohort admin sees the Staff section and none of the cohort-state text.
- Viewer roles:
  - a names-only viewer opens Staff and sees no Roles column;
  - a roles viewer sees roles and no buttons;
  - neither opens any other organisation section;
  - only organisation and site admins grant either role.
- A cohort admin who holds a viewer role gets Add role, Deactivate role and Change cohorts for
  their own cohorts on the Staff table.
- The search covers the minimum length, the cap, word matching and the 403 without the
  capability.
- `invariants/test_permission_matrix.py` gains rows for both viewer roles and the new
  `cohort_admin` permissions. `test_config_authorisation.py`, `test_organisation_isolation.py`
  and `panel_framework/tests/invariants/test_templates.py` pass.
- Playwright, at 375, 768 and 1280 px:
  - In `educator_interface/tests/playwright/test_members_flow.py`, an organisation admin
    searches, picks a person and adds a cohort role. They then add a no-role staff member and use
    Add role on that row.
  - In a new file, with a cohort-admin logged-in fixture added to that directory's `conftest.py`,
    a cohort admin changes and removes a role from the Settings tab.
  - The combobox is driven by keyboard alone.

## Open questions

- Q: Does a no-role add get its own audit entry? → A: Yes, `staff_member.added` (R10). (assumed
  default: without it, History would not show the add. Overrule by editing R10 and R11.)
- Q: Does an organisation admin's search include inactive learners and staff? → A: No; they're
  reached by full email. (assumed default: search lists people who can act now. Overrule by
  editing R12 and R13.)
- Q: Is `view_staffmember` retired now that the Staff section gates on `view_organisation_staff`?
  → A: No. It stays held by organisation and site admins, and the plan removes any use that the
  new permissions replace. (assumed default: removing a permission forces a migration on
  downstream overrides. Overrule by editing R20.)
- Q: How does the code tell organisation roles from cohort roles? → A: An `ORGANISATION_ROLES`
  constant, with no `target` field on the generic `Role` type. (assumed default: it touches no
  downstream role override. Overrule by editing R6.)
- Q: Where does the search endpoint live? → A: A GET on the Add-staff action URL (R30). (assumed
  default: it reuses the action's permission and denial handling. Overrule by editing R30.)
- Q: Do the identifiers `pickable_staff` and `target` replace `staff_candidates` and "scope"?
  → A: Yes. (assumed default: "candidate" and "scope" already mean other things in the glossary
  and in `RoleEntry`. Overrule by editing Terminology.)
- Q: Which Playwright file does each flow go in? → A: Organisation-admin flows extend
  `test_members_flow.py`. Cohort-admin flows go in a new file with a new fixture. (assumed
  default: a flow stops at its first failure, and the logins differ. Overrule by editing
  Testing.)
- Q: The Dashboard, Learners, Cohorts and Courses sections have no `view_capability`; today
  entering the organisation is their only gate, so R5 alone would open them to the viewer
  roles. What gate do they get? → A: `OrganisationSectionConfig.is_visible` shows a section
  with no `view_capability` only to a viewer holding `view_cohort` on the organisation or on
  one of its cohorts. (assumed default: every existing staff role holds `view_cohort` somewhere
  in the organisation, so nobody loses a section, and the viewer roles stop at Staff. Overrule
  by editing R5.)
- Q: Does the Roles column header disappear for a names-only viewer? → A: No; the header stays
  and the cells render empty. (assumed default: `DataTable.get_columns` takes no request, so a
  per-viewer column would be a panel-framework change. Overrule by editing R20.)

## Success criteria

- An organisation admin finds a person by typing part of their name.
- An organisation admin adds a staff member with "No roles yet". The person appears in Staff
  with an Add role button.
- One person holds `cohort_admin` on Cohort A and `cohort_viewer` on Cohort B, set through the
  interface. Each Roles line names its cohorts.
- An organisation admin downgrades themselves to a cohort role through Add role and then
  Deactivate role, while another organisation admin exists.
- A cohort admin changes a viewer to an admin on their cohort from the Settings tab. History
  shows `role.deactivated` then `role.assigned`.
- A cohort admin cannot create an account, add a learner who isn't staff, or add staff outside
  their pickable staff, through the UI or a forged POST.
- A holder of `organisation_staff_viewer` alone opens the Staff page, sees names and no roles,
  and can't open the Dashboard.
