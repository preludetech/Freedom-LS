# Spec: staff roles per cohort

Spec 13 of the Educator interface rebuild. Source: `idea.md`, with the user's answers in
`answers.md`. Research: `research_*.md` beside this file.

## Purpose

An organisation admin can't add someone without remembering their exact email. They can't give a
person different roles on different cohorts, can't downgrade an organisation admin to cohort
access, and can't add a staff member before deciding their roles. Staff changes for a cohort
can't be made from that cohort's Settings tab, and a cohort admin can't manage their own cohort's
staff at all. This spec fixes all of that without loosening who may see or reach which accounts.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `organisation_staff_viewer` | coined | A new organisation-scoped role. Its holder sees the organisation's whole staff list and changes nothing. No existing role grants `view_staffmember` without edit rights. |
| visible staff | coined | For one actor and one organisation: the users they may pick when adding staff to a cohort. Defined under "Who may add whom". |
| Remove from cohort | narrowed | Button label for `deactivate_role` on one cohort. A deliberate copy exception to the glossary, where "removed" means a deleted row. The audit verb stays `role.deactivated`. |

## Scope

In:

- `StaffMember` (`freedom_ls/learner_management/models.py:211`) becomes a source for the Staff
  list, so a staff member with no roles exists and shows.
- An organisation admin adds a staff member with no roles.
- **Add role** on each Staff table row, opening the Add-staff dialog at its role step with the
  person fixed.
- The new `organisation_staff_viewer` role.
- Cohort admins add, change and remove staff on their own cohorts, from the cohort's Settings tab.
- **Change role** and **Remove from cohort** on each person in the Settings tab's Staff list.
  Organisation-wide role holders appear there read-only.
- As-you-type search in the Add-staff dialog's email step, over a candidate set scoped to the actor.
- Spec 12 QA bug B6: the Roles cell reads "Added <date> by Not recorded" when `assigned_by` is null.

Out:

- Changing a role in place. A change is a deactivation plus an assignment, as spec 9 decided.
- A `role.changed` audit verb.
- Any change to `StaffMember` deactivation. It still suspends every grant and leaves the
  assignments untouched, and reactivation restores them.
- A guard against leaving a cohort with no cohort admin. Organisation admins can always manage it.
- Searching across every account on the site, and loosening `lookup_user`.
- Multi-person selection in the dialog.

## Decisions

1. **`staff_of` is the union of the organisation's `StaffMember` users and the holders of staff
   assignments.** It is not built from `StaffMember` alone after a backfill. `can()` ignores a
   grant without an active `StaffMember` row, so backfilling active rows would hand access to
   people who are blocked today.
2. **Change role is `deactivate_role` then `assign_role`, inside one `transaction.atomic()`.**
   Both functions already write their audit entries (`role_based_permissions/utils.py`
   `_record_role_change`). History shows `role.deactivated` then `role.assigned`, with the same actor.
3. **Only an actor who holds `add_staffmember` on the organisation may create staff**: a new
   account, a no-role staff member, or staff status for a user who isn't staff yet. Today that
   means organisation admins and site admins. Everyone else picks only from their visible staff.
4. **Cohort admins get `assign_cohort_admin` and `assign_cohort_viewer`.**
   `grantable_roles` already narrows each grant to the cohorts where the capability passes, so
   they grant only on their own cohorts, at their own level and below.
5. **`organisation_staff_viewer` widens what its holder can see, never what they can grant.** It
   carries `view_staffmember` and no `assign_*` permission. Only holders of
   `assign_organisation_staff_viewer` grant it: organisation admins and site admins.
6. **Search is a separate path from `lookup_user`.** The search query starts from the actor's
   candidate set and is then narrowed by the typed term. It never starts from `User.objects` and
   never takes the candidate set from request input. A full email outside the candidate set still
   resolves through `lookup_user`, for organisation admins only.
7. **"Change cohorts" stays** for a role held on several cohorts.
8. **Own row: no controls.** No one changes or removes their own roles, on the Staff table or on
   the Settings tab. This is the existing `RefusalReason.SELF` rule, applied to the new actions.

## Requirements

### Roles and permissions (`freedom_ls/role_based_permissions/`)

- R1. `roles.py` `BASE_ROLES` gains `organisation_staff_viewer`, display name "Organisation staff
  viewer", `SCOPE_OBJECT` on the `Organisation`. Its description: "Sees everyone on the
  organisation's staff and the roles they hold, and changes nothing." Its permissions are
  `freedom_ls_learner_management.view_staffmember`, plus whatever opening the Staff section
  read-only requires, which the plan confirms against `StaffConfig`.
- R2. `ObjectRoleAssignment.Meta.permissions` gains `assign_organisation_staff_viewer`. This is a
  new migration. `registry.py` gets its label. `site_admin` and `organisation_admin` hold it.
- R3. `cohort_admin` gains `freedom_ls_role_based_permissions.assign_cohort_admin` and
  `assign_cohort_viewer`.
- R4. `STAFF_ROLES` (`learner_management/queries.py`) lists `organisation_staff_viewer` after
  `organisation_admin`. Every place that tests `role == "organisation_admin"` to mean "a role on
  the organisation itself" instead tests the role's target. Known sites: `RoleEntry.scope` and
  `full_scope` (`educator_interface/staff.py`), `staff_role_entries`, `grantable_roles`
  (`learner_management/role_assignment.py`), `DeactivateRoleAction`,
  `AddStaffMemberAction._submit`, and the Change cohorts exclusion in `staff_roles.html`.
  `is_last_organisation_admin` keeps meaning `organisation_admin` only.
- R5. A test proves `is_widening` gives the right answer when the grantor holds
  `organisation_staff_viewer`.

### Staff membership (`freedom_ls/learner_management/`)

- R6. `staff_of(organisation)` returns the users with a `StaffMember` row in the organisation
  (active or not) together with the users holding staff assignments (Decision 1). The
  `staff_assignments` docstring loses the "nothing backfilled them" rationale for the list.
- R7. A new function `add_staff_member(actor, user, organisation)` in `role_assignment.py` is the
  checked entry point for a no-role add. It refuses `NOT_PERMITTED` unless the actor holds
  `add_staffmember` on the organisation. It creates an active `StaffMember` and writes a
  `staff_member.added` audit entry: target the organisation, subject the user. When the user
  already has an active row, it refuses with a new `RefusalReason.ALREADY_STAFF`. When the row is
  inactive, it refuses with `RefusalReason.INACTIVE_STAFF_MEMBER`, and the dialog points to
  Reactivate staff member.
- R8. `staff_member.added` is registered in `audit_log/actions.py` in the "Staff and roles" group.
- R9. A new query `visible_staff(actor, organisation) -> QuerySet[User]`:
  - holds `view_staffmember` on the organisation (organisation admin, site admin,
    `organisation_staff_viewer`): `staff_of(organisation)`, active `StaffMember` rows only;
  - otherwise: active staff members holding an active role on a cohort where the actor holds
    `assign_cohort_viewer`.
- R10. A new query `staff_candidates(actor, organisation) -> QuerySet[User]`: for an actor holding
  `add_staffmember`, `visible_staff` plus the users with an active `Learner` row in the
  organisation. For anyone else, `visible_staff` only. Both functions filter `site` explicitly,
  because the site-aware manager narrows only inside a request.

### Who may add whom (`educator_interface/actions.py`, `AddStaffMemberAction` and `AddStaffMemberToCohortAction`)

- R11. The email step resolves a typed email as follows:
  - **Actor holding `add_staffmember`:** behaviour as today. `lookup_user` finds anyone on the
    site, an unknown email creates a pending account through `match_or_create_user`, and the
    other-site message never names the account.
  - **Anyone else:** the email is matched only against `visible_staff`. With no match, the dialog
    answers 422 with "No staff member you can add has this email. Ask an organisation admin to
    add them." The same text appears whether or not the account exists, is a learner, or is staff
    elsewhere. No account is created.
- R12. The same rule is enforced again on submit and on confirm, inside the action. A POST naming
  a user outside the actor's `staff_candidates` is refused with a new `RefusalReason.NOT_VISIBLE`
  (422, same message). A cohort admin can never turn a learner who isn't staff into staff.
- R13. For an actor holding `add_staffmember`, the role step offers **No roles yet** as its first
  choice. Its hint: "They'll appear in Staff but can't see any cohort until you add a role." The
  submit button reads "Add staff member". Confirming calls `add_staff_member` (R7). The step is
  not offered on `AddStaffMemberToCohortAction`, where the cohort is fixed.
- R14. `AddStaffMemberAction.is_offered` stays true for an actor who may add a no-role staff
  member, even when `_grantable` is empty.

### Add role (Staff table)

- R15. A new row action **Add role** opens the Add-staff dialog at the role step with the person
  fixed. It is a new action class or a `user` parameter on `AddStaffMemberAction`; the plan picks.
  The person shows as text (avatar chip, name, email), not as an input. The title is "Add a role
  for <name>". The step lists their current roles. A role already held on every grantable cohort
  is unavailable, with the reason beside it. Duplicates keep today's 422.
- R16. Add role is offered on every row except the viewer's own, when the viewer has any grantable
  role, including on an inactive staff member. In `staff_roles.html` it is the first button in the
  row's actions, above Deactivate or Reactivate staff member.
- R17. A row with no role entries shows "No roles yet" in muted text in the Roles cell.
- R18. A role entry with no `assigned_by` reads "Added <date>", with no "by" clause (spec 12 B6).

### Cohort Settings tab (`CohortSettingsPanel`, `panels/cohort_settings.html`)

- R19. The tab opens for an actor holding `change_cohort` or `assign_cohort_viewer` on the cohort.
  The name field, Deactivate, Reactivate and Delete keep `change_cohort`. A cohort admin sees only
  the Staff section, with Add staff member.
- R20. `cohort_educators(cohort)` returns rows that carry the user, the role key, the display name
  and the scope (cohort or organisation), in place of `(User, str)` pairs. It adds active
  `StaffMember` holders of an organisation-scoped role that grants `view_cohort`. Site-level
  admins appear only through a role they hold in the organisation, as spec 9 decided. Its callers
  and tests change with it.
- R21. Each cohort-scoped row has **Change role** and then **Remove from cohort**, small, in
  `<c-data-table-actions>`. Each shows when the viewer may grant that row's role on this cohort.
  Neither shows on the viewer's own row.
- R22. **Change role** opens a dialog: a radio group of the cohort roles in `STAFF_ROLES` the
  viewer may grant on this cohort, with the current role selected. The title is a question, Cancel
  is focused, and the confirm text names before and after: "<name> will become <role> on
  <cohort> only." Confirming runs Decision 2. Choosing a role the person already holds on this
  cohort answers 422.
- R23. **Remove from cohort** opens a confirm dialog: "<name> will lose <role> on <cohort>. Their
  other roles are unchanged." Confirming calls `deactivate_role(grantor, user, role, cohort,
  reason=...)` for this cohort only. It uses the standard confirm variant, not `error`.
- R24. A row from an organisation-scoped role shows its role with "Whole organisation" and no
  buttons. In their place: "Set for the whole organisation. Change it in Staff." The link to the
  Staff section appears only when the viewer can open it.
- R25. Both new actions re-read the assignment on every request. An assignment that is inactive
  or gone answers `action_unavailable`, as `StaffRoleAction` does. Out-of-scope users and cohorts
  answer 404. Both refresh with `STAFF_CHANGED`.

### As-you-type search (Add-staff email step)

- R26. Typing two or more characters searches `staff_candidates(actor, organisation)` by first
  name, last name and email. Each word of the term must match one of the three (`icontains`).
  Results are ordered by name, capped at eight. Under two characters, the hint reads "Type at
  least 2 characters to search, or enter a full email." At the cap, a final non-selectable line
  reads "Showing the first 8. Keep typing to narrow."
- R27. Markup follows `comms/templates/comms/partials/recipient_picker.html`:
  `hx-trigger="input changed delay:300ms"`, `hx-sync="this:replace"`, and a
  `{% partialdef results %}` fragment. `educator_interface` does not import `comms` internals.
  Sharing the term-matching with `comms.messaging.search_recipients` through a neutral helper is
  the plan's call.
- R28. Each result is a person chip (`<c-panel-avatar-chip>`) showing name and email. Picking one
  fills the email and moves to the role step. When the candidate set is narrowed to visible staff,
  a note above the results reads "Searching staff you can add". No match: "No one matches
  '<term>'.", followed by "Type their full email to add someone else." for an actor holding
  `add_staffmember`.
- R29. The search endpoint carries the same capability as the dialog. A viewer without it gets
  403, not an empty list. Results expose name and email only.
- R30. Accessibility: the WAI-ARIA list-autocomplete combobox. The input has `role="combobox"`,
  `aria-autocomplete="list"`, `aria-controls`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-activedescendant`. Results
  are `role="option"` rows in a `role="listbox"` whose `id` is stable across swaps. Options are
  never `<a>` elements. Arrow keys move, Enter picks, Escape closes. A polite `role="status"`
  region outside the swapped fragment announces the result count, "No results" and loading.
  Options are at least 44 px tall.

## Testing

- `staff_of` includes a no-role `StaffMember`. `can()` still ignores grants without an active
  `StaffMember`, so no access is granted through the union.
- `add_staff_member`: an organisation admin succeeds, with one `staff_member.added` entry. A
  cohort admin is refused `NOT_PERMITTED`. An existing active member gets `ALREADY_STAFF`, and an
  inactive one `INACTIVE_STAFF_MEMBER`.
- `visible_staff` and `staff_candidates` for each actor kind. These include: a cohort admin
  without `organisation_staff_viewer` sees only staff on their cohorts, a learner who isn't staff
  is never in a cohort admin's set, and a user from another organisation or site is never in any
  set.
- Forged POSTs to the add, Change role and Remove from cohort endpoints answer the right status:
  a user outside `staff_candidates` (422 `NOT_VISIBLE`), a cohort outside scope (404), a role the
  actor may not grant (403), and the actor's own user (`SELF`).
- A cohort admin typing the full email of a learner, of an unknown address, and of staff they
  can't see gets the same 422 text, and no account is created.
- Change role writes `role.deactivated` then `role.assigned` in one transaction. A failure in the
  second step leaves the original role active.
- Remove from cohort deactivates only this cohort's assignment and leaves the same role on other
  cohorts.
- An organisation-scoped holder on the Settings tab has no buttons. A cohort admin opening
  Settings sees the Staff section only.
- `organisation_staff_viewer`: its holder opens the Staff section read-only and finds the whole
  organisation's staff when adding to their cohort. Only organisation and site admins can grant it.
- The search endpoint covers the minimum length, the cap of eight, word matching and the 403 for
  a viewer without the capability.
- `invariants/test_permission_matrix.py`, `test_config_authorisation.py` and
  `freedom_ls/panel_framework/tests/invariants/test_templates.py` pass with the new role, the new
  permissions and the new templates.
- Playwright (extend `educator_interface/tests/playwright/test_members_flow.py`), at 375, 768 and
  1280 px:
  - an organisation admin searches, picks a person and adds a cohort role;
  - the organisation admin adds a no-role staff member, then Add role from their row;
  - a cohort admin changes and removes a role from the Settings tab;
  - the combobox is driven by keyboard alone.

## Open questions

Assumed defaults. The user can overturn any of them.

1. A cohort admin's visible staff without `organisation_staff_viewer` is staff holding a role on a
   cohort where the cohort admin holds `assign_cohort_viewer`. It is not staff holding a role on
   any cohort the cohort admin can view.
2. The Settings tab opens for `assign_cohort_viewer` holders and shows them only the Staff
   section (R19). The alternative was a separate Staff tab.
3. A no-role add writes a new `staff_member.added` audit entry (R7). Without it, History would
   not show the add.
4. An organisation admin's search covers active `Learner` rows and active `StaffMember` rows
   only. Inactive people are reached by full email.

## Success criteria

- An organisation admin finds a person by typing part of their name, with no email remembered.
- An organisation admin adds a staff member with "No roles yet". They appear in Staff with "No
  roles yet" and an Add role button.
- One person can hold `cohort_admin` on Cohort A and `cohort_viewer` on Cohort B, set through the
  interface. Each Roles line names its cohorts.
- An organisation admin is downgraded to a cohort role through Add role and then Deactivate role,
  without losing access in between.
- A cohort admin changes a viewer to an admin on their cohort from the Settings tab. History shows
  `role.deactivated` then `role.assigned`.
- A cohort admin cannot create an account, add a learner who isn't staff, or add staff they can't
  see, through the UI or a forged POST.
- No search result ever names a user outside the actor's candidate set.
- Roles cells never read "by Not recorded".
