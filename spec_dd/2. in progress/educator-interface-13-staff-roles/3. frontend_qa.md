# Frontend QA: staff roles per cohort

Spec: `1. spec.md`. Plan: `2. plan.md`.

## Setup

- Start the server: `uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT`. Base URL: `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT`.
- Data: run `uv run python manage.py qa_create_staff_administration_scenario`. Its output lists
  every organisation, cohort (with `pk`) and persona. Re-run it before any test that needs the
  starting state back; it restores names, grants and staff rows.
- Every persona's password is its own email address. Log in at `/accounts/login/`. The superuser
  is `demodev@email.com` / `demodev@email.com`. Use a fresh private window, or log out, when a
  test switches persona.
- Personas in Northside (slug `northside`), all `@qa.test`:
  - `org.admin` Oscar and `org.admin2` Olga: organisation admins.
  - `lapsed.admin` Lars: organisation admin whose staff row is inactive.
  - `cohort.admin` Cora: cohort admin on Cohort A and Cohort B, no viewer role.
  - `viewing.admin` Vince: cohort admin on Cohort A and `organisation_staff_viewer`.
  - `roles.viewer` Rhea: `organisation_staff_roles_viewer` and nothing else.
  - `norole.staff` Nora: staff member with no roles.
  - `cohort.viewer` Vera: cohort viewer on Cohort A.
  - `many.viewer` Manny: cohort viewer on Cohort A, B, C and the inactive Cohort Old.
  - `viewer01` to `viewer12` (Vee One to Vee Twelve): cohort viewers on Cohort C.
  - `learner.only` Lena: a learner in Cohort A who is not staff.
  - `existing` Eve: an account with no role and no staff row.
  - `away` Avery: an account on another site.
- URLs: Staff is `/educator/northside/staff`. A cohort's Settings tab is
  `/educator/northside/cohorts/<pk>/__tabs/settings`, its History tab
  `…/__tabs/history`. The organisation's History is `/educator/northside/audit-log`.
- Viewports: 1280×800 (desktop) for every test; repeat Test 3, Test 5 and Test 7 at 375×812 and
  768×1024. At 375 nothing scrolls sideways (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth <=
  window.innerWidth`) and every dialog stays inside the viewport.

Record every finding with a screenshot in `qa-screenshots/`.

## Test 11: Panels, dialogs and columns stay consistent

Reference screenshots: `screenshots/fb-*.png`.

1. Dialogs, as Oscar on Staff: open Add staff member (email step), Add role on Nora (role step),
   a confirm (enter `existing@qa.test`, pick a role she does not hold, Next), the "This change
   wasn't made" answer (Test 2 step 3), and the discard prompt (pick a radio on Add role, press
   Escape). Every one is a header with the title and close control (the discard prompt has no
   close control), a body, and a footer. The header rule and the footer rule run the full width
   of the dialog. The footer's left button lines up with the body text and the right button with
   the close control. No dialog has a rule anywhere else.
2. Option lists: the Add role radios have no box around each choice and the search results have
   no line between people; choices and results are separated by space. An empty search shows
   only its message under the field's help text, with no empty box.
3. Staff table at 1280: four columns, Staff member, Roles, Role actions and Staff member actions,
   with nothing clipped at the right edge. Each role's Deactivate role and Change cohorts sit on
   that role's line. Every button label is on one line. At 375 each card lists the four parts,
   and nothing scrolls sideways.
4. Cohort A's Settings tab as Oscar: three separate panels, Name, Staff and Status, with no
   "Settings" heading inside any of them and no rule under any panel heading. The Staff rows line
   up in three columns at 1280 and 768, separated by space, with no line between rows.
5. A learner's Settings tab as the superuser: a Name panel with First name, Last name and Save on
   one row, and an Account panel with Deactivate (or Reactivate) in its footer. Saving a changed
   name keeps the page and shows the new name in the page header.
6. A learner's Messages tab as the superuser: a Messages panel, then an "Other educator
   conversations" panel. Neither panel has a rule under its heading or an uppercase sub-heading;
   the list separates conversations by space. Compare with the Courses tab: same panel heading
   style.

### Design checklist

- [ ] Every dialog: header, body, footer; full-width header and footer rules; footer buttons in
      line with the body text and the close control.
- [ ] No box around each option and no line between list items in a dialog or panel.
- [ ] One panel per job; no panel heading has a rule under it.
- [ ] Staff table: per-role buttons on their role's line; whole-person buttons in the last
      column; nothing clipped at 1280; button labels on one line.
- [ ] Cohort Settings staff rows in three aligned columns at 768 and 1280.
