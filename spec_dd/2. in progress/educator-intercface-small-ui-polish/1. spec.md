# Spec: educator interface small UI polish

Source: `idea.md`, with the answers in `answers.md`. Research: the `research_*.md` files beside
this spec, chiefly `research_codebase.md`.

## Purpose

The educator course page (`/educator/organisations/<org>/courses/<uuid>`) is one long stack of
panels with every outline part open. It lists cohort and direct registrations that the educator
cannot act on from the page. Previewing content means closing and reopening the modal for each
item, and a quiz preview says little about the quiz. Separately, button labels sit low inside
their buttons in the First Class theme.

## Scope

In:

- The course page split into two tabs, with the outline groups collapsed on load.
- Register and Deactivate (and Reactivate, where the learner page has it) on the course page's
  two registration panels, reusing the existing services.
- Previous and Next in the course content preview footer, in place of Close.
- Quiz facts and settings in a quiz form's top-level preview.
- Label centring in `.btn` buttons, fixed in FLS.
- The rule "a panel that lists a thing the educator can act on lets them act on it and links to
  it", in the skills.

Out:

- An Expand all / Collapse all control, and remembering expanded groups between visits.
- Keyboard arrow shortcuts and a position indicator ("3 of 12") in the preview.
- Learner results (attempts, pass rate, average score) in the quiz preview.
- Any change to the First Class repo. A cause found there becomes a note under Documentation and
  downstream.

## Decisions

- The tabs are "Overview" and "Content", as the cohort page names its first tab "Overview".
- "Remove a registration" is Deactivate: registrations are never deleted (`domain-glossary`:
  setting `is_active` to False is deactivating). Re-registering an inactive row reactivates it, as
  the existing services do.
- The course page's actions call the services the cohort and learner page actions call
  (`register_cohort_for_course`, `deactivate_cohort_registration`, `register_for_course`,
  `deactivate_registration`). They do not copy them.
- The Course content and Reference previews each step through their own list. Neither crosses
  into the other.
- Collapsing on load is an opt-in on `DataTable`. Other child-row tables keep opening their groups
  on load.

## Requirements

### Course page tabs (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/panels/course/instance_view.py`)

- `CourseInstanceView.panel` becomes a `CourseTabSet(TabSet)`, following `CohortTabSet` in
  `panels/cohort/instance_view.py`.
- The first tab, "Overview", is a `PanelStack` holding `CourseDetailsPanel`,
  `CourseCohortRegistrationsPanel` ("Cohort registrations") and `CourseLearnerRegistrationsPanel`
  ("Direct registrations"), in that order. It is the default tab.
- The second tab, "Content", is a `PanelStack` holding `CourseContentPanel` then
  `CourseReferencePanel`.
- The preview actions resolve their URLs under the new tab path. The `interface_walk.py` walk
  passes over both tabs.

### Outline groups start collapsed

- `DataTable` (`freedom_ls/panel_framework/tables.py`) gains a boolean opt-in, for example
  `child_rows_collapsed = False`. `CourseOutlineTable` and `ReferenceOutlineTable` set it to
  `True`.
- `cotton/data-table.html` passes the flag to the `childRows` Alpine component as a data
  attribute, because the CSP build passes no arguments. `childRows` in
  `panel_framework/static/panel_framework/js/alpine-components.js` seeds `collapsed` for every
  group row when the flag is set. `partials/child_rows_toggle.html` renders `aria-expanded` to
  match the initial state. This applies to the table and to the card list (`cards_when_narrow`).
- The server render without JavaScript still shows every row.
- An htmx swap of the table region starts collapsed again. This is the existing behaviour,
  inverted.

### Registration actions on the course page

Files: `panels/course/data_table_panels.py`, `panels/course/data_tables.py`, a new
`panels/course/actions.py`, and new pickers in `freedom_ls/educator_interface/forms.py`.

- **Cohort registrations panel:**
  - A footer Register action opens a picker over the cohorts the educator may register: visible
    to them (`cohorts_visible_to`), active, and holding no active `CohortCourseRegistration` for
    this course, plus inactive ones, which the service reactivates. It calls
    `register_cohort_for_course(cohort, course, actor=…, reason=…)`.
  - Each active row has a Deactivate action with the same confirmation and reason as
    `DeactivateCohortRegistrationAction`, calling `deactivate_cohort_registration`. The cohort name
    keeps its link to the cohort page.
- **Direct registrations panel:**
  - A footer Register action opens a picker over the learners visible to the educator
    (`learners_visible_to`) in the organisation in view who hold no active
    `LearnerCourseRegistration` for this course. It calls `register_for_course`.
  - Active rows have Deactivate and inactive rows have Reactivate, with the confirmations of
    `DeactivateRegistrationAction` and `ReactivateRegistrationAction`, including the "still has
    access through cohort X" text from `other_grants_for`. The learner cell keeps its link to the
    learner page.
- **Shared plumbing:** move the confirmation contexts, templates and reason handling the course
  actions share with the cohort and learner actions into `panels/common/` rather than copying
  them.
- **Permissions:** a capability check on a `Course` scope raises `TypeError` in
  `learner_management/capabilities.py` `can()`. Every new action therefore overrides
  `PanelAction.permission_object`:
  - The footer Register action uses the request's organisation.
  - A row action uses the row's cohort, or the row's learner.
  - A row the educator cannot act on shows its fields with an empty actions cell.
- **Course availability:** the Register actions are hidden when the course is not open to the
  organisation (`is_course_available_to_organisation`). The services' `CourseNotOpenError` is
  still answered with a 422 form error.
- **Refresh:** both panels set `refresh_events = (REGISTRATION_CHANGED, …)` as
  `CohortCoursesPanel` does, and each action fires the events its cohort or learner counterpart
  fires.
- **Look:** buttons follow `fls-dev:panel-framework`. Register is the panel's one `primary`. Row
  buttons are small `secondary` buttons inside `<c-data-table-actions>`, with `aria_label`s that
  name their object ("Deactivate Cohort A").

### Preview Previous and Next (`panels/course/content_preview.py`, `modal/content_preview.html`, `modal/course_details_preview.html`)

- One function next to `outline_rows`/`reference_rows` returns a list's preview sequence. It walks
  the rows depth first and keeps every row that previews: the Course details row first (Course
  content only), then each topic, form and form page. It skips part rows and note rows.
- `PreviewContentAction` and `PreviewCourseDetailsAction` add `previous_url` and `next_url` to
  their context, built from the current `(item, page)`:
  - In Course content, the sequence starts at Course details and ends at the last item or page.
  - In Reference (`course_list="reference"`), the sequence is the reference rows only.
- The footer holds Previous at the left and Next at the right. Each is an `hx-get` into
  `#app-modal-body`, as `<c-modal-trigger>` is. On the first item Previous renders `disabled`, and
  on the last item Next does. Both stay in place so the footer does not shift.
- The footer has no Close button. `cotton/modal-footer.html` gains an option that leaves out its
  cancel button. The header X (`closable`), Esc and a backdrop click still close the modal.
- After a step, focus goes to the new content's title (`focus_title`). It never drops to
  `<body>`.
- Building the sequence adds no queries beyond those the outline already makes (`_course_list_items`,
  `_prefetch_form_pages`).

### Quiz top-level preview

- When the previewed form is a quiz (`Form.strategy == FormStrategy.QUIZ`) and the row is the
  form's own row, not a page row, the preview shows a facts and settings block under the intro:
  - Pages and questions (`total_pages`, `question_count`, already in `_form_preview_context`).
  - Pass mark (`quiz_pass_percentage`, or "None set").
  - Shows incorrect answers (`quiz_show_incorrect`).
  - Submits on exit (`submit_on_exit`).
  - Shuffle pages, questions and options (`shuffle_pages`, `shuffle_questions`,
    `shuffle_options`).
- The block uses `<c-panel-definition-list>`. On a multi-page quiz it replaces the plain
  `form_engine/partials/form_facts.html` counts.

### Button label centring

- In the First Class theme, a `.btn` label's vertical centre is within 1px of the button's centre.
  This holds for every variant and size, including `btn-sm`, buttons in data-table rows and
  buttons in `[data-modal-footer]`, whether the brand font (DM Sans) is installed or the fallback
  renders.
- Before choosing a fix, measure the cause in a browser. Candidates from the code:
  - The theme names DM Sans and Outfit (`themes/first_class/.../theme.css`) with no `@font-face`,
    so a locally installed DM Sans renders with its own vertical metrics.
  - The first_class `.btn` override (`py-3 font-semibold`) sits against the `btn-sm` and
    data-table `py-1.5` rules.
- Fix at one source: `theme.css`, `.btn` in `tailwind.components.css`, and the duplicate
  `.panel-btn` in `panel_framework/css/panel_framework.css` changed in step, as
  `test_framework_copies_match_base` pins. Never patch individual buttons.

## Testing

- The course page renders two tabs, Overview and Content, holding the panels in the order above.
  Update `tests/panels/course/test_instance_view.py`.
- The outline tables start with every group collapsed, with `aria-expanded="false"` on the toggle.
  A table without the flag still starts open. Extend
  `panel_framework/tests/components/test_data_table.py`, and keep
  `test_every_expand_chevron_turns_when_its_control_opens` passing.
- For each new action:
  - Permission is allowed or refused for an organisation role, a cohort-only role and no role.
    Add the actions to `tests/invariants/test_permission_matrix.py`.
  - The picker excludes already-active registrations and inactive cohorts.
  - Register is hidden when the course is not open to the organisation.
  - A 422 comes back on `CourseNotOpenError`.
  - Deactivate and Reactivate write the same audit entries as their cohort and learner
    counterparts.
- The preview sequence covers a course with parts, a multi-page form, a course with a single item,
  an empty part and the Reference list. Previous is disabled on Course details, and Next on the
  last page. Add a query-count assertion in `tests/panels/course/test_content_preview.py`.
- The quiz facts block shows for a quiz form's own row and is absent for a non-quiz form and for a
  quiz page row.
- Playwright (`tests/playwright/test_course_preview_flow.py`):
  - Step forward across a part boundary and back.
  - The footer has no Close, and Esc and the header X close the modal.
  - Focus lands on the title after each step.
  - Register and deactivate a cohort and a learner from the course page.
- Playwright: label centring for `.btn` variants within 1px, next to the height checks in
  `tests/playwright/test_touch_targets.py`.
- The panel-framework template invariants (`panel_framework/tests/invariants/test_templates.py`)
  accept the stepping footer and still reject a hand-written button group.

## Documentation and downstream

- `docs/product/educator-interface.md`: the course page's tabs, its registration actions and the
  preview's Previous and Next.
- If the measurement puts the label cause in the First Class repo, record it in the upgrade notes
  as a change for that repo. Its current code holds no button, font or line-height override.
- If the fix ships the brand fonts or their metric overrides, update `docs/how tos/theme-fls.md`.

## Success criteria

- Opening a course page shows the Overview tab with details, Cohort registrations and Direct
  registrations. The Content tab shows Course content then Reference, with every group collapsed.
- An educator with an organisation role registers, deactivates and reactivates a cohort and a
  learner from the course page, and the panel refreshes in place.
- From any preview, Next and Previous reach every preview in the list in outline order. They are
  disabled at the two ends, and the footer has no Close.
- A quiz form's top-level preview lists its page and question counts, pass mark and the five
  settings.
- The label-centring Playwright check passes in the First Class theme.
- Both skills carry the act-on-it-and-link-to-it rule and describe the new course page.
