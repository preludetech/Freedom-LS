# Spec: course shares between organisations

Idea: `idea.md`. Decisions taken with the user: `answers.md`. Research: `research_*.md` in this
directory.

## Purpose

An organisation course with `audience: public` is open to every organisation, so every
organisation's educators see it on their Courses page, open its content previews, and register
their own learners and cohorts onto it. Educator registration never consults the course-access
backend, so any organisation can hand out a paid or application-gated course for free.

After this work an educator reaches another organisation's course only when an owning organisation
has shared it with their organisation in `course.md`, and only as far as the share allows.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| owning organisation | coined | Any `Organisation` listed in `Course.organisations`; a course may have several. Narrows the glossary's "a course is not owned by one" (the `peer` row) for organisation courses only. |
| course share | coined | One `CourseShare` row: an owning organisation lets one other named organisation `see`, `register` or `preview` the course. Not "grant", which the glossary reserves for role assignments, and not "audience", which `Course.audience` holds. |
| `see` / `register` / `preview` | coined | What a course share allows. `register` and `preview` each imply `see`. "Visible" in `courses_visible_to` and the expressions below means `see`, not `Course.visibility`. |

## Scope

In:

- A `shared_with:` front-matter key on `course.md`, loaded into a new `CourseShare` model.
- The educator interface for organisation courses: the Courses list, the course detail page and its
  panels, the content preview actions, the course filters on the cohort and learner lists, every
  course picker that registers a learner or cohort (single and bulk), and registration
  reactivation.
- The registration write gate, so a `register` share lets that organisation hold registrations on
  a course that is not public.
- Author tooling, demo content, product docs and the glossary.

Out:

- Learner-facing behaviour: browsing, the audience rule (`in_audience_expression`), self-join of
  public courses through the course-access backend, and the embed card. All unchanged.
- Courses with no `organisations:`. They keep today's behaviour for educators and learners.
- Seat caps or counts on a `register` share.
- Wildcard shares. Every share names one organisation.
- Badges or columns marking shared courses in the Courses list.

## Decisions

- **No share means hidden** (`research_cross_org_course_sharing.md`).
- **Three independent flags, not a ladder.** An owner can allow `register` without `preview`.
- **A `register` share opens the course to registration by that organisation's educators.**
  Educator registration through it skips the course's price and application gate.
- **The loader writes shares to a table.** `course.md` stays the source of truth.
- **The learner write gate is widened, never narrowed.** `is_course_open_to_organisation` also
  decides learner self-join (`research_codebase.md` §1); narrowing it would break self-join of
  public courses. The educator rule is enforced on the educator side instead.
- **Pages and actions deny with 404**, as `CourseConfig.authorise_instance` already does. The single
  register forms reject an out-of-scope course as a 422 form error, as they do today.

## Requirements

### What each organisation reaches

For an educator acting in organisation O, a course C is:

| C is… | listed and detail page | content preview | registerable (pickers, reactivation) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a course with no `organisations:` | yes | yes | yes |
| owned by O | yes | yes | yes |
| owned by others, shared with O allowing `preview` | yes | yes | only with `register` |
| owned by others, shared with O allowing `register` | yes | only with `preview` | yes |
| owned by others, shared with O allowing `see` only | yes | no | no |
| owned by others, not shared with O, but O holds a registration on it | yes | no | no |
| owned by others, not shared with O, no registration held by O | no | no | no |

`audience` plays no part in this table. Course visibility (`published`, `hidden`, `coming_soon`)
narrows the listing and registerable columns exactly as it does today (`courses_visible_to`,
`registerable_courses`); content preview has no visibility term. "O holds a registration" keeps
today's meaning: a registration, active or not, of a cohort or learner visible to the educator in O.

### `CourseShare` model and loader

- New `CourseShare(SiteAwareModel)` in `freedom_ls/content_engine/models/courses.py`: `course` (FK
  `Course`, `related_name="shares"`, cascade), `organisation` (FK `Organisation`,
  `related_name="course_shares"`, cascade), booleans `see`, `register`, `preview`. Constraint
  `unique_course_share_per_organisation` on `(course, organisation)`. A `see`-only row is legal.
- `content_engine/schema.py`: a `CourseShareEntry` sub-model `{organisation: str, allow:
  list[Literal["see", "register", "preview"]]}` and an optional `shared_with: list[CourseShareEntry]`
  on `Course`. Front matter:

  ```yaml
  organisations:
    - northwind-logistics
  audience: public
  shared_with:
    - organisation: harbour-college
      allow: [register]
  ```

- A schema `model_validator` rejects `shared_with` on a course with no `organisations:`, an owning
  organisation listed in `shared_with`, the same organisation twice, and an empty `allow`.
- `content_save.save_course` resolves the slugs through `_resolve_course_organisations`, so an
  unknown slug fails the load with its existing `ValueError`. It adds `shared_with` to
  `exclude_fields` and replaces the course's `CourseShare` rows on every load, through
  `CourseShare._base_manager` with `site=site` (the loader runs with no request). Rows get
  `see=True` whenever `register` or `preview` is set; a removed entry or key deletes its row.
- `CourseAdmin` (`content_engine/admin.py`) shows the course's shares read-only beside
  `organisation_names`.

### Rules in `freedom_ls/learner_management/queries.py`

- Three expressions, each an `Exists` over `CourseShare._base_manager` filtered on the organisation
  (which pins the site) and the flag, OR-ed with "no organisations, or owned by this organisation":
  `visible_to_organisation_expression(organisation)` (tests `see=True`),
  `registerable_by_organisation_expression(organisation)` (tests `register=True`) and
  `previewable_by_organisation_expression(organisation)` (tests `preview=True`). Single-course forms
  go through `_course_matches`, as `is_course_open_to_organisation` does.
- `courses_visible_to` replaces `open_to_organisation_expression` with
  `visible_to_organisation_expression`; `held`, the superuser branch and the subquery structure stay.
  Superusers follow the same rule as everyone else, as today.
- `registerable_courses` (and so `registerable_courses_for`, `registerable_courses_for_learner` and
  `CoursePickerMixin`) uses `registerable_by_organisation_expression`.
- `is_course_open_to_organisation` becomes `open_to_organisation_expression` OR an `Exists` over
  `CourseShare` with `register=True` for the organisation. `require_course_open_to`,
  `validate_course_open_to`, the registration models' `clean()` and registration rules follow it.
- `open_to_everyone_expression`, `in_audience_expression` and `is_in_audience` do not change.

### Educator interface (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/`)

- `CourseDataTable.empty_message` becomes "Courses available to your organisation will appear here."
- `CourseContentPanel` reads the organisation from
  `cast(OrganisationScopedRequest, self.request).organisation`. When the course is not previewable
  by it, `get_context_data` skips `outline_rows`, `get_actions` returns no preview actions, and
  `panels/course_content.html` renders "Content preview isn't shared with your organisation."
  instead of the outline.
- `PreviewContentAction` and `PreviewCourseDetailsAction` raise `Http404` when the course is not
  previewable by the organisation, whatever the URL's `item` or `page`.
- `ReactivateRegistrationAction` (`actions.py`), and any other educator action that registers
  without going through a course picker, refuses a course that is not registerable by the
  organisation with `COURSE_NOT_OPEN_MESSAGE`.
- `CourseDataTable`, `CourseConfig.authorise_instance`, `VisibleCourseFilter` and
  `LearnerCourseFilter` take the change through `courses_visible_to`.

## Testing

- **The matrix.** Parametrised over every row of the table above × each surface, with the expected
  result per cell: the Courses list, the detail URL (404), the content panel, both preview action
  URLs (404), the course filter choices, the bulk register pickers (404 on a tampered target), the
  single cohort and learner register forms (422 on a tampered course), and reactivation (refused).
- **Write gate.** A `register` share on an `organisation_learners` course lets an educator register
  a cohort and a learner of the shared-with organisation, and model `clean()` accepts the result.
  Without the share it is refused with `COURSE_NOT_OPEN_MESSAGE`.
- **Learner side unchanged.** A learner of another organisation still self-joins a public
  organisation course through the course-access backend, gates applied; the course is still in
  their browse list. A learner registered by an educator through a `register` share reaches the
  content, under both the free backend and `ApplicationCourseAccessBackend`.
- **Revoking a share.** Removing a `shared_with` entry and reloading deletes the `CourseShare` row,
  keeps every registration and progress record, keeps the course listed for the organisation through
  its held registrations, removes it from the pickers, and refuses reactivation of its registrations.
- **Loader.** Each schema validator error, the unknown-slug error, `see` implied by `register` and
  `preview`, replacement on reload, rows carrying the course's site, and demo content loading.
- **Query count.** The Courses list issues the same number of queries for 1 and 10 shared courses.
- **Existing tests.** Tests asserting the old rule are amended with a share row in their fixtures,
  not deleted: `TestCoursesVisibleToOrganisationCourses` and `TestRegisterableCoursesOpenToOrganisation`
  in `learner_management/tests/test_queries.py` (and the `open_to_organisation_expression` filter
  near line 2380), and the "Most courses are shared across organisations" arrangement in
  `educator_interface/tests/invariants/test_organisation_isolation.py`.
  `test_a_public_course_of_another_organisation_is_open` stays true: it tests the write gate.

## Open questions

- Q: Should courses with no `organisations:` that are paid or application-gated also be hidden from
  educators or withheld from registration? → A: No; they keep today's behaviour. (assumed default:
  the user scoped the change to organisation courses, and such a course has no owner to share it.
  Overrule by editing Scope.)
- Q: Once a `register` share is revoked, should the bulk deactivate picker still offer the course so
  educators can deactivate the registrations they hold? → A: No; it keeps using
  `registerable_courses`, as it does today for a course that closes, and deactivation is per-row
  from the learner or cohort page. (assumed default: matches current behaviour for closed courses.
  Overrule by editing "Rules in queries.py".)

## Success criteria

- An educator in Harbour College does not see Northwind's public course without a share: list, filter
  and pickers omit it, and its detail and preview URLs return 404.
- With `allow: [register]` they see it, register a cohort onto it, and its preview URLs return 404.
- With `allow: [preview]` they open its content previews and it is absent from the pickers.
- After the share is removed and content reloaded, a Harbour College cohort's existing registration
  and progress remain, the course stays listed for Harbour College, and reactivating a deactivated
  registration on it is refused.
- A Harbour College learner registered by an educator through a `register` share opens the course
  content; a Harbour College learner with no registration still self-joins Northwind's public course.
- A load with an unknown, duplicate or owning organisation in `shared_with` fails with a message
  naming the course file.
