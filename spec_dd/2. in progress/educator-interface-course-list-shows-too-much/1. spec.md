# Spec: course shares between organisations

Idea: `idea.md`. Decisions taken with the user: `answers.md`. Research: `research_*.md` in this
directory.

## Purpose

An organisation course with `audience: public` is open to every organisation, so every
organisation's educators see it on their Courses page, open its content previews, and register
their own learners and cohorts onto it. Educator registration never consults the course-access
backend, so any organisation can hand out a paid or application-gated course for free.

After this work an educator reaches another organisation's course only when an owning organisation
has shared it with their organisation in `course.md`.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| owning organisation | coined | Any `Organisation` listed in `Course.organisations`; a course may have several. Narrows the glossary's "a course is not owned by one" (the `peer` row) for organisation courses only. |
| course share | coined | An owning organisation names one other organisation in `shared_with:`. That organisation's educators then use the course as the owner's do. Not "grant", which the glossary reserves for role assignments, and not "audience", which `Course.audience` holds. |
| available to an organisation | coined | The course has no `organisations:`, or the organisation owns it, or it is shared with the organisation. |

## Worked example

The content this rule was checked against has two organisations.

- `learnfirstclasstraining` owns three practical primers. They are `audience: public` and will carry
  a price or gate for the public. Each lists `rpas-training-academy` in `shared_with:`, so RPAS
  educators see the primers, preview them, and register their own learners at no cost.
- `learnfirstclasstraining` also owns application-gated qualification courses, renewals, business
  courses and a free introduction, all `audience: public` and none shared. RPAS educators reach none
  of them. Learners of any organisation still join them through each course's own gates.
- `rpas-training-academy` owns two `organisation_learners` theory courses and shares neither.
  `learnfirstclasstraining` educators reach neither.

## Scope

In:

- A `shared_with:` front-matter key on `course.md`, loaded into a new `Course.shared_with` relation.
- The educator interface for organisation courses: the Courses list, the course detail page and its
  panels, the content preview actions, the course filters on the cohort and learner lists, every
  course picker that registers a learner or cohort (single and bulk), and registration
  reactivation.
- The registration write gate, so a share lets that organisation hold registrations on a course
  that is not public.
- Author tooling, demo content, product docs and the glossary.

Out:

- Learner-facing behaviour: browsing, the audience rule (`in_audience_expression`), self-join of
  public courses through the course-access backend, and the embed card. All unchanged. A learner of
  a shared-with organisation who joins on their own meets the course's price and gates like anyone
  else.
- Courses with no `organisations:`. They keep today's behaviour for educators and learners.
- Partial shares (see without register, register without preview). A share is all or nothing.
- Seat caps or counts on a share.
- Wildcard shares. Every share names one organisation.
- Badges or columns marking shared courses in the Courses list. A course another organisation owns
  carries a muted "Shared by <organisation>" line under its title instead (see "Course names").

## Decisions

- **No share means hidden** (`research_cross_org_course_sharing.md`).
- **One kind of share.** A shared-with organisation's educators see, preview and register, the same
  as the owner's. No real course needed less, so there are no per-share flags.
- **Educator registration through a share skips the course's price and application gate.** The
  share is the licence.
- **Shares live in a many-to-many beside `organisations`.** `course.md` stays the source of truth.
- **The learner write gate is widened, never narrowed.** `is_course_open_to_organisation` also
  decides learner self-join (`research_codebase.md` §1); narrowing it would break self-join of
  public courses. The educator rule is enforced on the educator side instead.
- **Pages and actions deny with 404**, as `CourseConfig.authorise_instance` already does. The single
  register forms reject an out-of-scope course as a 422 form error, as they do today.

## Requirements

### What each organisation reaches

For an educator acting in organisation O, a course C is:

| C is… | listed and detail page | content preview | registerable (pickers, reactivation) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a course with no `organisations:` | yes | yes | yes |
| owned by O | yes | yes | yes |
| owned by others, shared with O | yes | yes | yes |
| owned by others, not shared with O, but O holds a registration on it | yes | no | no |
| owned by others, not shared with O, no registration held by O | no | no | no |

`audience` plays no part in this table. Course visibility (`published`, `hidden`, `coming_soon`)
narrows the listing and registerable columns exactly as it does today (`courses_visible_to`,
`registerable_courses`); content preview has no visibility term. "O holds a registration" keeps
today's meaning: a registration, active or not, of a cohort or learner visible to the educator in O.

### Model and loader

- `Course` (`freedom_ls/content_engine/models/courses.py`) gains `shared_with`, a
  `ManyToManyField` to `Organisation` with `blank=True` and `related_name="shared_courses"`,
  declared beside `organisations`.
- `content_engine/schema.py`: an optional `shared_with: list[str]` on `Course`, holding organisation
  slugs. Front matter:

  ```yaml
  organisations:
    - northwind-logistics
  audience: public
  shared_with:
    - harbour-college
  ```

- A schema `model_validator` rejects `shared_with` on a course with no `organisations:`, an owning
  organisation listed in `shared_with`, and the same organisation twice.
- `save_course` (`content_engine/management/commands/content_save.py`) resolves the slugs the way
  `_resolve_course_organisations` resolves `organisations`, so an unknown slug fails the load with
  the same `ValueError`. It adds `shared_with` to `exclude_fields` and sets the relation on every
  load; a removed entry or key removes the share.
- `CourseAdmin` (`content_engine/admin.py`) shows the shared-with organisations read-only beside
  `organisation_names`, and prefetches them.

### Rules in `freedom_ls/learner_management/queries.py`

- `available_to_organisation_expression(organisation)`: not an organisation course, or an `Exists`
  over `Course.organisations.through` for the organisation, or an `Exists` over
  `Course.shared_with.through` for the organisation. `is_course_available_to_organisation` is its
  single-course form, through `_course_matches`.
- `courses_visible_to` replaces `open_to_organisation_expression` with
  `available_to_organisation_expression`; `held`, the superuser branch and the subquery structure
  stay. Superusers follow the same rule as everyone else, as today.
- `registerable_courses` (and so `registerable_courses_for`, `registerable_courses_for_learner` and
  `CoursePickerMixin`) uses `available_to_organisation_expression`.
- `open_to_organisation_expression` gains the same `shared_with` `Exists`, so
  `is_course_open_to_organisation`, `require_course_open_to`, `validate_course_open_to`, the
  registration models' `clean()` and registration rules accept a shared `organisation_learners`
  course.
- `open_to_everyone_expression`, `in_audience_expression` and `is_in_audience` do not change.

### Educator interface (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/`)

- `CourseDataTable.empty_message` becomes "Courses available to your organisation will appear here."
- `CourseContentPanel` reads the organisation from
  `cast(OrganisationScopedRequest, self.request).organisation`. When the course is not available to
  it (the held-registration row), `get_context_data` skips `outline_rows`, `get_actions` returns no
  preview actions, and `panels/course_content.html` renders "Content preview isn't shared with your
  organisation." instead of the outline.
- `PreviewContentAction` and `PreviewCourseDetailsAction` raise `Http404` when the course is not
  available to the organisation, whatever the URL's `item` or `page`.
- `ReactivateRegistrationAction` (`actions.py`), and any other educator action that registers
  without going through a course picker, refuses a course that is not available to the organisation
  with `COURSE_NOT_OPEN_MESSAGE`.
- `CourseDataTable`, `CourseConfig.authorise_instance`, `VisibleCourseFilter` and
  `LearnerCourseFilter` take the change through `courses_visible_to`.

### Course names

A shared course can carry the same title as one the organisation owns (the demo's two "Workplace
Safety Essentials"). An educator tells them apart by the owner, named only on a course the viewing
organisation does not own:

- One helper on `Course` takes the viewing organisation and returns the owning organisations other
  than it, comma-separated, or nothing when the viewing organisation owns the course or the course
  has no owning organisation. Every surface below labels through it.
- Picker options, filter choices and dialog titles read "Workplace Safety Essentials (Northwind
  Logistics)": the course filters on the cohort and learner lists (`VisibleCourseFilter`,
  `LearnerCourseFilter`), the cohort and learner register forms, the bulk register target picker,
  and the register dialog titles and confirm prose.
- Table cells show the title with a muted "Shared by Northwind Logistics" line under it: the Courses
  list Title column and the cohort and learner registration tables.
- Harbour College's own Workplace Safety Essentials, and every course with no `organisations:`,
  shows its title alone.
- The Courses list prefetches `organisations`, so the label adds no query per row.

## Testing

- **The matrix.** Parametrised over every row of the table above × each surface, with the expected
  result per cell: the Courses list, the detail URL (404), the content panel, both preview action
  URLs (404), the course filter choices, the bulk register pickers (404 on a tampered target), the
  single cohort and learner register forms (422 on a tampered course), and reactivation (refused).
- **Write gate.** A share on an `organisation_learners` course lets an educator register a cohort
  and a learner of the shared-with organisation, and model `clean()` accepts the result. Without
  the share it is refused with `COURSE_NOT_OPEN_MESSAGE`.
- **Learner side unchanged.** A learner of another organisation still self-joins a public
  organisation course through the course-access backend, gates applied, shared or not; the course is
  still in their browse list. A learner registered by an educator through a share reaches the
  content, under both the free backend and `ApplicationCourseAccessBackend`.
- **Revoking a share.** Removing a `shared_with` entry and reloading removes the share, keeps every
  registration and progress record, keeps the course listed for the organisation through its held
  registrations, removes it from the pickers, 404s its preview URLs, and refuses reactivation of its
  registrations.
- **Loader.** Each schema validator error, the unknown-slug error, replacement on reload, and demo
  content loading.
- **Query count.** The Courses list issues the same number of queries for 1 and 10 shared courses.
- **Existing tests.** Tests asserting the old rule are amended with a share in their fixtures, not
  deleted: `TestCoursesVisibleToOrganisationCourses` and `TestRegisterableCoursesOpenToOrganisation`
  in `learner_management/tests/test_queries.py` (and the `open_to_organisation_expression` filter
  near line 2380), and the "Most courses are shared across organisations" arrangement in
  `educator_interface/tests/invariants/test_organisation_isolation.py`.
  `test_a_public_course_of_another_organisation_is_open` stays true: it tests the write gate.

  registration add shares first.

## Open questions

- Q: Should courses with no `organisations:` that are paid or application-gated also be hidden from
  educators or withheld from registration? → A: No; they keep today's behaviour. (assumed default:
  the user scoped the change to organisation courses, and such a course has no owner to share it.
  Overrule by editing Scope.)
- Q: Once a share is revoked, should the bulk deactivate picker still offer the course so educators
  can deactivate the registrations they hold? → A: No; it keeps using `registerable_courses`, as it
  does today for a course that closes, and deactivation is per-row from the learner or cohort page.
  (assumed default: matches current behaviour for closed courses. Overrule by editing "Rules in
  queries.py".)

## Success criteria

- An educator in Harbour College does not see Northwind's unshared public course: list, filter and
  pickers omit it, and its detail and preview URLs return 404.
- With Harbour College in `shared_with:` they see the course, open its content previews and
  register a cohort onto it.
- After the share is removed and content reloaded, a Harbour College cohort's existing registration
  and progress remain, the course stays listed for Harbour College, its preview URLs return 404, and
  reactivating a deactivated registration on it is refused.
- A Harbour College learner registered by an educator through a share opens the course content; a
  Harbour College learner with no registration still self-joins Northwind's public course through
  its gates.
- A load with an unknown, duplicate or owning organisation in `shared_with` fails with a message
  naming the course file.
- A Harbour College educator tells the two Workplace Safety Essentials apart in the Courses list,
  the course filters and the register pickers: Northwind's reads "(Northwind Logistics)" or
  "Shared by Northwind Logistics", Harbour's own shows the title alone.
