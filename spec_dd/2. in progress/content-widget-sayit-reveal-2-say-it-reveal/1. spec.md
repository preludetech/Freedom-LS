# Spec: say it, then reveal (`c-audio-reveal`)

Idea: `idea.md`. Answers: `answers.md`. Research: `research_*.md` in this directory, and
`research_content_media.md` and `research_audio_widgets.md` in
`spec_dd/1. next/content-widget-sayit-reveal/`. Builds on `c-audio` from
`spec_dd/3. done/2026-11-19_08:05_content-widget-sayit-reveal-1-audio-player/`.

## Purpose

The phonetic-alphabet course needs learners to practise recall, not just listen. This spec adds
`c-audio-reveal`. The learner sees a prompt tile ("J"), says the answer aloud and clicks the
hidden-answer area. The answer ("Juliett") appears and its audio file plays. The spec also makes nested widgets,
`c-audio` among them, work inside the six widgets whose body is markdown, so a course can practise
with `c-flashcard` faces that play sound.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| slot widget | coined | A cotton content widget whose body is author markdown: `c-accordion`, `c-admonition`, `c-card`, `c-flashcard`, `c-pull-quote`, `c-table`. Not `c-slot`, which is a named slot inside `c-flashcard`. No existing term; the templates say "the body is the default slot, rendered as markdown". |

## Scope

In:

- The `c-audio-reveal` cotton component, its Alpine component and its allow-list entry.
- One markdown pass for slot widget bodies, so any widget nests inside any slot widget.
- A `c-audio` play button in the showing `c-flashcard` face plays without flipping the card.
- Demo content, authoring docs and upgrade notes.

Out:

- Building `c-audio`. Scheduled flashcards, self-rating and spaced repetition
  (`learner-interface-features-4-flashcards`,
  `content-widget-sayit-reveal-3-audio-on-revision-flashcards`).
- Microphone, speech recognition, recording, storing anything about the learner.
- Shuffling a set of prompts, and reveal-all or hide-all controls.
- Playing a clip when a `c-flashcard` flips. The reveal click is the only autoplay.
- A Hide control. The answer hides itself on a timer.
- Changing the `markdown` template tag in `content_tags.py`.

## Decisions

- **`symbol` is the prompt, so it is required here**, though optional on `c-audio` (`answers.md`).
- **Reveal plays from the start and never stops.** `audioFile.toggle()` stops a clip that is already
  playing, which would make a quick reveal after an auto-hide go silent.
- **The hidden answer's own space is the reveal control; there is no Reveal or Hide button**
  (user feedback after QA, overriding the disclosure button in `answers.md`). While hidden, the
  answer region's space shows a subdued eye-closed icon with a pointer cursor; clicking it reveals.
- **The answer hides itself 5 seconds after its audio stops.** Pressing play again restarts the
  countdown, so it never hides mid-clip. Focus moves to the play button on reveal and back to the
  reveal control on hide, because each of them turns inert in the other state.
- **Slot widgets emit their slot as-is, as `c-grid` does** (`answers.md`). The second
  `{% markdown slot %}` pass re-converted rendered widget markup and turned a nested `c-audio`
  button into escaped text.
- **The pointer-events rule matches every `button` in a flashcard face**, so `c-audio`,
  `c-audio-reveal` and the picture lightbox need one rule between them.

## Requirements

### Authoring syntax

```html
<c-audio-reveal src="audio/juliett.mp3" symbol="J" label="Juliett" respelling="JOO-lee-ett"></c-audio-reveal>
```

| Attribute | Required | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `src` | yes | Path to an audio file, relative to the markdown file, resolved as `c-audio` does. |
| `symbol` | yes | The prompt, shown in the tile before and after reveal. |
| `label` | yes | The answer word, shown on reveal and used in the play button's accessible name. |
| `respelling` | no | Shown under the label on reveal, with stress marked as in `c-audio`. |

- `config/settings_base.py` `MARKDOWN_ALLOWED_TAGS` gains
  `"c-audio-reveal": {"src", "label", "respelling", "symbol"}`.
- `base/tests/invariants/test_app_templates_use_app_components.py` `BANNED_TAGS` gains
  `"c-audio-reveal"` beside `"c-audio"`.

### Shared card body: `freedom_ls/content_engine/templates/content_engine/partials/audio_card_body.html`

- `audio.html` and `audio-reveal.html` both `{% include %}` this partial for the text column (label
  and respelling) and the play button, so the markup exists once. The error line stays in each
  root, after the card. It takes `label`, `respelling` and `file_obj`, passed explicitly with
  `with … only`. The including root supplies `x-id="['audio-respelling']"`.
- `c-audio`'s rendered output is unchanged and its existing tests pass as they are.

### Component: `freedom_ls/content_engine/templates/cotton/audio-reveal.html`

- `<c-vars src="" label="" respelling="" symbol="" />`; resolve the file as `audio.html` does.
- Red boxes, using `c-content-error` as `audio.html` does: `Audio not found: {{ src }}`,
  `Audio needs a label: {{ src }}`, and `Audio reveal needs a symbol: {{ src }}`.
- Otherwise one root with `my-6`, `x-data="audioReveal"`,
  `x-id="['audio-respelling', 'audio-answer']"` and
  `data-src="{% url 'content_engine:audio_file' file_obj.pk %}"`, holding one card styled like
  `c-audio`'s:
  - the symbol tile, visible in both states. Unlike `c-audio`'s tile it is not `aria-hidden`,
    because here it is the prompt;
  - the answer region, `x-bind:id="$id('audio-answer')"`, holding the shared card body. While hidden
    it carries `invisible`, `x-bind:inert` and `x-bind:aria-hidden`, so it is neither seen,
    focusable nor announced, and it keeps its height, so a list never shifts on reveal;
  - the reveal control: a `<button>` stacked in the same grid cell as the answer region, filling
    it, at least 44 px tall. It shows the `reveal_hidden` (eye-closed) icon in `text-muted` with
    `cursor-pointer`, and has the visually hidden name "Reveal answer",
    `x-bind:aria-expanded="revealed"`, `x-bind:aria-controls="$id('audio-answer')"` and
    `x-on:click="reveal"`. While revealed it is `invisible` and inert. There is no separate
    Reveal or Hide button;
  - a visually hidden `role="status"` element whose text is the label while revealed and empty
    while hidden, so a screen reader announces the answer once when it appears.
- Colours from role tokens only. It works at 320px wide and in the educator preview modal.

### Alpine: `freedom_ls/content_engine/static/content_engine/js/alpine-components.js`

- A factory returns the player state (`idle`, `loading`, `playing`, `failed`, a per-instance `_id`)
  and the methods `_start(src)` and `_stop()`. `_start` takes ownership from the current owner and
  plays from the start; it never stops. `audioFile` and `audioReveal` both spread a fresh result of
  the factory, so the shared element and owner logic exists once and one audio file plays at a time
  across both widgets. `audioFile.toggle()` keeps its behaviour, built on `_start` and `_stop`.
- `Alpine.data("audioReveal", ...)` in the `alpine:init` block, listed in the header comment. `x-*`
  attributes hold method and property names only (CSP build).
  - State: the player state plus `revealed` (initially false).
  - The play button calls the factory's `toggle()`, which reads the button's own `data-src`
    (`this.$el.dataset.src`), as `c-audio`'s does: the shared card body puts `data-src` on the play
    button in both widgets. `reveal()` reads the root's copy (`this.$root.dataset.src`).
  - `reveal()`: set `revealed` and call `_start` synchronously in the click handler (iOS needs
    this), then move focus to the play button.
  - Auto-hide: when the widget goes idle while revealed, a 5000 ms timer starts; leaving idle
    clears it. `hide()` clears `revealed`, stops this widget's clip if it is sounding, and moves
    focus to the reveal control if focus was inside the answer.
  - `destroy()` releases ownership as `audioFile.destroy()` does, so the educator preview can swap
    content.
- A failed play reveals the answer anyway and shows the error line. Nothing plays on page load.

### Slot widgets: `freedom_ls/markdown_rendering/markdown_utils.py` and `content_engine/templates/cotton/`

- `render_markdown` adds python-markdown's `md_in_html` extension and, before converting, injects
  `markdown="1"` into the opening tags of the six slot widgets, `c-slot`, and the layout wrappers
  `c-grid` and `c-image-grid`. `md_in_html` only converts an element whose parent is also marked,
  so without the wrappers a `c-card` inside a `c-grid` would show its body as raw markdown. A
  wrapper's widget children are block-level and stay raw, so wrapper output is unchanged. The list of tags lives
  beside the allow-list handling in `markdown_rendering`. `md_in_html` consumes the attribute, so
  nh3 never sees it.
- The one `nh3.clean` then sanitises slot bodies with everything else, before the Cotton compile.
- `accordion.html`, `admonition.html`, `card.html`, `flashcard.html` (both faces),
  `pull-quote.html` and `table.html` emit the slot without `{% markdown %}`. `table.html` applies
  `slot|inject_table_caption:caption`, and the filter's docstring names its new input.
- Slot widget output stays the same apart from nested widgets surviving: markdown in slots,
  inline one-line bodies, tables without blank lines, and nh3 stripping of disallowed tags,
  attributes and URL schemes. Before the templates change, run `test_markdown_utils.py` and the demo
  content against the new pass, and list in this section any output difference kept on purpose.
  - Kept difference: a heading in a slot body is shifted down once, with the rest of the page,
    instead of twice. The second `{% markdown %}` pass ran `mdx_headdown` again, so a `###` in a
    flashcard face rendered as `<h4>`; it now renders as `<h3>`, the level its position in the
    page calls for.
- The `table.html` comment and the flashcard test docstring that say nested widgets are stripped
  are rewritten.

### `c-flashcard`: `freedom_ls/content_engine/templates/cotton/flashcard.html`

- The `@layer components` block gives `.flashcard-face button` `pointer-events: auto`, beside the
  existing `:is(table, pre)` rule, and the "Layering" comment names it.
- A play button in the showing face plays its audio file and does not flip the card. Clicking face
  prose still flips. The hidden face stays `inert`, so its buttons are unreachable.
- The `c-audio` card on the answer face keeps its opaque `bg-surface`. Its muted respelling must
  meet WCAG AA contrast there.

### Demo content: `demo_content/functionality_demo_content_widgets/4. interactive-widgets/content.md`

- A `## Audio reveal` section after `## Flashcard`: one paragraph telling the learner to say the
  answer aloud before revealing, then a `c-grid` of four `c-audio-reveal` (Bravo, Charlie, Oscar,
  Romeo).
- A flashcard in `## Flashcard` with a `c-audio` on its answer face.
- Both point at the CC0 mp3s with `src="../2. media/audio/<word>.mp3"`. No copies.

## Open questions

- Q: Do the toggle buttons carry icons? → A: No; the visible text "Reveal" and "Hide" only, so no
  semantic icon names are added (assumed default: no reveal or hide name exists in
  `icons/semantic_names.py`, and text alone is unambiguous. Overrule by editing Requirements,
  Component).
- Q: Does the widget print its own "say it aloud" cue? → A: No; the author writes the instruction in
  prose, as the demo does (assumed default: the idea asks for no cue, and a cue on each of 26
  widgets is noise. Overrule by editing Requirements, Component).
- Q: Does flipping a `c-flashcard` stop a clip playing from the face that turns away? → A: No
  (assumed default: clips are a word long and the idea asks for no flip hook. Overrule by editing
  Requirements, `c-flashcard`).
- Q: Can the learner reveal without sound? → A: No; Reveal always plays and the learner mutes the
  device (assumed default: the idea says "The reveal click plays the answer's clip", and a second
  control on every widget adds clutter. Overrule by editing Requirements, Component).

## Success criteria

- In the demo, a learner reveals Bravo, hears it, hides it and reveals it again: by tap on iOS
  Safari, and by keyboard alone on desktop Chrome.
- A `c-audio` or `c-table` nested in any slot widget renders as it does at top level.
- A `c-audio` in the showing face of a `c-flashcard` plays when pressed, and the card stays put.
