# Spec: say it, then reveal (`c-audio-reveal`)

Idea: `idea.md`. Answers: `answers.md`. Research: `research_*.md` in this directory, and
`research_content_media.md` and `research_audio_widgets.md` in
`spec_dd/1. next/content-widget-sayit-reveal/`. Builds on `c-audio` from
`spec_dd/3. done/2026-11-19_08:05_content-widget-sayit-reveal-1-audio-player/`.

## Purpose

The phonetic-alphabet course needs learners to practise recall, not just listen. This spec adds
`c-audio-reveal`: the learner sees a prompt tile ("J"), says the answer aloud, presses Reveal, and
the answer ("Juliett") appears while its audio file plays. It also makes nested widgets, `c-audio`
among them, work inside the six widgets whose body is markdown, so a course can practise with
`c-flashcard` faces that play sound.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| slot widget | coined | A cotton content widget whose body is author markdown: `c-accordion`, `c-admonition`, `c-card`, `c-flashcard`, `c-pull-quote`, `c-table`. No existing term; the templates call it "a component whose body is author markdown". |

## Scope

In:

- The `c-audio-reveal` cotton component, its Alpine component and its allow-list entry.
- One markdown pass for slot widget bodies, so any widget nests inside any slot widget.
- A `c-audio` play button in the showing `c-flashcard` face plays without flipping the card.
- Demo content, authoring docs and upgrade notes.

Out:

- Building `c-audio`. Scheduled flashcards, self-rating and spaced repetition
  (`learner-interface-features-4-flashcards`,
  `content-widget-sayit-reveal-3-audio-on-revision-flashcards`).
- Microphone, speech recognition, recording, storing anything about the learner.
- Shuffling a set of prompts, and reveal-all or hide-all controls.
- Playing a clip when a `c-flashcard` flips. The Reveal click is the only autoplay.
- Changing the `markdown` template tag in `content_tags.py`. No FLS slot widget calls it after this
  spec, and downstream custom widgets that do keep today's behaviour.

## Decisions

- **A new component with `c-audio`'s attributes** (`answers.md`). `symbol` is the prompt, so it is
  required here, though optional on `c-audio`.
- **One Alpine player, two widgets.** `c-audio-reveal` plays through the same module-level audio
  element and owner as `audioFile`, so one audio file plays at a time across both widgets. Reveal
  always plays from the start and never toggles to a stop, which `audioFile.toggle()` would do.
- **Reveal and Hide are one disclosure button** (`answers.md`: "Reveal toggles to Hide"). Focus
  stays on it in both states, so nothing has to move focus.
- **The answer keeps its space while hidden** (`invisible` plus `inert`), so a list of 26 widgets
  never shifts when one is revealed.
- **Slot widgets emit their slot as-is**, as `c-grid` does (`answers.md`). The second
  `{% markdown slot %}` pass re-ran markdown and nh3 over rendered widget markup and turned a nested
  `c-audio` button into escaped text. The file lookup was never the fault. Cotton renders nested
  widgets with the outer context, where `content_instance` exists.
- **Every `button` in a `c-flashcard` face takes pointer events back.** That covers `c-audio`,
  `c-audio-reveal` and the picture lightbox with one rule. Prose still falls through to the flip
  trigger, which keeps the layering the flashcard comment describes.

## Requirements

### Authoring syntax

```html
<c-audio-reveal src="audio/juliett.mp3" symbol="J" label="Juliett" respelling="JOO-lee-ett"></c-audio-reveal>
```

| Attribute | Required | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `src` | yes | Path to an audio file, relative to the markdown file, resolved as `c-audio` does. |
| `symbol` | yes | The prompt, shown in the tile before and after reveal. |
| `label` | yes | The answer word, shown on reveal and used in the play button's accessible name. |
| `respelling` | no | Shown under the label on reveal, with stress marked as in `c-audio`. |

- `config/settings_base.py` `MARKDOWN_ALLOWED_TAGS` gains
  `"c-audio-reveal": {"src", "label", "respelling", "symbol"}`.
- `base/tests/invariants/test_app_templates_use_app_components.py` `BANNED_TAGS` gains
  `"c-audio-reveal"` beside `"c-audio"`.

### Component: `freedom_ls/content_engine/templates/cotton/audio-reveal.html`

- `<c-vars src="" label="" respelling="" symbol="" />`; resolve the file as `audio.html` does.
- Red boxes, using `c-content-error` as `audio.html` does: `Audio not found: {{ src }}`,
  `Audio needs a label: {{ src }}`, and `Audio reveal needs a symbol: {{ src }}`.
- Otherwise one root with `my-6` and `x-data="audioReveal"`, holding one card styled like
  `c-audio`'s:
  - the symbol tile, visible in both states. Unlike `c-audio`'s tile it is not `aria-hidden`,
    because here it is the prompt;
  - the answer region, with an id from Alpine's `$id`: the label, the respelling and the play button,
    marked up as in `c-audio`. While hidden it carries `invisible`, `x-bind:inert` and
    `x-bind:aria-hidden`, so it is neither seen, focusable nor announced, and it keeps its height.
    The text column, play button and error line come from the same markup as `audio.html`, not a
    copy;
  - the toggle: a `<c-button>` of at least 44×44 CSS px whose visible text is "Reveal" or "Hide",
    with `x-bind:aria-expanded="revealed"`, `aria-controls` naming the answer region, and
    `x-on:click="toggleReveal"`. It sits where it does not move between states;
  - a visually hidden `role="status"` element whose text is the label while revealed and empty
    while hidden, so a screen reader announces the answer once when it appears;
  - `c-audio`'s error line.
- Colours from role tokens only; it works at 320px wide and in the educator preview modal.

### Alpine: `freedom_ls/content_engine/static/content_engine/js/alpine-components.js`

- `Alpine.data("audioReveal", ...)` in the `alpine:init` block, listed in the header comment.
  `x-*` attributes hold method and property names only (CSP build).
- It has `audioFile`'s state (`idle`, `loading`, `playing`, `failed`) and its `toggle()` for the
  play button, plus `revealed` (initially false). The owner and shared-element logic exists once,
  shared by `audioFile` and `audioReveal`.
- `toggleReveal()`:
  - hidden: set `revealed`, and play from the start synchronously in the click handler (iOS needs
    this), taking ownership from whatever widget was playing;
  - revealed: stop the clip if this widget is loading or playing it, and clear `revealed`.
- A failed play reveals the answer anyway and shows the error line. Nothing plays on page load.

### Slot widgets: `freedom_ls/markdown_rendering/markdown_utils.py` and `content_engine/templates/cotton/`

- `render_markdown` converts the markdown inside slot widget bodies (and inside `c-slot` in
  `c-flashcard`) in its one markdown pass, before its one `nh3.clean` and the Cotton compile.
  Python-markdown's `md_in_html` extension does this.
- `accordion.html`, `admonition.html`, `card.html`, `flashcard.html` (both faces),
  `pull-quote.html` and `table.html` emit the slot without `{% markdown %}`. `table.html` still
  splices the caption into the slot with `inject_table_caption`.
- Slot widget output is otherwise unchanged: the blank-line rule, markdown inside slots, and nh3
  stripping of disallowed tags, attributes and URL schemes in slot bodies.
- The comments in `table.html` and the flashcard test docstring that say nested widgets are
  stripped are rewritten.

### `c-flashcard`: `freedom_ls/content_engine/templates/cotton/flashcard.html`

- The `@layer components` block gives `.flashcard-face button` `pointer-events: auto`, beside the
  existing `:is(table, pre)` rule, and the "Layering" comment names it.
- A play button in the showing face plays its audio file and does not flip the card. Clicking face
  prose still flips. The hidden face stays `inert`, so its buttons are unreachable.
- A `c-audio` card on the answer face meets contrast on the tinted panel.

### Demo content: `demo_content/functionality_demo_content_widgets/4. interactive-widgets/content.md`

- A `## Audio reveal` section after `## Flashcard`: one paragraph telling the learner to say the
  answer aloud before revealing, then a `c-grid` of four `c-audio-reveal` (Bravo, Charlie, Oscar,
  Romeo).
- A flashcard in `## Flashcard` with a `c-audio` on its answer face.
- Both reuse the CC0 mp3s in `2. media/audio/`. If `src` cannot reach that folder from this topic,
  copy them into `4. interactive-widgets/audio/` with a `LICENCES.txt` matching the original.

    survives;
  - markdown in the slot still renders (emphasis, lists, a table, fenced code);
  - `<script>`, an `onclick` attribute and a `javascript:` link in a slot body are stripped;
  - existing tests for these widgets pass unchanged.
- **Flashcard CSS**: the face rule includes `button`, next to the scrollable-content test.
- **Demo content** (`content_engine/tests/demo_content/`, `fls_internal`): every `c-audio-reveal`
  and flashcard `c-audio` `src` in the interactive-widgets topic resolves.
- **Educator course content preview**: a topic with `c-audio-reveal` renders it.
- **Browser** (Playwright, `django_db(transaction=True)`, `live_server_site`, the mp3 route fixture
  from the `c-audio` flow test):
  - no audio request on page load, and the answer is absent from the accessibility tree;
  - Reveal shows the answer, plays the audio file and sets `aria-expanded="true"`; the play button
    replays; Hide stops the clip and hides the answer; Reveal again plays from the start;
  - focus stays on the toggle throughout; Enter and Space work;
  - revealing one widget stops a `c-audio` or another `c-audio-reveal` that is playing;
  - a forced load error still reveals the text and shows the error line;
  - in a flashcard, pressing play on the showing face plays without flipping; clicking prose flips;
    the hidden face's play button cannot be focused until the card flips.
- **Manual QA**: iOS Safari and Android Chrome, where the Reveal tap plays; VoiceOver or NVDA
  announces the answer once on reveal.

## Open questions

- Q: Do the toggle buttons carry icons? → A: No; the visible text "Reveal" and "Hide" only, so no
  semantic icon names are added (assumed default: no reveal or hide name exists in
  `icons/semantic_names.py`, and text alone is unambiguous. Overrule by editing Requirements,
  Component).
- Q: Does the widget print its own "say it aloud" cue? → A: No; the author writes the instruction in
  prose, as the demo does (assumed default: the idea asks for no cue, and a cue on each of 26
  widgets is noise. Overrule by editing Requirements, Component).
- Q: Does flipping a `c-flashcard` stop a clip playing from the face that turns away? → A: No
  (assumed default: clips are a word long and the idea asks for no flip hook. Overrule by editing
  Requirements, `c-flashcard`).

## Success criteria

- Pressing Reveal shows the answer and plays its audio file; Hide hides it; Reveal again replays.
- Before Reveal, the answer cannot be seen, focused or read by a screen reader.
- A `c-audio` in the showing face of a `c-flashcard` plays when pressed, and the card stays put.
- A `c-audio` or `c-table` nested in any slot widget renders exactly as it does at top level.
- A missing file, an empty `label` or an empty `symbol` shows a red box naming the `src`.
