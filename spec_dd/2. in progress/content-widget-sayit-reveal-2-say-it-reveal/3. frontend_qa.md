# Frontend QA: say it, then reveal (`c-audio-reveal`) and widgets nested in slot widgets

## Setup

1. Load the demo content: `uv run python manage.py content_save ./demo_content DemoDev`.
2. Start the server: `uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT`. Base URL `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT`.
3. Log in at `/accounts/login/` with the QA login from `.claude/sdd/config.md` (`## QA Login`).
4. Open the "Content Widgets - Demo Reference" course and go to the "Interactive Widgets" topic.
   If the learner view will not open it (the course may be `coming_soon`), use the educator
   interface's course content preview for the same topic. The "Media" topic and the other topics
   of the same course are used by the regression tests below.

Check every test at desktop (1280 px), tablet (768 px) and phone (375 px) widths, plus 320 px where
a test says so. Keep the browser console open: no JavaScript errors and no CSP violations at any
point.

## Test 1: the reveal widgets render hidden

1. Scroll to `## Audio reveal`, after `## Flashcard` and before `## Accordion`.
   - One paragraph tells the learner to say the word aloud before revealing.
   - Four cards in a two-column grid (one column on phones): tiles B, C, O, R.
   - Each card shows only its tile: a filled primary-colour square with the bold letter. No word,
     no respelling and no play button is visible, but the card already has its full height.
   - There is no Reveal or Hide button. In each card the space right of the tile shows a
     subdued (muted-colour) eye-closed icon; the pointer turns into a hand over that space.
2. Open the network panel and reload. No request goes to `/content/audio/` or to any `.mp3`.
3. Inspect the accessibility tree (devtools Accessibility pane): "Bravo" and its respelling are
   absent; the tile letter "B" is present; a button named "Reveal answer" has
   `aria-expanded="false"` and `aria-controls` pointing at the hidden answer's id.
4. Press Tab through the section: focus lands only on the four "Reveal answer" controls, never on
   a hidden play button.

## Test 2: reveal, replay, auto-hide, reveal again

1. Click the eye-closed space on B.
   - The icon disappears and "Bravo" appears in bold in its place, "BRAH-voh" under it with "BRAH"
     bold in primary, and the round play button at the right. Nothing else on the page moves:
     the card does not grow.
   - The clip plays at once; the play button shows the playing icon on a filled primary circle and
     the card border turns primary.
   - `aria-expanded` is `"true"`.
2. Press the play button while it plays: it stops. Press it again: it plays from the start.
3. Let the clip end and wait. About 5 seconds after it stops, the answer hides and the eye-closed
   icon returns; the card keeps its height. It never hides while the clip is still playing.
4. Reveal B, let it end, and press play again within 5 seconds: the countdown starts over, so
   the answer stays up for 5 seconds after this clip ends.
5. Click the eye-closed space again. The clip plays from the start, never silent.

## Test 3: one sound at a time

1. Reveal B, and while it plays reveal C. B stops (its play button returns to the speaker icon,
   its answer stays revealed for about 5 seconds, then hides) and C plays.
2. While C plays, press B's play button. C stops and B plays.
3. On the same page, play the flashcard's `c-audio` (Test 6) and reveal O while it plays. The
   flashcard clip stops.
4. Let a clip run to the end: its play button returns to the speaker icon by itself; the answer
   stays revealed for about 5 seconds, then hides.

## Test 4: keyboard and screen reader

1. Tab to B's "Reveal answer" control. The focus ring is visible around the eye-closed space.
2. Press Enter: it reveals and plays; focus moves to the play button, whose accessible name is
   "Play pronunciation of Bravo".
3. Wait for the auto-hide: focus returns to the "Reveal answer" control, not to the page body.
4. Press Space on it: it reveals and plays.
5. With VoiceOver (macOS/iOS) or NVDA (Windows): activate "Reveal answer". The screen reader
   announces "Bravo" once. The auto-hide announces nothing extra. The tile letter is read as the
   prompt.

## Test 5: failure, and author mistakes

1. In devtools, block `*/media/*` (or `*.mp3`). Click the eye-closed space on R.
   - The answer still appears.
   - "Audio could not be played. Try again." shows in the error colour under the card.
2. Unblock and press R's play button: it plays and the error line disappears.
3. In the admin or by editing a scratch topic's markdown, add four widgets and check each shows a
   red box naming the src instead of a widget:
   - `<c-audio-reveal src="audio/missing.mp3" symbol="X" label="X"></c-audio-reveal>` →
     "Audio not found: audio/missing.mp3";
   - an existing image path as `src` → "Audio not found: …";
   - no `label` → "Audio needs a label: …";
   - no `symbol` → "Audio reveal needs a symbol: …".
4. A widget written with `onclick="alert(1)"` renders without the attribute and no alert fires.

## Test 6: `c-audio` inside a flashcard

1. In `## Flashcard`, find the card whose front asks which word stands for **B**.
2. Before flipping, Tab through the card: focus reaches the flip trigger but never the back face's
   play button.
3. Click the front prose: the card flips. The answer face shows "Bravo" in a `c-audio` card with
   an opaque surface background, the respelling under it.
4. Press the play button on the answer face: the clip plays and the card does **not** flip back.
5. Click the answer face's prose (not the audio card): the card flips back to the front.
6. Check the respelling's contrast against the audio card's background with the devtools colour
   picker: at least 4.5:1, in light and dark themes.
7. The other flashcards on the page flip, scroll their tables and code blocks, and look as before.

## Test 7: narrow screens

1. Set the viewport to 320 px wide. On Interactive Widgets, reveal all four widgets and flip the
   audio flashcard.
   - Nothing scrolls sideways. Long words and respellings wrap inside the card.
   - Each eye-closed reveal control is at least 44 px tall and inside its card.

## Test 8: educator preview

1. In the educator interface open the course content preview and select the Interactive Widgets
   topic.
2. The Audio reveal section renders as in Test 1. Reveal B: it plays, then hides itself about 5 seconds after it ends.
3. Switch the preview to another topic while a clip plays: the clip stops and there are no
   console errors. Switch back: every widget is hidden again.

## Test 9: nothing else changed (regression)

Judge each page on this branch's server against these checks.

1. "Media" topic, `## Audio`: four `c-audio` cards (Bravo, Charlie, Oscar, Romeo) look and behave
   as before: tile, bold word, respelling with the stressed syllable, round play button; play,
   switch, stop and the error line all work.
2. Every other topic of the demo course, scrolled top to bottom:
   - accordions open and close, their bodies render markdown (bold, lists, tables, code);
   - admonitions show their icon, title and markdown body; the checklist admonition shows its
     read-only checkboxes;
   - cards show header image, title and markdown body;
   - pull quotes show the quote, attribution and source;
   - `c-table` shows its caption, the bordered scroll wrapper and styled header row;
   - flashcards flip and show markdown on both faces;
   - grids line up their children;
   - no raw markdown (`**`, `- `, `|---|`) and no stray `markdown="1"` text appears anywhere.
3. Inspect one accordion, admonition, card, flashcard, pull quote and table: their bodies contain
   no `<script>`, no `on*` attributes and no `javascript:` links.
4. Add a scratch topic (or edit one in the admin) holding a `c-table` inside a `c-accordion`, and a
   `c-audio` inside a `c-card` inside a `c-grid`. The table keeps its bordered scroll wrapper and
   caption, the card's markdown body renders, and the audio plays.
