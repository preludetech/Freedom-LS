# Spec: Revision questions

Spec 3 of 5 in the Learner interface features effort (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`). Source:
`idea.md`. Decisions taken with the user: `answers.md`. Research: the `research_*.md` files beside
this spec.

## Purpose

A learner who finishes a course section can revise its questions on a per-course revision page. A
spaced-repetition scheduler (FSRS) picks which questions come back and when. Each answer is logged.
A wrong answer shows the author's explanation and links to the section to revise, and sections the
learner keeps getting wrong are flagged.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| section | narrowed | A `Topic` or `CoursePart` a question tests, through `FormQuestion.topics` / `.course_parts` (`content_engine/models/question_sections.py`, done spec `learner-interface-features-1-question-sections`) |
| bank form | existing | A form placed in no course whose questions carry `sections:` (`claude_plugins/fls-content/skills/content-types/resources/form-files.md`) |
| bank | coined | The questions one learner may revise in one course, drawn from quiz questions and bank forms alike. Derived on read, never stored |
| `record` | existing | The learner's `CourseProgress` for the course, from `course_progress_for` |
| revision session | coined | One run of up to `REVISION_SESSION_SIZE` questions on the revision page. The Django session is always called "Django session" |
| `RevisionSchedule` | coined | One learner's FSRS state for one `FormQuestion`. Its `due` is FSRS's next-review time, not a deadline |
| `RevisionReview` | coined | One answer given on the revision page (FSRS calls an answer a review); an append-only log row |

## Decisions

1. **Scheduler: FSRS via the `fsrs` package** (`>=6.3`), default parameters, no optimiser.
2. **Ratings:** wrong is `Again`, right is `Good`, and right with "I guessed" is `Hard`. The learner never sees rating names.
3. **The bank is derived on read, not written at completion.** A question is in a learner's bank for a course when it meets all of these:
   - It is a choice question (`multiple_choice`, `checkboxes`, `dropdown`).
   - It has at least one option with `correct=True`.
   - `dropped_from_source_at` is null.
   - It names at least one section placed in this course, and every section it names in this course is finished.
   - If its form is placed in this course, at least one of that form's placements is in `completed_collection_item_ids(record)`, so a quiz's questions and answers never show in revision before the learner has completed the quiz. A bank form is placed in no course and is unaffected.
4. **A section is finished** when:
   - a `Topic`: at least one of its placements in the course is in `completed_collection_item_ids(record)`;
   - a `CoursePart`: every placement nested under it in the course is.
5. **Bank forms need no flag.** A bank form is any form in a sibling directory of the courses, of any `strategy`, whose questions carry `sections:`. They reach the bank through the same section link as quiz questions.
6. **The schedule is keyed on (`User`, question), not `Learner` or course.** A person in two organisations, or with one question's sections placed in two courses, has one schedule. Each course's page shows only its own bank.
7. **Explanation:** one optional field per question, markdown. It is shown after every answer, right or wrong. When it is missing, the feedback still names the correct answer and the section link.
8. **Edits keep history.** `RevisionReview` stores `was_correct` and the selected option ids at answer time, so later edits to `correct` flags never re-mark it. Quiz history (`QuestionAnswer`) is untouched.
9. **Options are shuffled on every showing in revision**, whatever the form's `shuffle_options`, with `form_engine.served_paper.shuffle_with_locks` honouring `QuestionOption.locked`. This depends on `sacaa-compliance-2-shuffle` merging first.
10. **Backlog:**
    - A session holds at most `REVISION_SESSION_SIZE` questions.
    - Due reviewed questions come first, lowest retrievability first. Never-reviewed bank questions fill the remaining slots in course order.
    - No overdue total or "overdue" wording is shown: the due count caps at "40+ due".
    - A learner is never refused a session. With nothing due, the session holds the learner's weakest bank questions: most lapses first, then highest FSRS difficulty, ties in random order.
11. **Weak section:** among the learner's last `REVISION_WEAK_WINDOW` reviews of questions in that section, at least `REVISION_WEAK_MIN_REVIEWS` exist and the wrong share is `>= REVISION_WEAK_MISS_RATE`. It is computed on read. No screen shows it yet; the later dashboard spec does.
12. **Revision rows (`RevisionSchedule` and `RevisionReview`) never touch `FormProgress` or `QuestionAnswer`.** Reports, exports and completion receivers read those.

## Requirements

- `uv add fsrs` (`pyproject.toml`).

- Regenerate `docs/app_structure.md` and `test_organisation/import_contracts.toml` with `/ds:app_map`.
- Runtime edges: `revision -> form_engine, content_engine, learner_progress, accounts, site_aware_models, base`, and `learner_interface -> revision`. Importing `form_engine.served_paper` must pass the regenerated contract.
- `revision/config.py`, an `AppSettings` subclass:

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| `REVISION_SESSION_SIZE` | 15 |
| `REVISION_DESIRED_RETENTION` | 0.9 |
| `REVISION_WEAK_WINDOW` | 10 |
| `REVISION_WEAK_MIN_REVIEWS` | 5 |
| `REVISION_WEAK_MISS_RATE` | 0.4 |

### Models (`revision/models.py`, all `SiteAwareModel`)

- `RevisionSchedule`:
  - `user` FK; `question` FK `FormQuestion` CASCADE.
  - FSRS card columns: `state` int, `step` int null, `stability` float null, `difficulty` float null, `due` datetime, `last_review` datetime null.
  - `reps` int, `lapses` int.
  - Unique `(user, question)`, indexed `(user, due)`.
- `RevisionReview`:
  - `user` FK; `question` FK CASCADE; `course` FK `Course` (the page it was answered on).
  - `reviewed_at`; `selected_option_ids` (JSON list of UUID strings, since an option without a `uuid` is re-created on load); `was_correct` bool; `guessed` bool; `rating` int.
  - `schedule_before` JSON: the card dict before this review.
  - Append-only apart from the "I guessed" re-rating.
- Admin: read-only, `SiteAwareModelAdmin`.

### Scheduler (`revision/scheduler.py`)

- Pure functions over `fsrs.Scheduler(desired_retention=REVISION_DESIRED_RETENTION)`, with no ORM import:
  - `review(card_dict, rating, now) -> (card_dict, rating)`
  - `retrievability(card_dict, now) -> float`
  - `rating_for(was_correct, guessed) -> Rating`
- The wrapper converts between the `fsrs.Card` dict and the `RevisionSchedule` columns. `card_id` is not stored, and datetimes stay timezone-aware UTC.

### Bank and session (`revision/queries.py`, `revision/services.py`)

- `bank_questions(record) -> QuerySet[FormQuestion]` implements Decisions 3–4 and is the only bank entry point. It reads completion only through `learner_progress.queries.completed_collection_item_ids`.
- `ready_count(user, record, section=None, limit)` counts with a SQL `LIMIT` (41 for the course's due count) and no retrievability work.
- `practice_questions(user, record, rng)` returns every bank question, weakest first (Decision 10). `build_session` uses it when no question is due.
- `session_summary(user, state)` returns the session's counts: answered, knew (right), hard (right and "I guessed") and forgot (wrong).
- `due_questions(user, record, section=None)`:
  - Returns bank questions with no schedule row, or with `due <= now`.
  - Ordered as Decision 10. The retrievability scoring in Python happens only here, when a session is built.
  - `section` filters to questions that name that `Topic` or `CoursePart`.
- `build_session(user, record, section) -> RevisionSessionState` returns plain data for the view to store. The services take no `request`. It holds:
  - the question ids (at most `REVISION_SESSION_SIZE`);
  - one shuffle seed per showing;
  - the start time;
  - the current index.

  `learner_interface` keeps it in the Django session under a per-course key. A reload re-renders the same question in the same order, re-derived from the seed.
- `record_answer(user, record, question, selected_option_ids)`:
  - Marks the answer with `scoring.is_quiz_answer_correct`.
  - Creates the `RevisionSchedule` on the first answer.
  - Applies the Decision 2 rating, then writes the `RevisionReview`.
  - The answer is saved on submit, so quitting mid-session loses nothing.
- `mark_guessed(review)` re-rates per Decision 2, and tapping again undoes it. It only works when all of these hold:
  - the review was right;
  - it is the user's latest `RevisionReview` for that question;
  - it is the session's current question.

  It re-runs `review` from `schedule_before` at the original `reviewed_at`, and updates `rating`, `guessed` and the schedule in one transaction.
- `weak_sections(user, record)` implements Decision 11.

### Form schema, model and loader

- `form_engine/schema.py` `FormQuestion`: `explanation: str | None = None`.
- `form_engine/models.py` `FormQuestion`:
  - `explanation = TextField(blank=True)` and a `rendered_explanation` beside `rendered_question`.
  - `dropped_from_source_at = DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)`.
  - Migration.
- `content_engine/management/commands/content_save.py` `save_form_question` passes `explanation=item.explanation or ""` explicitly, as it does for `slug` and `group`, so a removed explanation is cleared.
- Once at the end of a `content_save` run, scoped to the forms whose files that run loaded:
  - set `dropped_from_source_at` on their questions whose uuid the run did not see;
  - clear it on those it did see;
  - send a new `form_engine.signals.form_questions_dropped(question_ids)`.

  A question moved between two form files is not flagged.
- `revision`'s `AppConfig.ready()` connects a receiver that deletes the `RevisionSchedule` and `RevisionReview` rows for those ids. `content_engine` and `form_engine` never import `revision`. The question rows stay, because `QuestionAnswer.question` is PROTECT.
- `content_engine/validate.py`: a warning, not an error, for a scored choice question with sections and no explanation.
- Sync the `claude_plugins/fls-content` schema and validate copies with `/fls-dev:update_claude_plugin_fls_content`.

- **Everything a learner sees of revision is inside the course player.** The revision page extends `learner_interface/_course_base.html`: the outline panel on the left, breadcrumbs "course > Revision", and one revision card (`#revision-card`) in a `max-w-2xl` column in the main area. The course details page shows nothing about revision.
- Outline entry (`partials/course_toc_revision.html`):
  - One row under the table of contents, after a divider: the `revision` icon, "Revision" in semibold, and a chip on the right reading "N due", capped at "40+ due".
  - It links to `course_revision` and is marked `aria-current="page"` on the revision page.
  - It shows whenever the learner's bank is non-empty, "0 due" included. It is absent before the learner has finished a section with revision questions.
- Dashboard (`partials/revision_dashboard.html`), shown when no session is in progress:
  - The `revision` icon in a tinted circle, the heading "Revision", one line "N questions due." (capped at "40+ questions due."), and a full-width "Start revision" button.
  - With nothing due the line reads "Nothing is due. You can still practise your weakest questions." and the button stays.
  - With an empty bank: "Questions appear here after you finish a section." and a full-width "Back to the course" button. No start button.
  - It is a placeholder. A later spec designs the real dashboard, so it holds nothing else: no section list, no "Needs revision" flag, no welcome-back line.
- Session:
  - One `#revision-card` HTMX target showing progress "3 of 15", the question and a full-width "Check answer" button.
  - The question renders through `form_engine/question.html` and its `inputs/*`. The `question_options` tag reads a new `revision_options` context variable (the shuffled order) ahead of `sitting_path.options_of(question)`. The existing three shells and the single-rendering test keep passing.
  - Submitting with no option returns 422 and the card with the error "Choose an answer."
- Feedback (the answer response), top to bottom:
  - The question, then its options in the order they were asked. The correct option has a success border and tint (`border-success bg-success/10`) and a check icon at the end of the row. The learner's wrong pick has an error border and tint (`border-error bg-error/10`) and a cross icon. Other options are neutral with muted text. `cotton/correct-answer-mark.html` draws the icons in its `icon_only` mode, which keeps "Correct answer" and "Your answer" for screen readers.
  - One `<c-callout>` in the result's tone (success or error). Its bold first line is "Correct." or "Not quite. The answer is {correct option}." ("Not quite." alone when more than one option is correct). Under it: the explanation if one exists, then a "Revisit: {section title}" text link with the topic icon, to the section's first placement in the course. For a `CoursePart`, that is the placement of its first child. The link is omitted when the learner can't open the page. There is no "Why" heading and no "comes back in" line.
  - On a right answer, an "I guessed" toggle with the help line "We'll ask you this one again sooner."
  - "Next", right-aligned with an arrow. The answer can't be changed once submitted.
  - The feedback region is `aria-live`, and focus moves to it after submit.
- "Revision done" card (`partials/revision_end.html`):
  - `course_revision_next` and `course_revision` render it when the session index passes the last question. Rendering it ends the session, so the next visit to the revision page shows the dashboard.
  - A check icon in a tinted circle, the heading "Revision done" and "You answered N questions."
  - Three counts in a row: "Knew it" (right answers), "Hard" (right answers marked "I guessed") and "Forgot" (wrong answers), from `session_summary`. Each is a plain figure over its label, with no border and no fill; space alone separates the three columns.
  - One full-width button, "Back to the course", to `course_home`, which resumes the course.
  - It shows no missed-question list, no next revision date and no "Revise more".

## Testing

- Bank:
  - A completion with no `TopicProgress` signal (test-out style) still banks the section's questions.
  - A `CoursePart` counts as finished only when all its nested placements are.
  - A question of a quiz placed in the course is banked only once the quiz is completed.
  - A topic placed in two courses gives a per-course bank but one shared schedule.
  - Excluded: free-text, all-`None`-correct and dropped questions.
- Retrievability ordering, and new questions held back while due ones fill the session.
- Editing `correct` flags after an answer doesn't change the stored `was_correct`.
- "I guessed":
  - It is refused on a wrong answer, a stale review and a non-current question.
  - Re-rating matches a first-time `Hard` at the same `reviewed_at`.
- Weak flag: below the minimum sample it doesn't show; at the threshold it does.
- Loader:
  - `explanation` clears when it's removed from the file.
  - A question dropped and then restored has its marker set and then cleared.
  - A question moved between two form files in one run is not flagged.
  - The validator warns on a missing explanation.
- Views:
  - Access: unregistered, paused, ended and other site.
  - 422 on an empty submit.
  - Reload keeps the question, the option order and the "Revision done" card.
  - The shuffled order honours `locked`.
- Playwright (`learner_interface/tests/playwright/`): a full session on desktop and mobile, covering a wrong answer with its explanation and section link, a right answer with "I guessed", and the end screen.
- Site isolation of `RevisionSchedule` and `RevisionReview`.

## Success criteria

- `uv run pytest`, `lint-imports`, `app-map-fresh` and `test-mirroring` pass.
- A question answered right with "I guessed" is due sooner than the same question answered right without it.
- Removing a question from its form file and running `content_save` removes it from every learner's bank. Its revision rows are deleted, and its quiz reports are unchanged.
- No `QuestionAnswer` or `FormProgress` row is created by a revision session.
- A learner with 100 due questions is shown a session of `REVISION_SESSION_SIZE` questions and "40+ due" on the outline's Revision entry.
- The course details page of a course with due questions is byte-for-byte free of the word "revision".
- A learner with nothing due can still start a session.
