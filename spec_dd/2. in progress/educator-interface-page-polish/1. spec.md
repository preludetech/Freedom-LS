# Spec: Educator interface page polish

## Purpose

QA on the shipped educator interface found seven pages that are hard to scan or controls that behave unexpectedly (`idea.md`). This spec fixes each one in place. No new pages, models or permissions.

Paths below use EI = `freedom_ls/educator_interface`, PF = `freedom_ls/panel_framework`. File-level detail for every item is in `research_codebase.md`.

## Scope

In:

1. Learners list: Courses column.
2. Learner page: Courses tab.
3. Learner quick view: the rule under "Messages".
4. Learner page: Settings tab shows the email.
5. Dashboard: everything it names is one click away.
6. Bulk confirm dialog: locked while an in-request run is under way.
7. Dialog footer buttons on phones.

Out:

- Show/hide columns and filters (`educator-interface-data-table-columns-and-filters`).
- Help icons (`educator-interface-contextual-help`).
- Messaging from the learner page (`educator-interface-message-from-anywhere`).
- Registration actions on the Courses tab.
- Page-header actions. They are already `size="small"`.

## Decisions

- Layouts are described here, with no Claude Design step (`answers.md`).
- The bulk dialog lock does not widen `hx-disabled-elt`. Cancel and the close button sit outside the form, and `test_every_dialog_disables_only_its_own_submit_buttons` holds the attribute to `find button[type='submit']`.
- Dialog footer buttons stop being the one full-size pair on a phone. This reverses a panel-framework rule. The skill text and both invariant copies change with it.
- The new dashboard rule goes in `fls-dev:educator-interface`, because it is about educator pages. The related sentence ("a panel that lists a thing … links to it") stays in `fls-dev:panel-framework` `### Placement`.

## Requirements

### 1. Learners list, Courses column

Files: `EI/templates/educator_interface/data-table-cells/learner_courses.html`, `learner_course_summaries` and `LearnerCourseSummary` in `EI/panels/learner/data_tables.py`, `LearnerDataTable`, and a new page-wide gather in `freedom_ls/reports/gather.py`.

**Row.**

- Each course is one row with three parts:
  - the course title, truncated, with its full text in `title`, and under it the existing muted "Shared by" line (`partials/course_shared_by.html`);
  - a thin progress bar;
  - the percentage as text.
- Rows are separated by space, at least 8px (`space-y-2`). There is no rule or box between them.

**Bar.**

- The bar is `<c-panel-progress-bar>`. Give it a size prop that draws the bar 4 to 6px tall and puts the percentage in a fixed-width, right-aligned `tabular-nums` span, so figures line up across rows.
- The bar is a native `<progress>`, which already exposes the `progressbar` role the idea asks for. Add no explicit `role`.
- Its accessible name names the course: "<course title> progress".
- Zero progress, or no course progress record at all, draws an empty bar and "0%".

**Cap and "+N more".**

- At most 3 courses show. The cap is one constant, and the view supplies the hidden count.
- With more than 3 courses, "+N more" follows, linking to the learner's Courses tab (`learners/{pk}/__tabs/courses`).
- The link's accessible name starts with its visible text and names the learner, for example "+2 more courses for Jane Smith".
- The link is a control with its own classes and has a 44px target on a phone.
- With 3 or fewer courses there is no link.

**Order, first to last.**

1. Flagged courses. A course is flagged if any of its registrations has at least one `AT_RISK_RULES` flag, evaluated as the Courses tab evaluates it.
2. In-progress courses (more than 0% and less than 100%).
3. Everything else.

Within each group, the latest `CourseProgress.last_accessed_time` comes first, and courses with no activity come last. Ties break by title. Latest activity is the maximum across a course's course progress records.

**Page-wide gather.** The flags and the percentage come from a new page-level gather in `freedom_ls/reports`:

- It runs once per page from a `LearnerDataTable.get_rows` override and attaches its results to the page's learners. `learner_course_summaries` reads them.
- It dedupes courses by `course_id` before calling `build_course_catalogue`.
- It loads progress with `load_progress_index` for all the page's learner pks, keyed by course progress record.
- Its query count does not grow with the number of learners or registrations on the page.
- It loads every course's items, and every course part's items, in one batched pass across all the page's courses, so the catalogue's query count does not grow with the number of distinct courses either.
- The percentage the cell shows is the gather's recomputed completion: the maximum across the course's registrations. The flags use the same figure, so the cell and the Courses tab agree.

**Export and phone card.**

- The CSV export (`get_export_columns`) keeps "the order found" and does not run the gather.
- The phone card renders the same cell inside its definition row (`data-table-card.html`): same cap, same order, with the bar filling the card's width.
- The empty state (`—` with "None") does not change.

### 2. Learner Courses tab

Files: `LearnerCoursesPanel` in `EI/panels/learner/panels.py`, `EI/templates/educator_interface/panels/learner_courses.html`.

**Table.** One `<c-panel-table cards="true" cards_when_narrow="true">`, one row per registration, in today's order, with a caption for screen readers and no entry-count line. Columns:

- **Course:** the title, then "via <cohort>" or "Individual", then the "Shared by" line.
- **Status:** `partials/learner_badges.html`.
- **Progress:** a bar and the percentage.
- **Items:** "N of M". This counts the course's children (topics and forms), as `completed_item_count` and `total_item_count` do today.
- **Last completed:** the title and date, or "Nothing completed yet".

**Card.** Each card puts the Course cell in its `content` slot. Status, Progress, Items and Last completed follow as `<c-panel-definition-row>`s.

**Sections below the table.**

- Each course with completed items or quiz results gets a section headed by its course title.
- The section holds today's "Completed items (N)" disclosure and the per-quiz attempt tables, unchanged.
- Sections are separated by space, never a border box.
- A course with neither gets no section.

The "Register for a course" footer button stays.

### 2a. Flag reasons

Files: `freedom_ls/reports/at_risk.py`, the flag type built by `_evaluate_at_risk_flags`, `EI/templates/educator_interface/partials/learner_badges.html`.

- Each rule declares whether its reason adds anything to its label. Add a field such as `reason_adds_information`. `NoRecordedActivityRule` sets it false. `FailedLatestQuizAttemptRule` and `InactiveForDaysRule` set it true.
- The flag carries the field. The partial prints a reason only when the field is true. The template names no rule id, so adding a rule still needs no rendering edit.
- Every surface that includes the partial follows the same rule: the Courses tab, the dashboard, the cohort report and the cohort needs-attention list. Update the partial's docstring to match.
- `NoRecordedActivityRule.evaluate` keeps its string, so report downloads keep printing it.

### 3. Learner quick view

File: `EI/templates/educator_interface/quick_views/learner.html`.

- Remove `border-b border-border pb-3` from the Messages heading row.
- The `border-t` on the section, above the heading, stays. No other rule appears under the heading.

### 4. Learner Settings tab

Files: `LearnerNamePanel` (`EI/panels/learner/panels.py`), `EI/templates/educator_interface/panels/learner_name.html`.

- The Name panel's body opens with a one-row `<c-panel-definition-list>` showing "Email" and the learner's address as plain text.
- The row sits above the form and outside the `<form>`, so a 422 swap keeps it and a Save never posts it.
- Long addresses wrap. The row is not an input and has no hint.
- The email shows only where the Name panel shows, to people who may rename the learner. The Overview row and the page subtitle do not change.

### 5. Dashboard

Files: `EI/templates/educator_interface/panels/dashboard_region.html`, `EI/panels/dashboard/panels.py`.

- **Learners tile.** Add an `href` to the learners list (`path_string='learners'`) and an `aria-label` naming its count, for example "12 learners", the same as the Cohorts tile.
- **Cohort names.** Each cohort name stays a link to the cohort page, and a quick view trigger from `panel_framework/partials/quick_view_button.html` sits beside it (`url` = cohort URL + `/__quick-view`), as a learner name has:
  - in an attention row, the trigger follows the cohort name inline;
  - in the Cohort progress card, it goes in `<c-panel-list-row>`'s trailing slot, beside the progress bar.
- **Course names.** Each course name in an attention row's "Courses" list links to that course's educator page (`courses/{pk}`). Link it only if the course is visible to the viewer in the organisation (`courses_visible_to`, the check the course page itself applies, so the link never 404s). `DashboardPanel` supplies the visible course ids. Any other course stays plain text.
- **Over-limit callout.** It becomes a template sentence that renders `excluded.cohort` as a link to its cohort page. `over_limit_message` stays for the places that print plain text.

### 6. Bulk confirm dialog, in-request run

Files:

- `Alpine.data("appModal")` in `PF/static/panel_framework/js/alpine-components.js`
- `PF/templates/panel_framework/partials/bulk_confirmation.html`
- `PF/templates/panel_framework/partials/bulk_confirmation_form.html`
- `PF/templates/cotton/modal-footer.html`
- `PF/templates/cotton/panel-close-button.html`

**The marker.** The confirm `<form>` carries a marker only when the run will happen in the request, that is when `BulkAction.runs_in_background(count)` is false (the view already computes `in_background`). No other form carries it.

**The `running` state in `appModal`.**

- `htmx:beforeRequest` sets it when `event.detail.elt` is the marked form.
- `htmx:afterRequest` clears it, whatever the outcome, and so does the dialog's `close` event.
- The picker's refresh requests do not set it.

**While `running`:**

- The submit button shows its loading text, as it does today.
- The footer Cancel and the header close button are disabled. `<c-modal-footer>` and `<c-panel-close-button>` own the binding, which reads as false wherever the scope has no `running` (quick-view drawers, the sheet host). Their `href` link forms, rendered without JavaScript, have no binding.
- Esc does nothing. Both the native `cancel` event and the `keydown` Esc handler check `running`, because Chromium allows only one prevented cancel without a user activation. A backdrop click already does nothing on a dialog that holds a form.
- A sentence in the dialog body says why: "This is running and can't be stopped. Wait for it to finish." It sits in a `role="status"` element that renders, empty, only for in-request runs and fills when the run starts, so screen readers announce it. Cancel points at it through `cancel_describedby`, and the close button through a new `describedby` prop on `<c-panel-close-button>`.

**When the request ends.**

- Success: the result replaces the body, as today, and the controls work again.
- A 422: everything is re-enabled and the form's errors show.
- A 5xx, a network error or a timeout: everything is re-enabled and a red `role="alert"` line shows in the dialog: "This did not finish. Some changes may not have been made. Reload the list to see which, then run it again." The bulk confirm form renders a `[data-request-error]` element carrying this text as `data-request-error-message`; the framework's request-error handler prefers that attribute over its generic message, and it handles `htmx:timeout` as well as `htmx:responseError` and `htmx:sendError`.

A run that goes to the background does not change: the dialog closes and the selection bar shows progress.

### 7. Dialog footer buttons on phones

Files:

- `PF/css/panel_framework.css` (`.panel-btn`) and `tailwind.components.css` (`.btn`). These change together.
- `PF/templates/cotton/modal-footer.html`
- `PF/HOST_CONTRACT.md`

- Below `md`, a dialog footer's buttons are the small size, about 32px to look at. The same invisible `::after` as every other button gives them a 44px hit area. The `[data-modal-footer] … min-h-11` rule goes from both CSS files.
- Each button is as wide as its label, and the gap between them keeps the hit areas from overlapping.
- The footer's 16px vertical padding holds the 6px the hit area reaches above and below the button, so the dialog's `overflow: hidden` doesn't clip it.
- Keep `max-sm:flex-wrap`. Remove it only if the Playwright test shows the longest real footer pair wrapping at 375px.
- Above `md`, nothing changes.

## Testing

Each item gets a failing test first.

**1. Courses column**

- The cell caps at 3 courses and shows "+N more". The link goes to the Courses tab, and the learner's name is in its accessible name. There's no link with 3 or fewer courses.
- Order: a flagged course outranks a more recent in-progress one; in-progress outranks complete and not-started; recency orders within a group. Include a course held both through a cohort and individually, where only one registration is flagged.
- Each bar is named for its course.
- The query count is the same for a page of 1 learner and a page of several learners on different courses, not one course repeated.
- The phone card renders the capped, ordered cell with its "+N more" link.
- The export keeps the order found.
- The five existing Courses-cell tests in `EI/tests/panels/learner/test_data_tables.py` stay green. The two that check a percentage (40%; 70% and not 20%) keep their assertions, and their fixtures seed a course with items and completed topic progress per course progress record, because the cell now shows recomputed completion rather than `CourseProgress.progress_percentage`.

**2. Courses tab**

- The tab renders one table row per registration, with the five columns, and a card with the same fields.
- A course with quiz results or completed items gets a section below the table, headed by its title. A course with neither gets no section.
- No template hand-writes a `<table>` (the existing invariant).

**2a. Flag reasons**

- The `no_activity` reason is absent and a `failed_latest_quiz` reason is present. Test this through the partial, on the dashboard, in the cohort report and in the needs-attention list.
- `EI/tests/panels/cohort/test_report_panels.py:570` changes, because it asserts the no-activity reason on screen. The at-risk and download tests in `freedom_ls/reports/tests/` stay as they are.

**3. Quick view**

- `test_the_messages_heading_sits_above_a_rule_with_room_below` in `EI/tests/panels/learner/test_quick_view.py` becomes a test that the heading row has no `border-b`.
- The stale docstring of `test_the_composer_draws_no_rule_of_its_own_under_the_messages_heading` is reworded.

**4. Settings tab**

- The Settings tab shows the email above `form#learner-name-form`, outside the form.
- A viewer who may not rename the learner does not see it there.

**5. Dashboard**

- The Learners tile is one link to the learners list, named by its count.
- Each cohort name in the attention rows and the Cohort progress card has a quick view trigger whose `hx-get` ends `/__quick-view`.
- A course visible to the viewer in the organisation is a link. Any other course is plain text.
- The over-limit callout's cohort name is a link to the cohort.

**6. Bulk dialog**

- A Playwright test holds the POST with `page.route` and checks that:
  - Cancel and the close button are disabled;
  - Esc pressed twice leaves the dialog open;
  - the reason text is visible;
  - everything is re-enabled after a held 422 response.
- A background-sized run's form carries no marker and renders no status element.

**7. Footer buttons**

- `test_only_the_dialog_footer_makes_a_panel_button_44px_tall_on_a_phone` (`PF/tests/invariants/test_templates.py`) and `test_only_the_dialog_footer_makes_a_button_44px_tall_on_a_phone` (`freedom_ls/base/tests/invariants/test_panel_template_rules.py`) are each replaced by a test that no button rule sets `min-h-11`.
- In `EI/tests/playwright/test_touch_targets.py`, rewrite the synthetic `[data-modal-footer]` fixture and the flow test's "Cancel is 44px tall" assertion. At 375px, footer buttons look under 40px tall, have a hit area of at least 44px, and their hit areas don't overlap.
- At 1280px, footer buttons keep today's size.

## Open questions

- Q: Does "every thing the dashboard names" include course names and the over-limit callout's cohort name? → A: Yes, both link (assumed default: the idea's rule says "every thing". Overrule by editing Requirements §5.)
- Q: Should the email show on Settings to someone who can see the learner but not rename them? → A: No. It lives in the Name panel, which they don't see, and the page subtitle already shows the email. (Assumed default, because each panel has one job. To overrule, edit Requirements §4.)

- Q: How does the gather keep its query count flat when the page's learners hold many different courses? → A: A batched loader fills every course's and course part's items in one pass. (Decided by the user while planning.)
- Q: The cell shows recomputed completion, so the two existing percentage tests seed only `progress_percentage` and would read 0%. → A: Keep the recomputed figure and reseed those tests' fixtures with real completions. (Decided by the user while planning.)

## Success criteria

- The query count for a page of learners does not change with page size.
- "Has not started any course item." appears on no educator page, and still appears in report downloads.
- At 375px, dialog footer buttons are under 40px tall to look at and have a hit area of at least 44px.
- During an in-request bulk run, pressing Esc twice, clicking Cancel or clicking the close button leaves the dialog open.
