# Spec: Educator interface page polish

## Purpose

QA on the shipped educator interface found seven pages that are hard to scan or controls that behave unexpectedly (`idea.md`). This spec fixes each one in place. No new pages, models or permissions.

Paths below use EI = `freedom_ls/educator_interface`, PF = `freedom_ls/panel_framework`. File-level detail for every item is in `research_codebase.md`.

## Scope

In:

1. Learners list: Courses column.
2. Learner page: Courses tab.
3. Learner quick view: the rule under "Messages".
4. Learner page: Settings tab shows the email.
5. Dashboard: everything it names is one click away.
6. Bulk confirm dialog: locked while an in-request run is under way.
7. Dialog footer buttons on phones.

Out: show/hide columns and filters (`educator-interface-data-table-columns-and-filters`), help icons (`educator-interface-contextual-help`), messaging from the learner page (`educator-interface-message-from-anywhere`, which also changes the learner quick view and learner page; whichever lands second rebases). Registration actions stay off the Courses tab. Page-header actions are already `size="small"` and do not change.

## Decisions

- Layouts are described here, with no Claude Design step (`answers.md`).
- The flag reason that only repeats its badge is hidden on every page that uses `partials/learner_badges.html`, so a learner's badges read the same everywhere. `NoRecordedActivityRule.evaluate` keeps its string, so report downloads and the at-risk tests keep it.
- The bulk dialog lock is driven by Alpine state in `appModal`. `hx-disabled-elt` cannot do it: Cancel and the close button sit outside the form, and `test_every_dialog_disables_only_its_own_submit_buttons` holds the attribute to `find button[type='submit']`.
- Dialog footer buttons become small on a phone, with the 44px hit area every other small button has. This reverses the panel-framework rule that the footer pair is the one full-size pair. The skill text and its tests change in the same work.
- The new dashboard rule goes in `fls-dev:educator-interface`, because it is about educator pages. The related sentence ("a panel that lists a thing … links to it") is in `fls-dev:panel-framework` `### Placement`, and stays there.

## Requirements

### 1. Learners list, Courses column

Files: `EI/templates/educator_interface/data-table-cells/learner_courses.html`, `learner_course_summaries` and `LearnerCourseSummary` in `EI/panels/learner/data_tables.py`, `LearnerDataTable.get_queryset`.

- Each course is one row: the course title, a thin progress bar and the percentage as text. The title truncates with its full text in `title`. The percentage is `tabular-nums` and right-aligned, so the figures line up.
- Rows sit apart with space, at least 8px (`space-y-2`), with no rule or box between them. The skill's list-value rule (`space-y-1`) gets a carve-out for this cell.
- The bar is `<c-panel-progress-bar>` with a thin size (4 to 6px). Add a size prop if there isn't one. Its accessible name names the course ("<course title> progress"). It is a native `<progress>`, so it gets no extra `role`. Zero progress still draws an empty bar with "0%". A course with no progress record shows "0%" too.
- At most 3 courses show. The cap is one constant, and the template reads the hidden count from the view. With more than 3 courses, "+N more" follows: a link to the learner's Courses tab (`learners/{pk}/__tabs/courses`). Its visible text is "+N more" and its accessible name starts with that text and names the learner ("+2 more courses for Jane Smith"). It is a control with its own classes and a 44px target on a phone. With 3 or fewer courses there's no link.
- Order, shown first to last:
  1. Courses with at least one at-risk flag, evaluated by `AT_RISK_RULES` exactly as the Courses tab evaluates them.
  2. In-progress courses (more than 0% and less than 100%).
  3. Everything else.

  Within each group, the most recent `CourseProgress.last_accessed_time` comes first, and courses with no activity come after those that have some. Ties break by title. The latest activity is the maximum across a course's progress records, the same way the percentage already takes the furthest.
- `LearnerCourseSummary` gains whatever the order needs (latest activity, flagged or not). The number of queries for a page of learners must not grow with the page size. `load_progress_index` already takes a list of learner pks, so flags can be gathered for the whole page at once.
- The phone card renders the same cell inside its definition row (`data-table-card.html`). It shows the same cap, the same order, and the bar grows to fill the card's width.
- The empty state (`—` with "None") is unchanged.

### 2. Learner Courses tab

Files: `LearnerCoursesPanel` in `EI/panels/learner/panels.py`, `EI/templates/educator_interface/panels/learner_courses.html`.

- One `<c-panel-table cards="true" cards_when_narrow="true">` with one row per course, in today's order. Columns:
  - **Course:** the title, then "via <cohort>" or "Individual", then the "Shared by" line from `partials/course_shared_by.html`.
  - **Status:** `partials/learner_badges.html`.
  - **Progress:** a bar and the percentage.
  - **Items:** "N of M".
  - **Last completed:** the title and date, or "Nothing completed yet".
- The table has a caption for screen readers. The footer states the entry count, as the framework does for every table.
- Below the table, each course with completed items or quiz results gets a section headed by its course title. The section holds today's "Completed items (N)" disclosure and the per-quiz attempt tables, unchanged. Sections are separated by space, never a border box. A course with neither gets no section.
- The "Register for a course" footer button stays.

### 2a. Flag reasons

File: `EI/templates/educator_interface/partials/learner_badges.html`.

- A flag's reason prints beside its badge only when it says more than the badge. The `no_activity` reason ("Has not started any course item.") is never printed. The `failed_latest_quiz` and `inactive` reasons keep printing.
- The partial decides this itself. Every surface that includes it shows the same result: the learner Courses tab, the dashboard, the cohort report and the cohort needs-attention list. The docstring says how a reason is chosen.

### 3. Learner quick view

File: `EI/templates/educator_interface/quick_views/learner.html`.

- Remove `border-b border-border pb-3` from the Messages heading row. The rule above the section (`border-t` on the section) stays, and no other rule appears under the heading.

### 4. Learner Settings tab

Files: `LearnerNamePanel` (`EI/panels/learner/panels.py`), `EI/templates/educator_interface/panels/learner_name.html`.

- The Name panel's body opens with a one-row `<c-panel-definition-list>`: "Email" and the learner's address as plain text. It sits above the form, outside the `<form>`, so a 422 swap keeps it and a Save never posts it. Long addresses wrap. It is not an input and adds no hint.
- The email shows only where the Name panel shows, to people who may rename the learner. The Overview row and the page subtitle are unchanged.

### 5. Dashboard

Files: `EI/templates/educator_interface/panels/dashboard_region.html`, `EI/panels/dashboard/panels.py`.

- The Learners tile gets an `href` to the learners list (`path_string='learners'`) and an `aria-label` naming its count ("12 learners"), as the Cohorts tile does.
- Every cohort name on the dashboard opens the cohort quick view, the same way a learner name does, through `panel_framework/partials/quick_view_button.html` (`url` = the cohort URL + `/__quick-view`). This covers the attention row's cohort list and the Cohort progress rows. Without JavaScript, the name still goes to the cohort page.
- Every course name the dashboard prints links to that course's educator page. This covers the attention row's "Courses" list.
- A cohort named in an over-limit callout (`ExcludedCohort.message`) links to its cohort page.

### 6. Bulk confirm dialog, in-request run

Files: `Alpine.data("appModal")` in `PF/static/panel_framework/js/alpine-components.js`, `PF/templates/panel_framework/partials/bulk_confirmation.html`, `bulk_confirmation_form.html`, `PF/templates/cotton/modal-footer.html`, `PF/templates/cotton/panel-close-button.html`.

- `appModal` has a `running` state. It is set when a form inside `#app-modal-body` starts its request and the server has rendered that form as an in-request run (`BulkAction.runs_in_background(count)` is false; the form carries a marker). It clears when the request ends, whether it succeeded or failed.
- While `running`:
  - The submit button shows its loading text, as it does today.
  - The footer Cancel and the header close button are disabled. `<c-modal-footer>` and `<c-panel-close-button>` own this; no dialog template writes it by hand.
  - Esc does nothing: the native `cancel` event is prevented. A backdrop click already does nothing on a dialog that holds a form.
  - A sentence in the dialog body says why: "This is running and can't be stopped. Wait for it to finish." It sits in a `role="status"` element that is present before the run starts, so screen readers announce it. Cancel and the close button point at it with `aria-describedby`.
- When the request ends: on success, the result replaces the body as today and the controls work again. On an error (422 or 5xx), everything is re-enabled and the error shows.
- A run that goes to the background is unchanged: the dialog closes and the selection bar shows progress.

### 7. Dialog footer buttons on phones

Files: `PF/css/panel_framework.css` and `tailwind.components.css` (the `.panel-btn` and `.btn` copies change together), `PF/templates/cotton/modal-footer.html`, `PF/HOST_CONTRACT.md`.

- Below `md`, a dialog footer's buttons are the small size, about 32px to look at. The same invisible `::after` gives them a 44px hit area. The `[data-modal-footer] … min-h-11` rule goes.
- The footer buttons stay on one line, each as wide as its label. The gap between them stays wide enough that their hit areas don't overlap. No ancestor of the footer clips the hit area.
- Above `md`, nothing changes.

## Testing

Each item gets a failing test first.

- **1:**
  - The cell caps at 3 courses and shows "+N more", linked to the Courses tab, with the learner in its accessible name.
  - There's no link with 3 or fewer courses.
  - Order: a flagged course outranks a more recent in-progress one, in-progress outranks complete and not-started, and recency orders within a group.
  - Each bar is named for its course.
  - The query count for a page of learners is the same for 1 learner and for many.
  - The five existing Courses-cell tests in `EI/tests/panels/learner/test_data_tables.py` stay green.
- **2:**
  - The Courses tab renders one table row per course with the five columns.
  - A course with quiz results or completed items gets a section below the table headed by its title, and one with neither gets none.
  - There's no hand-written `<table>` (the existing invariant).
- **2a:**
  - The `no_activity` reason is absent and a `failed_latest_quiz` reason is present. Test this through the partial and on the dashboard, the cohort report and the needs-attention list.
  - `EI/tests/panels/cohort/test_report_panels.py:570`, which asserts the no-activity reason on screen, changes. The at-risk and download tests in `freedom_ls/reports/tests/` stay as they are.
- **3:** `test_the_messages_heading_sits_above_a_rule_with_room_below` in `EI/tests/panels/learner/test_quick_view.py` becomes a test that the heading row has no `border-b`. The stale docstring of `test_the_composer_draws_no_rule_of_its_own_under_the_messages_heading` is reworded.
- **4:** The Settings tab shows the email above `form#learner-name-form` and outside it.
- **5:**
  - The Learners tile is one link to the learners list, named by its count.
  - Each cohort name in the attention rows and the Cohort progress card has a quick view trigger with `hx-get` ending `/__quick-view`.
  - Each course name links to the course.
- **6:** A Playwright test holds the POST with `page.route` and checks four things: Cancel and the close button are disabled, Esc leaves the dialog open, the reason text is visible, and everything is re-enabled after a held 422 response. A template test checks that a background-sized run's form carries no in-request marker.
- **7:**
  - `test_only_the_dialog_footer_makes_a_panel_button_44px_tall_on_a_phone` is replaced by a test that no button rule sets `min-h-11`.
  - `EI/tests/playwright/test_touch_targets.py` checks that footer buttons at 375px look small (under 40px) with a hit area of at least 44px, and that their hit areas don't overlap.
- Run `uv run pytest freedom_ls/panel_framework/tests/invariants freedom_ls/base/tests/invariants freedom_ls/educator_interface/tests/invariants` and `test_touch_targets.py` after the template changes.

## Open questions

- Q: Does "every thing the dashboard names" include course names and the over-limit callout's cohort name? → A: Yes, both link (assumed default: the idea's rule says "every thing". Overrule by editing Requirements §5.)
- Q: Should the email show on Settings to someone who can see the learner but not rename them? → A: No. It lives in the Name panel, which they don't see, and the page subtitle already shows the email (assumed default: one job per panel. Overrule by editing Requirements §4.)

## Success criteria

- At 375px and at 1920px, the Learners list Courses cell shows at most 3 courses, each with a bar and a percentage, then "+N more" leading to the Courses tab.
- A learner with a flagged course sees it first in that cell.
- The learner Courses tab is one table, followed by a titled section for each course that has completed items or quizzes, with no border boxes.
- "Has not started any course item." appears on no educator page.
- The learner quick view has no rule under "Messages".
- The learner Settings tab shows the email, read-only, above the name form.
- On the dashboard, the Learners tile, each cohort name and each course name is a link, and cohort names open the quick view.
- During an in-request bulk run, Cancel, the close button and Esc do nothing, and the dialog says why.
- At 375px, dialog footer buttons are under 40px tall to look at and have a hit area of at least 44px.
- The invariant suites and `test_touch_targets.py` pass.
