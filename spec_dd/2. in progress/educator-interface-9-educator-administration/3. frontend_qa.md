# Frontend QA: member administration

This change adds a Members section under a new "Administration" group in the educator interface.
From it an organisation admin (or a site-level admin) can list the people who hold a role in the
organisation, add a member by email (an existing account or a brand-new one that receives the
setup email), change the cohorts a cohort role covers, remove a role, and suspend or reactivate a
member. Every change that widens access or takes it away is confirmed inside the dialog first. The
cohort overview's Educators panel gains an "Add member" action with the cohort preset, and
deleting a cohort now removes the role rows that pointed at it.

What a browser can see:

- **The members list** shows one row per member: an avatar chip with name and email, markers
  ("You", "Suspended", "Account turned off", "Also a learner"), one entry per role held with its
  scope and who added it and when, and per-entry and per-row actions. It searches, filters by role,
  pages at ten, and has three empty states.
- **Add member** is a dialog with an email lookup, radio cards for the role (with descriptions), a
  cohort checklist with search and a count, and a confirmation step.
- **Remove**, **Change cohorts**, **Suspend** and **Reactivate** each confirm before writing.
- **The cohort overview's Educators panel** offers Add member with the cohort preset and refreshes
  when a member is added.

Beyond the golden path, this run hunts for:

- **Over-granting.** A cohort admin or cohort viewer reaching the Members section, the Add member
  action on a cohort page, or any row action by URL.
- **Self-change and lock-out.** Nobody can change their own roles; an organisation admin cannot
  remove or suspend the last organisation admin; a site-level admin can, and is warned.
- **Stale pages.** A second tab that still offers an action after the member changed must answer
  with a reason in the dialog, never silently succeed or silently do nothing.
- **Enumeration.** Another organisation's members, cohorts and actions answer 404; the other-site
  email message never reveals that account's name.
- **Repeat submissions.** Adding a role already held, changing cohorts to the same set, suspending
  an already-suspended member: each is refused in the dialog and writes nothing.
- **Site-level admins.** They work in every organisation but are listed only through a role they
  hold there; their site-level access is never touched from here.
- **Mid-session removal.** A person whose role is removed loses access on their next request.
