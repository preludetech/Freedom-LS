# Spec: Educator administration

Spec 9 of 12 in the educator interface rebuild (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`, "Educator interface
rebuild"). Source: `idea.md`. Research beside it: `research_codebase.md`, `research_prior_art.md`,
`research_best_practices.md`, `research_reference_implementations.md`, `research_ux_patterns.md`,
`research_known_complaints.md`.

## Purpose

Today the only way to give someone an educator role is a shell or a QA helper (`idea.md`, "Why").
This spec adds an Educators section to the educator interface. From it, an admin can list, add,
re-scope, remove, suspend and reactivate the people who hold a role in an organisation. Every
change that widens or removes access is confirmed first.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| educator | narrowed | Narrowed from the everyday "educators" in `docs/product/educator-interface.md`, and distinct from the glossary's "educator of a learner" (`educators_of`). Here it means a user holding an active `organisation_admin`, `cohort_admin` or `cohort_viewer` `ObjectRoleAssignment` on this organisation or one of its cohorts. Suspended members are included. |
| suspended (organisation member) | narrowed | An educator whose `OrganisationMember` row is inactive or missing. The word comes from the `set_organisation_member_active` docstring. Their assignments stay active, but `can()` ignores them. This is not `Learner` removal. |
| widening | coined | A change after which a person can do something they could not do before: a new role, a new cohort in a scope, or reactivation. No existing term covers this. |

## Scope

In:

- An Educators section, listed under each organisation's navigation. It lists educators, with
  search and a role filter.
- Add an educator by email. The address matches an existing user, or an account is created
  through spec 7's path. The admin picks one role and, for cohort roles, the cohorts.
- Change the cohorts on a cohort role. Remove a role. Suspend and reactivate an `OrganisationMember`.
- Confirmations for widening and for loss of access.
- Rewritten role descriptions, used in the form, the confirmations and the product docs.
- An add action on the cohort overview's educators panel (`CohortEducatorsPanel`).
- Cleanup of role assignments and guardian permissions when a cohort is deleted.
- Three generic panel framework additions the section needs (R4).

Out:

- New roles, custom roles, editing permissions one by one, and a read-only "roles and permissions"
  page.
- Granting, removing or listing `site_admin`, and anything else site-level. A site admin appears
  only through an organisation or cohort role they hold in this organisation.
- Changing a role in place. Roles add up: to move someone from cohort viewer to cohort admin, add
  the new role and remove the old one.
- Notifying the person of a grant or removal. A new account still gets the setup email.
- Resending the setup email to an educator, a "pending" marker, last activity, undo, and an
  educator detail page.
- Whatever the Django admin does with `SystemRoleAssignment` or `SiteRoleAssignment`.

## Decisions

1. **Only the checked entry points.** Views call `assign_role`, `remove_role` and
   `set_organisation_member_active` in `freedom_ls/learner_management/role_assignment.py`. They
   never call the raw `role_based_permissions/utils.py` functions. The grant check, the self check
   and the last-admin locks live in the checked functions and must not be duplicated.
2. **The three educator roles are a fixed tuple.** `EDUCATOR_ROLES = ("organisation_admin",
   "cohort_admin", "cohort_viewer")`. A downstream role in `get_role_config` is never listed or
   offered, because it has no `assign_` capability.
3. **One function decides what a grantor may hand out.** `grantable_roles` is the source for every
   form choice and every POST validation in this spec, so the two cannot drift. `assign_role` and
   `remove_role` stay the authoritative check.
4. **A match shows the account's name and email before confirming.** An organisation groups
   people inside a site; it does not isolate them (`docs/product/multi-tenancy-and-isolation.md`).
   Showing the match is what makes a typo visible.
5. **Cohort deletion deletes the cohort's role rows, not deactivates them.** Once the target is
   gone, deactivated rows would still leave guardian `UserObjectPermission` rows behind, and
   assignments whose `target` resolves to `None` would trip up the list's prefetch. Spec 11's audit
   log is where the history lives.
6. **Reactivating an assignment resets `assigned_at`.** "Added on" then means the latest grant,
   not the first one.
7. **The framework gaps are fixed in `panel_framework`, generically.** Working around them inside
   `educator_interface` would need an educator detail page nobody asked for.

## Requirements

### R1. Role descriptions (`freedom_ls/role_based_permissions/roles.py`)

Replace `description` on the four `BASE_ROLES` entries. These strings are the single source. The
form, the confirmations, the help paragraph and the product docs all read `Role.description` or
copy it word for word.

| Role | `description` |
| --- | --- |
| `site_admin` | Full access to every organisation on this site. |
| `organisation_admin` | Manages learners, cohorts and educators in this organisation. |
| `cohort_admin` | Manages the learners and course registrations in their cohorts. |
| `cohort_viewer` | Sees the learners and progress in their cohorts, and changes nothing. |

`display_name` values stay as they are.

### R2. Grant rules (`freedom_ls/learner_management/role_assignment.py`)

- Add `EDUCATOR_ROLES` (Decision 2).
- Add `grantable_roles(grantor: User, organisation: Organisation) -> list[GrantableRole]`, where
  `GrantableRole` is a frozen dataclass of `role: str` and `cohorts: list[Cohort] | None`.
  - `organisation_admin` is included when `can(grantor, ASSIGN.format(role=...), organisation)`.
    Its `cohorts` is `None`.
  - A cohort role is included with the organisation's active cohorts on which that check passes,
    and left out when there are none.
  - It is computed once per request and reused by every row cell and action, so its cost does not
    grow with the number of rows.
- Add `is_widening(before: set[tuple[str, str]], after: set[tuple[str, str]]) -> bool`. Each pair
  is a role and a target key, which is the organisation's pk or a cohort's pk. It returns true when
  `after` holds any pair `before` lacks. It is a pure function, unit tested on its own.

### R3. Reactivation timestamp (`freedom_ls/role_based_permissions/utils.py`)

When `assign_object_role` reactivates an inactive row, it sets `assigned_at` to now, along with
`is_active` and `assigned_by`. `assign_site_role` does the same.

### R4. The Educators section

Panel framework additions (`freedom_ls/panel_framework/views.py`), each tested in
`panel_framework`:

- A `view_capability` on `SectionConfigBase`, checked against `get_scope`. `_build_menu_items`
  leaves out a section the user lacks it for. The list root answers spec 5's denied response.
- A list root can render row-scoped actions. `_main_for` keeps `renders_in_footer = False`
  actions out of the page header, and `ListViewPanel` exposes them to cell templates through
  `row_action_urls`.
- `ListViewConfig` may have no `instance_view`. `walk_interface`
  (`educator_interface/tests/interface_walk.py`) skips the detail walk for such a list.

`EducatorConfig(OrganisationSectionConfig, ListViewConfig)` in
`freedom_ls/educator_interface/views.py`:

- `model = User`, no `instance_view`, no row link, and a `check_access_exempt_reason`.
- `view_capability` is `freedom_ls_learner_management.view_organisationmember` on the
  organisation, so cohort admins and cohort viewers never see the section.
- Register it in `interface_config` under a new `NavGroup("Administration", [EducatorConfig])`.
- It must pass `invariants/test_config_authorisation.py` and `invariants/test_permission_matrix.py`
  with no new capability strings.

`EducatorDataTable(DataTable)`:

- A `User` queryset with one row per person who holds an active assignment in `EDUCATOR_ROLES`,
  either on this organisation or on one of its cohorts (active or not). The holder's
  `User.is_active` must be true.
- The list is built from assignments, not `OrganisationMember` rows, because spec 5 shipped no
  backfill of member rows.
- Each row prefetches those assignments with their targets and `assigned_by`, plus the person's
  `OrganisationMember` and `Learner` for this organisation. The list must not issue a query per
  row.

Columns:

- Name, with email as the secondary line. A person with no name shows their email.
- Markers beside the name:
  - "You" for the viewer.
  - "Suspended" when the `OrganisationMember` row is inactive or missing.
  - "Also a learner" when an active `Learner` exists in this organisation. This marker is neutral
    in tone, not a warning.
- Roles. One entry per role held: the role's `display_name` and its scope.
  - The scope reads "Whole organisation" for `organisation_admin`.
  - For a cohort role it lists cohort names: the first two, then "+N more". The full list is
    available in the cell. An inactive cohort carries "(inactive)".
- Added by and added on, per role entry, from the newest active assignment for that role. A null
  `assigned_by` reads "Not recorded".

Search, filter and surrounding copy:

- `search_fields` covers first name, last name and email. Rows are sorted by name.
- `EducatorRoleFilter` in `educator_interface/filters.py` is modelled on `LearnerStatusFilter`. It
  offers "All roles" (the default) and the three roles.
- One help paragraph above the table explains roles in plain words. The role descriptions appear in
  the add form, not in the table.
- Three empty states follow the voice in `brand-guidelines`:
  - No matches, with a clear-filters link.
  - "No educators in this organisation yet", with the add action when it is offered.
  - An organisation whose only educator is the viewer.

### R5. Row actions (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/actions.py`, cell templates in `templates/educator_interface/data-table-cells/`)

Remove and Change cohorts act on the role in their row. At render time, `get_capability` returns
`assign_<role>` when the request carries the role. Otherwise it returns `assign_cohort_viewer`,
asked on the organisation, which keeps the matrix walk static. `assign_role` and `remove_role`
make the authoritative check on each target.

Per role entry:

- **Change cohorts**, for cohort roles. The admin ticks cohorts from that role's `grantable_roles`
  entry.
  - Cohorts outside that entry, and inactive cohorts the person already holds, are shown
    read-only and left untouched.
  - The confirmation names added cohorts as a widening and removed cohorts as a loss. A change
    that does both names both.
  - A change that removes every cohort uses the remove wording and removes the role.
  - All calls run in one `transaction.atomic()`.
- **Remove**. The confirmation names the person, the role, the scope and what they lose. It also
  names what is kept: their account, their other roles and any `Learner`.
  - When a site-level admin removes an organisation's last `organisation_admin`, the confirmation
    adds that the organisation will have no organisation admin.
  - When the target holds an active `site_admin` on this site or is a superuser, the confirmation
    drops the "loses" wording and says their site-level access is unaffected.
  - Removing someone's last role leaves their `OrganisationMember` row as it is. They drop off the
    list.

Per row:

- **Suspend** and **Reactivate**, with capability `change_organisationmember`, through
  `set_organisation_member_active`.
  - Suspend confirms like a removal: "keeps their roles, can do nothing in this organisation until
    reactivated". When a site-level admin suspends the last `organisation_admin`, it adds the same
    no-organisation-admin warning as Remove.
  - Reactivate confirms as a widening and lists the roles that take effect again. With no member
    row, it calls `ensure_organisation_member` and then `set_organisation_member_active(..., True)`.
  - Both are hidden when the target holds an active `site_admin` on this site or is a superuser.

The viewer's own row shows no actions. In their place are a lock icon and "You can't change your
own roles. Ask another organisation admin." Actions the viewer lacks the capability for are hidden.

Each successful action fires `EDUCATOR_CHANGED` (`educator_interface/events.py`).

### R6. Add an educator (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/actions.py`, `forms.py`, `freedom_ls/accounts/account_setup.py`)

`AddEducatorAction(PanelAction)` is a top-level action on `EducatorConfig`. It is offered when
`grantable_roles` is non-empty. It uses a plain `forms.Form`, following `AddLearnersAction`, and
invalid input answers 422.

Extract the read-only part of `match_or_create_user` into a lookup helper in `account_setup.py`.
The helper strips the address, calls `normalize_email`, matches with `email__iexact`, and makes the
other-site check. `match_or_create_user` then calls it.

The dialog's steps:

1. **Email.** An htmx lookup through the helper creates nothing. It shows one of three things:
   - "Existing account: <name>, <email>".
   - "No account yet. One will be created and a setup email sent." For a new address, the form
     adds optional first-name and last-name fields.
   - The `EmailInUseOnAnotherSite` message, never that account's name.
2. **Role.** Radio cards, one per `grantable_roles` entry, each showing `display_name` with
   `description` under it. Nothing is preselected. When the organisation has no active cohorts,
   the cohort roles are absent and a line says "This organisation has no active cohorts yet."
3. **Cohorts**, for cohort roles. Checkboxes over that entry's `cohorts`, with a search box and a
   selected count. At least one cohort is required.
4. **Confirmation**, inside the same dialog. It restates the role description and the scope, and
   says whether an account will be created.
   - If the person already holds roles here, it shows their current roles beside what they will
     have after.
   - Cohorts they already hold for that role are named and skipped. If nothing new remains, the
     form answers 422: "<name> already holds this role on these cohorts."
   - If the person is suspended, it says the role takes effect only once they are reactivated.

On submit, everything runs inside one `transaction.atomic()`:

1. `match_or_create_user(site, email, first, last)`.
2. `assign_role` once per target.
3. If the account was created, `send_account_setup_email(user, organisation.name)`, which sends on
   commit.

A refusal rolls back the new account too.

Error copy, each answered at 422 inside the dialog:

| Cause | Message |
| --- | --- |
| `EmailInUseOnAnotherSite` | This address belongs to an account on another site. Use a different address. |
| `RefusalReason.INACTIVE_USER` | This person's account is turned off, so they can't be given a role. |
| `RefusalReason.SELF` | You can't change your own roles. Ask another organisation admin. |
| `RefusalReason.LAST_ORGANISATION_ADMIN` | <name> is this organisation's only organisation admin. Add another organisation admin first. |

`RefusalReason.NOT_PERMITTED` gets spec 5's 403 denied response (`denied_response`), whichever
action raised it. R5's actions and R7 use the same mapping.

### R7. Cohort overview educators panel (`CohortEducatorsPanel` in `views.py`)

- `get_actions()` returns an `AddEducatorToCohortAction`. It is `AddEducatorAction` with the cohort
  preset as a hidden input, following `AddLearnersToCohortAction`.
- Its role choices are the cohort roles that `grantable_roles` allows on that cohort. The action is
  offered only when there is at least one.
- The panel refreshes on `EDUCATOR_CHANGED`, as it does today.

### R8. Stale submissions

Every action re-reads the target assignment or member row when it is submitted. If the assignment
is no longer active, or the cohort has left scope, the action answers with spec 5's
`action_unavailable` fragment. It must not silently change nothing.

### R9. Cohort deletion cleanup (`freedom_ls/learner_management/signals.py`)

- A `pre_delete` receiver on `Cohort` runs inside the deleting transaction. It deletes every
  `ObjectRoleAssignment` whose target is that cohort, active or not, along with the matching
  guardian `UserObjectPermission` rows. Because it is a receiver, deletes from the Django admin are
  covered too.
- A data migration in `learner_management` deletes the `ObjectRoleAssignment` rows whose cohort
  target no longer exists, and the guardian rows that match them.

## Testing

Grant rules and timestamps:

- `grantable_roles` for each grantor: site admin, organisation admin, cohort admin, cohort viewer,
  and a user with no role. It excludes inactive cohorts, never offers a downstream role, and is
  computed once per request.
- `is_widening`: the add, remove, mixed and unchanged cases.
- Reactivation resets `assigned_at` in both `assign_object_role` and `assign_site_role`.

Panel framework:

- `view_capability` hides the section from the menu and denies the list root.
- Row actions render on a list root and stay out of its header.
- The walker skips a list with no `instance_view`.

The list:

- It shows exactly this organisation's educators: organisation and cohort grants. It leaves out
  site-only admins, other organisations' educators, inactive users and removed assignments.
- The "You", "Suspended" (inactive and missing member row) and "Also a learner" markers.
- Inactive cohorts carry "(inactive)".
- Search, the role filter and its default, and each of the three empty states.
- The query count stays fixed as rows grow.

Add:

- The lookup matches an existing user with different case and surrounding spaces, and creates
  nothing.
- An address on another site shows the message and not the account's name.
- A new address creates one account and sends one setup email.
- A duplicate role on the same cohorts, and a partial overlap of cohorts.
- A refusal rolls back the new account.
- A hand-made POST with a role or cohort the grantor may not grant answers 403 and writes nothing.
- The cohort roles are absent when there are no active cohorts.

Change cohorts:

- Adding, removing, both together, and removing all, which removes the role.
- Cohorts outside the grantor's reach stay untouched.

Remove:

- The confirmation copy names what is lost and what is kept.
- An organisation admin cannot remove the last organisation admin. A site-level admin can, and is
  warned.
- For a site-level target, the copy says their site-level access is unaffected.
- The member row is left as it is.

Suspend and reactivate:

- Access through `can()` stops and then returns.
- An organisation admin suspending the last organisation admin is refused. A site-level admin is
  warned.
- Reactivate with no member row creates an active one.
- Both actions are hidden for a site-level target.

Elsewhere:

- Self: the own row has no actions, and a hand-made POST against yourself is refused.
- A stale submission returns `action_unavailable`.
- Mid-session removal: a person whose role is removed gets spec 5's denied response on their next
  full-page request and on their next htmx request.
- Cohort page: the add action presets the cohort, and is hidden from anyone without a grantable
  cohort role.
- Cohort deletion removes that cohort's assignment and guardian rows and leaves other cohorts'
  rows alone. The data migration removes existing orphans.
- `EDUCATOR_CHANGED` fires on every successful action.
- Playwright: add an existing user as cohort admin on two cohorts, change their cohorts, then
  remove the role. Run it at the QA viewports, including the mobile card layout.

## Success criteria

- An organisation admin can add an existing user and a brand-new address as a cohort admin on
  chosen cohorts without a shell. The new address receives the setup email.
- No hand-made POST lets anyone do any of these:
  - grant a role or cohort outside `grantable_roles`;
  - change their own roles;
  - leave an organisation with no organisation admin, unless a site-level admin did it.
- Every removal, suspension and widening shows a confirmation naming the change before anything is
  written.
- A person whose role is removed gets the denied response on their next request.
- Deleting a cohort leaves no `ObjectRoleAssignment` or guardian row pointing at it.
- `roles.py`, the add form and `docs/product/educator-interface.md` show the same four
  descriptions.
- The educators list issues the same number of queries for 5 rows as for 50.
