# Spec: educator interface small UI polish

Source: `idea.md`, with the answers in `answers.md`. Research: the `research_*.md` files beside
this spec, chiefly `research_codebase.md`.

## Purpose

The educator course page (`/educator/organisations/<org>/courses/<uuid>`) is one long stack of
panels with every outline part open. It lists cohort and direct registrations that the educator
cannot act on from the page. Previewing content means closing and reopening the modal for each
preview, and a quiz preview says little about the quiz. Separately, button labels sit low inside
their buttons in the First Class theme.

## Scope

In:

- The course page split into two tabs, with the outline groups collapsed on load.
- Register, Deactivate and (for direct registrations) Reactivate on the course page's two
  registration panels.
- Previous and Next in the course content preview footer, in place of Close.
- Quiz facts and settings in a quiz form's top-level preview.
- Label centring in `.btn` buttons, fixed in FLS.
- The rule "a panel that lists a thing the educator can act on lets them act on it and links to
  it", in the skills.

Out:

- An Expand all / Collapse all control, and remembering expanded groups between visits.
- Keyboard arrow shortcuts and a position indicator ("3 of 12") in the preview.
- Learner results (attempts, pass rate, average score) in the quiz preview.
- A Reactivate row action for cohort registrations. Registering the cohort again reactivates it,
  as on the cohort page.
- Any change to the First Class repo.

## Decisions

- The tabs are "Overview" and "Content", as the cohort page names its first tab "Overview".
- "Remove a registration" is Deactivate: registrations are never deleted (`domain-glossary`:
  setting `is_active` to False is deactivating).
- The course page actions call the services the cohort and learner page actions call
  (`register_cohort_for_course`, `deactivate_cohort_registration`, `register_for_course`,
  `deactivate_registration`), so educators get the same actions with the same effects on every
  page.
- Course content previews and Reference previews each step through their own list, never into the
  other.
- Collapsing on load is an opt-in on `DataTable`, so other child-row tables keep opening their
  groups on load.
- An inactive cohort refuses actions on the course page, as it does on the cohort page
  (`RequiresActiveCohortMixin`).

## Requirements

### Course page tabs (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/panels/course/instance_view.py`)

- `CourseInstanceView.panel` becomes a `CourseTabSet(TabSet)`, modelled on `CohortTabSet`.
- "Overview", the default tab, is a `PanelStack` of `CourseDetailsPanel`,
  `CourseCohortRegistrationsPanel` ("Cohort registrations") and `CourseLearnerRegistrationsPanel`
  ("Direct registrations").
- "Content" is a `PanelStack` of `CourseContentPanel` then `CourseReferencePanel`.
- The preview actions resolve their URLs under the new tab path, and the `interface_walk.py` walk
  covers both tabs.

### Outline groups start collapsed

- `DataTable` (`freedom_ls/panel_framework/tables.py`) gains `child_rows_collapsed = False`.
  `CourseOutlineTable` and `ReferenceOutlineTable` set it to `True`.
- `cotton/data-table.html` passes the flag to the `childRows` Alpine component as a data attribute
  (the CSP build passes no arguments), on both the table and the card list. When the flag is set,
  `childRows` in `panel_framework/static/panel_framework/js/alpine-components.js` marks every
  group row collapsed on init, reading the group rows from the DOM, nested groups included.
- `partials/child_rows_toggle.html` keeps rendering `aria-expanded="true"` server-side, and Alpine
  sets it to `false` on init. Without JavaScript every row shows and the toggle says expanded.
- An htmx swap of the table region starts collapsed again.

### Registration actions on the course page

Files: `panels/course/data_table_panels.py`, `panels/course/data_tables.py`, a new
`panels/course/actions.py`, `freedom_ls/educator_interface/forms.py`, and `panels/common/`.

- **Shared base.** The existing `DeactivateCohortRegistrationAction`, `DeactivateRegistrationAction`
  and `ReactivateRegistrationAction` take `ctx.instance` as a Cohort or Learner. Extract a shared
  base into `panels/common/` that resolves the registration from the row alone and carries the
  existing confirmation contexts, templates and reason handling. The cohort, learner and course
  actions all extend it, and the cohort and learner pages keep their current behaviour.
- **Cohort registrations panel:**
  - A footer Register action opens `CohortPickerForm`, given a queryset of active cohorts visible
    to the educator (`cohorts_visible_to`) that hold no active `CohortCourseRegistration` for this
    course. A cohort whose registration is inactive is listed, and registering it reactivates the
    registration. The action calls `register_cohort_for_course(cohort, course, actor=…, reason=…)`.
  - Each active row whose cohort is active has Deactivate, with the confirmation and reason of
    `DeactivateCohortRegistrationAction`, calling `deactivate_cohort_registration`. A row whose
    cohort is inactive shows no actions. The cohort name keeps its link to the cohort page.
- **Direct registrations panel:**
  - A footer Register action opens a new single-learner picker over the learners visible to the
    educator (`learners_visible_to`) in the organisation in view who hold no active
    `LearnerCourseRegistration` for this course. It calls `register_for_course`.
  - Active rows have Deactivate and inactive rows have Reactivate, with the confirmations of
    `DeactivateRegistrationAction` and `ReactivateRegistrationAction`, including the "still has
    access through cohort X" text from `other_grants_for`. The learner cell keeps its link to the
    learner page.
- **Permissions.** `can()` in `learner_management/capabilities.py` raises `TypeError` for a
  `Course` scope, so every new action overrides `PanelAction.permission_object`.
  - At render time each action checks whether the educator may hold its capability anywhere in
    the organisation in view.
  - The actions cell then narrows per row from a precomputed set of permitted cohort or learner
    ids, as `membership_row_ids` does in `data-table-cells/membership_actions.html`, with no
    per-row `can()` call.
  - On submit, the action checks the row's own cohort or learner.
  - A row the educator cannot act on shows its fields with an empty actions cell.
- **Course availability.** The footer Register buttons are hidden when the course is not open to
  the organisation in view (`is_course_available_to_organisation`). Inside each action, the
  services check the chosen cohort's or learner's own organisation (`require_course_open_to`). A
  `CourseNotOpenError` becomes a 422 form error.
- **Refresh.** Each course page action answers with `changed_response`:
  - Cohort rows fire `REGISTRATION_CHANGED` and `COHORT_CHANGED`.
  - Learner rows fire `REGISTRATION_CHANGED` and `LEARNER_CHANGED`.
  - Both panels list those events in `refresh_events`, so they refresh in place.
- **Look.** Buttons follow `fls-dev:panel-framework`. Register is the panel's one `primary`. Row
  buttons are small `secondary` buttons inside `<c-data-table-actions>`, with `aria_label`s naming
  their object ("Deactivate Cohort A").

### Preview Previous and Next (`panels/course/content_preview.py`, `modal/content_preview.html`, `modal/course_details_preview.html`)

- One function next to `outline_rows`/`reference_rows` derives a list's preview sequence from
  those rows alone. It walks them depth first and keeps every row with a preview: the Course
  details row (Course content only), then each topic, form and form page row. It drops part rows
  and note rows.
- `PreviewContentAction` and `PreviewCourseDetailsAction` find the current row in that sequence by
  its `preview_query` and add `previous_url` and `next_url` to their context.
  - The Course content sequence starts at the Course details row and ends at the last topic, form
    or form page row.
  - The Reference sequence (`course_list="reference"`) holds reference rows only.
- `cotton/modal-footer.html` gains a stepping variant with `previous` and `next` slots. The footer
  keeps its `justify-between` shape, with Previous at the left and Next at the right, and has no
  Close button.
  - On the first row of the sequence Previous renders `disabled`, and on the last row Next does.
    Both stay in place so the footer does not shift.
  - The header X (`closable`), Esc and a backdrop click still close the modal.
- Previous and Next swap the same target `<c-modal-trigger>` swaps, so the header, body and footer
  change together. Focus lands on the new title (`focus_title`).

### Quiz top-level preview

- When the previewed form is a quiz (`Form.strategy == FormStrategy.QUIZ`) and the row is the
  form's own row, not a form page row, the preview shows a `<c-panel-definition-list>` under the
  intro:
  - Pages and questions (`total_pages`, `question_count`, from `_form_preview_context`).
  - Pass mark (`quiz_pass_percentage`, or "None set").
  - Shows incorrect answers (`quiz_show_incorrect`).
  - Submits on exit (`submit_on_exit`).
  - Shuffle pages, questions and options (`shuffle_pages`, `shuffle_questions`,
    `shuffle_options`).
- On a multi-page quiz the block replaces the `form_engine/partials/form_facts.html` counts.

### Button label centring

- In the First Class theme, a `.btn` label's vertical centre is within 1px of the button's centre.
  This holds for every variant and size, including `btn-sm`, data-table rows and
  `[data-modal-footer]`, with the brand font (DM Sans) installed or with the fallback.
- The fix goes in one place, not in individual buttons. The suspects are the theme's DM Sans with
  no `@font-face` metrics and the first_class `.btn` padding override (detail in
  `research_codebase.md`).
- The fix changes `themes/first_class/.../theme.css`, `.btn` in `tailwind.components.css` and the
  duplicate `.panel-btn` in `panel_framework/css/panel_framework.css` together.
  `freedom_ls/base/tests/invariants/test_panel_framework_css_copies.py` gains a pin for the
  alignment rule shared by `.btn` and `.panel-btn`.

## Testing

- The course page renders Overview and Content with the panels in the order above. Update
  `tests/panels/course/test_instance_view.py`.
- Every requirement of each new action is covered. Add the actions to
  `tests/invariants/test_permission_matrix.py`, including a cohort-only role that sees row actions
  on its own cohort's rows only. Each action fires the events listed under Refresh.
- The preview sequence covers a course with parts, a multi-page form, a single-row course, an
  empty part and the Reference list. The Reference sequence holds no Course details row and no
  Course content row. A query-count assertion in `tests/panels/course/test_content_preview.py`
  pins the total.
- The quiz facts block shows for a quiz form's own row, and not for a non-quiz form or a quiz's
  form page row.
- `panel_framework/tests/components/test_data_table.py`: a table without the flag still starts
  open, and `test_every_expand_chevron_turns_when_its_control_opens` passes.
- `panel_framework/tests/invariants/test_templates.py` accepts the stepping footer and still
  rejects a hand-written button group.
- Playwright (`tests/playwright/test_course_preview_flow.py`):
  - Outline groups are collapsed after init, with `aria-expanded="false"`.
  - Previews step across a part boundary and back.
  - The footer has no Close, and Esc and the header X close the modal.
  - Focus lands on the title after each step.
- Playwright (`tests/playwright/test_course_registration_flow.py`, its own journey and data):
  register and deactivate a cohort and a learner from the course page.
- Playwright label centring for the `.btn` variants within 1px, in `test_touch_targets.py`. The
  test builds the First Class CSS with `build_fls_css_with_theme(build_dir, "first_class")`, as
  `freedom_ls/base/tests/playwright/test_panel_surface_cascade.py` does.

## Success criteria

- An educator with an organisation role registers and deactivates a cohort from the course page,
  and registering it again reactivates it. The same educator registers, deactivates and
  reactivates a learner, and each panel refreshes in place.
- The label-centring Playwright check passes in the First Class theme.
