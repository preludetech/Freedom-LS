# Upstream-change review: educator-interface-looks-good

direction: changed

## What main gained

The finished spec `educator-interface-3-panel-framework-dialogs` replaced the dialog model. `freedom_ls/base/templates/cotton/modal.html` and `freedom_ls/panel_framework/templates/panel_framework/partials/modal_form.html` no longer exist (verified on HEAD). Every panel action now loads a fragment into one shared native `<dialog id="app-modal">` (`panel_framework/partials/modal_host.html`, behaviour in `Alpine.data("appModal")`), opened by `cotton/modal-trigger.html` (`hx-get` into `#app-modal-body`). The host owns the box (`rounded-lg border border-border bg-surface p-6 shadow-xl`, `width: min(92vw, 32rem)`, centred, with an absolutely positioned close button and a "Discard changes?" prompt). Fragments (`modal/form.html`, `modal/delete_confirmation.html`, `modal/read_only.html`, `partials/action_denied.html`, `partials/action_unavailable.html`, `partials/bulk_confirmation*.html`) supply their own `<h2 id="app-modal-title">`, body and a `<c-button-group variant="right">` footer, and use `x-on:click="requestClose"`. There is no bottom-sheet variant and no `c-modal` consumer left. A quick-view drawer (`quick_view_host.html`, `quick-view-trigger.html`) was added, docked at 1280px and up, a modal below that.

The content header moved out of `_base_interface.html` into the shared `cotton/content-header.html` (`<header class="pt-4 pb-8 sm:pb-10">` with a `top` slot, a `byline` slot and the page-title partial). `_base_interface.html` now fills the `top` slot, and `header_extra` sits inside that slot after the toggle row. The toggle button is still after the breadcrumbs with `p-3` and the `table_of_contents` icon. The wrapper `max-w-7xl` is in a `header_width` block.

`cotton/data-table-cells/link.html` now has two branches: `<c-quick-view-trigger>` when `column.quick_view`, and a plain `<a>` otherwise, and the blank case renders "-". `table_selection_bar.html` now wraps `<c-pagination>` inside the bar (so the pagination shares the bar's row), with `md:static md:mt-6 md:border-0 md:bg-transparent md:px-4 md:py-0`. `data-table.html` renders the bar and pagination through it when `bulk_actions` is set. `table_toolbar.html`, `table_sheet.html`, `panel-card.html`, `_tab_set_base.html`, `data-table-card.html` (still `items-start`, `border-b ... last:border-0`), `pagination.html` (still hard-codes `mt-6 px-4`), `panel-page-header.html` (now accepts `{{ attrs }}`, a `badge` slot and `title_id`) and `_base_interface.html`'s sidebar wrapper (line ~199) still match the plan's "today" descriptions. The test-organisation spec (import-linter contracts, test-mirroring check, app-map freshness hook) adds pre-commit and CI gates but no template or app-boundary change.
