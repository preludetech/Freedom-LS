# Frontend QA: HR attributes on learners

The Django admin gains three per-organisation lists (job titles, departments, locations), an "HR
attributes" panel on every Learner change page, and an "HR settings" panel on every Organisation
change page. This run walks the lists, the two panels, and the ways they could go wrong: a
duplicate name that differs only in case, a picker leaking another organisation's entries, a
deactivated entry still held by a learner, a learner moved between organisations, and deletion of an
entry in use.

Substitute the literal port number chosen by `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh` wherever
this document writes `<PORT>`. Shell variables do not survive between tool calls.

---

## §0 Setup

### 0.1 Server

1. `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh` — note the number and use it literally from here on.
2. Start `uv run python manage.py runserver <PORT>` backgrounded, as its own call.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:<PORT>/` and confirm `#debug-branch-badge` names the
   `corporate-job-course-recommendations-1-hr-attributes` branch. If it names another branch,
   another server holds the port; go back to step 1.

### 0.2 Seed data

Every item below must exist before §1. Missing data is created through the
`fls-dev:qa-data-helper` agent, never by hand. The names are fictional.

1. **Superuser:** `demodev@email.com` from `.claude/fls-dev/config.md`.
2. **Two organisations** on the dev site: `QA Org One` and `QA Org Two`.
3. **Job titles.** In QA Org One: `Driver`, `Dispatcher` and `Fleet Manager`, with
   `Fleet Manager` deactivated (`is_active=False`). In QA Org Two: `Driver` (same name, other
   organisation).
4. **Departments.** In QA Org One: `Operations` and `Finance`. In QA Org Two: `Finance`.
5. **Locations.** In QA Org One: `Cape Town` and `Durban`. QA Org Two: none.
6. **Learners.**
   - `qa_hr_held@email.com`, a learner of QA Org One whose HR attributes already hold the
     deactivated `Fleet Manager` job title, `Operations` and `Cape Town`, with
     `organisation_start_date` `2019-03-01` and the other three dates empty. Call this the
     *held learner*.
   - `qa_hr_blank@email.com`, a learner of QA Org One with no HR attributes row. Call this the
     *blank learner*.
   - `qa_hr_mover@email.com`, a learner of QA Org One whose HR attributes hold `Driver` (QA Org
     One's) and nothing else. Call this the *mover*.
7. **No `OrganisationHRSettings` row** for either organisation.

Sign in as `demodev@email.com` at `http://127.0.0.1:<PORT>/admin/` for every section.

## §1 The three list admins

### 1.1 They are there

1. On the admin index, expect an "HR attributes" section listing "Job titles", "Departments" and
   "Locations". Expect no entry for HR attributes rows or HR settings rows anywhere on the index.
2. Open "Job titles" (`/admin/freedom_ls_hr_attributes/jobtitle/`). Expect columns Name,
   Organisation, Is active; four rows: `Dispatcher` (QA Org One, active), `Driver` (QA Org One,
   active), `Driver` (QA Org Two, active) and `Fleet Manager` (QA Org One, inactive), in name
   order.
3. Expect the right-hand filter panel to offer "organisation" and "is active". Filter by QA Org
   Two: expect only its `Driver`. Clear it. Filter "is active" to No: expect only `Fleet
   Manager`. Clear it.
4. Search `disp`: expect only `Dispatcher`. Clear the search.
5. Open "Departments" and "Locations" once each and confirm their rows match §0.2.

### 1.2 Adding an entry, and the duplicate rule

1. On the Job titles changelist press "Add job title". Expect three fields in this order:
   Organisation (an autocomplete), Name, Is active (ticked by default). Expect no "Site" field.
2. Pick QA Org One, type ` driver ` (leading and trailing spaces, lower case), save. Expect the
   form to come back with "An entry with this name already exists in this organisation." under
   the Name field, and no new row on the changelist.
3. Change the organisation to QA Org Two, keep ` driver `, save. Expect the same error: QA Org Two
   already has `Driver`.
4. Change the name to ` Mechanic ` (with the spaces), organisation QA Org One, save. Expect the
   changelist to show `Mechanic` with no surrounding spaces, under QA Org One.
5. Open `Mechanic`, change nothing, save. Expect it to save without a duplicate error.
6. Open `Mechanic`, rename it to `MECHANIC`, save. Expect it to save: the only entry it could
   clash with is itself.

### 1.3 Deactivate and activate in bulk

1. On the Job titles changelist tick `Dispatcher` and `Mechanic`, choose "Deactivate selected
   entries", press Go. Expect the message "2 entries deactivated." and both rows now showing
   inactive.
2. Tick the same two, choose "Activate selected entries", Go. Expect "2 entries activated." and
   both active again.
3. Tick only `Mechanic`, deactivate. Expect "1 entry deactivated." Activate it again.

### 1.4 Deleting an entry

1. Open QA Org One's `Driver` and press "Delete". Expect the confirmation page to refuse: it
   says the job title cannot be deleted because it is referenced, and lists the mover's HR
   attributes row. Expect no "Yes, I'm sure" button that goes through. Go back.
2. Open `Mechanic` and press "Delete", then confirm. Expect it to delete and the changelist to
   drop it.
3. Open QA Org One's `Fleet Manager` (inactive) and press "Delete". Expect the same refusal as
   step 1, listing the held learner's row: deactivation does not free an entry for deletion.

## §2 The HR attributes panel on a learner

### 2.1 A learner who holds a deactivated entry

1. Open the Learners changelist (`/admin/freedom_ls_learner_management/learner/`), open the
   held learner.
2. Expect an "HR attributes" panel below the learner's own fields, with: Job title, Department,
   Location, then Organisation start date, Job title start date, Department start date, Location
   start date. Expect no "Site" and no "Learner" field in the panel, and no delete checkbox.
3. Expect Job title to show `Fleet Manager (inactive)` selected. Open the dropdown: expect
   `Dispatcher`, `Driver`, `Fleet Manager (inactive)` and the empty choice, and nothing named
   `Mechanic` (deleted) or belonging to QA Org Two. There must be exactly one `Driver`.
4. Expect Department to show `Operations` and its dropdown to offer only QA Org One's
   `Operations` and `Finance` (one `Finance`, not two). Expect Location to offer `Cape Town` and
   `Durban`.
5. Expect Organisation start date `2019-03-01` and the other three dates empty.
6. Change nothing and press "Save and continue editing". Expect it to save: holding a deactivated
   entry is not an error.
7. Set Job title start date to `2030-06-01` (a future date) and Department start date to
   `2010-01-01` (before the organisation start date). Save. Expect both to persist: dates are
   independent and unordered.
8. Clear Job title (empty choice), keep the Job title start date, save. Expect it to save with the
   date kept and the job title empty. Reopen the dropdown: expect `Fleet Manager (inactive)` to
   be gone now that nothing holds it.

### 2.2 A learner with no row yet

1. Open the blank learner. Expect the "HR attributes" panel with every field empty.
2. Press "Save" without touching the panel. Expect the save to succeed. Ask
   `fls-dev:qa-data-helper` to confirm no `LearnerHRAttributes` row exists for this learner.
3. Reopen the blank learner, pick Department `Finance` and set Organisation start date
   `2024-01-15`, leave Job title and Location empty, save. Reopen: expect `Finance` and the date
   to be there and the rest empty.

### 2.3 The add page

1. Open `/admin/freedom_ls_learner_management/learner/add/`. Expect no "HR attributes" panel
   and no other panels. Do not save anything.

### 2.4 Moving a learner between organisations

1. Open the mover. Expect Job title `Driver` and the dropdown to offer QA Org One's job titles.
2. Change Organisation to QA Org Two, leave the panel as it is, press "Save". Expect the page to
   come back with "Choose a job title from this learner's organisation." under Job title, and
   the learner still shown under QA Org One on the changelist.
3. Back on the mover's page, set Organisation to QA Org Two and clear Job title, then save.
   Expect it to save and the changelist to show the mover under QA Org Two.
4. Reopen the mover. Expect the Job title dropdown to now offer QA Org Two's `Driver` only, and
   Department to offer only QA Org Two's `Finance`. Location offers only the empty choice.

### 2.5 Erasing a user takes the attributes along

1. Open the Users changelist (`/admin/freedom_ls_accounts/user/`), open `qa_hr_mover@email.com`,
   press "Delete". Expect the confirmation to list the learner row and its HR attributes among
   the objects that go with the user, and to go through on confirm.
2. Back on Job titles, open QA Org Two's `Driver` and press "Delete". Expect the confirmation to
   offer deletion now that nothing holds it. Go back without confirming.

## §3 The HR settings panel on an organisation

1. Open the Organisations changelist (`/admin/freedom_ls_organisations/organisation/`), open
   QA Org One. Expect an "HR settings" panel with a single unticked checkbox "Registration rules
   enabled", after the cohort and learner panels. Expect no "Site" field in it.
2. Press "Save" without touching the panel. Expect it to save. Ask `fls-dev:qa-data-helper` to
   confirm no `OrganisationHRSettings` row exists for QA Org One.
3. Reopen QA Org One, tick the checkbox, save. Reopen: expect it ticked. Ask the helper to
   confirm one row with `registration_rules_enabled=True`.
4. Untick it, save, reopen. Expect it unticked and the row still present with the switch off.
5. Open QA Org Two. Expect the checkbox unticked: one organisation's switch does not reach
   another.
6. Open `/admin/freedom_ls_organisations/organisation/add/`. Expect no "HR settings" panel. Do
   not save anything.

1. Open a cohort's change page (`/admin/freedom_ls_learner_management/cohort/`, any cohort).
   Expect its members and course registrations panels as before and no HR panel.
2. Open the held learner again. Expect the cohorts, course registrations and course progress
   panels still present above the "HR attributes" panel.
3. Open QA Org One again. Expect the cohorts and learners panels still present and still
   paginated, ahead of the "HR settings" panel.
