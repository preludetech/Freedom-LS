# Frontend QA: deadlines, course time limit and missed deadlines

In the course player's outline a learner sees "Deadline <date>" under each item that has one. Once
it has passed, the line reads "Missed deadline <date>", with "Missed deadline" as a chip. The dashboard card has one deadline line: the earliest
missed deadline, otherwise the next one. The course detail page shows no deadlines. It reads the same
for everyone, and its stats strip gives the course's time limit as a length ("Time limit · 90
days"). Admins set a per-site time zone and override deadlines from the Django admin, per cohort
course registration and per learner.

This run covers:
- the content defaults
- the three learner surfaces
- both override layers and the "no deadline" override
- the "default changed" note
- the site time zone and its label switch
- carry-over on re-sync
- the ways it could go wrong: a visitor who is not registered, a completed item, an inconsistent
  override row, a second override for the same item, a bad time zone name and a removed content
  default.

**Wording.** Learner pages say only "Deadline", "Missed deadline", "Next deadline:" and "Time
limit". "Time limit" appears only in the course detail stats strip, never on a dashboard card. If
"Due", "Overdue", "Finish by", "Time limit ended", "Course time limit" or "N overdue" shows anywhere
on a learner page, that is a bug. So is "Missed deadline" followed by "Deadline" on the same line.

Substitute the literal port number chosen by `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh` wherever
this document writes `$PORT`. Shell variables do not survive between tool calls.

Dates below are written relative to a learner's registration date, because the demo content uses
relative offsets. "Reg" is the date part of that learner's `CourseProgress.created_at` in the site
zone, and "today" is the date of the run. The fresh learner registers today, so on any run date
every one of its dates except `Welcome`'s fixed 2024 date is in the future. Every dated item of
the cohort learner (Reg 1 Mar 2026) and the late learner (Reg = today - 100) is in the past.

The site zone for this run is `Africa/Johannesburg` (UTC+2, no DST, abbreviation `SAST`), so an
end-of-day instant is 21:59:59.999999 UTC. Dates print in the browser locale, and the browser is
set to the same zone. For en-US the end-of-day instant prints as "Mar 8, 2026, 11:59 PM", and hours
are zero-padded ("05:00 PM").

## §0 Setup

1. `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh`: note the number and use it literally from here on.
2. Start `uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT` backgrounded, as its own call.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/` and confirm `#debug-branch-badge` names the
   `deadlines-1-dates-and-overdue` branch. If it names another branch, another server holds the

### 0.2 Browser

Set the Playwright context's time zone to `Africa/Johannesburg` for every test unless a test says
otherwise. Where a test says "the New York browser", open a new context with `America/New_York`.

## §1 Deadlines in the player outline, never on course detail

### 1.1 The course detail page shows no deadline to anyone

1. As the cohort learner open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/courses/functionality-demo-course-parts/detail/`
   and expand every part.
2. Expect no row to show a date, a "Deadline" line or a "Missed deadline" chip, and no `<time>`
   element anywhere in the "Course content" list.
3. Repeat as the fresh learner, the late learner, the visitor and an anonymous visitor (logged
   out). Expect the "Course content" list to look the same each time: the same rows and no
   deadline content.

### 1.2 The player outline shows each deadline

1. As the fresh learner open `Welcome` in the player (the "Start course" button on the detail page).
2. In the sidebar outline, expand all three parts and expect:
   - `Welcome`: a **Missed deadline** chip (error tone) followed by "Jan 31, 2024, 11:59 PM". The
     word "Deadline" does not appear again after the chip.
   - `What to Expect`: "Deadline <today + 7>, 11:59 PM", no chip.
   - `Key Ideas`: "Deadline <today + 14>, 05:00 PM", no chip.
   - `Knowledge Check`: "Deadline <today + 21>, 11:59 PM"; `Course Feedback`: "Deadline <today +
     30>, 11:59 PM". No chip on either.
   - `Going Deeper` and `Summary`: no line.
3. Expect no date and no chip on the part rows ("Getting Started", "Core Concepts", "Wrapping Up").
4. **Layout.** Each deadline line sits on its own line under the item's title. Its left edge (the
   chip, or "Deadline" when there is no chip) lines up with the item's type icon, not with the
   number. This holds for rows inside a part and for top-level rows. The title stays on one line
   and is not squeezed.
5. Inspect one date element: it is a `<time>` whose `datetime` attribute ends in `+00:00` (UTC)
   and whose text shows no zone label.

### 1.3 Completing an item clears its deadline

1. Still in the player as the fresh learner, mark `Welcome` complete (the completion control at the
   foot of the topic).
2. Expect the sidebar row for `Welcome` to show neither the chip nor a date. The other rows are
   unchanged.

### 1.4 Dates before today are missed

1. As the cohort learner (Reg 1 Mar 2026) open the parts course in the player.
2. Expect every dated row to show the **Missed deadline** chip: `Welcome` with Jan 31, 2024, `What
   to Expect` with Mar 8, 2026, `Key Ideas` with Mar 15, 2026, 05:00 PM, `Knowledge Check` with Mar
   22, 2026 and `Course Feedback` with Mar 31, 2026.
3. A learner registered after a fixed date misses it at once. The fresh learner saw this in 1.2 on
   `Welcome`.

## §3 Dashboard card line

Each card has at most one deadline line. It sits below "Next up" and above the progress bar, never in
the eyebrow. A missed deadline wins over the next one, and the earliest missed deadline is the one
shown. The course time limit counts as a deadline named **Course**, so the line reads "Missed
deadline Course <date>" or "Next deadline: Course <date>".

### 3.1 Earliest missed deadline

1. As the cohort learner open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/`.
2. On the parts course card in "In progress" expect the **Missed deadline** chip (error tone),
   followed by "Welcome Jan 31, 2024, 11:59 PM". There is no "Next deadline" line. The eyebrow still
   reads "Registered" or "In progress".
3. As the late learner, expect the same line on the parts course card. The time limit has also
   passed, but `Welcome` is earlier. The plain course card shows no deadline line.

### 3.2 Next deadline

1. As the fresh learner, who completed `Welcome` in 1.3, open the dashboard. Expect the parts
   course card to show the muted line "Next deadline: What to Expect <today + 7>, 11:59 PM" and no
   chip.
2. Complete `What to Expect` through the player and reopen the dashboard. The line advances to
   "Next deadline: Key Ideas <today + 14>, 05:00 PM".

### 3.3 Only the time limit is missed

1. Through the data helper, complete `Welcome`, `What to Expect`, `Key Ideas`, `Knowledge Check`
   and `Course Feedback` for the late learner. Leave `Going Deeper` and `Summary` open, so the
   course stays in progress.
2. As the late learner open the dashboard. Expect the **Missed deadline** chip followed by "Course
   <Reg + 90>, 11:59 PM", a date 10 days ago. The card never says "time limit".

## §4 Cohort deadline override

### 4.1 Add an override on the cohort course registration

1. As the superuser open Admin → Learner management → Cohort course registrations → the row for
   `QA Deadlines Cohort` / parts course.
2. In the **Deadline overrides** inline, open the collection item picker. Expect it to list only
   this course's topics and forms as "Title (path)", with the empty choice reading **Course time
   limit**.
3. Add a row: collection item `Welcome`, kind "Days after registration", days `3`, reason
   `QA cohort override`. Save.
4. Reopen the page. Expect the row to show, read-only: Content default "Fixed 31 Jan 2024,
   23:59 SAST" (or that wording's equivalent naming the zone), Effective "3 days after
   registration", set by `demodev@email.com`, and no "Default has changed" note.

### 4.2 The learner sees it

1. As the cohort learner (Reg 1 Mar 2026) open the parts course in the player and expand "Getting
   Started". Expect `Welcome` to show the Missed deadline chip followed by "Mar 4, 2026, 11:59 PM"
   (Reg + 3), no longer the 2024 date.
2. Open the dashboard: the parts course card shows "Missed deadline Welcome Mar 4, 2026, 11:59 PM".
3. The fresh learner (individual registration, not in the cohort) still sees `Welcome` with Jan
   31, 2024 in the player, or no line if it was completed in 1.3: the cohort override does not
   reach an individual registration.
4. The course detail outline still shows no deadline to anyone (as 1.1).

### 4.3 Inconsistent rows are refused

1. Back on the cohort course registration page, add a row with kind "Fixed date" and no date.
   Save. Expect the inline row error "A fixed deadline needs a date." and nothing saved.
2. Add a row with collection item "Course time limit" and kind "Fixed date" with a date. Expect
   "The course time limit is counted in days after registration."
3. Add a second row for `Welcome` (any kind). Expect a uniqueness error on the inline.
4. Add a row with kind "No deadline" and days `5`. Expect "No deadline takes no date, time or days."

### 4.4 Override the course time limit for the cohort

1. Add a row: collection item "Course time limit", kind "Days after registration", days `200`.
   Save.
2. As the cohort learner, the course detail cell **still** reads "Time limit · 90 days": the page
   shows the authored length and ignores overrides.
3. Through the data helper complete every dated item for the cohort learner, then reset them
   again. While they are complete, the dashboard card reads "Next deadline: Course Sep 17, 2026,
   11:59 PM" (Reg + 200) if the run is before that date, or "Missed deadline Course Sep 17, 2026,
   11:59 PM" if after. As the late learner (not in the cohort), nothing
   changes.

## §5 Learner deadline override

### 5.1 "No deadline" for one learner

1. As the superuser open Admin → Learner progress → Course progress → the fresh learner's record
   for the parts course.
2. Expect a read-only **Effective deadlines** field under a "Deadlines" heading listing each
   collection item with a deadline, its due instant in `SAST`, its source (`content` for most,
   `cohort_registration` for none, since this learner is not in the cohort), and the time limit
   end.
3. In the **Deadline overrides** inline add: collection item `Welcome`, kind "No deadline",
   reason `QA no deadline`. Save.
4. Reopen: the Effective deadlines field no longer lists `Welcome`; the inline row shows
   Content default "Fixed 31 Jan 2024 …", Effective "No deadline".
5. As the fresh learner, the player outline shows `Welcome` with no date and no chip; the
   dashboard card shows "Next deadline: What to Expect <today + 7>, 11:59 PM", or Key Ideas if What
   to Expect was completed in 3.2.

### 5.2 A learner override beats the cohort override

1. On the cohort learner's Course progress record add: collection item `Welcome`, kind "Fixed
   date", date = today + 1, time `09:00`. Save.
2. Expect the row's Effective "<today + 1>, 09:00 SAST" and the Effective deadlines field naming
   source `learner` for `Welcome`.
3. As the cohort learner expect "Deadline <today + 1>, 09:00 AM" with no chip on `Welcome` in the
   player sidebar. The dashboard card still shows a missed deadline: `What to Expect` (Mar 8, 2026)
   is now the earliest missed.

### 5.3 Deleting the override returns to the layers beneath

1. Delete the learner override row from 5.2 and save.
2. As the cohort learner, `Welcome` in the player sidebar shows the Missed deadline chip followed
   by "Mar 4, 2026, 11:59 PM" again (the cohort override's 3 days after Reg): the cohort layer,
   not the content default, is what is beneath.

## §7 Site time zone

### 7.1 Zone label switch

1. As the superuser open Admin → Site aware models → Site time zones → the dev site row. Tick
   `show_time_zone_to_learners`. Save.
2. As the cohort learner open the parts course in the player. Expect every sidebar date to carry
   a short zone label, e.g. "Mar 8, 2026, 11:59 PM GMT+2" (the browser's wording for the zone). The
   dashboard card's date carries it too.
3. In the New York browser, log in as the same learner and open the same page. Expect the same
   instant rendered in New York time, e.g. "Mar 8, 2026, 04:59 PM EST" (16:59 because 23:59 SAST is
   21:59 UTC).
4. Untick the switch and save; the New York browser now shows "Mar 8, 2026, 04:59 PM" with no label.

### 7.2 A changed zone moves the instant

1. Change the row's `time_zone` to `Pacific/Auckland`. Save.
2. As the cohort learner, in the Johannesburg browser, `What to Expect` in the player sidebar now
   reads "Mar 8, 2026, 12:59 PM" (end of day in Auckland is 10:59 UTC in March) and the `datetime`
   attribute ends in `T10:59:59.999999+00:00`.
3. Open the fresh learner's Course progress record in the admin: the Effective deadlines field
   names `NZDT` on its instants.
4. Set `time_zone` back to `Africa/Johannesburg`.

### 7.3 A bad name is refused

1. Set `time_zone` to `Mars/Olympus`. Save. Expect the field error "Not an IANA time zone name,
   e.g. Africa/Johannesburg." and the row unchanged.
2. Try to add a second row for the dev site. Expect a uniqueness error.
