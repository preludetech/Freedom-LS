# Spec: cohort administration

Spec 6 of 12 in the educator interface rebuild. The source is `idea.md` in this directory. It
depends on specs 2, 3 and 5, which are done, and runs beside spec 7. Read the "Educator interface
rebuild" section of `spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md` first.

Layout follows `spec_dd/1. next/educator-interface-full-polish/Educator LMS Interface Design/`:
screens 05 and 07 of `Educator Cohorts and Admin.dc.html`, and M08 and M10 of
`Educator Mobile Cohorts and Admin.dc.html`, cut to this scope and drawn with FLS components.
`spec_dd/1. next/educator-interface-full-polish/comparable-systems-learner-management.md` sections
4, 6 and 7 back the deactivate and unregister semantics.

## Purpose

This spec finishes the cohorts section: list, detail, create, edit, deactivate, reactivate, delete
when empty, and registering and unregistering a cohort for courses. It also rebuilds the courses
section as a read-only, organisation-scoped view, which closes the disclosure gap
`CourseConfig.check_access_exempt_reason` declares.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inactive cohort | coined | A `Cohort` with the new `is_active=False`. The status word is "inactive", settled in `spec_dd/3. done/2026-09-29_14:19_educator-interface-4-panel-framework-components/research_status_vocabulary.md`. |
| access-granting cohort registration | coined | A `CohortCourseRegistration` with `is_active=True` whose `cohort.is_active` is also true. Only these give members access. |
| empty cohort | coined | A cohort with no `CohortMembership` rows and no `CohortCourseRegistration` rows, active or not, whatever the learners' `Learner.is_active`. |

## Out of scope

- Membership actions and the learners tab content (spec 7). The learners tab stays as it is.
- Row selection and bulk actions (spec 8). The selection column appears once spec 8 declares the
  cohort table's first `BulkAction`.
- Editing the educators block (spec 9), needs-attention data (spec 10) and the audit log (spec 11).
- Deadlines, including how `deadline_utils.py` treats an inactive cohort's deadlines.
- Case-insensitive name uniqueness.

## Decisions

1. **An inactive cohort grants no course access.** Its registration rows are not changed, so
   reactivating restores exactly what was there. A member keeps access through any other route: an
   individual registration, or an access-granting registration in another cohort.
2. **Fan-out ignores `Cohort.is_active`.** Progress records are harmless while access is gated at
   read time, and reactivating needs no catch-up.
3. **An inactive cohort is read-only.** Its page stays reachable for anyone who could see it, because
   that is how it gets reactivated. It offers only Reactivate, and Delete when it is empty. Edit,
   Register and Unregister are hidden, and a stale POST to any of them answers 422 with a fragment
   saying the cohort is inactive. Hiding and refusing are separate: a hidden action still resolves,
   through a `PanelAction.is_offered` hook `panel_framework` gains, so the stale POST reaches
   `handle_submit` and is refused there rather than answering 404.
4. **Capabilities follow the spec 5 matrix, with no new permissions.** They are checked against the
   cohort:

   | Action | Capability | Who holds it |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Edit, Deactivate, Reactivate | `change_cohort` | Organisation admins and site admins. |
   | Delete | `delete_cohort` | Organisation admins and site admins. |
   | Register, and re-register | `add_cohortcourseregistration` | Also cohort admins, on their own cohort. |
   | Unregister | `change_cohortcourseregistration` | Also cohort admins, on their own cohort. |

5. **Spec 6 and spec 7 run in parallel, and whichever lands second does the joining work.** If spec 7
   lands first, spec 6 wires `announce_cohort_registration_change` to the events spec 7 named, and
   excludes inactive cohorts from spec 7's membership add and move destinations. If spec 6 lands
   first, spec 7 does both.
6. **An inactive cohort does not make its members peers.** The messaging policy's peer queries treat
   it as if it did not exist: sharing the cohort makes no peers, and neither does sharing a course
   only through its registrations. A member keeps peers found another way: another cohort, or a
   course both learners hold through an individual registration or an active cohort. An inactive
   cohort keeps its educators. `educators_of` and `learners_visible_to` do not read
   `Cohort.is_active`, because Decision 3 keeps the cohort and its learners visible to the educators
   who have to reactivate it.

## Requirements

### Model and migration (`freedom_ls/learner_management/`)

- `Cohort.is_active = models.BooleanField(_("active"), default=True)` in `models.py`. The migration is
  `0005_cohort_is_active.py`, and every existing cohort becomes active.
- `CohortAdmin` in `admin.py` adds `is_active` to `list_display`, `list_filter` and its form.
- `CohortFactory` keeps `is_active=True` as its default.

### Access and progress resolution

Add one helper and use it at every course-access and course-peer read site, so the rule cannot
drift:

```python
# learner_management/queries.py
def access_granting_cohort_registrations() -> QuerySet[CohortCourseRegistration]:
    return CohortCourseRegistration.objects.filter(is_active=True, cohort__is_active=True)
```

Use a `Q` mirror where a site filters through a relation instead. `cohort` is a forward foreign key,
so chaining `cohort__is_active` onto a query is safe. The existing `cohort__cohortmembership__…`
conditions must still share one `filter()` call, as the comments at those sites explain.

| Read site | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| `learner_management/utils.py` `is_registered_for_course` | Cohort branch. |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `is_registered_for_course_expression` | Cohort `Exists`. |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `learner_for_course` | Skips inactive cohorts, so it falls through to the individual registration. |
| `learner_progress/queries.py` `course_progress_by_course_for` | Changes in step with `learner_for_course`. |
| `learner_progress/queries.py` `registrations_with_progress_for_learner` | Same rule. |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `registrations_of` | Cohort branch, so a sender's courses through an inactive cohort make no course peers (Decision 6). |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `holds_registration_for_any_expression` | Cohort `Exists`, the same rule on the candidate's side. |
| `learner_management/queries.py` `is_in_cohort_expression` | Adds `cohort__is_active=True`, so sharing an inactive cohort makes no cohort peers whichever cohorts the caller passes. |

Leave these alone: the fan-out in `learner_progress/signals.py` and `learner_progress/utils.py`,
`reports/indexes.py` `load_registrations`, so an inactive cohort still gets reports,
`deadline_utils.py`, and `educators_of`, `learners_visible_to` and `colleagues_of`, so an inactive
cohort keeps its educators (Decision 6).

### Registration functions (`freedom_ls/learner_management/utils.py`)

- `register_cohort_for_course(cohort: Cohort, course: Course) -> CohortCourseRegistration`: get the
  row or create it, set `is_active=True`, then `save()`, never `.update()`, so the `post_save`
  fan-out runs. Re-registering keeps the row's pk. The unique constraint
  `unique_cohort_course_registration` allows one row per cohort and course.
- `unregister_cohort_from_course(registration: CohortCourseRegistration) -> None`: set
  `is_active=False`, then `save(update_fields=["is_active"])`. Unregistering is reversible.
- Both call `announce_cohort_registration_change(registration)`. Until spec 7's events exist
  (Decision 5) it is a no-op, its docstring says spec 7 names the events, and nothing is added to
  `FLS_WEBHOOK_EVENT_TYPES`.

### Query helpers (`freedom_ls/learner_management/queries.py`)

They live here because in `educator_interface` they would add a runtime edge to
`role_based_permissions`. Here they add no edge to `docs/app_structure.md`.

- `courses_visible_to(user, organisation) -> QuerySet[Course]`: every published course, plus any
  course registered to a cohort or learner the user can see in this organisation, active
  registration or not. A hidden or coming-soon course appears only through such a registration. It
  scopes through `cohorts_visible_to` and `learners_visible_to`, and follows `_resolved_or_none` for
  anonymous, inactive and superuser users.
- `registerable_courses_for(cohort) -> QuerySet[Course]`: published and hidden courses, never
  coming-soon ones, excluding courses the cohort already holds an active registration for. A course
  with an inactive registration is listed, and picking it reactivates that row.
- `cohort_educators(cohort) -> list[tuple[User, str]]`: active users holding an active
  `cohort_admin` or `cohort_viewer` `ObjectRoleAssignment` on the cohort, gated on an active
  `OrganisationMember` of `cohort.organisation`. Copy the pattern from `active_organisation_admins`.
  The `str` is the role's `display_name`, read from `get_role_config(cohort.site.name)` so a site's
  own role names apply.
- `members_keeping_access(registration) -> QuerySet[Learner]`: active members of
  `registration.cohort` who keep access to `registration.course` after an unregister. A member
  qualifies through an active `LearnerCourseRegistration`, or through membership of another cohort
  that holds an access-granting registration for the course. One query, not one per member.

### Cohort list (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/views.py`, `CohortDataTable`)

- Columns:
  - Name: a link with the quick view, sortable.
  - Status: a new cell template using `<c-panel-status-badge>`, showing "Active" or "Inactive".
  - Learners: `Count("cohortmembership", filter=Q(cohortmembership__learner__is_active=True), distinct=True)`, sortable.
  - Courses: active registrations only, via a `Prefetch` of `course_registrations`.
  - Created: `created_at`, sortable.
- Default order is name, then pk as the tiebreaker.
- `search_fields = ["name"]`.
- Two filters, on the cohorts section's list only:
  - A "Show inactive" toggle. Unset, the default, the list leaves out inactive cohorts. Set, it
    includes them. `BooleanFilter` only narrows when set, so the plan picks how this one widens.
  - `RelatedChoiceFilter("course", "Course", ...)`, backed by `courses_visible_to`. It narrows to
    cohorts with an active registration for the chosen course. An out-of-scope value is dropped.
- `LearnerCohortsPanel` on the learner page has neither filter. It keeps listing every cohort the
  learner belongs to, with the status badge. Its table is `LearnerCohortDataTable`, a
  `CohortDataTable` subclass that declares no filters and leaves inactive cohorts in.
- A query-count test shows the list costs the same for 1 cohort as for 10.

### Cohort detail

- **Header** (`panel-page-header`): name, a status badge, an inline stat row (learners, courses) and
  actions: Edit when the cohort is active, plus the state-appropriate settings action (Deactivate or
  Reactivate). `InstanceView` gains `get_status_badge` and `get_stats`, which the instance page
  template renders into the header's badge and stats slots. Delete lives on the settings tab only.
- **`CohortTabSet`** becomes `{"overview", "learners", "courses", "settings"}`, with overview as the
  default. `details`, `CohortDetailsStack` and `CourseRegistrationsPanel` go. `learners` keeps
  `CohortLearnersPanel`, its `table_key = "learners"` and its tab count, unchanged. Every new panel
  sets `refresh_events = (COHORT_CHANGED,)`. The courses tab panel also refreshes on
  `REGISTRATION_CHANGED`.
- **Overview tab.**
  - A details card: name, status, created, learner count, course count.
  - Course completion per active registration, whatever the cohort's state: course title, then
    completed records over members with a record (`CourseProgress.cohort_registration`,
    `completed_time` not null), then a `panel-progress-bar`.
  - A needs-attention card holding a `panel-empty-state` placeholder, to be replaced by spec 10.
  - The educators block, from `cohort_educators`. It is read-only and shown only to holders of
    `view_organisationmember`, the rule the denied experience uses before naming staff. It lives in
    its own panel class so spec 9 can extend it.
- **Courses tab.**
  - A `DataTablePanel` over the cohort's registrations, `table_key = "cohort_courses"`, with columns
    course (link), status (registration `is_active` as a badge) and registered.
  - The Register action: a `FormPanelAction` whose single course choice comes from
    `registerable_courses_for`. A course outside that set answers 422, and no row changes.
  - The Unregister action on each active row.
  - Register and Unregister are hidden when the cohort is inactive or the user lacks the capability.
- **Unregister confirmation.** A new `PanelAction` with a confirmation fragment modelled on
  `panel_framework/modal/delete_confirmation.html`. The registration is identified in the POST, and
  the action resolves it only within `cohort.course_registrations`. The fragment says:
  - how many members lose access;
  - who keeps access, from `members_keeping_access`, naming the first 10 and then "and N more";
  - that everyone keeps their progress;
  - that the cohort can be registered again.

  The confirm button is not styled as danger.
- **Settings tab.** Its capability is `change_cohort`, so the tab is absent for anyone without it.
  - Active cohort: Deactivate.
  - Inactive cohort: Reactivate.
  - Empty cohort: Delete.
  - Non-empty cohort: a sentence saying why delete is unavailable, counting every membership and
    every registration, removed learners and inactive registrations included.
- **Deactivate confirmation.** It says:
  - members stay in the cohort and keep their progress;
  - while the cohort is inactive, its registrations stop giving access to its N courses;
  - members with another registration for a course keep access to it;
  - it can be reactivated;
  - why delete is unavailable, when the cohort is not empty.

  On success it sets `is_active=False` and navigates back to the same page with `HX-Location`, so
  the header, tabs and settings tab render the new state; no domain event is needed because the
  page re-renders.
- **Reactivate confirmation.** It says the registrations give access to N courses again. On success
  it sets `is_active=True` and navigates back to the same page the same way.
- **Delete.** Offered only for an empty cohort, because a plain `DeleteAction` would cascade
  memberships and registrations away whenever no progress record protects them. A `DeleteAction`
  subclass whose `is_offered` is false unless the cohort is empty, so the trigger is hidden while a
  stale submit still resolves; `has_permission` stays the plain `delete_cohort` check. `handle_submit` re-checks
  emptiness inside `transaction.atomic()`, with `select_for_update()` on the cohort. A cohort that is
  no longer empty answers 422 with the fragment, and nothing is deleted.
- Copy follows `.claude/skills/brand-guidelines/voice.md`. Titles are a verb plus the cohort name, and
  each button repeats its verb.
- **Quick view.** `quick_views/cohort.html` reads the status from `Cohort.is_active`, and its TODO is
  removed.

### Courses section (`CourseConfig`, `CourseDataTable`, `CourseInstanceView`)

- `CourseDataTable.get_queryset` starts from `courses_visible_to(request.user, request.organisation)`.
  Its counts and cohort cell stay scoped as they are today, and "Active Cohorts" and "Active
  Learners" both skip inactive cohorts.
- `CourseConfig.authorise_instance` raises `OrganisationScopeDenied` unless the course is in
  `courses_visible_to`.
- Delete `check_access_exempt_reason` and the `@claude` comment above it. The gap they declare is
  closed.
- Course detail shows:
  - the course details;
  - its cohort registrations in this organisation, each linking to its cohort, with a status for
    both the cohort and the registration;
  - its individual registrations in this organisation.
- No actions anywhere in the section.
- `educator_interface/tests/interface_walk.py` `_seed_instance` seeds a course that is visible in the
  walked organisation. Its docstring stops citing the exemption.

## Testing

- **Access.**
  - A member of an inactive cohort loses access through that cohort.
  - The member keeps access through an individual registration, and through an access-granting
    registration in another cohort.
  - Reactivating restores access.
  - `is_registered_for_course` and `is_registered_for_course_expression` agree on every case.
- **Resolution.** `learner_for_course` and `course_progress_by_course_for` skip an inactive cohort
  and fall through to the individual registration.
- **Peers.** Sharing an inactive cohort makes no peers, and neither does a course shared only through
  an inactive cohort's registration, on either learner's side. `peers_through` ignores an inactive
  cohort it is passed. A member keeps peers found another way. An inactive cohort keeps its
  educators, and `educators_of` still agrees with `learners_visible_to`.
- **Fan-out.** Use `django_capture_on_commit_callbacks(execute=True)`.
  - Unregister then re-register leaves one row with the same pk.
  - Re-registering mints records for members who joined while the registration was inactive.
  - It leaves existing records and their progress untouched.
- **Delete.** These cohorts are refused: one with only a removed learner's membership, and one with
  only an inactive registration. A stale delete POST against a cohort that has since gained a member
  answers 422 and deletes nothing. An empty cohort deletes.
- **Inactive is read-only.** Edit, Register and Unregister are absent on an inactive cohort. A POST to
  each answers 422 and changes nothing.
- **Permissions**, covering every new action and tab in `test_permission_matrix.py` and through
  `interface_walk`:
  - a cohort admin can register and unregister on their own cohort;
  - a cohort admin cannot see the settings tab;
  - a cohort viewer sees no write control;
  - a pasted action URL from a role without the capability answers 403.
- **Organisation isolation**, in `test_organisation_isolation.py`:
  - another organisation's cohort or registration pk answers 404 on every new tab and action;
  - a register POST naming a coming-soon course, or another site's course, answers 422 with no row
    created;
  - the course list and detail never show another organisation's cohort names, learner names or
    counts;
  - a hidden course with no registration in this organisation, including one registered only in
    another organisation, is absent from the list and its detail answers 404;
  - an out-of-scope `?cohorts-course=` value is ignored.
- **Config authorisation.** `CourseConfig` has no `check_access_exempt_reason`. A behavioural test
  shows its `authorise_instance` denies, because the existing introspection test cannot tell a stub
  from a real check.
- **Webhook absence.** Registering and unregistering a cohort create no `WebhookEvent`, unless spec 7
  landed first (Decision 5).
- **List.** Search, the show-inactive toggle and its default, the course filter, sorting on the three
  columns, and the learner count excluding removed learners. The learner page's Cohorts panel shows
  inactive cohorts and neither filter.
- **Unregister confirmation.** It names members kept by an individual registration and members kept
  by another cohort, and counts the rest.
- **Overview.** An inactive cohort still shows completion for its active registrations.
- **Educators block.** It lists cohort admins and viewers with their roles. It excludes a user whose
  `OrganisationMember` is inactive. It is absent for a cohort admin.
- **Playwright.**
  - Create with "Save and add another".
  - Deactivate then reactivate from the settings tab.
  - Register then unregister from the courses tab.
  - Delete an empty cohort.
  - The detail page at 375px.

## Open questions

These are defaults taken. Overrule any of them before planning.

- **The Interest column stays on the courses list.** It is a site-wide count of users who expressed
  interest, not organisation data. Dropping it would also close the last cross-organisation count in
  the section.
- **A cohort deadline in an inactive cohort still applies** to a member who has access another way,
  because deadlines are out of scope.
- **The learner detail page's Cohorts panel lists inactive cohorts with a badge**, rather than hiding
  them. The learner interface never lists cohorts, so "hide from learners" needs no change there.
- **The needs-attention card shows a placeholder**, not nothing, until spec 10 lands.
