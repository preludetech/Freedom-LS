# Spec: Member administration

Spec 9 of 12 in the educator interface rebuild (`spec_dd/1. next/roadmap.md`, "Educator interface
rebuild"). Source: `idea.md`. Research beside it: `research_codebase.md`, `research_prior_art.md`,
`research_best_practices.md`, `research_reference_implementations.md`, `research_ux_patterns.md`,
`research_known_complaints.md`. The idea and research say "educator" for what this spec calls a
member.

## Purpose

Today the only way to give someone a role in an organisation is a shell or a QA helper (`idea.md`,
"Why"). This spec adds a Members section to the educator interface. From it, an admin can list, add,
re-scope, remove, suspend and reactivate the people who hold a role in an organisation. Not all of
them teach: some only do administration. Every change that widens or removes access is confirmed
first.

## Terminology

| Term | Status | Meaning / source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| member | narrowed | Narrowed from `OrganisationMember`, whose docstring reads "a user who helps run an organisation's learners, whether or not they teach". Here it means a user holding an active `organisation_admin`, `cohort_admin` or `cohort_viewer` `ObjectRoleAssignment` on this organisation or one of its cohorts. A site-level admin (an active `site_admin` on this site, or a superuser; `_is_site_level_admin` in `role_assignment.py`) is a member only through such a role. Suspended members are included, and so is a member whose `OrganisationMember` row is missing. A learner is not a member unless they also hold one of these roles. Distinct from `CohortMembership`, a learner's place in a cohort, and from the glossary's "educator of a learner" (`educators_of`). |
| suspended (member) | narrowed | A member whose `OrganisationMember` row is inactive or missing. The word comes from the `set_organisation_member_active` docstring. Their assignments stay active, but `can()` ignores them. This is not `Learner` removal. |
| widening | coined | A change after which a person can do something they could not do before: a new role, a new cohort in a scope, or reactivation. No existing term covers this. |

## Scope

In:

- A Members section, listed under each organisation's navigation. It lists members, with search
  and a role filter.
- Add a member by email. The address matches an existing user, or an account is created through
  spec 7's path. The admin picks one role and, for cohort roles, the cohorts.
- Change the cohorts on a cohort role. Remove a role. Suspend and reactivate an `OrganisationMember`.
- Confirmations for widening and for loss of access.
- Rewritten role descriptions, used in the form, the confirmations and the product docs.
- An add action on the cohort overview's educators panel (`CohortEducatorsPanel`).
- Cleanup of role assignments and guardian permissions when a cohort is deleted.
- Generic panel framework additions the section needs (R4).
- Renaming the `EDUCATOR_CHANGED` event to `MEMBER_CHANGED` (R5).

Out:

- New roles, custom roles, editing permissions one by one, and a read-only "roles and permissions"
  page.
- Granting or removing `site_admin`, and anything else site-level.
- Changing a role in place. Roles add up: to move someone from cohort viewer to cohort admin, add
  the new role and remove the old one.
- Notifying the person of a grant or removal. A new account still gets the setup email.
- Resending the setup email to a member, a "pending" marker, last activity, undo, and a member
  detail page.
- Renaming `CohortEducatorsPanel`, `cohort_educators` or the cohort overview's "Educators" panel.
- Whatever the Django admin does with `SystemRoleAssignment` or `SiteRoleAssignment`.

## Decisions

1. **Only the checked entry points.** Views call `assign_role`, `remove_role` and
   `set_organisation_member_active` in `freedom_ls/learner_management/role_assignment.py`. They
   never call the raw `role_based_permissions/utils.py` functions. The grant check, the self check
   and the last-admin locks live in the checked functions and must not be duplicated.
2. **A match shows the account's name and email before confirming.** An organisation groups
   people inside a site; it does not isolate them (`docs/product/multi-tenancy-and-isolation.md`).
   Showing the match is what makes a typo visible.
3. **Site-level admins work in every organisation but are listed only through a role they hold
   in it.** A site-level admin (a superuser or an active `site_admin` on this site) has everything
   an organisation admin has in any organisation on their site. Without an `organisation_admin` or
   cohort role there, they are not listed, so the organisation's members never read them as one of
   their own.

## Requirements

### R1. Role descriptions (`freedom_ls/role_based_permissions/roles.py`)

Replace `description` on the four member-facing `BASE_ROLES` entries below. These strings are the single source. The
form, the confirmations, the help paragraph and the product docs all read `Role.description` or
copy it word for word.

| Role | `description` |
| --- | --- |
| `site_admin` | Full access to every organisation on this site. |
| `organisation_admin` | Manages learners, cohorts and members in this organisation. |
| `cohort_admin` | Manages the learners and course registrations in their cohorts. |
| `cohort_viewer` | Sees the learners and progress in their cohorts, and changes nothing. |

`display_name` values stay as they are.

### R2. Grant rules (`freedom_ls/learner_management/role_assignment.py`)

- Add `MEMBER_ROLES = ("organisation_admin", "cohort_admin", "cohort_viewer")`, a fixed tuple. A
  downstream role in `get_role_config` is never listed or offered, because it has no `assign_`
  capability.
- Add `grantable_roles(grantor: User, organisation: Organisation) -> list[GrantableRole]`, where
  `GrantableRole` is a frozen dataclass of `role: str` and `cohorts: tuple[Cohort, ...] | None`
  (a tuple, so the frozen dataclass stays hashable). It is
  the source for every form choice and every POST validation in this spec, so the two cannot
  drift. `assign_role` and `remove_role` stay the authoritative check.
  - `organisation_admin` is included when `can(grantor, ASSIGN.format(role=...), organisation)`.
    Its `cohorts` is `None`.
  - A cohort role is included with the organisation's active cohorts on which that check passes,
    and left out when there are none. The cohorts come from one queryset built from the existing
    grant builders (`_granted_cohorts` in `capabilities.py`, the `cohorts_visible_to` pattern in
    `queries.py`), not from a `can()` call per cohort.
  - A helper caches the result on the request. Every row cell and action reuses it, so its cost
    grows with neither the number of rows nor the number of cohorts.
- Add `is_widening(before: set[tuple[str, str]], after: set[tuple[str, str]]) -> bool`. Each pair
  is a role and a target key, which is the organisation's pk or a cohort's pk. It returns true when
  `after` holds any pair `before` lacks. It is a pure function, unit tested on its own.

### R3. Reactivation timestamp (`freedom_ls/role_based_permissions/utils.py`)

When `assign_object_role` reactivates an inactive row, or changes `assigned_by` on an active one,
it sets `assigned_at` to now. `assign_site_role` does the same. "Added on" is then the time of the
grant that set "Added by".

### R4. The Members section

Panel framework additions (`freedom_ls/panel_framework/views.py`), each tested in
`panel_framework`:

- A `view_capability` on `SectionConfigBase`, checked against `get_scope`. `_build_menu_items`
  leaves out a section the user lacks it for, and its pages answer 404, like any other section
  they cannot reach. Spec 5's 403 `denied_response` stays for actions.
- A list root can render row-scoped actions. `renders_in_footer` and
  `DataTablePanel.row_action_urls` exist already. What is new: `_main_for` keeps
  `renders_in_footer = False` actions out of a list root's header, and `ListViewPanel` passes
  `row_action_urls` through to cell templates.
- `ListViewConfig` may have no `instance_view`. Any instance path under such a list answers 404,
  not the `ValueError` that `get_instance_view` raises today. `walk_interface`
  (`educator_interface/tests/interface_walk.py`) skips the detail walk for such a list.
- An intro slot above a list's table, for a section's help copy.
- Empty-state copy that a table supplies, through a template or context, in place of the fixed
  "No data available".
- Extra context a section supplies to every cell template, computed once per request. The Members
  section passes the viewer and `grantable_roles` through it.

`MemberConfig(OrganisationSectionConfig, ListViewConfig)` in
`freedom_ls/educator_interface/views.py`:

- `model = User`, no `instance_view`, no row link, and a `check_access_exempt_reason`.
- The section's title and menu label are "Members".
- `view_capability` is `freedom_ls_learner_management.view_organisationmember` on the
  organisation, so cohort admins and cohort viewers never see the section.
- Register it in `interface_config` under a new `NavGroup("Administration", [MemberConfig])`.
- It must pass `invariants/test_config_authorisation.py` and `invariants/test_permission_matrix.py`
  with no new capability strings.

`MemberDataTable(DataTable)`:

- A `User` queryset with one row per member: each person who holds an active assignment in
  `MEMBER_ROLES`, either on this organisation or on one of its cohorts (active or not). Neither a
  `site_admin` assignment nor `is_superuser` adds a row or a role entry (Decision 3).
- A member whose account is turned off (`User.is_active` false) is still listed, because they
  still hold the role. Their row carries an "Account turned off" marker and offers only Remove.
- The list is built from assignments, not `OrganisationMember` rows, because spec 5 shipped no
  backfill of member rows.
- Each row prefetches those assignments with their targets and `assigned_by`, plus the person's
  `OrganisationMember` and `Learner` for this organisation. The list must not issue a query per
  row.

Columns:

- Name, with email as the secondary line. A person with no name shows their email.
- Markers beside the name:
  - "You" for the viewer.
  - "Suspended" when the `OrganisationMember` row is inactive or missing.
  - "Account turned off" when `User.is_active` is false.
  - "Also a learner" when an active `Learner` exists in this organisation. This marker is neutral
    in tone, not a warning.
- Roles. One entry per role held: the role's `display_name` and its scope.
  - The scope reads "Whole organisation" for `organisation_admin`.
  - For a cohort role it lists cohort names: the first two, then "+N more". The full list is
    available in the cell. An inactive cohort carries "(inactive)".
- Added by and added on, per role entry, from the newest active assignment for that role. A null
  `assigned_by` reads "Not recorded".

Search, filter and surrounding copy:

- `search_fields` covers first name, last name and email. Rows are sorted by name.
- `MemberRoleFilter` in `educator_interface/filters.py` is modelled on `LearnerStatusFilter`. It
  offers "All roles" (the default) and the three roles.
- One help paragraph above the table explains, in plain words, that members are the people who
  help run the organisation, whether or not they teach, and what each role allows. The role
  descriptions appear in the add form, not in the table.
- Three empty states follow the voice in `brand-guidelines`:
  - No matches, with a clear-filters link.
  - "No members in this organisation yet", with the add action when it is offered.
  - An organisation whose only member is the viewer.

### R5. Row actions (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/actions.py`, cell templates in `templates/educator_interface/data-table-cells/`)

Remove and Change cohorts act on the role in their row. At render time, `get_capability` returns
`assign_<role>` when the request carries the role. Otherwise it returns `assign_cohort_viewer`,
asked on the organisation, which keeps the matrix walk static. `assign_role` and `remove_role`
make the authoritative check on each target.

Per role entry:

- **Change cohorts**, for cohort roles. The admin ticks cohorts from that role's `grantable_roles`
  entry.
  - Cohorts outside that entry, and inactive cohorts the person already holds, are shown
    read-only and left untouched.
  - The confirmation names added cohorts as a widening and removed cohorts as a loss. A change
    that does both names both.
  - A change that removes every cohort uses the remove wording and removes the role.
  - A change that changes nothing answers 422: "Nothing changed. Tick or untick a cohort
    first."
  - All calls run in one `transaction.atomic()`.
- **Remove**. The confirmation names the person, the role, the scope and what they lose. It also
  names what is kept: their account, their other roles and any `Learner`.
  - When a site-level admin removes an organisation's last `organisation_admin`, the confirmation
    adds that the organisation will have no organisation admin.
  - When the target is a site-level admin (an active `site_admin` on this site, or a superuser),
    the confirmation drops the "loses" wording and says their site-level access is unaffected.
  - Removing someone's last role leaves their `OrganisationMember` row as it is. They drop off the
    list.

Per row:

- **Suspend** and **Reactivate**, with capability `change_organisationmember`, through
  `set_organisation_member_active`.
  - Suspend confirms like a removal: "keeps their roles, can do nothing in this organisation until
    reactivated". When a site-level admin suspends the last `organisation_admin`, it adds the same
    no-organisation-admin warning as Remove.
  - Reactivate confirms as a widening and lists the roles that take effect again. With no member
    row, it calls `ensure_organisation_member` and then `set_organisation_member_active(..., True)`.
  - Both are hidden when the target is a site-level admin, since a member row does not limit them.

The viewer's own row shows no actions. In their place are a lock icon and "You can't change your
own roles. Ask another organisation admin." Actions the viewer lacks the capability for are hidden.

Rename `EDUCATOR_CHANGED = "educatorChanged"` in `educator_interface/events.py` to
`MEMBER_CHANGED = "memberChanged"`, and update `CohortEducatorsPanel.refresh_events`. Each
successful action fires `MEMBER_CHANGED`.

### R6. Add a member (`freedom_ls/educator_interface/actions.py`, `forms.py`, `freedom_ls/accounts/account_setup.py`)

`AddMemberAction(PanelAction)` is a top-level action on `MemberConfig`, labelled "Add member". Its
capability is `add_organisationmember` on the organisation. It is offered when `grantable_roles` is
non-empty. It uses a plain `forms.Form`, following `AddLearnersAction`, and invalid input answers
422.

One action URL serves every step of the dialog. A `step` field selects the lookup or the submit,
and the submit carries `confirmed=1` once the in-dialog confirmation is accepted, following the
bulk-action `confirmed` pattern. The capability is the same at every step.

Extract the read-only part of `match_or_create_user` into a lookup helper in `account_setup.py`.
The helper strips the address, calls `normalize_email`, matches with `email__iexact`, and makes the
other-site check. `match_or_create_user` then calls it.

The dialog's steps:

1. **Email.** An htmx lookup through the helper creates nothing. It shows one of three things:
   - "Existing account: <name>, <email>".
   - "No account yet. One will be created and a setup email sent." For a new address, the form
     adds optional first-name and last-name fields.
   - The `EmailInUseOnAnotherSite` message, never that account's name.
2. **Role.** Radio cards, one per `grantable_roles` entry, each showing `display_name` with
   `description` under it. Nothing is preselected. When the organisation has no active cohorts,
   the cohort roles are absent and a line says "This organisation has no active cohorts yet."
   When `grantable_roles` leaves out, for any other reason, a role this dialog could offer, a line
   says "You can only give roles at or below your own." On the Members page that is any of the
   three member roles; on a cohort page (R7) only the two cohort roles count, so an organisation
   admin never sees the line there just because Organisation admin is not on offer.
3. **Cohorts**, for cohort roles. Checkboxes over that entry's `cohorts`, with a search box and a
   selected count. At least one cohort is required. A note says that choosing every cohort is not
   the same as organisation admin: the role covers only the cohorts chosen, not ones added later.
4. **Confirmation**, inside the same dialog. It restates the role description and the scope, and
   says whether an account will be created.
   - If the person already holds roles here, it shows their current roles beside what they will
     have after.
   - Cohorts they already hold for that role are named and skipped. If nothing new remains, the
     form answers 422: "<name> already holds this role on these cohorts." An `organisation_admin`
     they already hold answers 422: "<name> is already an organisation admin here."
   - If the person's `OrganisationMember` row is inactive, it says the role takes effect only once
     they are reactivated.
   - If the person has no `OrganisationMember` row, the grant creates an active one (the
     `post_save` receiver on `ObjectRoleAssignment` calls `ensure_organisation_member`). The
     confirmation says so, and that their existing roles here take effect with this grant. Change
     cohorts uses the same wording when it adds a cohort for such a person.
   - If the person is a site-level admin, it says they already have access to every organisation,
     and that the role lists them among this organisation's members.

On submit, everything runs inside one `transaction.atomic()`:

1. `match_or_create_user(site, email, first, last)`.
2. `assign_role` once per target.
3. If the account was created, `send_account_setup_email(user, organisation.name)`, which sends on
   commit.

A refusal rolls back the new account too.

Error copy, each answered at 422 inside the dialog:

| Cause | Message |
| --- | --- |
| `EmailInUseOnAnotherSite` | This address belongs to an account on another site. Use a different address. |
| `RefusalReason.INACTIVE_USER` | This person's account is turned off, so they can't be given a role. |
| `RefusalReason.SELF` | You can't change your own roles. Ask another organisation admin. |
| `RefusalReason.LAST_ORGANISATION_ADMIN` | <name> is this organisation's only organisation admin. Add another organisation admin first. |

`RefusalReason.NOT_PERMITTED` gets spec 5's 403 denied response (`denied_response`), whichever
action raised it. R5's actions and R7 use the same mapping.

### R7. Cohort overview educators panel (`CohortEducatorsPanel` in `views.py`)

The panel keeps its name and its "Educators" heading: on a cohort, everyone it lists holds a cohort
role there.

- `get_actions()` returns an `AddMemberToCohortAction`. It is `AddMemberAction` with the cohort
  preset as a hidden input, following `AddLearnersToCohortAction`. Its capability is
  `assign_cohort_viewer` on the cohort.
- Its role choices are the cohort roles that `grantable_roles` allows on that cohort. The action is
  offered only when there is at least one.
- The dialog's email and role steps are titled "Add a member to {cohort}", following "Add learners
  to {cohort}".
- The panel refreshes on `MEMBER_CHANGED`, as it does today on `EDUCATOR_CHANGED`.

### R8. Stale submissions

Every action re-reads the target assignment or member row when it is submitted. If the assignment
is no longer active, or the cohort has left scope, the action answers with spec 5's
`action_unavailable` fragment. It must not silently change nothing.

### R9. Cohort deletion cleanup (`freedom_ls/learner_management/signals.py`)

- A `pre_delete` receiver on `Cohort` runs inside the deleting transaction. It deletes every
  `ObjectRoleAssignment` whose target is that cohort, active or not, along with the matching
  guardian `UserObjectPermission` rows. Because it is a receiver, deletes from the Django admin are
  covered too.
- It deletes rather than deactivates: deactivated rows would leave guardian rows behind, and
  assignments whose `target` resolves to `None` would break the list's prefetch. Spec 11's audit
  log is where the history lives.
- A data migration in `learner_management` deletes the `ObjectRoleAssignment` rows whose cohort
  target no longer exists, and the guardian rows that match them. It looks up the `Cohort` content
  type inside the migration, and does nothing when that content type does not exist yet.

## Testing

Grant rules and timestamps:

- `grantable_roles` for each grantor: superuser, site admin, organisation admin, cohort admin,
  cohort viewer, and a user with no role. It excludes inactive cohorts, never offers a downstream
  role, is computed once per request, and its query count does not grow with the number of
  cohorts.
- `is_widening`: the add, remove, mixed and unchanged cases.
- Reactivation and a change of grantor reset `assigned_at` in both `assign_object_role` and
  `assign_site_role`.
- `roles.py`, the add form and the docs section show the R1 descriptions word for word.

Panel framework:

- `view_capability` hides the section from the menu, and its pages answer 404.
- Row actions render on a list root and stay out of its header.
- An instance path under a list with no `instance_view` answers 404, and the walker skips such a
  list.
- The intro slot, a table's empty-state copy and section-supplied cell context render.

The list:

- It shows exactly this organisation's members: everyone with an `organisation_admin` grant here
  or a cohort grant on one of its cohorts, including suspended members and members whose account
  is turned off.
- It leaves out other organisations' members and removed assignments.
- It leaves out superusers and site admins who hold no `organisation_admin` or cohort role here.
  A superuser or site admin who does hold one is listed with that role only.
- A learner with no role in the organisation is not listed.
- A superuser or site admin with no role in the organisation can open the section in any
  organisation on their site and use every action an organisation admin has there.
- The "You", "Suspended" (inactive and missing member row), "Account turned off" and "Also a
  learner" markers. A turned-off account's row offers only Remove.
- Inactive cohorts carry "(inactive)".
- Search, the role filter and its default, and each of the three empty states.
- A full page stays under a fixed query bound (`django_assert_max_num_queries`) when there are more
  members than one page holds (the table pages at 10).

Add:

- The lookup matches an existing user with different case and surrounding spaces, and creates
  nothing.
- An address on another site shows the message and not the account's name.
- Adding a role to a site-level admin works, and the confirmation says they already have access
  to every organisation.
- A new address creates one account and sends one setup email.
- A duplicate role on the same cohorts, and a partial overlap of cohorts.
- A refusal rolls back the new account.
- A hand-made POST with a role or cohort the grantor may not grant answers 403 and writes nothing.
- Each row of the R6 error table answers 422 inside the dialog with its message, and
  `NOT_PERMITTED` answers the 403 `denied_response`.
- Adding a role to a person with no member row makes them active, and the confirmation says so.
- The cohort roles are absent when there are no active cohorts.

Change cohorts:

- Adding, removing, both together, and removing all, which removes the role.
- Cohorts outside the grantor's reach stay untouched.

Remove:

- The confirmation copy names what is lost and what is kept.
- An organisation admin cannot remove the last organisation admin. A site-level admin can, and is
  warned.
- For a site-level target, the copy says their site-level access is unaffected.
- The member row is left as it is.

Suspend and reactivate:

- Access through `can()` stops and then returns.
- An organisation admin suspending the last organisation admin is refused. A site-level admin is
  warned.
- Reactivate with no member row creates an active one.
- Both actions are hidden for a site-level target.

Elsewhere:

- Self: the own row has no actions, and a hand-made POST against yourself is refused.
- A stale submission returns `action_unavailable`.
- Mid-session removal: a person whose role is removed gets a 404 on their next full-page request,
  and a 403 `denied_response` or `action_unavailable` on their next htmx action.
- Cohort page: the add action presets the cohort, and is hidden from anyone without a grantable
  cohort role.
- Cohort deletion removes that cohort's assignment and guardian rows and leaves other cohorts'
  rows alone. The data migration removes existing orphans, and runs cleanly on a fresh database.
- `MEMBER_CHANGED` fires on every successful action, and `CohortEducatorsPanel` refreshes on it.
- Playwright: add an existing user as cohort admin on two cohorts, change their cohorts, then
  remove the role. Run it at the QA viewports, including the mobile card layout.

## Success criteria

- An organisation admin can add an existing user and a brand-new address as a cohort admin on
  chosen cohorts without a shell. The new address receives the setup email.
- No hand-made POST lets anyone do any of these:
  - grant a role or cohort outside `grantable_roles`;
  - change their own roles;
  - leave an organisation with no organisation admin, unless a site-level admin did it.
- Each removal, suspension and widening answers with a confirmation step and writes nothing until
  it is confirmed.
- A person whose role is removed loses access on their next request: a 404 for a page, a 403 for
  an action.
- Deleting a cohort leaves no `ObjectRoleAssignment` or guardian row pointing at it.
- `roles.py`, the add form and `docs/product/educator-interface.md` show the same four
  descriptions.
- A full page of the members list stays under a fixed query bound however many members there are.
