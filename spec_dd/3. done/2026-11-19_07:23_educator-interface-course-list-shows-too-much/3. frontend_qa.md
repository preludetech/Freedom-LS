# Frontend QA: course shares between organisations

An educator now reaches another organisation's course only when an owning organisation has shared
it with their organisation in `course.md` (`shared_with:`). This run checks the Courses list, the
course detail page and its content panel, the preview dialogs, the course filters, the register
pickers and reactivation, from both demo organisations, and that learners are unaffected.

Substitute the literal port number from `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh` wherever this
document writes `$PORT`. Shell variables do not survive between tool calls.

## §0 Setup

### 0.1 Server

1. `uv run python manage.py migrate`.
2. `.claude/ds/scripts/find_available_port.sh`: note the number and use it literally from here on.
3. Start `uv run python manage.py runserver $PORT` backgrounded, as its own call.
4. Open `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/` and confirm `#debug-branch-badge` names
   `educator-interface-course-list-shows-too-much`. If not, go back to step 2.

### 0.2 Data

1. `uv run python manage.py content_save demo_content DemoDev`. It must finish with no error.
2. Through `sdd:qa-data-helper`, on site DemoDev:
   - an educator `qa_harbour_educator@email.com` (password = email) with the
     `organisation_admin` role on Harbour College (`harbour-college`), and
     `qa_northwind_educator@email.com` with `organisation_admin` on Northwind Logistics
     (`northwind-logistics`);
   - in Harbour College: a cohort `QA Harbour Cohort` with one learner
     `qa_harbour_learner@email.com`, and a second learner `qa_harbour_solo@email.com` with no
     registrations;
   - no registration of any Harbour College cohort or learner on either Northwind course yet.

### 0.3 Courses and pages

| Course | Owner | `audience` | Shared with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workplace Safety Essentials (`functionality_demo_organisation_course_northwind`) | Northwind | public | Harbour College |
| Forklift Safety Basics (`functionality_demo_organisation_course_northwind_unshared`) | Northwind | public | nobody |
| Workplace Safety Essentials (`functionality_demo_organisation_course_harbour`) | Harbour | organisation_learners | nobody |

Two courses share the title "Workplace Safety Essentials". In the educator interface Northwind's
names its owner wherever a Harbour educator sees it: "Workplace Safety Essentials (Northwind
Logistics)" in a picker, filter or dialog title, a muted "Shared by Northwind Logistics" line under
the title in a table. Harbour's own shows the title alone. The course id in the detail URL (admin >
Content engine > Courses lists each with its organisation and shares) confirms which is which.

- Login: `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/accounts/login/`. Log out between users.
- Educator Courses list: `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/educator/organisations/<org-slug>/courses`.
- Course detail: `http://127.0.0.1:$PORT/educator/organisations/<org-slug>/courses/<course-id>`.

Run every test at 1920x1080. Repeat 1.1 and 2.1 at 375x812 and 768x1024.

## §1 Harbour College educator: what they reach

### 1.1 Courses list

1. Log in as `qa_harbour_educator@email.com` and open `/educator/organisations/harbour-college/courses`.
2. Expect: Harbour's own Workplace Safety Essentials (title alone), Northwind's shared Workplace
   Safety Essentials with a muted "Shared by Northwind Logistics" line under its title, and the
   courses with no organisation (the other demo courses, title alone). The two same-titled rows are
   told apart at a glance; the "Shared by" line stays inside its cell at every viewport.
3. Expect: no "Forklift Safety Basics" row.
4. Scan the whole list (it has no search box; page through it when it is paginated): expect no
   "Forklift Safety Basics" row on any page.

### 1.2 Shared course detail and previews

1. Open Northwind's Workplace Safety Essentials from the list.
2. Expect the details panel, the "Course content" panel with its outline (Course details row and
   "Safety on site"), and the cohort and direct registration panels.
3. Press Preview on the Course details row: the course details dialog opens, titled with the course
   title and "(Preview)". Close it.
4. Press Preview on "Safety on site": the topic opens read-only in the dialog. Close it.

### 1.3 The unshared course is unreachable by URL

1. As the superuser, find Forklift Safety Basics' id in admin > Content engine > Courses.
2. As the Harbour educator, open `/educator/organisations/harbour-college/courses/<forklift-id>`.
3. Expect the interface's not-found page, not the course.
4. Open `/educator/organisations/harbour-college/courses/<forklift-id>/__panels/content/__actions/preview_course_details`
   directly. Expect a 404.

### 1.4 Filters and pickers

1. Open `/educator/organisations/harbour-college/cohorts`. Open the course filter. Expect "Workplace Safety
   Essentials (Northwind Logistics)" and Harbour's plain "Workplace Safety Essentials" among the
   choices, and no Forklift Safety Basics.
2. Same on `/educator/organisations/harbour-college/learners`.
3. Open `QA Harbour Cohort` and use its register-for-course action. Expect the course picker to
   offer "Workplace Safety Essentials (Northwind Logistics)" beside Harbour's plain "Workplace
   Safety Essentials", and not Forklift Safety Basics. Register the cohort on the shared course.
   Expect the registration to appear in the cohort's course registrations with the "Shared by
   Northwind Logistics" line under its title.
4. Open `qa_harbour_solo@email.com`'s learner page and its register-for-course action. Expect the
   same picker contents. Close without registering.
5. On the learners list select `qa_harbour_solo@email.com`, open the bulk register-for-course
   action, and check the course picker the same way. Cancel.

## §2 A course reached only through a held registration

### 2.1 Revoke the share

1. In `demo_content/functionality_demo_organisation_course_northwind/course.md` delete the
   `shared_with:` key.
2. `uv run python manage.py content_save demo_content DemoDev`.
3. As the Harbour educator open `/educator/organisations/harbour-college/courses`. Expect Northwind's Workplace
   Safety Essentials still listed (the cohort registration from 1.4 holds it).
4. Open it. Expect the details panel and the registration panels as before, and the "Course
   content" panel showing "Content preview isn't shared with your organisation." with no outline
   and no Preview button.
5. Open `.../courses/<northwind-id>/__panels/content/__actions/preview_course_details` directly.
   Expect a 404.
6. Open `qa_harbour_solo@email.com`'s learner page and its register-for-course action (the learner
   register picker uses the same registerable-course queryset as the cohort picker). Expect it no
   longer offers Northwind's Workplace Safety Essentials. Close without registering.

### 2.2 Reactivation is refused

1. Through `sdd:qa-data-helper`, give `qa_harbour_solo@email.com` an inactive individual
   registration on Northwind's Workplace Safety Essentials.
2. Open that learner's page, find the "Registrations" panel on the Overview tab, and press
   Reactivate on that row, then confirm.
3. Expect the dialog to stay open with "This course is not open to the organisation." and the
   registration still inactive.

### 2.3 Restore

1. `git checkout -- demo_content/functionality_demo_organisation_course_northwind/course.md` and
   re-run `content_save`.
2. Reload the course detail: the outline and Preview buttons are back.
3. Repeat 2.2 step 2: reactivation now succeeds and the row shows active.
