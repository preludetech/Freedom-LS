No JavaScript is needed — the browser toggles the open attribute natively, and CSS responds to it. Editorial note: Accordions are for elective depth — supplementary detail, optional context, or reference material that not every reader needs. Do not hide content that is required reading, forms part of assessed material, or that every learner needs to see.
