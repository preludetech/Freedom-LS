A region that cannot be built because its body is too large shows the reason in its place. A panel's region template extends this and fills loaded_body, and may fill skeleton_body to shape the placeholder or override region_content to wrap or replace the whole state switch. A region that overrides region_content includes partials/as_of.html itself when the panel shows the stamp. {% endcomment %}
