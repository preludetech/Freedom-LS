The panels answer a validation error with a 422 form and a role change or a vanished object with a 403 or 404 fragment marked data-htmx-swap-error, and all three have to land in the page. A response of any error status that carries an out-of-band toast is swapped too, so the toast reaches the regions below. {% endcomment %}
