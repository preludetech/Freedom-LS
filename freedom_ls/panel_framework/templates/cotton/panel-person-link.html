Attributes: name Display name (required). user_id Picks the avatar colour slot (required). secondary Email or role on a second line (default none). href The person's page. Without it the chip and name render as plain text, for a person with no page of their own. quick_view_url When set, a small trigger at the end edge opens that URL in the #quick-view drawer; at least 44px square below md. hx_nav On: the link navigates with HTMX into #main-content, as the data-table link cell does. Read through the `flag` filter. picture_url Profile picture URL for the avatar chip (default none: initials). Pass it bound (`:picture_url="..."`).
